## What this phase is, and what it is not

**This phase writes no prose.** It does not open a chapter file, it does not add a scene, it does not pay anything, it does not walk the road, and it does not resolve anything. **It consolidates the volume, it states what is owed and to whom, it hands Volume 06 a question it has not been given the answer to, and it makes the debts visible in one place.**
