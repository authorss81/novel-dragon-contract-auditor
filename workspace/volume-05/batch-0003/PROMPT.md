## Craft bounds, bound on both measures, and the five tests that find mechanical damage

1. **Emphasis is an exception and not a default. About a fifth of a chapter's lines and about a fifth of its prose text, and you must state in your batch summary which of the two you measured, and with what method, before you claim the bound holds.** **The governing measure is the text measure**: prose characters inside paired `**` runs over prose characters, where prose is every non-blank line that is not a chapter heading and is not a blockquoted line, and where runs are paired per file after a per-paragraph parity check. **Batch 0002 measures 9.3% to 16.8% on that measure, median 12.1%, and 11.5% to 19.2% on the all-lines measure, ten of ten under a fifth on both.** **The repair that works is structural and is the same repair three batches running: a run of more than one sentence is cut back to its first sentence and the sentences that follow are left in the chapter unemphasised, and a long single-sentence run is split so its first clause carries the emphasis and the rest does not. Do that from the start, and not by cutting words, and never by cutting a canon document.** **Six files in Batch 0002 were over a fifth on a first measurement and all six were fixed structurally: 0213 at 25.1%, 0215 at 29.8%, 0216 at 24.0%, 0217 at 30.0%, 0219 at 32.4%, 0220 at 33.4%.** **No more than one emphasised clause in any one speech turn**, and a speech turn is a contiguous run of one speaker's dialogue with no narration between, so a turn of four paragraphs carries one run and not four. **Pair your asterisks inside a paragraph and never across a blank line, and run a paragraph-level parity test before you close, because a `**` opened and never closed pairs with the next paragraph's `**` and swallows everything between them.** **`**` goes inside the quotation marks: `"**Text.**"` and not `"**Text."**`.** A blockquoted form, a minute entry or a schedule may keep its own typography; that is the document, not a default. **A chapter that carries a document is the chapter that will be over, and the guarantee and a finding are canon word for word: report the cost and do not break the canon.**
