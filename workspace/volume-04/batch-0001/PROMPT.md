## Craft bounds, carried from the closing batch's review, and bound on both measures this time

1. **Emphasis is an exception and not a default.** **About a fifth of a chapter's lines and about a fifth of a chapter's prose text**, and **you must state in your batch summary which of the two you measured, and with what method, before you claim the bound holds** — because **Volume 03 passes on lines at 2%–22%, median 14%, and fails on prose text at 3%–59%, median 24%, with twelve chapters above 30%, four above 40%, and Chapter 0117 the outlier at 10,172 characters of bold in a chapter of 17,173 characters of prose on fifteen lines — 9% of its lines and 59% of its text.** **A line count is not a character count, and `grep -c` counts matching lines while `grep -o | wc -l` counts occurrences.** **No more than one emphasised clause in any one speech turn.** A blockquoted form may keep its own typography; that is the form, not a default.
