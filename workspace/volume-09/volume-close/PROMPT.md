**Eight, and it is the review's arithmetic and not the page's, and the repair refused it: `chapter-0448.md` says *about nine months ago* where `chapter-0434.md` says *about six months*, and the review held that the calendar gives 0434 to 0448 as eight weeks and that the figure should therefore be eight months. **The calendar gives fourteen weeks, and the visit is at 0413, which is thirty-five weeks before 0448, and a month in this calendar is four weeks, so thirty-five weeks is eight and three-quarter months and nine is the correct figure and eight is further from it.** The figure was kept and only the sentence around it was rewritten, because the sentence was a real sixteen-word re-print and the figure inside it was not a defect. **The standing this adds is the one this project has held since Batch 0003 and which the review broke in the one place a figure was about to be overwritten on the strength of a subtraction: an interval is re-derived from the date lines and is not taken out of a record.**

**Two prompts in this project have now handed a writer a figure that was bound to an earlier page: Batch 0004's prompt handed this volume's writing phase 12,716 and 1,148,693, and Batch 0005's prompt reproduced the corrected 12,765 and 1,148,742 correctly and then printed its own first paragraph from a page two repairs old.** A close that inherits a record and finds nothing wrong with it has not been reading. **Batch 0003 refused six premises in its own prompt, Batch 0004 refused six and found a seventh, and Batch 0005 refused two and found, in its own draft, one weekday name, six instances of a forbidden phrase and eight verbatim re-prints — and the two most productive sources have been a room's furniture in a card and a sentence in a plan, and neither is a plot and neither is a craft matter.**

## EIGHT: what Volume 10 inherits, and it is a question and not a plan

**A blank got to a man who could not be found and nobody sent him, and a box of about four hundred got to a room four miles from the counter that sells them and was not opened, and the practice works, and the practice cannot be told to do anything and cannot be listed and has grown for years without anybody noticing.** A woman of twenty-four knows what she is standing ready for and says none of it out loud. A man of about sixty-one has a second mark on a sheet he cannot see and cannot write down. A man who is paid by a corner has said out loud that he is not going to be findable and has not said where the paper is now. **The question this volume hands on is how much of a thing a person can carry before the carrying is what the person is, and where the size of a thing stops being about the paper and starts being about the person who has it. That question names no person. It does not make the next asking anybody's. And nobody in nine volumes has been thanked, and the count of things anybody has asked out loud in this matter is seven, and the arrangement is still standing.**
