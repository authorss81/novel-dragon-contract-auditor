## The emphasis, the sentences, the frames, and the mechanical checks

**Emphasis, both measures, method first.** The governing measure is the text measure: **prose characters inside paired `**` runs over prose characters**, where prose is every non-blank line that is not a chapter heading, is not a blockquoted line **and is not a scene-break rule**, and runs are paired per file after a per-paragraph parity test. **Three traps: a line count is not a character count; a `**` opened and never closed inside a paragraph pairs with the next paragraph's `**` and swallows everything between them, which is why the parity test comes first; and a line measure that counts blank lines and scene-break rules in its denominator reports a chapter as disciplined because it is broken into more paragraphs, which is not a craft measure at all.** The lines measure is **non-blank prose lines carrying at least one mark over non-blank prose lines**, it does not govern, and it is reported. **A chapter that carries a document is the chapter that will be over — a guarantee, a finding, a sheet, a notice and a question in a minute book are all inherited word for word and cannot be cut at all. Report the cost. Do not break the canon.** Batch 0002's first draft ran 30.9% to 62.3% on the governing measure, sixty-four runs were cut to thirty-one, and it closed at **9.0% to 12.8% with a median of 10.9%** on the text measure and **13.2% to 21.6% with a median of 16.9%** on the lines measure, and that is a reasonable band to aim at and not a quota to hit. **No chapter of Batch 0002 carried a document and that is why its band is where it is, and a batch that must carry documents will be over and should say so rather than cut the canon.**

**No emphasis run holds more than one sentence, and no more than about one emphasised clause in any one speech turn. A run in a mouth is not exempt.** A run that ends with a full stop before its closing asterisks is the closed volume's established form and is not a two-sentence run, and a batch that counts a trailing full stop as an internal boundary will report a false fault.

**`about nine` is bound to about eight per chapter and is thinned and never varied.** The cure is to delete a counted clause or to refer to a thing instead of its number. **A sibling figure may not be substituted for one to make a number go down**, and the sibling figures are page figures: about four, about nineteen, about thirty, about forty, about forty-one, about sixty, about four hundred and thirty, about nine hundred, about two hundred and forty, about a hundred and forty, about eleven, about seven. **Exclude the page figures from the count, say which you excluded, and print the per-file count and not a total.** Note for a later writer: a method that counts `about nine` on a word boundary also counts *about nine hundred*, and Batch 0002 measured the raw figure and the page-figure-excluded figure separately and the difference was up to six a chapter, and **the figure a verifier gets is the raw one, so print both.**

**The `no form` frame is bound as variation and never as deletion.** The closed volume's last movement carried it forty-one times in twenty-eight distinct wordings, **and those twenty-eight wordings are the real state of the motif, and a writer who wants to vary should vary from those and not from a shorter list, because a list of variations that were never written is a list a later writer goes looking for.** Batch 0002 carried seventy-eight instances in seventy-seven distinct wordings on a seventy-character window and printed the new wordings in its closing record. **The frame is in mouths, not in narration, and the two narration instances in that batch were the same fact about the box of blanks. A chapter whose whole subject is a procedure that does not exist should not be a chapter that never says so, and a chapter carrying the frame zero times is a fault.**

**A question mark is used sparsely, in a bare short line, in the mouth of a person. Not one in a narrator's line and not one in a statement. Print the per-file count and not a flat total, because a batch that reports a flat count has not reported a decision.** Batch 0002 carried fourteen, per file 1 / 1 / 2 / 2 / 2 / 1 / 2 / 1 / 1 / 1.
