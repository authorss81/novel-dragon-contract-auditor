## The batch shape you are writing to

**Batch goal. Open the road and pay for opening it.** The first ten chapters found the shape of the problem and put a person in a box and a body nowhere. **This batch takes the one thing that was found and not followed — a name on a ninety-one-year page that is in four houses at the north of the ford — and the one door that was found and found shut, and it puts them in the same district, and it makes the district pay for both.** The batch's work is procedural and human and its climax is not a fight: it is the moment at which somebody has to decide whether a household will be made a party on purpose, in advance, by a person who has found out what a party is, or whether nobody will be harmed and therefore nobody will ever find out what the cut owes.

**Ordinary competence keeps winning.** A name checked against a map. A line ruled with nothing under it. Four shillings a year. A sheet counted at a door. A word chosen in a ledger in four minutes. A page read out twice for nothing. A hearing entered in the wrong form on purpose. A figure kept out of a minute on purpose. A room four hundred and thirty miles away with a method in it.

**Every win has a named cost.** A household that is a party is a household a man with a tray can ask about. A person made findable on purpose is findable for everything. A certificate of a kind that certifies itself. A shed, a store, a mill yard, a weigh-house, a kitchen and a flood terrace, and **none of them is a court.**
