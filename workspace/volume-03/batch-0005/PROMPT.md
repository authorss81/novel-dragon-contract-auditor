## What this batch is for

**This is the volume's fifth and last movement, and it is the volume close. Its subject is a rotating duty with a public expiry, and the whole of the work of Volume 03 is in it: a body has to be made able to stop.** The concrete shape of that sentence has been built across four batches and nothing is left to invent. On a day fixed by a chair, in a bay, in **the third week of the seventh month**, about thirty people in ninety-one households are going to be asked whether the Slade Cut Company will accept a duty that stops. **A body cannot answer that, cannot refuse it, and cannot be made to stop, and the only arrangement that works is a person, and there are about sixty of them needed, and there is no list.** That is the chapter the volume has been walking to and it must be the batch's spine.
