**One: the ban is on asking and not on talking, and a room with no contradiction in it is a defect.** Two people in a room may argue, refuse, explain, instruct, ignore each other, contradict each other, and finish each other's sentences. Every chapter carries at least one exchange in which another person resists what the point of view wants. What no chapter may carry is a question put to a person.

**Two: a chapter has a scene and not a description of a room.** A change of situation inside the chapter, a person who wants something in the room, another person in the way, and an ending that is a decision or a cost and not a description. A room is described once in the volume and referred to afterwards. **A room described in the same words twice across the volume is the standing failure, and the last volume's five worst instances were all the same room described twice in the same words in two chapters, and the largest was ninety-nine words, and it is the measureable kind, and Movement Two visits three of the six rooms in this volume and all three have been described in earlier volumes, so all three must be referred to and not described.**

**Three: no chapter closes on a formula.** A closing line is written for that chapter and for no other chapter in the volume, and if the same sentence shape appears in two closings, one of the two is rewritten. The motif is not the sentence. **The motif is that nothing is asked and the arrangement holds, and it can be said in a hundred ways, and a formula is not one of them, and the refrain that carries the count of things asked out loud is not to be written in a closing line of this volume.** Batch 0001 wrote ten closings and no two of them are in the same sentence shape; that is the standard, not a high bar.

**Four: the hedge is not a voice.** *About* is kept where the imprecision is the point and where the figure is on the page, and cut everywhere else. **A figure repeated in every chapter of a volume is a page figure and not a voice, and a chapter that hedges every measurement is a chapter written from a field list.** Volume 09 carries 802 lower-case instances of *about* across fifty files and 830 case-insensitively; Batch 0001 carried 176 lower-case across ten files and 184 case-insensitively after its repair, and 174 and 182 before it.

**Five: the metrics are subordinate to the craft and are recorded after the last edit, not instead of the writing.** The sentence bound, the emphasis band, the windows, the paragraph sweeps, the string sweeps and the month and weekday sweep are run, with the method named before each figure and the blind spot of each written beside its result. **A check that returns zero is evidence of nothing until the shape of the damage it is blind to has been checked, and a blind check is written down as blind in the same record that claims it clean. A corrected figure is named as corrected beside the wrong one and never overwritten.** The sentence ceiling is 85 to 88 words, and the splitter must allow a closing quotation mark between the full stop and the space, or a rule requiring whitespace immediately after the full stop swallows every closing quotation mark and reports a maximum of 239 words where the real maximum is 87.

**And the order of the work, which is the thing the size of what you have been handed actually tells you.** `outline/volume-10.md` is over 140,000 bytes and this prompt is over 20,000 and the ten cards a writer works from are a fraction of both. That is inherited project style and it is not a reason to trim a file, and the ten fields, the five movements, the locks and the declarations are load-bearing. **The order is: the cards, then the ten chapters, then the state, and the measurements last, and the measurements are not the deliverable.
