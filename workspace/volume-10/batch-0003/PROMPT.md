## Seven: the craft rules that come before every measurement in this prompt

**One: the ban is on asking and not on talking, and a room with no contradiction in it is a defect.** Two people in a room may argue, refuse, explain, instruct, ignore each other, contradict each other, and finish each other's sentences. Every chapter carries at least one exchange in which another person resists what the point of view wants. What no chapter may carry is a question put to a person.

**Two: a chapter has a scene and not a description of a room.** A change of situation inside the chapter, a person who wants something in the room, another person in the way, and an ending that is a decision or a cost and not a description. **A room is described once in the volume and referred to afterwards.** **A room described in the same words twice across the volume is the standing failure, and the last volume's five worst instances were all the same room described twice in the same words in two chapters, and the largest was ninety-nine words, and it is the measureable kind, and Movement Three visits the counter in at least four of its ten chapters and the counter was described in the volume that closed and in the movement before this one, so every one of those four must refer and not describe.** Batch 0002's repair record is the working example: it found the same sill, the same pot, the same box at the end of a shipping floor and the same nine foot of a shed floor described a second time in this volume's own words, and it replaced all four with references.

**Three: no chapter closes on a formula.** A closing line is written for that chapter and for no other chapter in the volume, and if the same sentence shape appears in two closings, one of the two is rewritten. The motif is not the sentence. **The motif is that nothing is asked and the arrangement holds, and it can be said in a hundred ways, and a formula is not one of them, and the refrain that carries the count of things asked out loud is not to be written in a closing line of this volume.** The ten closings of Batch 0002 are in ten shapes and the closing of 0470 is on a hand on a case and not on the drawer, the box and the fourth item, which three of the other nine in that batch close on.

**Four: the hedge is not a voice.** *About* is kept where the imprecision is the point and where the figure is on the page, and cut everywhere else. **A figure repeated in every chapter of a volume is a page figure and not a voice, and a chapter that hedges every measurement is a chapter written from a field list.** Volume 09 carries 802 lower-case instances of *about* across fifty files and 830 case-insensitively; Batch 0001 carried 176 and 184 across ten files; **Batch 0002 carried 148 and 152, which is 14.8 a chapter against Volume 09's 16.0, and the direction of the difference is the finding: the hedge count has come down across three batches and a hedge count that falls is a voice and not a field list.**

**Five: the metrics are subordinate to the craft and are recorded after the last edit, not instead of the writing.** The sentence bound, the emphasis band, the windows, the paragraph sweeps, the string sweeps and the month and weekday sweep are run, with the method named before each figure and the blind spot of each written beside its result. **A check that returns zero is evidence of nothing until the shape of the damage it is blind to has been checked, and a blind check is written down as blind in the same record that claims it clean. A corrected figure is named as corrected beside the wrong one and never overwritten.** The sentence ceiling is 85 to 88 words, and the splitter must allow a closing quotation mark between the full stop and the space, or a rule requiring whitespace immediately after the full stop swallows every closing quotation mark and reports a maximum of 239 words where the real maximum is 87. **Batch 0002's first draft had sixteen sentences over the ceiling, the largest a hundred and forty-eight words, and broke all sixteen; expect your own first draft to have some and expect the fixes to be cheap and to move the word count by more than a hundred.**

**And the order of the work, which is the thing the size of what you have been handed actually tells you.** `outline/volume-10.md` is over 140,000 bytes and this prompt is not small and the ten cards a writer works from are a fraction of both. That is inherited project style and it is not a reason to trim a file, and the ten fields, the five movements, the locks and the declarations are load-bearing. **The order is: the cards, then the ten chapters, then the state, and the measurements last, and the measurements are not the deliverable. Ten finished scenes is the deliverable. A word count, a sweep and a state row are what a finished batch is also made of, and they are not a summary of one.** If you find yourself writing about a check instead of into a room, that is the failure, and the room in the card is the work.
