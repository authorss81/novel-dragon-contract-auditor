## Seven: the craft rules that come before every measurement in this prompt

**One: the ban is on asking and not on talking, and a room with no contradiction in it is a defect.** Two people in a room may argue, refuse, explain, instruct, ignore each other, contradict each other, and finish each other's sentences. **Every chapter carries at least one exchange in which another person resists what the point of view wants. **Two: a chapter has a scene and not a description of a room.** A change of situation inside the chapter, a person who wants something in the room, another person in the way, and an ending that is a decision or a cost and not a description.

**Three: no chapter closes on a formula.** A closing line is written for that chapter and for no other chapter in the volume, and if the same sentence shape appears in two closings, one of the two is rewritten. **Read your ten closing lines in one column before you close and not as a similarity figure, because no n-gram measure in this repository can see a sentence written twice with a substitution every few words, and two chapters of the volume before this one close that way and four more pairs in the volume before that.** A column of ten is short enough to read and the reading comes first and the numbers second.

**Four: the hedge is not a voice.** *About* is kept where the imprecision is the point and where the figure is on the page, and is cut everywhere else. **A figure repeated in every chapter of a volume is a page figure and not a voice, and a chapter that hedges every measurement is a chapter written from a field list.** Read the figure for your own ten files at the end with `grep -roE '\babout\b' chapters/volume-12/ | wc -l` once the ten exist, and read it as a symptom of the batch and not as a target.
