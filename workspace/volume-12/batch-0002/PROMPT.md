## Seven: the craft rules that come before every measurement in this prompt

**One: the ban is on asking and not on talking, and a room with no contradiction in it is a defect.** Two people in a room may argue, refuse, explain, instruct, ignore each other, contradict each other, and finish each other's sentences. **Every chapter carries at least one exchange in which another person resists what the point of view wants. **Two: a chapter has a scene and not a description of a room.** A change of situation inside the chapter, a person who wants something in the room, another person in the way, and an ending that is a decision or a cost and not a description.

**Three: no chapter closes on a formula.** A closing line is written for that chapter and for no other chapter in the volume, and if the same sentence shape appears in two closings, one of the two is rewritten. **Read your ten closing lines in one column before you close and not as a similarity figure, because no n-gram measure in this repository can see a sentence written twice with a substitution every few words, and two chapters of the volume behind this one close that way and four more pairs in the volume behind that.** The previous batch read its ten twice before it closed and found a pair on the first reading that no measure would have found. A column of ten is short enough to read and the reading comes first and the numbers second.

**Four: the hedge is not a voice.** *About* is kept where the imprecision is the point and where the figure is on the page, and is cut everywhere else. **A figure repeated in every chapter of a volume is a page figure and not a voice, and a chapter that hedges every measurement is a chapter written from a field list.** Read the figure for your own ten files at the end with `grep -roE '\babout\b' chapters/volume-12/ | wc -l` once the ten exist, and read it as a symptom of the batch and not as a target.

**Five: the male lead is in the room, and the floor is a floor on the page and not a number.** **The floor is counted by reading and never by a string, and the two obvious strings in this matter are carried by other living people who are not merges.** In the previous batch `grep -l 'Kest'` returned nothing at all across its ten files and `grep -il 'thirty-eight'` returned two, and the two were exactly the chapters he is in person in, and the third chapter that carries him was invisible to both, which is the whole of what the two sweeps cannot see. **A ten-file check is not a smaller version of a volume-wide check; it is a different instrument, and the one thing it is good for is finding out what it cannot see.**

**Six: the metrics are subordinate to the craft and are recorded after the last edit, not instead of the writing.** **A check that returns zero is evidence of nothing until the shape of the damage it is blind to has been checked, and a blind check is written down as blind in the same record that claims it clean.** A corrected figure is named as corrected beside the wrong one and never overwritten, and **a figure in a state file is a copy of a figure, and a copy is how this project's numbers go stale, and a phase that measures a live file and then writes to that file has printed a figure that was true at the moment it was taken and false about the file its reader opens.
