## Six: the craft rules that come before every measurement in this prompt

**One: the ban is on asking and not on talking, and a room with no contradiction in it is a defect.** Two people in a room may argue, refuse, explain, instruct, ignore each other, contradict each other, and finish each other's sentences. **Every chapter carries at least one exchange in which another person resists what the point of view wants. A room is not a scene until somebody in it is contradicted by somebody else in it, and a movement is not a list of rooms, and a batch of vignettes about unnamed functionaries is not a batch of this novel.**

**Two: a chapter has a scene and not a description of a room.** A change of situation inside the chapter, a person who wants something in the room, another person in the way, and an ending that is a decision or a cost and not a description. A room is described once and referred to afterwards.

**Three: no chapter closes on a formula.** A closing line is written for that chapter and for no other chapter in the volume, and if the same sentence shape appears in two closings, one of the two is rewritten. **The motif is not the sentence — the motif is that nothing is asked and the arrangement holds, and it can be said in a hundred ways and a formula is not one of them, and the refrain that carries the count of things asked out loud is not to be written in a closing line.** The last volume closed two of its fifty chapters on the same sentence written twice in different words, with a substitution every few words, and every n-gram measure in this repository was satisfied by the pair at once and no instrument in this repository could see it; a batch of ten measured against itself cannot see a pair that is seven chapters from the batch boundary.

**Four: the hedge is not a voice.** *About* is kept where the imprecision is the point and where the figure is on the page, and is cut everywhere else. **A figure repeated in every chapter of a volume is a page figure and not a voice, and a chapter that hedges every measurement is a chapter written from a field list.** For scale, `grep -roE '\babout\b' chapters/volume-10/ | wc -l` and the same with `-i` give the count over the last volume; re-run them over your own ten files before you close and take the answer your run gives.

**Five: the male lead is in the room, and the floor is a floor on the page and not a number.** He is on the page, in person or as a person whose absence has a named cost in the room he is not in, in at least three chapters of every movement, in person in at least two of the three, and his want is visible in at least one. **For this batch the three chapters are fixed in the cards: one in person with the want visible, one named in a mouth with the absence priced in the room he is not in, and one in person.** The floor is counted by reading and never by a string, and **the two obvious strings in this matter are carried by other living people who are not merges**: `grep -l 'Kest' chapters/volume-10/*.md | wc -l` and `grep -il 'thirty-eight' chapters/volume-10/*.md | wc -l` give the two sweeps over the last volume and the two sets are not one set with one extra in it, because a man of about thirty-eight who is not him is on the page and a chapter can carry the age and not the name. **A ten-file check is not a smaller version of a volume-wide check; it is a different instrument, and the one thing it is good for is finding out what it cannot see.**

**Six: the metrics are subordinate to the craft and are recorded after the last edit, not instead of the writing.** **A check that returns zero is evidence of nothing until the shape of the damage it is blind to has been checked, and a blind check is written down as blind in the same record that claims it clean.** A corrected figure is named as corrected beside the wrong one and never overwritten, and **a figure in a state file is a copy of a figure, and a copy is how this project's numbers go stale, and a phase that measures a live file and then writes to that file has printed a figure that was true at the moment it was taken and false about the file its reader opens.** When you print a figure, print when you took the reading. **And a sentence bound is a ceiling and not a target, and the two errors are opposite: a batch that writes everything short has over-corrected and a batch that writes a hundred-and-eighty-word sentence has under-corrected, and only one of those shows up in a sweep.**

**And first, the thing that is true about the size of what you have been handed, because it is a warning and not a defect.** The outline, the card file, the state layer and this prompt together are a great deal more text than the ten cards you actually work from, and it is inherited project style and it is not a reason to trim a file, and the fields, the movements, the locks and the declarations are all load-bearing. **It is a reason to know the order of the work, and the order is: the cards, then the ten chapters, then the state, and the measurements last, and the measurements are not the deliverable. Ten finished scenes is the deliverable. If you find yourself writing about a check instead of into a room, that is the failure, and the room in the card is the work.**
