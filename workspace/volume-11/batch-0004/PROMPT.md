## Seven: the craft rules that come before every measurement in this prompt

**One: the ban is on asking and not on talking, and a room with no contradiction in it is a defect.** Two people in a room may argue, refuse, explain, instruct, ignore each other, contradict each other, and finish each other's sentences. **Every chapter carries at least one exchange in which another person resists what the point of view wants. A room is not a scene until somebody in it is contradicted by somebody else in it, and a movement is not a list of rooms, and a batch of vignettes about unnamed functionaries is not a batch of this novel.** And this movement is the one where a room with two people in it and no contradiction between them is the exact failure the volume is about.

**Two: a chapter has a scene and not a description of a room.** A change of situation inside the chapter, a person who wants something in the room, another person in the way, and an ending that is a decision or a cost and not a description. A room is described once in the volume and referred to afterwards. **A room described in the same words twice across the volume is the standing failure of this manuscript and it is measured, and the instrument for it is at rule nine.**
