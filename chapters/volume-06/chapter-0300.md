# Chapter 300: Two People, No List, And About Nine Hundred Buildings Nobody Has Worked Out

The room over the chandler's shop is taken by the week and there is a table in it and a chair stood against the wall the bed is against, and a window about a foot wide that looks at another house's back, and about four feet of that wall is bare with a nail in it and nothing has ever hung on the nail. There are four steps and a landing. The shop underneath keeps tar and rope and lamp oil and the smell comes up between the boards all day and nobody on that lane has ever said a word to him about it.

He is thirty-four or thereabouts, and he writes out other men's sums so that they come out even, and he is not on anybody's list this week. He has a house with a door on it and a wife. He did a thing on purpose in a room with a rail about four feet high in it about three weeks ago and it was four words long, and it was about twelve days ago that he said the figure out loud to this room once and not again.

This is the second day of the second week of the twelfth month of the year after the year after next, and it is about the fifth hour, and the lamp is not lit.

---

He heard about the store four hundred yards off a bay on the third day of this week, at second hand, and then at second hand's second hand, and he did not go and look, and there was nothing in it he could act on and he would have gone if there had been.

A man of about fifty-five with a chain, who cannot read a paragraph, was in it and could not read what was said in it and repeated it that evening to a man of about twenty-eight in a bay, who repeated it on the second day to a man of about thirty at a gate, who said it in a room where a fair hand was writing out a sum and did not say it as a thing that had been done to him.

A man who says a thing out loud in a room is findable, and about four hours later a man he has never met has said it in a room two streets off a street that goes down to the river road, and neither of them can be asked about it, and the whole of what it says is that a man of thirty-eight took the next one onto himself and said out loud what it would cost and the cost was a day of work and a mark.

**He did not go.** **A man who goes and finds out what came of a thing he has heard about is a man who has been findable by the hearing of it.**

---

And then he got to the part of it that took the rest of the afternoon, and it is the whole of what this last two years has been for, and it is not a speech and it took about four minutes.

There is a practice, and a practice is a count of things done and not of people, and a practice with one person in it is stopped by that person.

**There are two of it in now, and they are about four hundred and thirty miles apart, and there is no list of the two of them and no form and no heading and no fund, and nobody in either building knows that the other one exists.**

One of them is a man of about thirty-four with a fair hand and a room taken by the week. The other one is a man of thirty-eight with a labour a gate can refuse and a mark about three inches long on the inside of his wrist that reads nothing and decides nothing and does not know where the ford is, and the wage is the price.

And neither of them has said the other's name out loud, and there is no instrument in this empire that would let either of them say it to the other, and no fund behind a thing anybody could pay for, and nobody is going to be told.

---

And then the part that is not a practice and is the handoff, and he worked it out at about the fifth hour with the table empty in front of him.

About nine hundred entries a year still come in under a heading, and the heading is a class, and a class is a heading, and about nine hundred of them go into about nine hundred buildings.

**A single return goes to one district, and this empire has about nine hundred districts, and a sheet that goes to one district tells the one district and nobody else, and about nine hundred people a year are entered by a second hand and not one of the second hands has been asked whether the box ought to have been filled.**

**And nobody has worked out how a thing is asked in about nine hundred buildings at once by people who cannot be instructed to ask it.**

That is the whole of what is handed on, and it is a shape and not an instrument, and there is no form for it, and he has not got a way of doing anything about it and is not going to have one by the end of this month.

---

**And none of the other things moved, and he went through them at about the sixth hour because he goes through them about once in four weeks and this is the once.**

The two people in the boxes are in the boxes. The form that put them there has not been asked to be void by anybody, and there is no procedure for it, and there never has been, and a woman of twenty about nine miles off a building off the old river road is in one and a foreman of fifty-one on a bank about four hundred and thirty miles down this river is in the other, and nobody has asked either of them anything.

The question in the second of the eleven books is still a question. It did not become an answer and it did not become an order, because a minute with an answer in it is an order and a clerk of that office has not entered one in nineteen years and there is no form that lets her, and she said out loud in a room in the third week of the eleventh month that nobody is going to ask her a second time, and nobody is going to.

Nobody was thanked. The woman of about forty with a scuttle has not been thanked and is not going to be and is not in this matter. The man of about thirty from the second gang has not been thanked for a word he said out loud in a bay in the first week of the first month and is not going to be. The man of thirty-eight was not thanked in a store four hundred yards off and did not expect it. His wife was not thanked for a thing she said in a kitchen about six weeks before a room with a rail in it, and nobody has been thanked anywhere in this matter, and there is not one form anywhere in this empire that calls for a person to be thanked for asking a question, and there never is going to be one.

---

And about nineteen people are still behind about four hundred and forty foot of bank, and nobody has asked them again and nobody is going to, and a guarantee is still standing offered and unanswered with about sixty children under sixteen inside it, and it is not in anybody's words tonight and no child in it has a name anybody has said.

A bill at Lowcross stands at nineteen pounds three and fourpence and is unpaid and nobody is liable, and he did not pay it and he is not the reason it is unpaid.

About four men in that bay cannot read a paragraph and have never been asked one question about a number, and were not asked one this week either, and a girl of seventeen at the back of that bay on a written engagement at the rate the list is set at has not been spoken to, has not been thanked, has not been sent for, and is not going to start.

---

And the two documents stay two documents, four hundred and thirty miles apart, in two buildings, with no instrument anywhere joining them, and the one that is in no reply is still in no reply, and neither of them is named out loud tonight and neither of them is going to be.

The office of a seat is a document and not an office, and its holder is not named and is not examinable, and the name at the foot of the first notice of a seat has not been said out loud in this room and is not going to be.

**And nobody has worked out who it is, and the number is seven, and a count that counted people would be six.**

That is the difference and it is the whole of the difference, and the count does not care that the two men in it are about four hundred and thirty miles apart and have never met and are not going to be put in a room by anything in this empire, and there is no form for it and there is not going to be one.

---

He put his hand flat on the table at about the seventh hour and the table had nothing on it and the nail in about four feet of bare wall had nothing on it, and the light had gone off the lane about an hour ago and the lamp was not lit and he did not light it.

The lamp in that room is not lit until about the seventh hour and it is past the seventh hour, and a man who is on nobody's paper is not handed a count and is not going to be handed one. The fourth day of the third week of the eleventh month was the day he did the second one, and he is not going to be asked about it, and nobody is going to be thanked for it. The room over the chandler's shop has a table in it and a chair stood against a wall and about four feet of bare wall, and nothing is hanging on any of it.

And the number of things anybody has asked out loud in this matter is seven, and it was six a year ago, and the whole of what has happened in between is that one person in a bay said a word out loud, and one person in a room with a rail asked a question out loud, and nobody was thanked for either, and the two of them are about four hundred and thirty miles apart. A single return goes to one district, and this empire has about nine hundred.
