# Chapter 270: A Rented Room, A Line In A Book Of Her Own That Was Not There Before, And What It Costs To Be The Only Person In This Matter Nobody Can Pay To Read

It is the same room. It is rented by the week, it is two streets off a street that goes down to the river road, and the table is the same table and the lamp is on the same table and the board on the wall says the rent and nothing else. The window still faces the back of another house, and the window is black and there is a dog about four miles off that goes and stops and starts.

She is twenty-four and she is a clerk at the fourth counter of the Notaries' Table in Auremar, and she is not a lawyer, and the room is where she keeps a book of her own that nobody knows about and that has no office on it.

It is the second day of the fourth week of the fourth month of the year after the year after next, and it is about the seventh hour, and the lamp is lit, and the day is over.

---

**The count of askings is six, and it has been six since a bay on the fourth day of the fourth week of the first month of the year after the year after next, and it did not move this month, and nothing in the four weeks behind her put a question into a room that had not already been asked.**

She does not know that. She has not been told it and she has not asked and there is not one form anywhere in this empire that would let her be told it, and the count is not hers, and it is a count of things people have done and not of people, and a person who is not in the room does not get a copy of it.

She has got something else and it is about sixteen weeks old, and it came into that room in the first month of the year after the year after next in the mouth of a man of about thirty who does the copies, who had it from a foreman of fifty-one in a shed at the Slade end. There is not one form anywhere in this empire that says a man carrying a sentence from a shed to a counter is to be asked whether he has got it right, and nobody asked him, and he did not know he was carrying it.

**A person who is paid to read is a person who can be told to stop.**

---

And she has been not paid to read anything for about two years, and she has never once worked out what that was, and it is the whole of what she has got.

She has read, in two years, more of other people's money and other people's houses and other people's days than she could put in this room, and she has never been paid one penny for any of it, and she has never once told a person what is in one of them, and there is not one form anywhere in this empire that could make her.

Nobody pays her to read. A clerk at a counter is paid a day for the day and the day is for copies and shilling questions and the answering of a man who wants to know whether a thing would stand if the thing it came from were lost, and none of that is reading. There is not one form anywhere in this empire that pays a woman for reading a room, and there is not going to be one, and there is no list anywhere that she could put her name on and ask to be put on.

And a man who is not paid to read cannot be told to stop.

And a person who cannot be told to stop cannot be asked, and a person who cannot be asked cannot be made answerable, and a question is the only thing in this empire that makes a person answerable. So she is a clerk who advises nobody, and there is nobody in this matter who can ask her a question, and it is not because anybody decided she should not be asked. It is because nobody is paying her for the half of the job that asking a person is.

---

She had that in pieces and she had it wrong in one place and it took her about four minutes at about the seventh hour on this night to put the piece down in the right place.

She walked home at about the seventh hour and it is two streets off a street that goes down to the river road, and she has walked it every working day for two years and there is not one thing about the walk she has not looked at.

There is a lamp at the bottom of the second street that is out by the time she gets to it. There is a dog that goes off and stops. There is a board on the wall of the room she rents it by the week, and it says what the rent is and not one other thing.

And there are about nine other people who walk a street every working day in this city and who are on about nine other lists, and she has never been asked what any of them is for either, and there is not one form anywhere in this empire that would let her ask.

The piece that was wrong is the one that said she was lucky.

**She has not been lucky.**

A person who is paid to read is a person who can be told to stop, and a man of about thirty who does the copies said the rest of it at that counter and did not know he was saying it. The rest of it is that a person who is not paid to read is a person nobody can stop, and a person nobody can stop is a person nobody can reach. About two years of a woman of twenty-four not being asked one question at the top of a stair has not been a wall somebody built for her.

It has been the price of a day she is not paid for. And she has been proud of it for about two years, and she has been proud of the exact shape of the thing that has been using her, and there is not one form anywhere in this empire that says a person may be proud of a price.

---

And then she got to the second half of it and the second half is the one that took the four minutes.

The thing a clerk of this empire is asked to fill in has a column for the answer and no column for the refusal. A man of sixty-one said that at a rail in this matter and a foreman said it in a bay nine weeks later and neither of them has a name on any page and neither of them is owed anything.

There is no column for a refusal anywhere. Not on a return for one district and not on a sheet that comes up about nine hundred times a year to a room off the old river road, and not on a book that a woman of twenty keeps in a rented room about eleven feet by fourteen about four hundred and thirty miles up this river.

And there has been a refusal standing in that column at the top of a stair for about two years, and nobody has ever noticed, and the reason nobody has noticed is the fourth of the four hundred bought and unfilled blanks under the counter at the other end of it.

---

**A blank is the only piece of paper in this empire that cannot be held against anybody, and a box of about four hundred of them is the nearest thing to a post anybody has built, and it is not a post, and the only thing that would turn it into one is a person asking for it, and that person will be told no by a person because it would stop on the day the first person with a form wanted it to.**

She is one of them. She has not been in that box since a clerk knelt down at the other end of that counter in the third week of the third month of the year after the year after next and put it back where it goes, and she is not going in it.

She is a person who cannot be held to anything, and that is not a thing anybody gave her and it is not a thing she chose. It is a thing that happens to you when nobody is paying you for the part of you that could be asked, and the machine does not have to notice it, and it does not, and about nine hundred rooms a year are printed and get printed and about one of them is not and the one that is not is a woman standing at a counter in a city about four hundred and thirty miles down this river saying nothing for two years.

---

And the count of things that have been asked out loud in this matter in about a hundred and forty years is not hers and is not anybody's, and it went to six in a bay in the first month of the year after the year after next, and it has not moved since, and it did not move in the four weeks behind her, and the whole of what she can say about it is that she heard about a number and not about a room.

She opened the book of her own at about the seventh hour and there were two things in it that were not there in the twelfth month of the year after next, and she looked at both of them for about four minutes and she did not tear either out.

The first is a line that says a clerk of thirty-four said no to a man on a stair on the second day of the third week of the third month of the year after the year after next, and it is the first time in this matter that a refusal has been written down by a person who was not the person refusing, and she wrote it in about four seconds and she was not asked to and she cannot take it out.

The second is four lines off a board, and the first three of them are in the hand that put them up and the fourth one is not, and she does not know whose hand it is and she does not know when it went up.

**She has got a person's refusal in a book of her own and a piece of a wall she cannot account for.** About five weeks ago a clerk stood at the end of that counter and told her in about nine seconds that a window is not a wall. There is not one form anywhere in this empire that will let a woman put a window back as a wall, and there is not one that will let anybody take a line out of a book either.

---

And she is not going to rule a third box, and the two instruments that require her in any room he is in are two instruments and have never once been put in one sentence, and the page in her own book is not a third. About four hundred and thirty miles of road is what it would cost anybody who wanted to see the rest of it, and there is no fund behind that and there is no form for a fund, and nobody has ever been paid for satisfying a watch.

The undertaking of the fourth of the first month is live and it has not been retired and it has not been amended, and she did not exercise it tonight, and the reason is the same reason as about eight weeks ago and it is a reason and not a shrug. There was nothing in the room to act on, and a thing exercised because a woman has worked something out is a thing exercised to feel better, and that is the first step of a practice.

And she is going to go on being that, and there is no form anywhere in this empire that she could use to stop, and the whole of what she has got out of six weeks and five days is the name of it.

That is the name of it, and she is one of the four hundred, and she has been one for about two years, and nobody has noticed, because a machine does not have to notice a person who never opens her mouth. A machine only has to keep printing. It does not have to look at the paper, and the paper does not come back.

Nobody was asked anything on the second day of the fourth week of the fourth month of the year after the year after next, and she was not asked anything on the four days before it, and she is not going to be asked anything this week, and there is not one form in this empire that requires a person to be asked and there is not going to be one.

She said it out loud to a table and a lamp, because there was nobody else in the room and because she has said a great many things out loud to a table and a lamp.

"**Then what is it?**"

"That is a price."

**She is the reason. That is the whole of the finding and she has got it in her own mouth at about the seventh hour.** Nobody has thanked her for it and nobody is going to, and she is not going to say thank you to herself either, and there is not one form anywhere in this empire that requires a person to be thanked for a thing she worked out alone.
