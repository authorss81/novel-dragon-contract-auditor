# Chapter 261: Six Weeks And Five Days After A Day On Which Nothing Was Asked, And A Woman Of Twenty-Four Who Has Worked Out What Her Own Habit Is

The room is rented by the week and it is two streets off a street that goes down to the river road, and there is a table in it and a lamp on the table and a board on the wall and a window that faces the back of another house, and about nine miles off in the other direction there is a counter at the top of a stair where she has stood every working day for two years.

She is twenty-four and she is a clerk at the fourth counter of the Notaries' Table in Auremar, and she is not a lawyer, and the room is where she keeps a book of her own that nobody knows about and that has no office on it.

It is the second day of the third week of the second month of the year after the year after next, and it is about the seventh hour, and the lamp is lit, and the day is over.

---

Nobody asked her one thing, and it is the eleventh time in a row, and she has stopped keeping count of what that does to a person.

About nine people came up that stair and about four of them wanted something out of her and got it. A man wanted to know whether a copy made in a room with a broken window carried the same weight as one made anywhere else, and it does, and she told him so, and he paid fourpence and said thank you and went off down four flights without hurrying.

A woman of about forty wanted a blank and asked whether she had to hand it in filled, and she does not.

"Do I have to hand it in filled?"

"You do not. I have never once had to and there is no form anywhere in this empire that says you do." The woman said that the last one she had was from a man who told her the opposite, and Marn Ottery said then that that man was wrong about two things.

And a boy of about fifteen stood at the wrong end of the counter for about eleven minutes with a copy in his hand, and read it, and did not buy anything, and went down again.

---

The fourth day of the fourth week of the twelfth month of the year after next is six weeks and five days ago, and on that day she noticed that nobody asked her one question, and she wrote down in her own book that nobody asked her one question, and she treated it as a thing that had been decided.

Nobody decided it. That is the whole of what she has in the six weeks and five days.

**There is no form anywhere in this empire that requires a person to be asked, and there is not going to be one, and if there were one it would be the only dangerous thing anybody in this building ever put their name to.** A protection somebody is maintaining can be withdrawn. A thing that is simply still true on a second day because it was true on a fourth day is not a protection at all, it is a coincidence that has lasted two years, and she has been standing behind it calling it a wall and it is not a wall and there is nobody on the other side of it holding anything up.

She worked the whole of that out on the fourth night after the day in the twelfth month and she has been over it on about nine evenings since and it has not got a seam in it, and the not-getting is what has changed and it is not a small thing.

---

And the day itself was ordinary, and she has noticed for about two years that the days on which nobody asks her a question are the days on which she gets the most done, and she has never worked out whether that is a fact about her or a fact about a counter.

And the wall is not in the room either. The wall is that nobody has ever wanted to know what she thinks, and that is a thing about nine hundred people who came up that stair in two years and about four of whom wanted something out of her on any given day, and not one of whom has ever wanted a second thing from her that was not on a wall behind her.

She has had two years of being the only person in a room who knows what a question is, and there is not one form anywhere in this empire that says a person may be promoted for it.

There are three people in that room and she is the youngest of the three and the only one of the three who came to it on purpose. A man of about thirty does the copies and has been there four years. A woman of about twenty has been there a year and is not in this matter at all. Neither of them has ever asked her what she is working out at about the seventh hour, because they are not there at about the seventh hour, because the counter shuts at the sixth.

That is the arrangement and it has been the arrangement for two years and it is the whole of what she has got, and the whole of what she has got is that two people in a room are not in a room at the seventh hour.

The book of her own is on the table and one page of it is ruled by hand in her own hand, and on that page there are two boxes.

Both of the boxes have the same name in them.

She has not ruled a third and she is not going to, and the reason is the reason she gave in the twelfth month of the year after next and it is the same reason and not a new one, and it has not moved, and it is that a day will come when she wants to and it has not been one of them, and she is not going to tell anybody how many there are going to be.

She looked at the two of them for about four minutes and then she did the thing she has been not doing, which is that she put the open book face down on the table with her hand flat on the back of it, and left it there while she ate.

---

And the two things that require her in any room he is in are two things, and she has never once put them together, and she is not going to.

One is a paper that puts her in his rooms. It is dated and it has her name on it and it is a document and it can be produced.

The other is a watch, and the watch requires a person who is not from the office that cannot be named to be in any room he is in, and she is that person, and the watch has no name on it and no seal and no place it can be sent to.

She went at it from a different end this week, which is the only thing that is new, and the end she went at it from was the money, because the money is the part nobody argues about.

There is no fund behind that watch. There is no form for a fund anywhere in this empire, and there has never been one, and nobody has ever been paid for satisfying it, and nobody ever is going to be. Four years of it and not one penny has come out of a room to pay a woman for being in one, and she has been the whole of that arrangement, and she has never asked for the money and there is nowhere to ask.

---

The four of them in the drawer under the table are a thing she is not going to think about tonight and she has written a rule about herself that says so, and the rule is from the twelfth month of last year and it says that if she uses a thing then it needs a reason, and she has not used it.

**And the reason she is going to give, when she gives it, is not the reason she gave in the third week of the second month, and she checked that too.** In the third week of the second month the reason was that she was proud of a thing nobody could check. Tonight the reason is that a habit is a practice and she has spent six weeks and five days finding out what a practice is, and she is not going to start one at about the seventh hour in a room that is rented by the week.

Then she did the part that took about four minutes and it is the reason the lamp is still lit.

A watch that cannot be sent to anybody and cannot be produced and cannot be produced against anybody is not enforced by anything at all except her, and she had not known that until she wrote it out in her own hand in her own book on a table in a rented room, and now she knows it and she cannot unknow it.

She has been in every room he has been in for about four years, on nearly every day that was not a working day at that stair, and she has never once been late, and she has never once been asked whether she would come, and there has never once been a question in the room about where she was.

If she stopped this week then nothing would happen.

**Nothing would happen for about four days.** A man would be a man who had been in a room with nobody, and a room with nobody in it has no clerk's name at the foot of it, and there is not one form anywhere in this empire that would put a person in a room and could not, and that is not a sentence anybody has ever had to think about because nobody has ever done it to see.

And then, on about the fifth day, something would come, from somebody, and it would not come to her, because there is no address to send it to and no person to serve, and she would find out what had happened the way everybody else in this matter finds out, which is that a lane is a lane and a door is a door and about nine mouths inside a week.

---

She sat with that for about four minutes and it did not get better and she did not try to make it better.

**Then she worked out the other half of it, and the other half is the part she is going to have for the rest of her life.**

A thing that one person does every day for four years, with no form behind it and no fund behind it and no register and no heading and nobody else in it, is a practice. A practice is the only thing in this empire that grows into a post without anybody deciding anything, and the reason it grows is that it works, and the reason it works is that nobody has ever tested it.

**She is the only person who can stop it.**

And then, out loud, to a table and a lamp, in a room that is rented by the week:

"I am not going to do it a second time on purpose to find out whether it stops, and I want it written down that I thought about it for about four minutes and that is the whole of what I did with it, and that the four minutes are the first four minutes anybody in this building has ever spent on a thing they were not going to do."

---

There is a rule from the fourth of the first month that she made for herself and it is live and it has not been retired and it has not been amended, and tonight she looked at it for about four minutes and she did not exercise it. There is another one, older, from the twelfth month of last year, and it says that if she uses a thing then it needs a reason, and she has not used it, and a correct non-use is as good as a use.

The reason is a reason and it is a new one and she checked that it was new before she used it. A thing exercised because a woman has worked something out in a room is a thing exercised to feel better, and that is the first step of a practice, and she has just spent a week learning what a practice is and she is not going to build one out of the only instrument she has.

The four of them in the drawer under the table were where they were, and not one has been opened, and there is not one form anywhere in this empire that says a person has to send one of them, and she did not open it and did not think about it.

---

**There is a thing she has not said out loud in a room in about two years and she is going to say it to a lamp, and it is not a confession and it is not a complaint.**

If she stopped, and nobody found out for about four days, and then something came, then she would find out about it in the ordinary way. A lane is a lane. A door is a door. A woman of about thirty-five with a wet hood is a woman of about thirty-five with a wet hood, and she came to a door about four hundred yards from a bay in the first month of the year after the year after next asking about a day, and nobody sent her.

That is the whole of the arrangement and the whole of the protection and it does not have a single person in it. There is nobody standing anywhere holding this up on purpose. That is what is new and it has taken her six weeks and five days and it is not going to get better by being looked at.

About four miles off somebody's dog went and stopped and started again, and the lamp made the window black, and she squared the two boxes towards each other and did not count them because she knows the number.

**She has wanted to be asked every working day since about the seventh month of next year and she has not been asked since the second day of the first week of the first month of the year after the year after next, and the thing she has found out in six weeks and five days is that nobody was keeping her safe.**

Nobody is going to ask her. Nobody is going to thank her, and there is not one form anywhere in this empire that requires a person to be thanked for having worked something out at about the seventh hour, and there is not going to be one.
