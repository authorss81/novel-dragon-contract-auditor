# Chapter 272: A Corridor About Four Hundred Yards Long Off A Flood Terrace, A Lamp On A Hook At The End Of It, A Door Nine Foot From That, And A Man Of About Fifty-Five With A Chain Who Has Gone Past The Mouth Of It Twice A Day For Nine Years And Been Sent Down It Once

The shed at the Slade end is nine foot by eleven, with a bench along its north wall and a lamp in it that is not lit until about the seventh hour, and the bay where nine men work stands about four hundred yards off the door of it. Between the two is a cut in a flood terrace and a way in along the top of it, about four hundred yards of it, and nobody who has ever been inside that way has ever called it a corridor, and he has used the word himself twice, both times in his own head.

He is about fifty-five and he has a chain, and the chain is on him, and he cannot read a paragraph. This is the second day of the second week of the fifth month of the year after the year after next, and it is about the fourth hour, and the lamp in the shed is not lit.

---

**The mouth of it is on his way.** It has been on his way twice a day for about nine years, going to the bay in the morning and coming back in the afternoon, and he has gone past the mouth of it four thousand times or more and he has never once been inside it.

The mouth of it is a step down off the terrace and a step up out of it, and he goes down the step and up it twice a day for nine years without turning his head at the bottom of it, and his knees know the step and nothing else about the place does.

There is nothing at the end of it that is his. That is the whole of the reason and it has never needed saying out loud to anybody, and there is not one form anywhere in this empire that says a man is to go into a place where there is nothing of his.

---

A foreman of fifty-one put a lamp in his hand at about the fourth hour and said one sentence, which was that the hook at the far end wants looking at.

"Which hook?"

"The far one."

"And who looks at it."

"That will be you," she said, and went back to the bench, and did not look up while she said it and did not look at him while he was standing there, and that woman has looked at him four seconds a week for about nine years, and this is the first time in all of it that she has sent him anywhere.

There is no form anywhere in this empire that says who looks at a lamp hook. It goes on a list of about four hundred things a year and the list does not have a column for a hook and it does not have a column for the man, and the reason a man of about fifty-five with a chain is the one who is sent is not that anybody decided he would be and it is that there is nobody else in the building anybody would be able to say they had decided it about.

---

**That is the ordinary use of a man who is not looked at, and it is not a punishment and it is not a favour.** It is the whole of what nine years have given him, and nine men know it, and it is not a wall and it is not a door and it has never once got him anything.

It is that he can be sent four hundred yards with a lamp and nobody has to think about it, and the thinking costs nothing, and nothing comes back afterwards, and in nine years there has not been one of these that anybody has come back and asked him about.

The lamp is not lit and the hook is at the far end and the floor is stone and it is colder than the shed by about as much as a door is.

---

There is a door in it about nine foot from the far end. It has been there the whole of the nine years and it has not been opened in the nine years and there is no name on it and it is not on a list and nobody has ever stood where he was standing and said a word about it. The stone floor under it is a different colour from the rest of the floor and he noticed that on his first afternoon and has not thought about it since until this one.

The colour has neither spread nor faded, so whatever put it there stopped at the edge of the same nine foot it started in, and he has got a thumbnail under the paint at the edge where the paint has lifted, and under the nail it is the same colour as on top.

He got to it with the lamp in his hand and stood in front of it, and the lamp was not lit, and there is not one form anywhere in this empire that says a man is to light a lamp, and there is not one that says a man is to open a door, and he was sent to look at a hook.

**He did not open it.** That took him about four seconds and the four seconds were the whole of the thinking.

He was sent with a lamp and not with a key. Nobody asked him to open it. Nobody is going to ask him whether he opened it. And a man who opens a door that has not been opened in nine years is a man who has done a thing, and the thing would be about what was behind it, and there is no version of the next four hours in which anybody asks him what was behind it.

---

**His sister is in her fourth month.**

**She is about nineteen and she is in a room about four miles off with her mother, and he has not been since the second week of the second month, and there is nothing whatever about it on any page in this matter.** He has never once been glad of that in a way that cost him anything. He has not been frightened of it either. He has never once been able to explain to himself the difference, and he has not tried to explain it to anybody else, and nobody in this building has ever heard her said out loud.

There is no form in this empire with a column for it. There is no heading over it, and no office it is entered under, and no day against it, and nothing anywhere that would let a person say the name of the woman and have it be a thing anybody could do with.

And the whole of what he has got out of that is that it is a thing nobody can be held to, and he has never had that about anything else in nine years.

---

He stood at the door for about as long as it takes to read four minutes out loud, and there was nothing on the other side of it that he could hear, and the lamp was in his hand and was not lit, and the hook was where the lamp was supposed to be and it was not on it.

Four minutes is a long time to stand on a stone floor with a lamp in your hand, and his feet went first and then the cold came up the front of his legs, and he heard nothing at all through the door and then he heard water under the terrace, which is what a cut is for.

**The hook is not the lamp.** That is the finding and it is about four hours old and it is not a large one, and it is this: the lamp is somewhere else, and it has been somewhere else for about as long as the door has been shut, and nobody in this building has said so out loud, and he is the man they sent to find out and he is going to have to go back and not have found it.

**He could not have said that to anybody in about nine years.** He is the man in this building who says things out loud that nobody can hold him to, and what he has just said out loud is a lamp.

---

He came back up the cut with the lamp in his hand and put it on the bench in the shed and stood in about nine foot by eleven while the foreman read the day's tally, and she did not ask him what was at the end of the corridor and she is not going to ask him tomorrow either.

Four hundred yards back with a lamp in his hand, and the lamp got heavier or he did, and about halfway along the cut the wind came up off the water and went in at the front of his coat, and at the mouth of it there was ordinary light on the ground again and a cart on the cut going the other way.

Nobody thanked him. There is not one form anywhere in this empire that requires a person to be thanked for being sent four hundred yards with a lamp, and there is not going to be one, and a man who is not thanked for it is a man who cannot be asked whether he did it, and both of those are the same thing and he is not going to say so in a bay.

The four in that bay are still four and were not asked one thing this week and were not named, and a girl of seventeen at the back of that bay is on a written engagement at the rate the list is set at, and she was not spoken to and she was not thanked and nobody is going to send for her.

The door is shut. The lamp is not on the hook. The lamp in the shed is not lit until about the seventh hour, and it was not lit when he came back, and he will be sent down that corridor again inside about a year because there is a hook at the end of it and no form anywhere in this empire that says who else.
