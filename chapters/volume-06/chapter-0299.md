# Chapter 299: A Store Four Hundred Yards Off A Bay, And A Thing Said Out Loud In It

The hired stone store is four hundred yards up the lane off the bay and it is a cold building with a bench in it and about four men in it at about the fifth hour, and the south end of it has never been measured and is not measured now. A bay is about nine foot by eleven. This one is longer than that and there is a lamp in it that is not lit until about the seventh hour. The day has been shut at the gate since about the fourth hour and the man at it is a man of about forty who does not argue and never has.

He is thirty-eight. He chose this labour out of his own hand in the fourth week of the eighth month of the year after the year after next, a kind of labour a man is free to refuse, and that is a thing that has already happened and is not happening today. There is nothing in this empire that anybody could produce and say he ought to have been given it. No office, no fund, no list, no name in a book that anybody keeps.

This is the second day of the first week of the twelfth month of the year after the year after next, and it is about the fifth hour, and he has told the man at the gate that he will not be there in the morning and the man at the gate said nothing back, and that was the whole of the arrangement and it took about four seconds.

---

He is in that store because a foreman of fifty-one is in it, and a man of about thirty from the second gang is in it, and a man of about fifty-five with a chain is in it, and about four men are in it, and none of the four has been asked one thing in nine years and is not going to be asked one this month.

The foreman has been in a box since the first week of the seventh month of the year after and has never been asked about it. The man of about thirty from the second gang said a word out loud in a bay in the first week of the first month of the year after the year after next, in front of nine men, and was answered in about four minutes, and nobody has asked him one word about it since, and he is a party from the day he said it and cannot be taken back out of it.

And the two of them have not been in a room together with anybody in it since that afternoon, and no form in this empire would let either of them be brought to one, and neither of them has been sent for.

---

And he said the shape of it first, in about nine words, standing up, in a store, to two people and to about four men who cannot read a paragraph.

"I am going to say a thing out loud that is not a rule and is not in a book, and neither of you can be made to do anything about it, and I am saying it in front of them on purpose so that it is in five people and not two."

"Say it, then," the foreman said, "and be quick, because the lamp is not lit."

**And what he said was that the next time a word is asked in that bay, it will be asked of him, and that the price of it will be his and not hers, and that she is not going to be made findable a second time, and that the man who asked the first one is not going to be asked about it either, and that nothing he has just said takes a single thing off any record in this empire.**

---

That is the whole of what he said, and it moves one consequence and one only, and the consequence is hers.

**A woman of fifty-one who is in a box because a class entered her said out loud what a class is, and then became findable a second time because a man of about thirty-four came about four miles into that bay and asked a foreman what a word on a sheet meant, and the finding is hers and it does not come off.**

"That is not nothing," the man of about thirty said. "You have just moved a thing off a woman who has not asked you to have it moved, in a room with four men in it who cannot read what you said, and **if that is a kind of shield then it is the brittle kind.**"

"**And what does it leave me with?**"

"With the word you said and the day it was said and the answer you gave," Marek Kest said, "which is three more things than you had this morning, and all three of them are yours, and I have not taken one of them, and I am not going to."

---

And he had that coming and he had it written down before he came, and he did not take it back, and the foreman of fifty-one is the one who answered it, and it took her about four seconds.

"You have not moved anything," she said. "You have taken the next one onto yourself, and I did not ask you to, and I am not going to thank you for it, and I want you to hear the whole of that in the order I have said it."

"I hear it."

"And I am not going to be asked again and neither is he, and that is not a favour from me, that is the only arrangement there is, and there is no form in this empire that would let anybody ask me a second time and there is not going to be one."

---

**And the cost was named in the same room, out loud, before any of it was said, and it is two days of work and a mark.**

"The day's wage is what I am losing and I have lost it and the man at the gate said nothing and I did not ask him to say nothing. It is a shilling and sixpence and I have been at that gate about nine times this month and this was one of the nine that I am not having."

About four men in a store four hundred yards up a lane and about nine times at a gate in a month and one shilling and sixpence for the day, and about eleven years of days put in one at a time, and a cold building with a bench along one wall, and four hundred yards of lane separating it from the bay.

He put the inside of his left wrist on the bench with the sleeve back and there is a line on it about two inches long and it is not a figure and it is not a word.

"That has been there about two years and it has never said anything and it has never settled anything and it does not know where the ford is, and it is not what I have just paid, and the wage is what I have just paid, and this has gone about an inch further up my arm since the fourth hour and it is going to keep going and it is not going to close."

About an inch up the inside of the forearm since the fourth hour, and about an hour of cold building in the time, and the skin at the edge of the line is a different colour from the skin beside it, and the colour has not spread and is not going to.

---

**And nobody in that store is saved, and he said so, and it is the part of the thing he said that cost him the most to say out loud and it is about four seconds.**

"You are still in a box," he said, to the foreman. "The form that put you there has not been asked to be void by anybody and there is no procedure for it and the person who administers it is a clerk who is not a villain and has done about nine hundred of them correctly, and nobody has asked her to do this one differently and nobody is going to."

"And the word is still on a sheet," the man of about thirty said.

"And the word is still on a sheet, and the day is still on it, and the hat is still the hat, and the answer you gave in that bay is still true and nobody has come to take it off you, and the man of about thirty-four who came about four miles into it in the eleventh month of the year after next is still findable by his own question and I have not gone nine miles to find out and I am not going to."

---

Nobody thanked anybody. Nobody in that store thanked him and he did not thank either of them, and there is not one form anywhere in this empire that requires a person to be thanked for taking a thing onto himself that nobody asked him to take, and there never is going to be one, and about four men in that store cannot read a paragraph and were not asked one question and would not have understood it if they had been.

**He is not at a stage and he is not going to get to one, and no form in this empire would let a man in a store be examined about one.** He said the reason out loud in about nine words and said it to the bench. There is no form in this empire for compelling a person to be brought to a room, and a hearing that cannot compel attendance is a room with people in it who came.

The lamp was not lit until about the seventh hour and it was not lit at about the seventh hour either, because the man of about fifty-five with a chain went out at about the sixth hour and the foreman went with him and the man of about thirty from the second gang went out before that and said nothing, and about four men left at about the sixth hour and none of them was asked anything by anybody.

About six hours of a cold building and about four men and two people who had been in a box, and a bench against one wall and an unlit lamp in it, and four hundred yards of lane between the gate and the bay and the whole of it undone.

He put his sleeve back down and shut the store. The mark on the inside of his wrist was about three inches by then. There is a building about nine miles off with a rail in a room at the end of a cold passage, and a man of about thirty-four had been in it about twelve days ago and had told nobody and was not going to. Marek Kest is not in that room and is not sent and does not go, and the road is walked zero times, and he went home to a room with nothing nailed up in it.
