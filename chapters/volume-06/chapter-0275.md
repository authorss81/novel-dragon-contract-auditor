# Chapter 275: A Rented Room Two Streets Off A Street That Goes Down To The River Road, A Book With No Office On It That Has Two Things In It And One Of Them Is A Person, And Five Weeks Of A Woman Of Twenty-Four Not Being Asked Anything By Anybody

Nobody has ever been in this room but her, and the rent is on a board nailed by the door in a hand that is not hers, and the board says one thing. The window looks at the back of another house and there is a dog about four miles off that goes and stops and starts, and the drawer with the four things in it is in the bed and the drawer does not lock and has never locked.

Twenty-four still, a clerk at that counter still, not a lawyer still, and the book on the table is the one with no office on it, and it has had two things in it for about ten weeks.

This is the second day of the first week of the sixth month of the year after the year after next, and it is about the seventh hour, and the lamp is lit, and the day is over.

---

**The first of the two things in it is a refusal and the second is four lines off a fee board, and about ten weeks ago neither of them was a thing about anybody at all.**

The refusal is one line and it says that a clerk of thirty-four said no to a man on a stair on the second day of the third week of the third month of the year after the year after next. It is in her own hand. She wrote it in about four seconds at the other end of that counter and nobody asked her to and there is no form anywhere in this empire that says a clerk at a counter has to write down what another clerk said.

The one line is short enough to read standing up in a lamp-lit room and about long enough that it takes her eyes to the end of it, and she has read it about nine times without ever reading it out, and the four lines under it take about four seconds and she knows them by the shape of the fourth one.

The four lines are off a board behind that same counter, and the first three of them are in the hand that put them up and the fourth one is not, and she does not know whose hand it is and she does not know when it went up, and about eleven years of that counter have gone past and nobody has ever asked her about the fourth.

---

**And about ten weeks is what it took her to work out that one of those two things can be used and the other one cannot, and that it is the one she is proud of.**

A thing about a person is a thing that can be produced against a person. It has a day in it and the shape of a person in it and it is in a hand, and if anybody ever wanted to know what a clerk of thirty-four refused and when, then that line is the answer, and she wrote it, and it will answer for about as long as the paper lasts.

A thing about nobody is a thing that cannot be produced against anybody.

**A fee board is a document about nobody.** It is a list of what a counter does, and a day, and a sum, and there is not one person in any of the four lines, and so there is nobody anybody can hold anything against, and so there is nobody anybody could ask either, and that is the reason the fourth of those four lines has been up on that wall for about eleven years with a hand on it that nobody has ever been able to account for.

She is not going to find out about the fourth one. She looked at it for about four seconds in the third week of the third month and wrote down that it was in a different hand, and she has not looked at it since and she is not going to.

Four seconds at a board on a wall and then a line in a book at about the seventh hour, and the two of them together are the whole of her investigation, and there will not be a third one, and she would rather be a worse investigator than a more careful one.

---

A window is not a wall. A clerk of thirty-four told her that at the end of that counter in about nine seconds about ten weeks ago and it has been the truest thing anybody has said to her in two years.

**It has not stopped anything and it has not started anything, and that is what she has got out of it, and a fact is not a decision, and she is going to stop treating the two as the same thing.**

She is not going to put the window back. There is not one form anywhere in this empire that will let a woman put a window back as a wall, and there is not one that will let anybody take a line out of a book either, and she has been a clerk for two years and has never once been able to un-write anything.

---

The two instruments that require her in any room he is in are two instruments and have never once been put in one sentence and are not going to be in this one.

One of them is a paper and it is dated and it has her name on it and it can be produced. The other is a watch and it requires a person who is not from the office that cannot be named, and it does not have her name on it anywhere and it cannot be produced at all. The page ruled by hand in her own book with the same name in both boxes is not a third instrument and it is not going to become one, and she has not ruled a third and she is not going to, and she is not going to say how many there are going to be.

About four hundred and thirty miles of road is what it would cost anybody who wanted to see the rest of it. There is no fund behind that and there is no form for a fund, and nobody has ever been paid for satisfying a watch.

---

The undertaking of the fourth of the first month is live and it has not been retired and it has not been amended, and she did not exercise it tonight, and she is going to write down why, because she has now not exercised it on three separate nights for three separate reasons.

**An undertaking you take up on three separate nights for three separate reasons is not a decision.** It is a habit, and a habit held by one person is the only kind of thing in this empire that grows into a job without anybody putting it in writing, and there is no form anywhere that would let her stop one.

The reason tonight is about four seconds long and it is that there is nobody to exercise it on. A thing that requires two people is not a thing that can be done to feel better, and she has worked that out twice now and the working out is not the point. The point is that a woman who has not been asked one question since the second day of the first week of the first month of the year after next has about two years of very good at being left alone, and there is not one form anywhere in this empire that would let somebody take that off her.

---

About nine people came up that stair today and about four of them wanted something out of her and got it, and a man wanted a copy and paid fourpence and said thank you, and a woman wanted to know whether a copy would stand if the thing it came from were lost and it would not and she said so without softening it, and a boy of about fifteen wanted to know whether he could buy a blank and leave it, which he can.

Nobody asked her a thing that was not a request. That is the ordinary shape of that counter and she is extremely good at it and she has been extremely good at it for about two years.

"**And nobody is going to ask you either?**" she said, to the lamp, "**and there is not one form anywhere in this empire that requires a person to be asked, and there is not going to be one, and I have made my peace with that about four years ago and I do not want it taken off me.**"

And she did not want it taken off her and she would have said so to anybody who had come up that stair with a form, and about nine people came up it and not one of them had one.

The stair is four flights, and about five people in a day go up it for nothing at all, and she has watched it happen from the top of the stair for two years, and has never once asked one of them what they came up for.

And the fourth of the four things in the drawer is where it was, and it was not touched, and she did not open it and she is not going to send it, and it has been there for about two years and it is the only one of the four that is hers.

It is in the bed and it does not lock and it has never locked, and a thing put in there is a thing nobody will ever look at, and about two years is how long four things have been in there and not one of them has been looked at by her either.

---

Nobody thanked her and nobody was thanked. The lamp is lit and the board on the wall says the rent and nothing else, and the dog about four miles off went off and stopped, and she sat at that table for about four minutes with a book that has one thing about a person in it and one thing about nobody in it, and she is not going to take either of them out, and there is not one form anywhere in this empire that would let her.
