# Chapter 0488: A Thing Said At A Man's Back Going Away, About Nine Foot Of It Coming Off The Flags, And A Figure That Stays Where It Was

The rack came past her four times with nothing riding on it. On the last ordinary morning before this one it came past eight times, and the difference between four and eight is the whole of what happened on that run this week, and it is not going in front of anybody.

Four years she has stood at that end. She is not on a roll anywhere in that building and has never been one of its clerks, and not one of them has ever wanted a thing out of her, and nothing has ever been put into her hands for her to take that stone with, and that building has not thanked her and is not going to. At the other end of the run, four hundred yards off, there is a room that is not hers and belongs to somebody else and has had nothing stood in it since before she came to that lock, and nobody went to it this week and nobody is going to.

Since the spring of her fourth year at that end of it there has been a number in her head and it has never once been on paper, and it is not going onto paper, and it has already been carried up out of this building once. There is nobody in that building who could set a heading over it and nowhere in this empire that a person could set one. She has never set a mark of it beside a mark cut into a printed strip, and there is nothing in her head that resembles anything else in this matter, and if anybody put the two side by side she would not be able to say why they belong in the same sentence.

It is the second day of the second week of the twelfth month of the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is the fourth hour.

---

He came down at the fourth hour with the rack and went on by the way he goes on by, and he did not stop, and that was the whole of the difference between this morning and the last one he said anything on.

He said it going past, to the stone, at a volume that fitted about four feet of it.

"**A mark cut into a printed strip is the only thing in this empire that travels at the speed of paper and is attached to nobody.** It goes down a lane at four miles an hour and up a river on a cart, and it is on about four hundred of those a year, and it does not arrive anywhere, because there is nowhere for it to arrive and nothing at the far end of anywhere is expecting it. That rack of mine has carried things down that stone with nothing written on them for as long as either of us has stood in this building. It has never carried one with a line cut in the top edge of it. **I decided in a month that is gone not to make a rule about one and I have not changed my mind and nobody is going to make me, and I am not going to be thanked for a decision I made when nobody had asked me for one.**"

---

She had to say it at a man who was already moving away from her, and that is a thing nobody in this matter has an arrangement for.

He was already past her by the time she had the first of it and he was further off by the time she had the rest of it, and the passage took what she said and gave back a piece of it and then gave up on the rest.

"You have decided that again, walking. I am going to say one thing at your back and I have said a thing on that stone in a month that is gone and this is not it, and after this morning neither of us says anything else on this run for a month."

About nine feet of it came back off the flags and the rest of it went down the passage.

"**A sheet with a line in the top of it is a thing nobody can be shown reading either.** There is no column over it, so there is no page it can be read out of, and that cuts both ways. It cannot be produced against anybody and it cannot be produced for anybody. And this run is the cheapest road in the building for one, because nothing coming down here has to be stopped, and every year a floor nine miles off from here sends out nine hundred sheets with a heading printed over each column of each of them, and every one of those nine hundred goes through two hands somebody could put a name to. **A sheet like that would come down this stone and go up the four hundred yards and stand in a shut room at the far end that has been empty since before I came to that lock, and there is nothing on it for anybody over there to decide about.** And nobody at this end of the run could be shown holding one either. A line in a strip is attached to nobody, and that includes the woman standing next to it."

He did not stop. He got the rack up off his hip about nine foot further down and went on.

What she had said stayed where he had left it, and the flags took his feet the way they take everybody's feet on a run of stone, and he went up the stone without putting his head round at her going.

"You have not asked me for my figure."

"You have never asked me for it and you are not going to, and I have not handed it to anybody and I am not going to this week, and it is not going on anything and it is not going to have anything printed over the top of it."

---

The rack came past her four more times before the light went and every one of them went by with the empty, and the week closed with the run the way it closed the week before, and the difference between the two weeks is a number of passes and is not going anywhere.

The light went off the flags at the far end before it went off them at her end, and the lock stayed fastened where it has been fastened for the whole of the time, and she stood at the near end of it for the rest of the week without going up the stone once.

An empty week is not the same as a held week, and the two sit close enough together in a building like that to cost a person a month out of her own count, and she lost one in the spring. Her number is where it stood at the fourth hour. Nobody in that building is holding it, nothing went onto anything this week, nobody printed a heading over it, it never went the length of that run to anybody, and the shut room at the end of it stayed shut with nothing in it and was not opened.

Nothing came down that run of stone in that week and the not coming is not the same as a thing being held back, and she stood at that lock for the whole of it and nobody in that building has ever once come and asked her which of the two it was. The run went on being the length it is, with a shut room at the end of it and no rule on any part of it, and a figure in a woman's head at this end of it that nobody in that building has ever been given.

There is no mark cut into a printed strip anywhere in this empire that she can be shown holding one, and there is one going out of a building in another city four times this week for every two of them, and there is no form anywhere in this empire for asking one woman at one lock which of the two she would rather it was. He went by four times with an empty rack this week and eight times the week before, and neither number is going anywhere, and she will be at that lock in a week.
