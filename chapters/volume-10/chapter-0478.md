# Chapter 0478: A Man Who Can Be Found Standing At The End Of Those Boards, A Bill That Has Changed Sides, And A Sheet He Cannot Ask About

He came up the four flights at about the fourth hour with a sheet rolled inside his coat and he put nothing on the boards, and he has not put a coin on those boards on any morning he has been up that stair, and there have not been many of those mornings.

**He is about thirty-four and he writes a hand that is better than it needs to be. What he is paid for, four days a season, is making one man's figures come out identical to another man's, and to be paid for it at all he has to be on a list, and there is one, and it is four days long.** He is a man who can be found. In this matter that has been the safest thing about him for two years and he has known that for both of them and has never once said so out loud to anybody who could use it.

There is a figure he has carried in his head since a spring that is gone. **It is not printed in this room and it is not going to be printed, it is not confirmed and it is not denied, and the reason it is not is not politeness. A figure in a person's head is a fact about that person, and a fact about a person is the one thing in this matter that cannot be entered anywhere. The moment the same figure is on a piece of paper it stops being about him and starts being about a thing, and a thing can be carried up a lane and put in a coat.** There is no form in this empire for asking a man to confirm one and none for asking him to deny one, and nobody is going to try either this week.

It is the fourth day of the fourth week of the ninth month of the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is the fifth hour.

---

Marn Ottery said it to the boards and not to him, from the far end, and she said it once.

"**I told this room about two months ago that a man who cannot be found carries the price of not being found on somebody else's account, and that a man who can be found cannot be a holder of anything, because a holder of a copy is holding a stand-in and a stand-in is not the thing. Both of those are still exactly where I left them. What has moved in a fortnight is the bill and I am going to say where it has moved to, once, and then I am not going to say it again this month.**"

---

He did not turn round and he did not move.

"**There is an object in this empire now that can be made twice without a single person finding out, and it costs fourpence, and it comes out of a case at the end of this counter, and there is no form in it for stopping one and there is no form in it for finding out that one has been made.** A man who can be found is a man a second of a thing can be made against. That is all it takes. It does not take anybody knowing that you have a thing. It takes one person in one week deciding to be unlucky twice, and about four hundred of them come down those stairs in a year and each of them is carrying something that is not the thing."

"Nobody in this building is going to make one against me."

"Nobody in this building is not going to. **Nobody in this empire is going to.** I have said that to a set of boards and not to a person, and I have not asked you one thing and you have not said one word back, and the whole of what I have just done is tell a man who can be found that his bill has changed sides. It has changed from an account he cannot see to an account he is going to be able to keep, and that is not a rescue and it is not a kindness. I am not going to be thanked for it and I am not going to be asked about it."

---

"**Marek Kest was not on this floor this morning.**" She squared the edge of the boards. "**That is the first morning in four years this floor has had nothing on it that could be made twice, and he is the only person in this matter who has got nothing anybody could copy, and he was not here, and there is no form anywhere in this empire in which a man's not being somewhere gets written down, and nobody has sent for him and nobody is going to.** The cost of that is not his. The cost of that is that this room went quiet about ten minutes earlier than it usually does, and I noticed it, and I am not going to be thanked for noticing it either."

The man with the fair hand said nothing to that and it went into the air and settled on the boards and stayed there.

"You have not asked me one thing."

"I have not asked you one thing and nobody on this floor has asked you one thing in any of the mornings you have been up that stair, and I am not going to start, and there is nobody in this building I could start with."

---

He went back down the four flights and then about two miles through a city at the walking pace of a man carrying something, and he did not put a coin on a single board on the way, and he got to a room with a table in it and shut the door, which is a thing he does and not a thing anybody could be shown.

Eight hours of a fair hand at a rate that is a rate and not a kindness. The fourth line of the fourth page does not agree with the line above it and has not agreed with it for about a fortnight, and he has known it the whole of that fortnight, and he is not the man who is going to say so out loud to anybody about it.

**He wanted, in that room, at about the sixth hour, to be able to find out whether a second of it existed. That is the whole of what he wanted and it took him about four seconds to work out that there was no way to it.** There is no register of copies. There is no column a copy could be entered under. Nobody makes a second in front of anybody. The fourpence is the same as the fourpence, and the ninety seconds are the same as the ninety seconds, and the two of them came out of the same case two years apart, and there is nothing in this empire that could put one of them beside the other and say which came first. **And a man who cannot be found would be a man nobody could make a second of anything against, and he had that for two years by accident, and one woman putting fourpence on a counter in a fortnight took the whole of it off him, and there is not a form anywhere in this empire that would put it back on him, and nobody sent for him and nobody is going to.**

He sat at that table for a while and then he did the next page.

**Nobody thanked anybody. The bill stands on an account and nobody has told him whose account, and the figure in his head is on nothing. About four hundred and thirty miles of this river stand above the point where that room is, and a woman of twenty is in a counting room at the back of a dye end about that far up it, and he is not going up there this month and nobody is going to make him. The room he is in is two rooms above a chandler's and it has no window at all, and there is a smell of tar under the door that he stopped noticing in the first week, and that is the whole of where he is.**
