# Chapter 0467: A Fair Hand At The End Of Those Boards, A Man Named Who Is Not In The Room, And A Bill That Stands On Another Account

He came up the four flights at about the fifth hour and put fourpence on the boards and did not say what for, and the woman at the other end of the boards took a blank out of the case and gave it to him, and he folded it and put it inside his coat and went down again.

That is the second time he has done that, and nothing anywhere in this empire has a line on it for a man who comes up a stair twice.

**He is about thirty-four and the hand is a fair one, and what that hand is paid for is writing out other men's figures until the two sets of them say the same thing, and a man has to be standing on somebody's list to be paid for that, and he stands on one four days a season.** He has a number in his head. He has had it two years. **It is not printed in this room and it is not going to be printed, and nobody is going to confirm it and nobody is going to deny it, and the not printing is not a courtesy and the not asking is not a courtesy either.** A number handed over is a roll of names, and a roll of names is a body, and a body is a caption over a man's head, and a man with a caption over his head is a man who has been written into the thing he was carrying.

It is the second day of the first week of the seventh month of the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is the fifth hour.

---

He had a sheet rolled inside his coat. It was eight hours of fair-hand work at a rate that is a rate and not a kindness, and the fourth line of the fourth page of it does not agree with the line above it, and he has known that since a week that is gone, and he is not the man who is going to say so out loud to anybody about it.

He stood at the end of the boards for about an hour and a quarter and he did not put it on the counter and he did not ask for a second of it, and **nobody at that counter asked him one thing in the whole of that hour, which is the arrangement, and the arrangement has held for four years at the two joined tables and eleven years at the far end.**

---

Then Marn Ottery said it to the boards, and she said it the way she says a thing that is not aimed at anybody in particular, and the man with the fair hand was the only person at that end of the room.

"**A man who cannot be found is a man whose bill for it is somebody else's. A man who can be found cannot be a holder of anything, and the reason is in a few words and I am going to say them once.** A holder of a copy is a person holding a stand-in. A stand-in is not the original. A man holding a stand-in cannot be shown the original and cannot be asked about the original, because the original is not his and never was. **The bill stands on somebody else's account and nobody has told him whose, and he has not found out in two years and he is not going to.**"

Nobody said anything to that and nobody asked her one word about it.

"I am not going to say the other one again. **The other one is a person who can be shown holding a thing and is not a party to it, and the whole of what makes that possible is that a copy is not the thing.** I said that one in this room in the third week of the fifth month, and I am not going to say it in this room again this month, and it has nothing at all to do with the man standing at the end of these boards. Except that he is the only person in this city who has been found in every room I have stood in since."

---

"There is a man called Marek Kest," she said, "and he has been up those four flights six times and he has put nothing on the boards one time out of the six, and he is thirty-eight, and he is the only person in this matter who cannot be shown holding anything, and I have said his name out loud in this room this morning, and I have not been asked to and I am not going to be thanked for it."

The man with the fair hand did not move and did not speak.

"**I have said it because a true thing about a second holder does not have one person in it, and if I had not put a name on him this morning then the only people in this empire who could be shown knowing about a copy would be the two of us standing at this counter, and one of us is a clerk.** Every person who was in that room when I said it is in it, and he was in it, and now there is a name attached to it, and I have done that out loud, in a room, at the fifth hour, on a working morning. **That is not a gift to him and it is not a thing I have done for him. It is the price of a sentence being said, and the price is always the same and it is always somebody who is not the person the sentence is about.**"

---

"You have not asked me anything."

"I have not asked you anything and nobody on this floor has asked you anything in the two times you have been up that stair, and I am not going to start, and I would like that noticed and I would like it noticed by nobody in particular." She squared the edge of the boards with the side of her hand. "**You are a man who can be found. There is a bill for that and it is not yours and it is not on this counter and it is not on anything, and you have known it for two years, and there is no form in this empire in which a man is given the account his bill is on, and I am not going to be the one to tell you which account it is even if I knew.**"

"I was not going to ask you."

"I know you were not, and I know what it costs a person not to ask, and I am not going to be thanked for noticing that, and I have noticed it about a woman of nineteen in a corridor nine miles off this building and I am not going to be thanked for that either."

---

Tamsin Rook was at the near end with her hand flat on the case and she had been in twenty minutes before the hour, and she had heard all of it. **She has not been told why she is early and she is not going to be told this month, and the not being told is the one part of that which has been left alone for two years, and the reason nobody in that room has told her is that telling her would make her a person somebody can be shown knowing about a copy.** She said nothing. She did not agree and she did not disagree and she did the eleven things in the order she does them.

**A name went out of that room at the fifth hour on an ordinary morning, and there is no form anywhere in this empire in which a person is entered afterwards as the one who said a man's name out loud in a working room, and nobody at that counter has thanked her for saying it and none of them is going to.**

**The man with the fair hand went down the four flights at about the sixth hour with a rolled sheet inside his coat that he had not left on that counter.** About four hundred and thirty miles of this river stand above the point where that building is, and nobody at that counter has ever been up them and nobody is going to, and a man who is about four miles off was in a room that week and did not come up that stair and was not sent for and is not going to be.
