# Chapter 0464: A Form On A Board By A Door, A Man With A Chain Across The Front Bench, And Half A Sentence In A Doorway

The form was on the board by the door where it is and it is the same form and it is older than anybody in that lane. She had it in her hand at about the fourth hour and she had been holding it long enough that the paper had started to follow the heat of her fingers.

**Halla Wray. Fifty-one. A foreman, standing inside a box that a class put her in, and a class is not a room and the form that stands behind the class is the nearest thing this empire has to the thing that put her there.** It has not been filled in since before she came to that bench. It is not going to be filled in this week. Nobody in that lane has ever stood at that board and tried to have a form set aside and there is nowhere to stand and try.

The lamp on its bracket at the high end of that shed is not lit and is not going to be before the seventh hour, and no day of the week will get it lit any earlier, and nothing happens in that room before the seventh hour that anybody would think of calling an event.

It is the second day of the second week of the sixth month of the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is the fourth hour, and the lamp is not lit.

---

She had it in her hand at the doorway and she said the first half of it and stopped where she has stopped every time.

"It is not a person who—"

And then nothing, and the door was behind her and the shed was in front of her, and the two at the back did not move.

The half sentence stayed in the room with her after it stopped, and the doorway stayed where she had left it, and the paper in her hand had gone back to the temperature of the floor by then, and it was the only thing in the room that had changed while she was standing there.

**The girl is not given the chance to say no about anything this week and she is not going to be given one. Nobody in that shed put an offer to her and nobody put a condition to her, and the one time in that week when a thing could have been said to her that she would have had to answer, the man at the front bench said it to the floor and not to her.** The reader of seventeen turned a page at about a quarter past the four and put his hand flat on the open one and looked at it, and nobody said one word to him, and nobody has said one word to him in a long time, and he is not going to start.

---

The man at the other side of the chain did not move and did not look up.

"You have had that form in your hand for a quarter of an hour and you have not filled in a space on it and you have not put it back on the board. I have watched that floor go past it nine hundred times in eleven years and I have never once seen anybody stop for it, and I have known what you were doing with it for about the length of time it takes you to cross that floor twice."

"I was not doing anything with it."

"You were standing at a board with a piece of paper in your hand and you were deciding something, and you have decided it and you have not put it down, and I have been on this side of a chain for longer than you have been in that class, and I know what a deciding hand looks like from about four foot away."

The chain across the front of the bench had not gone anywhere, and the lamp at the high end of the shed was still not lit, and there was enough floor between the door and the back of the shed for a person to cross without touching anything on the way.

---

She went the length of that shed and put her back to the front bench and looked at the back wall, where the two of seventeen were, and neither of them looked at her.

"**A holder who can be put back into a class is a holder who is already a party to it.** That is the whole of what I have and I am giving it to you now because I have worked out that it is the only answer there is going to be and I would rather hand it over than sit in it. The class is the box and the form is the lid, and the lid does not have to be shut today. **The form does not have to be filled in this week. The form only has to exist.** A thing that only has to exist is the cheapest thing in this empire, and you have not had one of those to do with in eleven years and I have not had one either."

"You are saying that as though I have asked you for something."

"I am saying it because you have got a piece of paper in your hand and you have not put it down, and a person who can be re-entered cannot be given a copy of anything without the copy being evidence, and a second of a form is not an exit. **A second of a form stands in the same class as the form, because the second carries the same heading, and the heading is the class.** I could make you one of those in about four seconds with a pen and a sheet of paper and a free half hour, and it would be worth nothing to you and it would be worth a great deal to whoever got hold of it, and that is the whole of what a second is for."

---

She put the form back on the board by the door. She put it on at the angle it goes on at, and she squared the bottom of it with the side of her hand, and her hand was not steady and she did not try to make it steady.

"That is the first thing you have said to me in this room that was aimed at me."

"I said four sentences in this bay about a year ago and nobody answered one of them and I never wanted an answer, and this is not a fifth sentence and I am not going to be thanked for it and you are not going to thank me." He shifted on the bench and the chain went a small way and came back. "**I can read a paragraph. I have always been able to read a paragraph. I am not going to read one out for anybody, and I am not one of the four, and I never was, and if you ever put me in with the four I will not be in the same shed as you again.**"

"I have never put you anywhere. There is nowhere to put anybody. That is the entire shape of this lane."

"Then you know the answer as well as I do and you did not need me to say it, and I have said it, and neither of us is going to be thanked and neither of us is going to be asked one thing about it."

It was said and it was over and the bay was a bay again with a man on one side of a chain and a woman on the other, and nothing in either of them had moved into the other one while it was being said.

---

She did the two ends of that shed twice after that and the form stayed on the board and she did not pick it up again, and nothing was filled in on it, and nobody in that lane has ever filled in that form since before she came to that bench.

She did them with her hands empty every time, which is not the ordinary way to walk a floor, and the ordinary way is what her hands knew, and nothing in the shed put a stop to the difference.

**A class can be re-entered by whoever fills in the next form, and this week nobody re-entered anybody, and the form that put her where she is is not being asked to be void by anybody.** Nobody in that lane has ever asked anything to be made void, and there is nowhere standing at which the asking could be made, and there has not been one in the whole time she has stood in that box. The chain is where it was. The bench is where it was. The two of seventeen are where they have been.

Then the lamp went on by itself at about the seventh hour, the way it goes on by itself, and she was still at the board by the door with her hand not on anything, and the sentence she begins in a doorway came out as far as it has ever come out and stopped where it has always stopped, and the second half of it has still not arrived.
