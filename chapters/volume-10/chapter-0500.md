# Chapter 0500: A Man Who Went Out Of A Building With Nothing In His Hands, And Two Things Left In A Coat On A Nail

He had the twenty minutes before the hour, the way he has had them every week for about two years, and no one came to the door, and that has been the shape of his working life since the morning a bundle he did not open came down a street in this city.

The coat is on the nail, and it has been on that nail since the evening it went up. The nail has been in that wall for about two years and it went in crooked, and it held nothing at all until an evening that is gone. There are two things in the inside pocket of that coat on opposite sides, and they have been in it about two years. He has not taken either of them out in this building or any other, and he has not told one person in this city that he is carrying both of them.

The chair is still against none of the walls, and the coat is not on it any more. There is one thing on that table and it came there on the end of an arm and it is still exactly where it was put, and no form in this empire has ever had a line for it, and nothing has been stood up on that table beside it in two years, and nothing in that room has ever been lit that makes heat and nothing is going to be this month.

It is the second day of the second week of the third month of the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is the second hour.

---

He worked the whole of it out standing in the middle of that floor, and it took the length of time a floor takes to go past twice.

A sheet went under the boards of a room at the top of four flights in the first week of the year. It was not in a case and not in a drawer and not in a hand. A man had let go of something in this city in the last month of the year before that, and could not be found afterwards, and had not been on a book, and had not been asked one thing. The two of them have never met and are never going to meet. There were two holders of that sheet and there is one, and the sheet is not lost, and nobody sent him.

And here is the whole of what a month of this has established. It is not a good thing, and it is not going to be made good.

A second of a second can be handed to a person who has nothing behind them to be liable for. A person who is not a party to it cannot be shown having made a decision about it. So a thing in this matter that used to require somebody to be alone now requires only two people to be unlucky.

A foreman of fifty-one has said it out loud about a girl of seventeen at the back of a shed at the end of a lane. A man of about thirty-four has put three pages that do not come out even into a pile, and then one of them in front of a woman with four headings over four columns. A woman of about twenty-six at the end of four hundred yards of cold flags is the cheapest person in this city and has been for about four years, and a man in a felt apron has said so out loud to her this month. A woman of twenty-four has a sheet under a floor that she did not make and cannot account for and has not been asked about. A man of about sixty-one has begun signing a sheet with the other hand.

That is five people in five buildings. Nobody in any of them was asked. Not one of them is safer than they were a month ago. There is no form for stopping any of it, and therefore no form for finding out who did any of it, and it is not going to be cured by anybody in the next ten years of it either.

---

He did not go up the four flights. He went down them.

He went down four flights and out of that door and along a street that runs down to the river road. He did not go to the top of four flights. He did not go to a floor at the end of a lane. He did not walk four hundred yards of cold flags, and he did not walk nine miles, and he did not go four hundred and thirty miles up any river. He had nothing in his hands, and he did not buy anything anywhere in this city that day.

He was out of that building for about four hours.

There is no form anywhere in this empire in which a man's being outside his own door gets entered. There is no form in which a coat on a nail is a record of a man. There is no form in which two pieces of paper in the inside pocket of that coat can be produced. There is no form in which any of it can be laid against him, or against anybody else. He is the cheapest person in this matter and he has been the cheapest person in this matter for about two years. This is the first time in about two years he has used it on purpose, and nobody knows that he did, and nobody is going to.

He had thought about the other thing. There is a thing he is able to do that he does not do. There is a mark in a printed strip that would go into a coat with nobody knowing whose it was. There is a second of a second that could be left on a counter in a room where a woman of twenty-four would find it.

He thought about the second one for the length of time it takes a person to cross a street. Then he walked to the end of that street and turned round and came back. Nobody sent him. He has not cut anything, and he has not left anything anywhere for anybody to find.

---

He put his hand on the outside of the pocket the way he has done about four times in two years, and did not open it, and the two things in it were on opposite sides and they had not moved since a wet morning about two years ago.

The erased name and the claim that travelled out of this empire with it are where they were. The name at the end of that struck line is not printed anywhere, and the hand that struck it is not printed anywhere, and there is no column in this empire that would take either of them. A name gone out of a leaf cannot be made twice, and it cannot be made once. He is thirty-eight, and he is not going to be forty before it happens, and he said that out loud to a room a month ago, and nobody heard it, and nobody is going to.

Nobody was thanked. Nobody was forgiven. Nobody was sent for, and nothing was resolved. The pair of them are still shut in whatever they were shut in, on the paper that shut them, and nobody has stood up yet and asked for that paper to be called bad. The question on the shelf under the window with no pane in it still carries no full stop, and it is not going to be printed out in any building in this empire. The second half of a sentence a foreman of fifty-one began in a doorway has still not arrived, and nobody has asked her for it.

He came back at about the sixth hour, and the only two things in this empire that had not moved were in the pocket of a coat on a nail.
