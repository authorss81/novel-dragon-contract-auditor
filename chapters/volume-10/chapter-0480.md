# Chapter 0480: A Man Paid By The Corner Findable For About Ninety Seconds, A Woman Buying A Second At The Boards, And An Arrangement That Did Not Move

He has said out loud, more than once, that he is not going to be thanked and that he is not going to be findable, and he said both of them in a room in this city and neither of them was on anything, and this morning he stood at the corner of that counter for about ninety seconds inside the door with the light on him and anybody in that building could have walked out and got him.

**He is about twenty-six and he is paid by the corner, and he came up those four flights at about the fifth hour and bought nothing and said nothing, and nobody told him he did not have to, and he has not been thanked for any part of it and is not going to be.** It is about two months since a woman in this building put fourpence on those boards and said the word second out loud, and he was at the end of those boards at the fourth hour that morning, and he has not said one word to anybody about it since, and nobody has asked him one word about it.

It is the second day of the second week of the tenth month of the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is the fifth hour, and the counter shuts at the sixth.

---

At about twenty past the five a woman came up with fourpence already in her hand and put it down. She said one flat sentence about wanting a second of a thing she was not going to describe. Tamsin Rook took a sheet out of the case and set it on the boards, and the woman put it inside her coat and went down the four flights. **That is the second time this room has made a second of a second, and the two of them were nine weeks apart and neither of them was asked one word, and neither time could anybody at those boards have told which of the two sheets it was, because there is nothing on either of them that says.**

The light did not move off him while she was doing it and it did not move off him when she had finished, and the boards at the near end took the fourpence and the sheet in the ordinary order while the light was still on him, and neither of the people who worked there looked over at the corner while they were doing it.

And he was standing at the corner for all of it, inside the door, where the light from the landing window comes down onto the boards and stays on anybody standing at that end of the room.

---

Marn Ottery said it at about half past the five, to the boards, and not to him, and she said it once, as an arrangement and not as a favour.

"**A copy handed to a person is a piece of paper changing hands, and a piece of paper changing hands is not a sentence put to a person, and nothing that has been said out loud in this matter in nine volumes of it moved this morning. I am not going to say the number of the things that have been said out loud in this matter and neither is anybody else on this floor, and it did not move this morning and it is not going to move this month.** Nobody in this room is going to be told what that sheet was for, and I am not going to tell them and I am not going to be asked and the man at the corner bought nothing and is owed nothing and is not going to be thanked for standing in a room."

Nothing came back from either end of the boards for a while after that, and the case stood where it was at the near end with the space the sheet had come out of still bare, and nobody went near it.

---

He said nothing back and he did not go and stand at the end of the boards, and after a while he said one thing that was not a question and was not for anybody.

"That is not the first time I have stood in a room where something like that happened."

He said it to the boards the way she says things to the boards, and she did not answer it at once, and the case went down behind the boards at her end while neither of them was looking at the other.

"You have it backwards and I have watched you have it backwards since the second day you came up here. **You think ninety seconds of light makes you findable and it does not, and what it does is make you a person somebody has seen, and those are not the same thing and the second one cannot be got back.** And I have not kept a count of anything in this room in eleven years, and you have not asked me one thing and I have not asked you one thing, and that is the arrangement and it has been the arrangement for eleven years."

She put one hand flat on the boards and left it there, and she did not take it off while he was standing there, and that was not an act of anything and she has never claimed it was. "**You have been standing in the light at that corner for about ninety seconds this morning, and nobody walked out of this room and got you. You are going to notice that more than you are going to notice anything else that happened on this floor today, and I am not going to be thanked for having said the true thing about a copy of a copy. I am not going to be thanked for noticing ninety seconds either, and I would like it noticed that I noticed it before I said it.**"

---

He was gone before the sixth hour. He did not put a coin on those boards, and he was not given anything, and he asked nothing, and nobody asked him anything, and nobody was sent for, and no line was cut into the strip along the top of anything in that room, and no space under any printed heading was filled in, and nothing at that counter was resolved in that week or in the fortnight before it.

The corner at the near end of the boards was not looked at by anybody after he had gone down the stair and the light off the landing window went on coming down onto the boards at the same place, and the boards stayed up for the rest of the hour with nobody at the end of them.

**Under that wall board the drawer is still shut and there are still four things in it, and two of them have never once been looked at by anybody. The box under the far end of those boards was where it was at the second hour and it will be there at the seventh, and of the four things on that wall the fourth is the one the three of them still have no account of, and it went up four years ago and it is still the only one of the four that has never been looked at by anybody who came after it.** Nobody thanked anybody. Nothing was put to anybody on that floor in that week and nothing in it was resolved, and the case went in behind the boards at the sixth hour, and the four went back up onto the wall in the places they have held for eleven years.

**And a man of about twenty-six walked out of a building a little before the sixth hour with nothing in his hands who had been standing in the light in a place where people can be found for about ninety seconds, and nobody came, and nobody is going to come, and there is no form anywhere in this empire by which a person is put back to not having been seen. That is the whole of what the ninety seconds cost him and it did not cost him a bill, and it took the only thing he had, which was that nobody knew he was there.**
