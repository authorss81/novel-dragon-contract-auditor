# Chapter 0463: The Sixth Time Up Those Four Flights, Two People Left On That Floor, And A Thing With One Holder In It

There were two people on that floor at about twenty minutes to the sixth hour and one of them was him. The other two had gone down the stair about an hour ago and the case was already in behind the boards and the boards were down to a foot and a half, and there was no light in that room except what came in at the stair head.

**He is thirty-eight and his name is Marek Kest, and there is no office over his name in this empire and there is not going to be one, and he has been up that stair six times in his life and he has not put a coin on those boards one of the six times.** He has refused the reading and he does not exercise the thing he is able to do, and he has given the reason for that out loud once, in about nine words, and nothing on that floor is going to get it out of him a second time.

It is the second day of the first week of the sixth month of the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is about twenty minutes to the sixth hour.

---

She had her hand on the wall and she was putting the four items up in the places they have held for eleven years, and she did the first three of them without stopping, and then she stopped with the fourth in her hand.

He was at the end of the boards where the boards were down to a foot and a half, and he was looking at the wall.

**He wanted two things and one of them was the fourth of those four, and it had been the fourth of those four for about four years.** It was in her own hand, and she had put it up there herself, and the three of them had looked at it every working day of that time, and not one of the three of them could say what it was for, and the last six times he had come up that stair he had come up it for that.

She put it in its place. She did not turn round and she did not speak for about as long as it takes a person to put a thing flat against a wall and be sure of it.

"You have come up here six times and you have never once asked me what that is, and I have never once offered it, and I want you to know which of those two of us is holding a door shut."

Then she said the other half of it, and she said it to the boards and not to the wall.

"**A thing with one holder can be stopped by stopping the holder, and stopping a holder is a thing you do by finding the person and not by finding the paper. A thing with two holders can only be stopped by stopping both, and that is a thing you cannot do cheaply, because neither of the two of them can be shown holding it.** A copy is not the thing and a holder of a copy is not a party to anything, and that is the whole of it, and it is not a safety and it is not a rescue, and it does not put one person anywhere safer than they were this morning."

---

He put his hand flat on the inside pocket of his coat and left it there.

"You have not asked me what is in there."

"I have not asked you what is in there and I am not going to, and you have not told me and I have not looked, and there is no form in this empire in which either of those two facts is entered against anybody." She took her hand off the wall. "**I said the half of it that is about paper and I said it to a room with a man in it who has something in a coat he has never shown anybody. That is not a gift and it is not a warning and it does not put you in it any more than you were in it at the second hour, and if you take one thing off that stair tonight, take the second half and leave the first, because the first half is the one everybody in this matter has been saying to each other for nine volumes and the second half is the one that costs you something.**"

"I am not going to give it to anybody."

"I know you are not. You have never told one person in this city that you have it and there is nobody I could tell, and that is not a virtue and it is not a plan. It is the arrangement. **A leaf with a line struck out of it has one holder and the one holder is you, and it is the only one of the two things on you that anybody could ever get at, and getting at it would not be done with paper.**"

He did not say anything to that. His hand stayed where it was on the pocket and he did not open it and nothing came out of it.

---

"I do not know what is in that coat and I am not going to be told. What I do know is that you have not opened it in front of me on any of the six times you have been up this stair, and a man carrying nothing does not do that, and there are two of them in there."

"You have not asked me which of the two I mean."

"I have not asked you which of the two I mean and I would not know what to do if you told me, and I have said the half of it that I can say, which is that a person holding something which is not the thing itself cannot be shown the thing and cannot be asked one thing about the thing, because the thing was never his to be shown." She went back to the boards and lifted them another foot. "**That is why the two of them are on opposite sides of the same coat. The one of them can be taken off you by finding you. The other one cannot be taken off you at all, and it cannot be got onto anybody else, and you are the only person in this city who is carrying something that is safer than he is, and safety is not what it is.**"

---

He went down the four flights a little after the sixth hour. There was nobody on any of the landings, and those four flights are the only way up to that floor, and a man coming down them at the end of a working day takes them faster than he takes them up.

**Nobody counted what he was carrying, and nobody was stopped. There is no form anywhere in this empire in which anybody could afterwards be entered as the one who counted what another person was carrying, and no form anywhere in this empire has a line for a person who was stopped, and both of those absences are one absence, and that is the reason a man with two things in a coat is the cheapest person he can be.**

Nobody sent for him. Nobody was sent for. He was not thanked and he was not going to be, and he had not been thanked on any of the six mornings and Marn Ottery had said out loud in the last one that he was not going to be thanked for standing in a room while she said something, and the room was not going to hold a claim on him for it either.

The fourth item on that wall is the one none of the three of them can account for, and it is in the hand of the woman who put it there about four years ago, and nothing on that floor this week turned it into anything else.
