# Chapter 0454: A Form Taken Off A Board By A Door, A Chain Across The Front Bench, And A Lamp Not Lit Until Seven

She took the form off the board by the door at about the fourth hour and carried it the length of that shed in one hand. That is the whole of what she did between the fourth hour and the fifth, and nobody in that lane saw her do it, because there is nobody in that lane at the fourth hour.

**Halla Wray. Fifty-one. A foreman, and standing in a box that a class put her in — not a room, a box.** The class has a form behind it, and that form has been on that board by the door for longer than anybody working that lane can remember, and nobody has ever stood in that lane and tried to have a form set aside, and there is no way anywhere of asking for one to be. The form that would take the two at the back of that shed out of the back of it is on the board now, and it will be on that board again on the second of the two days next week, and the two of them have been at the back of that shed the whole time.

The shed is about nine foot by eleven and the bench in it is longer than the room. There is a lamp on a bracket at the high end of it that is not lit until about the seventh hour, and it is not going to be before the seventh hour on any day of the week. A chain goes across the front of the front bench, and there is a man on the other side of that chain, and he has not gone under it.

It is the fourth day of the fourth week of the third month of the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is the fourth hour, and the lamp is not lit.

---

Neither of the two of seventeen at the back of that shed moved, and not one word was said to either of them by anybody. **The girl is not given the chance to say no about anything, and the reader of seventeen is unspoken to, unthanked and unsent for, and is not going to start.** The second half of a sentence she began in a doorway has still not arrived, and it has not arrived in a week, and she is not going to stand in a doorway and finish it this month.

The back of the shed is where the light does not get to, and the two of them had been standing at the back of it since before she came, and there was nothing over the far half of the floor at the fourth hour except the two of them and the chain in front of them.

She held the form in her left hand and did the two ends of that shed in the order she does them. The form went with her the whole length of the floor and back again.

She kept it off her coat the whole way and it did not touch anything on the way.

---

"You have taken it off the board."

"I have taken it off the board."

"That form has been on that board for the whole of the time you have been standing inside the class it stands behind. It has not been filled in since before you came to that bench, and it is not going to be filled in this week either. I have watched you go up and down that floor with it in your hand like a woman carrying a thing she has already decided about."

"It is a piece of paper with a heading printed over a space on it, and it is older than anybody in this lane, and about nine foot of this shed has been walked past it every working day of that time. I have decided one thing about it this week and I have not told anybody what it is."

"A person inside a class can be put back into it by whoever fills in the next form. A person who can be put back into a thing is a person who is already a party to it. A person who is already a party cannot be given a copy of anything without the copy being evidence against her. **And I can read a paragraph. I have always been able to read a paragraph, and I am not going to read one out for anybody, and I have said four sentences in this room and not one of them was ever answered, and I never wanted one answered, and that is the fifth and it is the last one.**"

---

He did not move the chain.

"You have that right and you have said it in the wrong order. The order is that a class is a box with a form behind it. The next person to fill in that form is put in the same box as you, and the box itself says nothing about who is standing in it. The copy comes after all of that. You do not get to the copy until you are a party, and by the time you are a party the copy is evidence, and you cannot be handed a substitute without the substitute being a thing somebody can put in front of you."

"The form has not been filled in since before I came."

"The form does not have to be filled in this week. The form only has to exist. That is the whole of what a form is, and I have been on the other side of a chain for longer than you have been in that box. I have watched about nine hundred of them go out of a building nine miles off this lane, and I have watched a man of about sixty-one sign the foot of every one of them without being able to read a paragraph. He is not one of the four who cannot make sense of a paragraph and he never was, and I am not one of them either, and I have never once pretended to be."

"You have never once pretended to be anything."

"I have not. I can read a paragraph and I have said four sentences in this room and I have not been answered by anybody and I never wanted to be. I am not going to read that form out to you, or to the two at the back of that bench, or to anybody else on any day of my life."

The form was in her left hand the whole of the time that was being said and she did not change hands with it once, and the angle it was held at did not vary, and nobody at the front of the bench looked at it.

---

"That is a fifth thing you have said, and you have not made it a speech, and I have not been asked for it and I have not been given a fifth sentence."

"You have not been asked for a fifth sentence by anybody. I have not given one and I am not going to. I said what I came in here with my hand in my coat to say, and that was not it, and you have just made me say a different thing, and I have not thanked you for it and I am not going to."

It was noticed, and nothing else came of it being noticed, and the lamp was not lit, and neither of the two at the back of the bench had moved while any of it was being said.

"You were not going to tell me what you came in here with your hand in your coat to say."

"I was not, and I have not, and I have not got a coat on me, and that was a figure of speech, and it is the first one I have used in this shed in about nineteen years and I would like it noticed."

---

The form went back on the board by the door before the fifth hour. It went on at the same angle it has gone on at every time it has gone on there, and she put it there with her own hand and squared the bottom of it with the side of her hand the way a person squares a thing they have decided about.

**A class can be re-entered by whoever fills in the next form. Nobody in that shed re-entered anybody this week, and nobody in that lane has filled in that form since before she came to that bench. The chain is where it was and the bench is where it was, and the lamp is on its bracket and is not lit and is not going to be lit before about the seventh hour.**

The form that put her where she is is not being asked to be void by anybody, and there is no procedure for asking, and there has never been one. The reason that is not a defect is that nothing in this empire has ever been asked to be void, and the whole of this matter is made of things that were not asked about.

Then she did the two ends again, and the lamp went on by itself at about the seventh hour the way it goes on by itself, and the two of seventeen were still at the back of that bench. Nobody was thanked in that shed this week and nobody was forgiven and nobody was sent for, and the second half of a sentence she began in a doorway in a month that is gone has still not come out of her. She is fifty-one and she has a shed to open in the morning.
