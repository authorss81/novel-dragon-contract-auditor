# Chapter 0486: A Coat Taken Off The Chair And Hung On A Nail, Two True Things Said To An Empty Room, And Twenty Minutes Before The Hour

There were twenty minutes before the hour and he spent them in that room the way he has spent them every week for about two years. Nobody came to that door, and nobody in this matter has any form that would put them on a stair with a reason for coming.

Marek Kest. Thirty-eight, and about a year ago a woman offered him a reading and he turned it down, and there is not one thing in this empire with his name against it that anybody could take off him, which is the difference between a man who holds nothing and a man who has been emptied. He turned the reading down and he keeps his hands off the one thing he is able to do. About nine words came out of his mouth once, in a bay, a long time ago, about why, and that is the whole of what there is on that subject, and it is not coming out a second time this month or any month.

It is the fourth day of the fourth week of the eleventh month of the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is about twenty minutes to the hour.

---

The coat was on the chair against no wall and it had been on that chair for about two years, and there were two things in the inside pocket of it and they were on opposite sides of him.

One of them is a leaf with a line struck through what stood at the foot of that line, and the hand that struck it was not his mother's, and what belonged there is the thing he has wanted since before the fire, and it is the reason he came down a street in this city about two years ago with a bundle he did not open. The claim that travelled with the name went out of this empire the same way the name did, and he has got one of those two and has never had the other, and he has told nobody about either of them and he is not going to tell anybody this week.

The second of the two is a sheet of the same stock that counter sells, and fourpence bought it out of a case at the top of four flights on a wet morning about two years ago, and it is not the leaf, and the leaf has never been copied, and a name gone out of a leaf is a name that cannot be made twice.

Neither of them is coming out of that pocket this week. The piece of paper with nothing printed on it is still where a man left it four miles off and did not open it, standing against the base of that wall on the table, and it is not on a form and it is not a record, and he is not going to move it and nothing is going to be stood up on that table tonight that is not already on it.

---

He worked it out at about twenty past.

It took the length of time a person needs to take a coat off a chair.

"A line cut into the strip," he said, out loud, to that room, and the room is the only party that has ever received anything from him. "**Every blank that counter has ever sold carries one printed band along its top edge, and it is the same band on every one of them, and the four things set along the top of it are the same four in the same order. About four hundred sheets a year come out of that case with a mark cut into that band, and the mark is the same mark on all of them, and there is nobody in this empire who could take one of those sheets in a hand and say whose hand it was.**"

He had the coat in his hands by then and had not decided to have it.

"That is the one. That is the only thing in ten years of this that could go into a coat and come out the other side of a room without anybody knowing whose it was. A name would go into it. A name is a shape, and a shape can be put into a strip with a knife.

I have stood at the top of four flights in a morning and been told in words, by a woman at a counter, that about four hundred of these go out in a year. That is not a rumour. **That is the thing I have been after since before the fire and it is in a drawer in a building and it takes about four seconds.**"

He hung the coat on the nail.

The nail went into that wall crooked about the height of his hand about two years ago and nothing hung off it until this evening.

There is nothing in this empire that would keep a record of a coat going onto a nail. He is a man whose coat is on a wall now instead of on a chair, and he did that in a room with nobody in it, and the two sheets are in the pocket of it and they are still in the pocket of it. The chair against no wall is where it has been for two years and is not what he looks at when he comes in.

---

Then he stood there and worked out the other half of it, out loud, because there was nobody to stop him and because that is the only way a thing gets said in this matter when there is no second person in the room to say it to.

"**I am not going to cut one, and nobody is going to cut one for me, and there is nobody who has been asked and nobody who is going to be.** And here is the reason and it is four seconds long. The first cut is the one that costs, and after that cut I am a man who cuts lines in strips, and a man who cuts lines in strips is a man a person could put in front of anything. There is no column over the mark and there never is going to be one, and that is exactly why the knife is the dangerous part and not the pen. **Anybody can put a name in a book. Almost nobody can put a hand in a strip and stay a stranger afterwards.** And I would rather have nothing than that, and I have had nothing for about two years and I know what it costs, and a name is worth more than it."

He did not say her name and he did not say the name at the end of that struck line and he did not say whose hand put it there, and none of the three is going to be said in this room this month.

---

He sat down on the stool and he did not do anything for the rest of it, and the light went out of that window the way it goes out of that window, and the piece of paper with nothing printed on it stood against the base of that wall where it has stood, and four feet of plaster beside that window had a coat on a nail in it and nothing else.

A man of thirty-eight carried two things for about two years and told nobody, and nothing in that room was thanked and nothing in it was sent for and nothing in it was resolved this week, and on one evening he moved them from a chair to a nail. One of the two has a name gone out of it. The other is the exact width of a name.

There is a third thing in this empire now that would fit in that pocket with them, and he has worked out what it is and who could say whose it is, and he is not going to use it. Nobody knew that he had worked it out, and nobody in this city is going to be told, and there is no form anywhere that would put him in a room to be asked about it, which is the whole of what the not using it amounts to.
