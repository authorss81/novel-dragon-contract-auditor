# Chapter 0434: A Knock Two Streets Back From The River Road, A Form Out Of A Coat And Not Put Down, And Four Feet Of Wall Left Bare

It is the same room as the other weeks, two streets back from a street that runs down to the river road, and everything in it is where it was the last time he stood in it. There has never been anything on the table. There is no fire in it and the cold comes off the plaster behind the middle chair.

**A common nail is in that wall at about the height of his hand and it went in crooked, and no picture and no list and no coat has ever hung off it, and nothing is going to hang off it this week either.** The window looks at the back of another house, and the plaster either side of the nail has been the same colour for the whole of the two years.

Marek Kest. Thirty-eight. **A stage was never reached and none is coming, and there is nothing behind him this week** — no office, no fund, no list, no leave, no certification, and not one of the five is on its way to that room.

It is the fourth day of the fourth week of the tenth month of the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is the fifth hour.

---

A folded leaf has been in the inside pocket of that coat for about two years. A line at the foot of it was struck through by a hand that did not belong to his mother, and what that line was to carry is the one thing he has wanted since before the fire. The claim that would have gone with it went with the line.

He has one of those two and has never had the other. **There is no form anywhere in this empire in which a man with nothing behind him can put down a disputed claim**, and he is not going to go looking for one, and looking would be the beginning of being a person who can be found.

**The space at the foot of a sheet is the exact width of a name.** A woman told him that at a counter four months ago, in the ordinary voice, and he did not buy a copy of anything that week and he is not going to buy one this week either, and there is no other counter he knows of that would sell him one.

---

Then a knock on the door, and it was not loud, and it was one knock and not two.

He was not expecting anybody. He has not been expecting anybody for about two years and has not been disappointed about it on any day of that time, and the arrangement of that is the only thing he has got and he is not going to break it by standing in a doorway.

He opened the door because a door in that building has to be opened.

---

The man on the step was about thirty-four and there was a fair hand on him somewhere, and he had been in a room like that one before in his life and Marek Kest did not know that and was not going to be told.

Marek Kest stood aside about two feet, which is not an invitation and is not an exclusion, and the man came in and the door went shut behind him, and neither of them said one word for about nine seconds.

"You have not come up those stairs."

"I have not come up those stairs and I am not going to say what I have come for, and I am not going to be thanked for standing in this room and you are not going to ask me for anything, and we are not going to get to the end of either of those in one evening."

---

He had a form in the inside of his coat. He took it out about a quarter of an hour after he had come in and he did not put it down.

**It had a printed heading over it and a ruled space under the heading, and the ruled space was empty, and the form had come through a letter slot about two years ago and had not been returned to anybody and it had not been in his house for about six weeks of that two years and he has never known who took it out and put it back.**

He held it in his hand the whole time he was in the room and he did not offer it and he did not put it on the table and he did not put it on the floor.

"There is a heading on that."

"There is a heading on that and there has been one on it since the day it was printed."

---

"And you have a number in your head and it is not in this room."

"It is not in this room and it is not on that and it is not going to be said out loud in here tonight, and nobody in this room is going to confirm it and nobody is going to deny it, and I have been carrying it about two years and I have not written it down and I am not going to start because there is a man in a room who is not a party to anything."

Nobody confirmed it and nobody denied it and the number is not in this room and it is not in any of the ten of these weeks.

---

"I have it in about four words."

He said it to the middle of the room and not to Marek Kest and he said it exactly the way a man says a thing he has said once before in a lane to a woman who keeps a book, and he did not say the four words.

**He has never said them out loud to anybody and he did not say them out loud to him, and there is no form anywhere in this empire for a person to be entered as the one somebody did not say a thing to, and nobody was entered and nobody was thanked and nobody was sent for.**

---

Marek Kest did not ask. That is the whole of what he did that evening and it took him about four minutes to do it and it is the only decision in the chapter.

He could have asked. **A piece of paper with nothing printed on it would have gone on that table and would have been found by whoever took that room next, and there is a man in this matter he would want it to go to and he does not know where that man is and he is not going to be the one who makes him findable.** He has nothing behind him to be liable for and that is a price and not a virtue and it is the only thing about him that this matter has ever been able to use.

So he did not put a paper on the table, and the nail stayed crooked in four feet of bare wall, and the four words stayed in the other man's mouth.

---

**And the chapter does not know whose the asking was, and neither of them did, and neither of them is going to find out.** A form with a printed heading over a ruled space went into that room and came out of it in the hand it came in, and a man who could not read a paragraph is not the reason and a man of about thirty-four with a fair hand is not the reason, and the four feet of wall between them was bare at the end of the evening and it was bare at the start of it.

**He did not reach a stage this week, and he did not use the one thing he is able to use, and nothing was put in front of him to refuse.** No office, no fund, no list, no leave and no wage came out of that room, because there is no one on earth who would have given him any of the five. Nobody was sent for and nobody sent anybody, and what belonged at the foot of that struck line is not in any mouth in any of the ten weeks of this part of the volume, and neither is the hand that struck it.

The man of about thirty-four left at about the sixth hour and went down a stair that has nothing to do with a door on the fourth floor and is not on anybody's list. Nobody thanked him for the visit and he did not expect to be and Marek Kest was not thanked for standing in a room with a man in it and would not have been, and the arrangement of that is the arrangement of the whole of this matter and it is still standing.
