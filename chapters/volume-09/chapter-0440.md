# Chapter 0440: A Man Who Came Up That Stair And Bought Nothing, Twenty Minutes That Are Still Twenty Minutes, And An Undertaking Nobody Has Exercised

She is twenty-four and she is at the near end of those boards and she has been on that floor two years, and nobody in that building has ever put a question to her and nobody is going to this morning.

**Her undertaking is live. It is unretired and it is unexercised and it has not been exercised on any day of the two years and it is not going to be exercised this week. The day and the month of it are not a thing she has said out loud in that room or out of it, and they are not a thing any record of that room carries, and they are not said out loud here.**

It is the second day of the second week of the twelfth month of the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is the sixth hour, and the counter shuts at the sixth hour.

---

She was in at twenty minutes to the fifth, which is what she has done every working day for about seventeen weeks. **It is still twenty minutes, and it is not a decision, and it is not written down anywhere and it is not going on anything.** This empire has no form in which the woman who sits at that counter alone for the length of a fifth of an hour is recorded as the one who sat there.

**What seventeen weeks has done to her is not a thing she did. It is that she has stopped saying things.** She said one in that room in the second week of the ninth month and she said one in the fourth week and she has said nothing in this one, and nobody has asked her for any of the three and nobody is going to, and the not being asked is the thing she has got and she has not lost it and she has not spent it.

---

Then the stair, and a man came up it at about the sixth hour and he had nothing in his hands.

**It was the third time in his life he had come up those four flights and the stair has no door at the foot of it, and the stair was empty from the bottom landing to the top, and he did not knock on it, and he has never knocked on that stair.**

He stood at the near end of the boards the way a man stands at the end of a counter he has no business at, for about the length of a sheet being squared, and nobody said one word to him.

**She put her hand flat on the boards about a foot from his and not touching them, the way she did in the third week of the third month, and she did not say anything, and he did not say anything, and there is no form anywhere in this empire for two people to be recorded as the ones who did not say anything in a room on a working afternoon.**

He did not buy a copy of anything. He was not served. He was not asked for anything and he asked for nothing, and he went back down four flights and there is no door at the foot of that stair and the stair has no other way up it.

---

Marn Ottery at the far end of those boards had her back about a foot off the case and she said one thing, and she said it to the boards and not to anybody, and nobody had asked her for it.

"**About four hundred blanks go out of that case every year and there has not been one of them on these boards since the second week of the eighth month, and I have not been keeping a count, and I have noticed, and I am not going to say what I have noticed.**"

"You have noticed that. You have said so out loud and you have said at the same time that you have not been keeping a count, and I am not going to be the one who makes those two into a question, because it is not one and you did not put it to me."

"I have not been keeping a count and I have noticed, and nobody in this room has to treat those two as connected, and I am not going to be the one who connects them."

---

The clerk of about twenty-nine at the far end of the two joined tables had a sheet squared in front of her and her pen down, and she had said one thing in that room in about four years and she said nothing this week and she is not going to be asked for a second one and she is not going to give one.

There were three people in that room and there had been four a quarter of an hour earlier, and the fourth had gone down four flights with nothing in his hands, and **nobody at the top of that stair has ever been told one thing about him and nobody in that building is going to be told and there is nobody in that building who could carry it if they were.**

---

**And the thing she did not do, and the reason, is the whole of the rest of that afternoon.** She did not say anything to Marn Ottery about the twenty minutes, and she did not say anything about a man coming up a stair, and she did not say anything about the case, and she did not decide anything at all, and the decision about whether she takes her colleague's judgment on a thing like this is not made and has not been made and is not going to be made this week or in any week of that year.

Nobody thanked anybody. Nobody was forgiven. Nobody was sent for and nothing was resolved, and the two people in their boxes are in their boxes and the form that put them there is not asked to be void by anybody.

---

**Out of sight of the door, past about six foot of bare deal, there is a box that has stood under the end of those boards for eleven years with about four hundred unfilled blanks in it. It did not open this week and it did not shift, and nobody took a wage out of it, and nobody could have been shown doing either.** The handle of the drawer under the wall board has not been turned in eleven years. Nothing was ruled, nothing under a printed heading was written in, and no fifth column is anywhere in that building.

**The count of things anybody has asked out loud in this matter is the same as it was on the morning this month began, and it is the same as it was on the morning the first man in a coat went down four flights with a thing in him.** A man came up those stairs at about the sixth hour and bought nothing and went back down them. Nobody in that building saw him arrive except a woman of twenty-four who is not going to say so, and she has not been thanked and is not going to be, and the twenty minutes were twenty minutes and the counter shut at the sixth hour.
