# Chapter 0422: A Case Nobody Can Account For, Nine Years Of Wanting To Know Where It Is Filled, And A Box That Does Not Move

The boards at the top of that stair are about nine foot of deal and the stair has no other way up it. **The case stands at the near end of them and the box is at the far end, out of sight of the door, and there is about six foot of bare deal between the two and nothing at all standing on it.** The drawer is under the wall board and it is shut, and the wall board has four items on it and not one of the four is time.

It is the fourth day of the fourth week of the seventh month of the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is the third hour, and the counter shuts at the sixth hour.

---

**He comes in on the second of the two days and has been coming in on the second of the two days for about nine years, and he is not a lawyer and has not been one for about nine years, and the two facts are the same fact and have never been put side by side by anybody.**

He had been in since the third hour. He had done the same eleven things in the same order, and he was at the head of the tables, and Marn Ottery was at the near end of the boards with her back about a foot off the case.

The boards are nine foot of deal and they are the only flat thing on the floor, and a man can lay a sheet down on them and square it and lift it again without anybody at the counter having to look up, and the squaring is done with the heel of a hand and never with any sort of rule, and he does it the same way whichever day of the week comes round.

"I am going to say a thing," he said, "and I have never said it on this floor and I have thought about it every week for about nine years, and I am not asking you anything."

"I know what you are going to say and you are not going to ask me for it."

---

"**I want to know where the case is filled.**"

He said it to the boards in the middle, not to her, which is how a man says a thing he has carried nine years to a person he is not giving it to.

"About four hundred of them a year come out of that case in the hand of somebody and go down four flights of that stair, and I have been in this room on the second of the two days for about nine years, and I have wanted to know where they are filled every working day of those nine years, and there is no form anywhere in this empire in which you could put that down."

---

"There is no form in which to put it down," Marn Ottery said, "and I have said that sentence in this room twice and it is true both times and I am not going to say it a third time, and I am going to say you something else instead and I am not going to be thanked for it and neither of us is going to be thanked for it by anybody in this building."

"I would not have used it."

"I am not going to be asked for the rest of it and I am not going to wait to be asked, because I have been in this room about nine years and I have had nothing else to do in it."

About four flights is not much stair, and a person crosses it at a steady rate four times a day going up and coming back down, and a person going up with an empty pair of hands takes longer over it than a person going up with four sheets under an arm, and nobody on the floor has ever timed either of the two or written either of them down.

"**A man who does not know where a thing is filled is a man who has to go on coming up a stair.**"

His hand went on with a thing his hand does and has been doing for about nine years, and Marn Ottery looked at the two of them once and then turned back to the boards, and nobody in the room has ever been told which of the two she was looking at.

He put his pen down, which he did not do for the rest of that morning.

---

"**A supply that is in a room is a supply that is a day's walk from about nine hundred rooms.**" She said it to the boards, the way she says everything she says in that room. "That box has been under the far end of those boards for eleven years. About four hundred of these go out of a case at the near end every year in the hand of somebody, and not one of them is filled in this building, and about nine hundred rooms is the whole of what this empire has got for a person to be in. **Four hundred a year is a great many pieces of paper and it is not anybody's plan, and there is nobody in this empire that it is fair to be angry at, and I am saying that to you as a fact and not as a comfort.**"

"I did not say it was a conspiracy."

The clerk of about twenty-nine kept her head down over the same page at the other end of the room, and she turned the page when the page was done and not before, and the pen she had was not the pen he had left off using, and the two of them are on the floor at the same rate every working day and have never once said so to each other.

"You did not and I am not giving you one. I am giving you a shape and a shape is what you have been asking me for in every way except out loud for nine years."

---

"**And here is the reason nobody can be told where the case is filled, and it is the plainest thing I have got, and you are not going to like it and I am not going to be thanked for saying it.**" She took her hands off the boards. "Producing it is an office. A person can only be shown a thing by somebody being made to produce it, and producing it is an office, and an office has a name at the end of it, **and there is nobody on this floor whose job it is, and there has never been one, and I did not draw it and neither did the three of them before me.**"

The clerk of about twenty-nine did not look up. She had said once that she would not say a second thing in that room and she has not, and she said nothing now, and there is no form anywhere in this empire for a clerk to be recorded as the one who did not speak.

---

"**You have just handed me a reason for not having what I have wanted for nine years, and I did not ask you for it, and I would like it noticed that I did not ask you for it.**"

"I did not offer it to you."

"You offered it to the room." He picked his pen up again and did not write with it. "**And I am not going to be the man who says thank you for it, and I am not going to be the man who says what he would have done with it, because I would have done something with it, and I have worked out in nine years that there is exactly one thing I would have done with it and I am not going to stand in this room and hand that to you at ten minutes to the fourth hour.**"

He did not say what the one thing was and Marn Ottery did not ask him for it and nobody in that room did.

---

A man came up those four flights at about the fourth hour with nothing in his hands and went along the boards and stopped where the boards end and stood there about the length of a sheet being squared, and did not buy anything, and went down the four flights again.

The man at the head of the tables watched him the whole way up and the whole way down.

"That is the third man in nine years who has come up that stair and not put a penny on this counter," he said, "and I have never once been able to work out what a person does with nine years of a thing like that."

"You have never asked him."

"He is the only person on this floor who would not have been given it if anybody gave it to anybody, and I have just been told the reason in that order, and I am not going to put the two together in a room with three people in it."

None of the three of them moves towards the counter when it shuts, and the shutting of it is done by a person who does not come up the stair for it, and all three of them have watched the shutting more times than any of them has ever counted, and every one of them went on with the work in hand at the moment it shut last.

---

**The box under the far end of those boards did not open and did not move. The drawer under the wall board did not open and has been shut for eleven years and there are four things in it and two of them have never been looked at.** No blank was filled in that room and no ruled space was ruled and no heading was printed over anything, and the case is still not the box and the board on the wall still carries four items and not one of them is time.

He stayed until the sixth hour and did not ask her for anything and she did not thank him and he did not thank her, and what went down those four flights after the boards went up was a man who had wanted one thing for nine years and had been handed the shape of it and the reason there was no more of it. **Both of those are now in a room where the third person in it could not have been given either one, and nobody on that floor said a word about that either.**
