# Chapter 0448: A Sheet Stood Against The Base Of That Wall, A Man At The Door With Nothing In His Hands, And A Space The Exact Width Of A Name

The piece of paper had been on that table for a week and it was not square with the edge and there was nothing to square it with, because there is nothing on that table to line it up against, and there never has been.

**He had not filled it in. He is not going to fill it in, and there is no use for it that he can put to anybody, and a thing with nothing printed on it asks a person for nothing, which is the only kind of paper this empire has and the only one he has ever wanted.**

It is the second day of the second week of the second month of the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is the fifth hour.

---

He stood it up on the table against the base of the wall, at the end nearest the window, where he can see it from the stool and from the bed and from the doorway, and the fold took about nine seconds to come out of it and it has stayed out.

**About four feet of wall beside that window has been bare for the whole of the two years and there is a nail in it that went in crooked and nothing has ever hung off the nail. The paper is not on the nail. It is standing on the table at the foot of that wall, and the wall is bare above it, and he has used the wall and not the paper for nothing, which is the whole of what he has done about it.**

The face of it was still blank when the week ran out. The room went its ordinary round around it every working day and found nothing in it worth stopping for, and nobody squared it and nobody set anything else down beside it.

---

There was a knock on the door at about the fifth hour and it was not loud and it was not two knocks, and it was the same man, and he was thirty-four or thereabouts, with a fair hand on him somewhere, and a room of that kind behind him in his own life that Marek Kest knew nothing whatever about and was never going to be told.

He stayed on the step and he had nothing in his hands, and there is no form anywhere in this empire in which a man turning up empty gets that written down, and nothing in this room got written down.

"**I am not going to be the man who takes it.**"

He said it before he was asked for it and nobody asked him for it, and that is the second time a man has said a thing in that room before being asked for it, and the first of the two was about three months ago and it was four words long and it was not said in a question.

"You have not come up those stairs."

"I have come up those stairs and I have not been in this room for about three months. I have come now because there is a piece of paper with nothing printed on it standing on that table, and I am not the one who is going to take it. I have not come in here to be thanked and I have not come in here to be asked anything, and there is not going to be a question in this room while I am standing in it."

The step held him neither in nor out. He kept his hands where they could be seen, empty and still, and made no move toward the table.

---

Marek Kest did not ask.

He could have asked. There is a man in this matter he would want a sentence finished for and he does not know where that man is, and there is a number in a different pair of hands that nobody has put to anybody, and there is a woman at the lock end of a cold passage with a figure in her head on no paper.

**None of the three of them is going to be put to him this week, and none of them was, and the number in his head was not confirmed and was not denied, and a man stood in a doorway and said a true thing out loud about a piece of paper and the two of them did not find out whether it was true.**

"You are not going to ask me what is in the inside pocket of this coat."

"Nothing has been in my coat since a week ago, and if I were to ask you about the inside pocket of that one you would tell me there is nothing in it, and neither of us would find out one thing by it, and I am not going to ask you."

Both coats hung the way coats hang when their wearers stand their ground. The cloth pulled at the shoulders and settled again, and neither man looked down.

---

Then the man said the thing he had come about, and he said it to the paper on the table and not to the man in the room, and it was the third true thing anybody has said out loud in that room and none of the three was asked for.

"**You are the only person anybody could hand a thing to and not be able to lay it against him afterwards, and I arrived at that in about four minutes after about two years of not arriving at it, and there is a thing on that table now and it did not come from me and it was not brought for me and I am not going to be the reason a piece of paper is standing on a table.**"

Then he stopped, and he had said more than he had come to say, and neither of them found out what to do about that.

It stood where he had set it, fold out and face blank, and the room went quiet around it.

---

He went down the stair at about the sixth hour and Marek Kest did not thank him and did not see him out and did not go to the stair himself, and nobody was sent for and nobody was forgiven and nothing was resolved.

**There is a folded leaf in that coat and it has been in the pocket for about two years, and a hand that was not his mother's struck a line through what stood at the foot of it. What that line would have carried out is the single thing he has wanted since before the fire. What would have gone out on it went with the line, and for two years he has had a piece of paper in his coat and not the claim.**

A woman told him, in the ordinary voice, in a month about nine months ago, that the gap at the foot of a sheet is the exact width of a name, and he bought nothing at that counter in the week of it, or the week after.

**He has not filled it in and he is not going to, and the name is not in that room and the hand that struck the line is not in that room either, and there is no form anywhere in this empire in which a man with nothing behind him can enter a claim that other people dispute, and there is not going to be one.**

He did not reach a stage. He did not bring into use the one thing he is able to bring into use, and there was nothing put in front of him to turn down, and there is no office and no fund and no list and no leave and no wage. The piece of paper is standing against the base of the wall where he can see it and it is not on a form and it is not in a book and it is not a record, and nobody in this empire sent it, and nobody is going to be thanked.
