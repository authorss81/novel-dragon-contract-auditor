# Chapter 0429: Four Headings Over Four Columns And A Sheet With None, A Thing Said Without Announcing It, And A Fifth Column Not Ruled

The counter is at the bottom of the stair and the shelf is behind it, and that is where the book lives with its spine flush to the front edge of the shelf, and this is the morning of the week it is out on the boards and open at this week's page. Four columns and a heading over each of them, and she could say all four in her sleep. **There has never been a fifth one and she has never once ruled a line for it, and it is the one thing in that house she has wanted for about nine years and not done.**

She is about thirty-five and the book is hers and the hand on it is hers, and about four rooms go by the week in that house, and this is a good week on the shelf. The girl in the back room is in her second season and she is not in the front of that house this morning.

**She is the woman next door, and she has been next door to that hall for eleven years, and she is not the woman who stands at the foot of those four steps and never has been, and there are two women in that lane and about four feet of wet between them at this hour, and not one word has ever gone from one of them to the other about the man who lives in between them.**

It is the fourth day of the third week of the ninth month of the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is the fourth hour.

---

He came in off the lane and he was not wet and he had nothing in his hand, and he had been in before, and she knew the shape of his week the way she knows the shape of her own shelves.

"You have not brought anything."

"I have not brought anything and I am not going to be thanked for standing here, and I am not going to say that I have come for anything, and I am not going to announce what I am going to say." He put one hand on the edge of the counter, about a foot from the book. "**I am going to say it in the middle of your week and you are going to carry on and that is the arrangement, because if I stand here and make a thing of it then you will have to make a thing of answering it and neither of us has a form for that.**"

"All right."

---

"**A book with four headings over it takes a thing in and gives it a column.**" He said it looking at the shelf and not at her. "**A sheet with nothing printed on it takes a thing in and gives it nothing back. There is no column and there is no place it can be put, and that is the whole of the difference and it is the only paper in this empire that works that way, and you are the only person on this street who would know in about four seconds whether that is any use to anybody.**"

She wrote the third of that week's names into the fourth column and did not stop and did not look up.

"**And those four have been taking whatever is put under them for about nine years and they have never once objected, and the reason they have never objected is not that they agree with you. It is that a thing under a heading has no way of saying it was never asked. That is what your four are. That is what they have been for nine years and they are good at it and I have never once seen one of them do anything else.**"

---

"You have come up this lane to tell me what my own book is."

"I have come up this lane because you are the only person I can say it to who is not going to put it in a column, and I do not know that, and that is not an answer, and you are not going to get one out of me this morning and you were never going to." He took his hand off the counter. "**I am not asking you for anything. I have not asked you for anything the two times I have stood here and I am not going to start, and I have not told you what I do four days a season and I am not going to, and that is not a confidence. It is that a thing told once a season is a thing somebody could be shown.**"

---

She finished the line and squared the book against the edge of the counter with the flat of her hand, and did not turn it round, and the spine was still flush to the front edge where it has been every week for nine years.

"**I have wanted a fifth column for nine years and you have just told me what it would be, and I am not going to thank you for it and you were not asking me to and I would rather neither of us did.**"

"That is fair."

"It is not fair and it is also true, and the two of those have been the arrangement on that counter for about two visits now." She put her hand flat on the book. "**I know what a column with a heading over it is. I keep four of them. I have wanted the fifth one because there are weeks when a person comes in and what was paid is not what was owed, and there is nowhere on that page to put the difference, and I have wanted to put it there for nine years.**"

---

"You have told me what you would use it for and you have not asked me what I think of it and I am not going to say."

"I have not asked you what you think of it and I am not going to ask you, and I am going to say one thing and then I am going to go, and I have thought about it for about a fortnight and it is not getting shorter." He looked at the four headings. "**A difference in a column is a difference that has been recorded as a difference. It is not a row. It is not a row because there is nobody standing at the bottom of it, and a column with a difference in it is a machine with a difference in it, and it cannot go anywhere, and it will not even be a row to anybody who comes in here in about two years and has not been here before.**"

---

"That is the answer to a thing I did not ask you and I am not going to take it as one."

"I did not give it to you as one. I gave it to you about a column, because you have four and I have none and that is the whole of the difference between us and it took me about two years to get round to." He went towards the door. "**I have a name and I am not going to use it here and I did not use it the first time, and I have not filled anything in in my life and I have told nobody where I have been, and I am not going to be thanked for standing at that counter and I am not going to be asked for anything and I am going up that lane now.**"

He did not knock on anything. She did not knock on anything. The two of them have not started a third of anything.

---

**The book was not read and was not turned round and went back to the shelf where it lives, and nothing was added to it, and the thing that man is carrying about in his head is not written down anywhere by him, and she neither put it down nor rubbed it out, and it is still not down.** He told her nothing whatever about his four days a season, and she did not ask him, and there is no form in that lane in which she could have. The girl in the back room came out about half an hour later and stood at the other end of the counter with her arms folded and neither of them said a word about it, and the week went on the fourth column in her own hand with the difference not in it, and she has wanted it there for nine years and she is going to go on wanting it there and she is not going to rule it.
