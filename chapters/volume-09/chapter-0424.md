# Chapter 0424: A Door That Went Out Of This Room A Minute Ago, A Fact Given As A Fact, And Twenty Minutes That Are Not A Decision

There is no door at the foot of that stair, so the first sound of a working day in that building is one long grey noise with a person walking up the middle of it. **The case is at the near end of the boards and the box is at the far end, and the wall board behind the counter has four items on it and not one of the four is time, and the drawer under that board has been shut for eleven years.** Two women are on that floor and the second of the two days is not this week and the man who comes in on it is not in the building.

It is the second day of the second week of the eighth month of the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is the fourth hour.

---

The door at the bottom of that stair had gone about a minute and a half before Marn Ottery said anything, and it had not gone loudly, and the stair has no door at the foot of it anyway, so what the room heard was a man going down the middle of it the way men do.

**Tamsin Rook had watched him do it. She was not going to describe it to anybody, and not because anybody had told her not to, and she had been in that room two years and had not been asked one thing by anybody in it.**

She had handed him a piece of paper with nothing printed on it and fourpence had gone into the ordinary place and the paper had gone into a coat, and it had taken about a minute and a half, and she was not going to say one word about it in that room or out of it.

---

"You saw him."

Marn Ottery had not turned round from the boards.

"I hand about four hundred of them a year off that case."

"That is not what I said and you have known that for about two years and I have watched you not say so every working day since."

---

"**The man who just went out of that door is not doing anything anybody asked for.**" Marn Ottery put both hands flat on the boards, which she has done four times in eleven years and three of which were followed by a sentence, and this one was going to be the fourth. "**I said the other half of that in this room in the fourth week of the sixth month and I said it once and I am not going to say it again, and this is not me saying it again. This is me saying the part of it that I have had to myself since and have not put to anybody.**"

"I am not going to take it."

"I know you are not going to take it and I would like it noticed that I have not handed it over for taking."

---

"**Here is the part that is mine.** There is no form in this empire in which a person can be told what a thing is for, and there is not going to be one, and the reason there is not going to be one is not that it is forbidden and never has been forbidden, it is that nobody has ever needed to print one and nobody ever is going to. **And that is not a wall. That is a room with the door open in it. He walked out of it this morning with the only paper in this empire that cannot be laid against him, and I could not have stopped him, and there is nothing in this room that would let me stand there and say that I tried.**"

The clerk of about twenty-nine was at the far end of two joined tables and did not look up.

"I am not going to thank you for that," Tamsin Rook said.

"You are not going to and I am not going to, and I would rather neither of us did than have one of us do it in front of the other."

---

"**And I am going to say the other thing now and it is about you and not about him, and I have thought about it for six days and I have had it in a mouth for about four minutes and half.**" Marn Ottery turned round. "**A person who comes in early for a reason has just watched the reason arrive, and it did not come for her.** You have not been early on the hour since the fourth week of the sixth month. You have been in at twenty minutes to the fifth every working day for about six weeks and you are in at twenty minutes to the fifth this morning, and I have watched you do it, and I have not asked you about it, and I am not going to."

"You have not asked me about anything this morning."

"I have not and I am not going to and I want that said in this room before either of us starts."

---

"I have not come in early for a reason."

"You have not come in early for a reason and you are here twenty minutes early, and the two of those are allowed to sit next to each other in a room and neither one of them has to be true of the other." She took her hands off the boards. "**You are not standing ready for anything. You were told that in this room in the fourth week of the sixth month and you are not standing ready for it now either, and what you are standing ready for is a man with a coat on going down a stair, and that is not the same trade and you know it is not.**"

"I know it is not the same trade."

"Say nothing else about it."

"I am not going to say anything else about it."

---

Nobody did. **The twenty minutes were twenty minutes and they were not a decision, and they were not on anything, and there is no form anywhere in this empire for a person to be recorded as the one who sat at that counter with a thing in her head for the length of a fifth of an hour before the room filled.** Her undertaking is live and unretired and unexercised and its date is not a thing she has said out loud in that room or out of it, and it is not a thing any record of that room carries, and it is still there this morning and it was still there at the fourth hour and it will be there at the sixth.

The clerk of about twenty-nine at the far end of the two joined tables heard all of it and wrote none of it down and has not said a second thing in that room in about four years and did not begin this morning.

---

**The board on the wall came off at the sixth hour and went back on with the four items in their places, and the box under the far end of those boards did not open and did not move, and the drawer did not open. No blank was filled in anywhere on that floor that day. **One had been filled in on that floor a week before, and the woman who handed it over has told nobody and is not going to and is not going to be asked, and the whole of what she has done with it is to be here twenty minutes early on a morning when there is nothing to be early for.****
