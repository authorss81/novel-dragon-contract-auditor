# Chapter 0420: The Fourth Of Four Items On A Board, A Box Under The Boards Eleven Years, And A Reason Given At The Sixth Hour

The board on the wall behind that counter has carried four items for eleven years and not one of the four is time. The fourth of the four is the one none of the three of them can account for; it is in Marn Ottery's own hand and it is about four years old and she put it there herself. The drawer under the board is shut and has been shut for eleven years and there are four things inside it and two of them have never been opened. Under the far end of those boards there is a box of about four hundred bought and unfilled blanks and nothing has ever been left in it.

**She is thirty-four and those boards have been hers for eleven years and she is not a lawyer, and she is the only person on that floor who can say what a piece of paper with nothing printed on it is for, and she said it out loud once, in the first week of the second month, and nobody thanked her, and she said in terms that she was not going to be.**

It is the second day of the second week of the seventh month of the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is the third hour, and the counter shuts at the sixth hour.

---

The clerk of about twenty-nine had been on that floor since the fourth hour and had her pen down and a sheet squared in front of her, and she is not one of the two people who have said a number out loud in that room and she has not said one since the first week of the second month.

"There is no form in this empire in which a person may be told what a thing on a board is going to be used for," she said. "You have said that sentence out loud in this room twice and it is true both times and it does not help you at all, and I have watched you use it twice as though it did."

"It has never helped me."

"**No. And here is the half of it that makes it not help, and I am not asking for anything for saying it, and neither of us is going to be thanked for it by anybody in this building.**" She did not look up. "The reason nobody can be told is not that they will not be. It is that there is no form in which to tell them. A person can only be shown a thing by somebody being made to produce it, and producing it is an office, and an office has a name at the end of it, and there is nobody on this floor whose job it is."

---

"That is not the reason."

"That is the reason. What is underneath it is worse and I am not going to get to it before the sixth hour." She put her pen down. "**A thing nobody can be shown is a thing that cannot be asked about. And a thing that cannot be asked about is the only kind of paper this empire has that nobody can be held to. That is the fourth item on that board. That is the drawer under it. That is the box at the far end.**"

"That box has been under those boards for eleven years."

"That box has been under those boards for eleven years and the fourth of those four items has been on that board about four, and you have just said the two figures out loud in the wrong order to make them sound like one thing, and I have sat at the other end of these tables for every day of both of them." She squared the sheet. "**They are the same shape. That is all I am saying. Not the same age. The same shape.**"

---

Marn Ottery put both hands flat on the boards, which she has done four times in eleven years and three of which were followed by a sentence, and she did not say one.

"I am not treating that as an answer and I am not going to thank you for it," she said. "**You have told me a reason. A reason is a thing that stops the question. I have not asked a question, and I am not going to, and you have just handed me a reason for not asking one, and I do not use it, and I would like it noticed that I did not use it while it was being offered.**"

"I did not offer it to you to use."

"You have offered it to the room and not to me, and that is a different thing, and I am not going to be the one who turns it into a favour by asking what it was for." She did not look at the drawer. "That is the end of it and I am saying that it is the end of it."

"I do not know," said the clerk of about twenty-nine, "and that is the first thing I have said in this room in about four years that I have not known the end of, and I am not going to say a second thing about it, and you will not get a third thing out of me this week."

---

A woman of about thirty came up at about the fourth hour and put fourpence on the boards and was handed a piece of paper with nothing printed on it out of the case at the near end, and she put it inside her coat and went down the four flights, and nobody asked her what it was for.

Then a man of about twenty-six did the same thing, and neither of them said anything at all.

And then, at about the fifth hour, a man of thirty-eight came up those four flights and put nothing on the boards at all.

**He has a name, Marek Kest, and he is not a lawyer, and there is no office behind him of any kind and no money, no list, no leave and no certification, and none of those five is on its way.** He is on that floor for the third time in his life. He carries a page in the inside of that coat, and a hand that is not his mother's has been through it, and the only thing he has wanted out of any room in eight volumes is what that page used to say at the end of that line.

He has not asked anybody for anything. Nobody has asked him anything.

---

"You have not bought a copy," Marn Ottery said.

"I have not bought a copy."

"**That is the third time you have been up those four flights and you have never once put fourpence on that counter, and I have watched three people buy a piece of paper off it this morning who could not tell you what it was for either.**"

"That is the reason I am not going to."

He said it to the boards and not to her, and she looked at the top of his head for a moment, and there is a case at the near end of those boards with about four hundred of them in it and every one of them costs fourpence and none of them has anything printed on it and the space at the foot of a sheet is the exact width of a name.

---

"**You are not going to buy one because buying one is asking for something, and there is no form in this empire in which a man at that counter could be handed one, and I have not got one to give you and neither has anybody on this floor.**" She took her hands off the boards. "And I am not going to be thanked by you for that, and you are not going to thank me, and I would like it entered that neither of us is going to."

Nobody entered it.

---

He stayed until the counter shut and did not buy anything, and the case at the near end went behind the boards and the boards went up and the board on the wall came off at the sixth hour and went back on the wall with the four items in their places, the fourth of them the one none of the three of them can account for.

**Nothing was put to anybody in that building this week, which is what this matter has been doing for as long as anybody in it can remember, and the count of things anybody has asked out loud in this matter is the same as it was on the second day of the first week of the fifth month.** A man went down four flights with nothing in his hands, and the case they come out of is still not the box, and the box did not move, and the drawer did not open, and a reason was given at the sixth hour by a woman who has not given another one, and it has not been taken up.
