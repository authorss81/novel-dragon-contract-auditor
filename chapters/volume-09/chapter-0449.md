# Chapter 0449: A Woman Who Has Worked Out What She Is Standing Ready For, A Box Four Miles Off In A Room, And The Part She Is Not Going To Say

She was in at twenty minutes to the fifth and the twenty minutes are the twenty minutes, and they are not a decision and they have not been a decision for two years, and there is no form anywhere in this empire in which the woman who sits at that counter alone for a fifth of an hour is entered as the one who sat there.

**About six foot of bare deal at the far end of those boards, and dust on it in the shape of the bottom of a box, and the box is not under there and has not been under there for about two months.**

She is twenty-four. Her undertaking is live, unretired and unused, and in two years it has not been brought into use on a single day of them, and it is not going to be brought into use in this one. The day and the month of it are not said out loud here.

It is the fourth day of the third week of the second month of the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is the fourth hour.

---

"I worked it out about a week ago and I have not said one word to anybody and I am not going to this week, and I want it noticed that I noticed."

"I have noticed that you have not said one word for about a week, and I have noticed that every week for about two months, and I have noticed it for about two years, and I am not going to be thanked for noticing and I am not going to be asked what you worked out."

"You are not going to be asked what I worked out."

"No. And I am going to say one thing and then I am going to put my hands back on this boards and that will be the end of it. **You are not the one who is going to carry that box anywhere and neither am I, and I have known that since the week that box came out from under those boards, and I have not been going to be the one to say it out loud, and you are not going to be the one to say it out loud either.**"

---

"**Then somebody has to say it out loud.**"

"No. And you have just said the sentence that everybody in this room has been not saying for about two years, and you have said it as a fact and not as a want, and that is the one thing that could have been done about it and I have watched you do it in about nine seconds."

---

"You do not want to be the one who has nothing behind her."

"I do not want to be anything. I have wanted to be nothing at that counter for two years and I have been nothing at that counter for two years, and it is the only thing I have ever had that nobody could be shown."

"You are not going to say the reason."

"**I am not going to say the reason in this room and I am not going to say it out of it, and I have worked out the reason and I have had it for about a week, and it is not a small thing to work out and I am not going to be thanked for working it out and I am not going to be asked for it either.**"

---

Marn Ottery turned about a foot and looked at the far end of those boards, at the six foot of bare deal and the dust on it, and then she looked back at the near end.

"**You are not going to tell me what you worked out.**"

"I am not going to tell you what I worked out and you are not going to look at me for about nine seconds and then ask me again in a different way, and if you do that then I will say something, and what I will say will not be the thing I worked out."

She did not do it.

---

The clerk of about twenty-nine at the far end of the two joined tables had the book open in front of her and her pen down, and in about four years in that room she has put one sentence into the air, and she put nothing into it this week. She wrote none of it down, and the not-writing is going on at about the rate it has always gone on, and nowhere in this empire is there a way of entering the person who said nothing as the person who said nothing.

There were two people in that room, and that room keeps its own count of who was standing in it on a working afternoon, and the count is nobody. At the sixth hour the boards came down, the case went in behind them, the wall board came off the wall and went back on it with the four items on it and not one of the four being time, and the drawer underneath it did not open, and the six foot of bare deal at the far end of the boards got swept by the woman who sweeps that floor and who was not asked to sweep it and was not told to.

---

**And the whole of what she worked out, and it is not a small thing and it has not been said out loud to anybody in this empire and it is not said out loud here: a person with something behind her is not available. The practice that has been running for two years in a stair with no door at the foot of it has never once gone looking for a person with something behind her. The reason it has not is that it does not have to, and the reason it does not have to is that there is one person in this matter it can always have instead. That person is at the top of four flights and has nothing at all behind him.**

She did not say it. She has not said it to Marn Ottery and she is not going to, and she is not going to say it to the man who comes in on the second of the two days, and there is nobody else in this city she would say it to.

---

Nobody thanked anybody. Nobody was forgiven. Nobody was sent for and nothing was resolved. The count of things anybody has asked out loud in this matter is the same as it was on the morning this month began, and the box is four miles off in a room that nobody has opened and that does not need opening. A woman of twenty-four was at the near end of those boards at the fourth hour and she said nothing that is going to cost her, and she was not thanked for the nothing and there is no form in which she could be.
