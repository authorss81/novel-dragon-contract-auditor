# Chapter 0443: Four Miles With A Box On Its Side, A Courier From The Yard Who Said One Thing And Went On, And A Room Nobody Named

He is about twenty-six and he has carried sheets up four flights for about two years and he is paid for a corner and not for a page, and this week he was paid for a corner and carried a box about four miles, and there is no form in this empire in which those two facts are both allowed to stand on the same line.

**About four hundred blanks in it, and the lid has not been off it in six years, and it went under the end of those boards eleven years ago and came out of them in nine minutes in the last month of a year, and he is the only person alive who has had both of those in the same hand.**

It is the second day of the first week of the first month of the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is the fourth hour.

---

It went down the four flights on its side with his arms under it, and the stair is nine foot wide and there is no other way up it, and at the second landing he had to turn it on its edge to get his foot past it and he did that without stopping, and there is nobody in that building who knows he did it.

The case is at the top of those stairs and the box is not the case, and nobody on that floor has ever been told where the case is filled, and it was said out loud in that room about nine years ago and it is still true, and nothing that happened to a box under the end of those boards has moved a single sheet into that case or out of it.

**He knows what is in the box. Everybody who has ever worked that floor knows what is in the box. What he does not know and is never going to be told is why it came out of that room in nine minutes in the last month of a year, and he is the wrong shape of person to be told, and that is not a complaint.**

The corners pressed through the sleeves of his coat, and he went on without setting it down, and he did not need to look at it to know which way up it was.

---

The road out of that yard is about a mile before it is a road anybody uses and then it is four miles to a street that runs down to the river road, and he went the whole way on the same side, and the box was on its side the whole way with his arms under it, and it was heavier at four miles than it was at the first landing and he has thought about that a good deal since.

The man from the yard came past him on the far side about halfway, on his own road, with the satchel on his shoulder.

He has been a courier since the first chapter of this, he cannot be told anything, and he has never asked anybody anything in that building in as long as anybody there can remember, and this is the first time in about two years that a person from outside it has been on a road with him while he was carrying something.

"That is heavy for one."

"It is."

"You are not going to get anybody to sign for it."

"There is no form anywhere in this empire in which a man is entered as the one who signed for a box, and there is not going to be one, and I am not going to stand on a road and explain that to a man I have met once."

The far side had emptied behind him by then, and neither of them looked back once the other was behind.

---

"I am not going to say one word about it."

"You are not going to say one word about it and I am not going to thank you for that, and the reason I am not going to thank you is that thanking is a thing two people have to agree about and you have not agreed to anything and neither have I."

"That is the whole of it."

"That is the whole of it, and I have been getting to it for years, and I have been getting to it properly since the week that box came out from under those boards."

**And that is the price, and it is entered in a man and not in a figure. The one person in this matter who goes to places and cannot be told anything has been on a road with a box for about a quarter of an hour, and a courier is findable in a way that nothing else on that road is findable, and there is no form anywhere for asking a man to forget what he saw, and the man who carried it could not have prevented it and would not have been thanked for preventing it.**

The road was thin and even under his boots the whole way, and the box made no sound of its own with the lid shut, and he matched his pace to the long stretch ahead.

---

The room was on the second floor and there was a woman at the door of the building who gave him a key and did not say one word about the box and did not look at it, and he has thought since that she did not look at it on purpose, and he has thought since that a person who does not look at a thing on purpose has been put in a building where things are brought and left.

He put it on the floor against the wall that had nothing on it, on its side, the way it had been on its side for eleven years.

**The lid did not come off. The lid has not been off it in six years and it was not off it in that room and it does not need to be, because nothing in that room is waiting on what is in it and nothing in this empire has ever been left in it.**

He put the key on the table, which is the only thing on the table in that room and has been for as long as that room has been let by the week, and he went back down two flights and out and about four miles, and nobody saw him go by and nobody saw him arrive and he was not thanked at either end of it.

Stairs took him down without pause. Boots found each tread cleanly in the dimness. Air below hung cool and still.

Yard lay quiet around him as he crossed it. No voice followed from above. Road opened ahead, empty and clear, and he took it.

---

The piece of paper with nothing printed on it that he put fourpence into the case for is still on him and he has not taken it out and he is not going to, and he is not going to fill it in, and nobody in that building has been told any of that, and nobody in it could be shown it either.

**Nobody at either end of that road thanked him and nobody has been forgiven and nobody was sent for, and nothing was settled anywhere on that road, and a box that has been under a counter for eleven years is four miles off in a room with about four hundred blanks in it and nothing on the top of it, and nobody in this empire has been told that the two of those are the same box.**
