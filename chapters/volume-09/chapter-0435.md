# Chapter 0435: A Form Put Down By A Man With A Satchel, A Clerk Who Did Not Say The Thing Again, And A Fourth Item None Of The Three Can Account For

She is at the far end of two joined tables and she is about twenty-nine and she has been on that floor two years, and she said once that she would not say a second thing in that room and she has not said one, and she is not going to begin this week.

The boards are about nine foot of deal and the case stands at the near end of them and the box is at the far end, and the wall board behind the counter has carried four items for eleven years and not one of the four is time, and the drawer under that board is shut.

It is the second day of the first week of the eleventh month of the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is the fourth hour.

---

The man from the yard came up the stair at about the fourth hour, took the strap off his shoulder, and put a thing on the boards the way he puts one there most days, which is flat and at the near end and not squared.

She did not look up. **It was a form, and there was a heading printed over the top of it and one ruled space under that, and the space had nothing in it at all.** It had been folded once, along a crease that is not the middle of it, which is what a piece of paper does in a coat and not in a file.

"It came back."

He said it the way he says the thing about the cold. He has said it to a counter that gave him nothing to say it to and nobody has ever asked him where it came back from.

---

**And the clerk of about twenty-nine did not say the thing she said in that room three weeks ago about a sheet with no column, and she had it ready, and she had had it ready since about the fourth hour, and she did not say it.**

Marn Ottery at the near end of those boards turned about a foot and looked at the form and then looked at the wall behind her own shoulder.

"That came back."

"It came back and it is not the case and it is not the box, and there is nothing in the space under the heading, and I have not opened the drawer and I am not going to open the drawer, and I want that said out loud in this room before anybody in this room puts a hand on it."

---

The man from the yard did not take it off the boards and did not say anything about what it was for.

"You are not going to tell me what that is."

"I have never told you what is in the satchel and you have never asked and there is nowhere on this floor to tell you." She put both hands flat on the deal. "**And I am not going to tell you what came back in it either, and the reason is that I do not know, and the second reason is that a man who says out loud on this floor that he does not know is a man who has said a thing in a room, and there is no form anywhere for a woman to be entered as the one who did not know.**"

---

Tamsin Rook was at the near end with her back to the case and both hands flat on the deal in front of her where they have no work to do, and she said one thing, and she said it to the boards and not to anybody.

"It has not got a column either."

Nobody thanked her for it. **Marn Ottery did not take it, did not answer it, and did not look round, and the clerk of about twenty-nine at the far end of the two joined tables wrote none of it down, and that is the second of those in about seven weeks, and this empire has no form in which the one who stayed quiet can be entered as the one who stayed quiet.**

"You are all going to be very quiet now," Marn Ottery said. "The two of you. There is a man standing at a counter and he is not one of us and he is not going to be in a room with a thing said in it, and there is not one blank in this building that could keep him out of it."

---

The man from the yard took the satchel back onto his shoulder and went down the stair and did not take the form with him, and nobody stopped him, and nobody asked him one thing, and the form stayed where it had been put down.

**She could have put it in the box.** About four hundred blanks are under the far end of those boards and about four hundred of them go out of the case at the near end every year, and one more piece of paper in that box would have cost nothing and would have taken about four seconds. She did not do it and the reason is not tidiness.

"A box is a supply and a blank is a supply and that is what a supply is for. **A thing that has been filled in and brought back is not a supply and a person who puts one in a box has done a thing on purpose in a room with two other people in it, and everything either of us has got in this building is that nobody can be shown doing anything on purpose.**"

---

The fourth item on the wall board behind her is in her own hand and it is about four years old and she put it there herself, and there are three of them on that floor who have looked at it and there is nobody in this empire who can account for it.

"That is still the one none of us can do anything with."

"It is the one none of us can do anything with and it has been there four years and it is not going to be a different thing this morning because there is a form on my boards that came back with nothing in it."

---

The form was still on those boards at the sixth hour.

**It went down the four flights at the end of that day with nobody's name on it, and the boards went up, and the case went behind them, and the box under the far end did not open and did not move, and the drawer under the wall board did not open, and it has been shut since before she came and there are four things in it and two of them have never been looked at.** Nothing was ruled and no heading was printed over anything and no space under a printed heading was filled in that day by anybody, and nobody was thanked and nobody was asked anything and nothing in that building was resolved, and the fourth of the four items on that wall was still the one that none of the three of them could account for when the lights went off.
