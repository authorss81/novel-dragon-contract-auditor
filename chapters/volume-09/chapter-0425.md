# Chapter 0425: Nine Words Twice, A Man Who Will Not Hear It Twice In A Week, And A Nail In Four Feet Of Bare Wall

The room is taken by the week and it is the same room it was a week ago, with the table in it and the stool and the bed against one wall and a chair standing against none, and about four feet of bare wall beside the window that looks at the back of another house. **There is a nail in that wall about the height of his hand and it is a common nail and it is driven in a little crooked, and nothing has ever hung on it and nothing is going to this week.** There is no fire in that room and the cold comes off the plaster behind the middle chair.

He is thirty-eight and his name is Marek Kest, and he is not at a stage and is not going to arrive at one. He holds nothing this evening: no office, no fund, no list, no leave, no certification, and none of those five is on its way. Nobody asked him anything today and nobody is going to.

It is the fourth day of the third week of the eighth month of the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is the fifth hour.

---

There is a leaf in the inside pocket of that coat and it has been there about two years. Somebody struck a name out of it in a hand that is not his mother's, and what belonged at the end of that line is the one thing he has wanted since before the fire, and he has the claim that would go with it no better than he has the name, and he has not opened it out on that table again since a week that is gone.

It has been folded and unfolded and folded again until the fold in it has gone soft, and it has been in every room he has stood in since and it has not been out of the pocket once, and a piece of paper kept in a pocket is not a record of anything and cannot be put in front of anybody and is not lost either, and the pocket is the whole of it.

A room taken by the week comes back the same every week and nobody sweeps it for him and nobody turns the bed, and there is a ring on the table that was in it in the first week, and about two years of one man's coat over the back of the chair is what a room like that has got in it, and the wall beside the window has had nothing on it for the whole of it.

The head of it is wider than the shank by about as much again, and the crook in it is to the left, and it has been to the left since the day it went in, and a man can get a thumb under the head of it and take the thumb out again, and he has done that as often as anybody does a thing he has no reason to do.

He put his hand flat on the wall beside the window, over the four feet of it, and there was nothing under his hand but plaster and the one nail.

---

Then a voice in the street below, and it was not raised, and it was a voice he had heard on a shipping floor and on four flights of a stair.

"**No column means no place to put a person.**"

**That was the whole of it. Nine words, said from the pavement to a shut door, and it was true, and it had been true for about a fortnight, and Marek Kest had not said it and had not given it to anybody and had been living behind a door for about two years with the want that those nine words are about.**

He went to the door and did not open it and did not go down.

"I heard you."

"I know you did. I said it to the door and the door is not anybody."

---

"**You may say it once this week and you may not say it again this week.**" He said it to the door and not to the street, and he heard how that sounded, and he did not take it back and he did not soften it. "You may say it on any other day. I am not going to tell you which days. **I have heard the same true thing from two people this month and I am not going to be the place it is said a third time, and that is not a rule and it is not a form and it is me.**"

The street is not much of a street, and a person standing on a step in it can be heard by every window on both sides of it and by anybody inside a shut door, and nothing gets back out through one, and the distance between a shut door and a step is not much, and nine words is a short thing to say across it.

"That is a small thing to say to a man in a street."

"It is the largest thing I have to give anybody and I have given it to a door."

---

The voice in the street did not go away and did not come up. Then it turned round, and Marek Kest heard it move across about nine foot of pavement and stop where the step was.

"**It cannot be filed and it cannot be put on me.**"

That was the second one and it was also nine words and it was also true, and it was said to a woman who was on the step with nothing in her hands, and she had not been asked for anything by anybody and she was not going to be asked for anything now.

---

"That is the second one I have heard this week and I am not going to hold it." She did not go up. "I have got a thing of my own in that house that nobody gave me and that nobody can be shown, and I have carried it about nine years, and I am not going to be handed a second one off a stranger in a street at the fifth hour, and I would rather have neither than one more."

"I am not handing you anything."

"You are standing on my step saying nine words and waiting for me to be the one who takes them, and I have watched men do that off that step for about nine years, and I am not doing it now, and I have not said one word in this house in nine years that I did not have to say, and I am not starting with a man I do not know."

---

**Nobody in that street was thanked for anything, and nothing was asked of anybody, and the woman at the foot of those stairs did not go up them and did not go up them this week and is not going to.** He shut the door and stood in the room with his back against it for longer than a person stands against a door.

Then he sat down on the bed and took the leaf out of the inside pocket and put it on his knees, and he did not open it, and he has not opened it out on that table since a week that is gone and he was not going to open it tonight either.

**What belongs at the end of that line is the only thing he has wanted since before the fire, and what is on the other side of that line is the claim that would go with it, and he has one of those two and has never had the other, and the name is not in any mouth in this volume and the hand that struck it is not in any mouth in this volume either.** And there is no form anywhere in this empire in which a man with no office behind him could put a disputed claim, and there is not going to be one, and he has known that for about two years and has not gone looking for a way round it, because there is no way round it and looking would be the beginning of being a person who can be found.

---

**And what a street did to him at the fifth hour, and it is not a thing anybody did to him.** A young man he has never met carried a thing out of a counter a fortnight ago and put it in his coat, and has carried it since, and has not been asked about it and has not been thanked. This week he came up a street and put the finding of it in nine words against a door, and then put nine more words of it at a woman on a step. Both of them were true, and he was stopped twice, and **neither stop was unkind and neither stop took the thing back out of the two people it had already been said to.**

**He is the cheapest party in this matter and he is not a carrier and nothing in that street made him one, and the bill for being unfindable is not his and has never been his, and a woman told him that in this room about a month ago and he gave her nothing back then and he is not going to give anything back now.** What is new is not the bill. What is new is that the nine words went past his own door on their way to a piece of paper, and they were right, and there is not one form in this empire in which he could put a want where a thing with no column could be put, and there never is going to be one, and he has now heard his own want described in the street by a stranger who was talking about paper.

He folded the leaf back into the pocket and buttoned the pocket and did not write anything down, and there is no pen in that room and no paper on that table.

---

He did not reach a stage and he did not exercise the thing he is able to do and he did not take an office or a fund or a list or a leave or a wage from anybody, and nobody sent for him and nobody was sent for, and the name at the end of that struck line is not in any mouth in this volume and the hand that struck it is not either.

The four feet of wall was bare at the end of that evening and it had been bare at the start of it. The nail was in it and it was crooked and nothing has ever hung on it and nothing is going to hang on it this week. **He holds nothing at the end of it that he did not hold at the start of it, and a man came up a street he does not live in and said nine words about a man he has never met, twice, and was stopped twice, and neither stopping was unkind, and he was not thanked for either of them, and the street knows something about him that he did not put there.**
