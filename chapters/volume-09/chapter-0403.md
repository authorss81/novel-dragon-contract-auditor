# Chapter 0403: The Same True Thing Twice In Front Of Two People, A Man Who Has Loaded Here Nineteen Years, And A Box Three Inches Forward

The shipping floor is at the back of a yard two streets off, and there is a long bench on it, and the loading is done off the bench by about nine men and it has been done off that bench for nineteen years. Everything that goes up the four flights of the building across the end of that yard goes up on a man's arm, and the man who goes up with it is not the man who made it and is not the man it is about.

He is on that floor because he packs. He has packed for two years. Nobody has ever told him what he packs and he has never wanted to know, and in two years he has carried up those stairs about four sheets a week and has read the top of about six of them by accident.

It is the fourth day of the third week of the second month of the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is the fifth hour, and the sheet at the top of his arm is the one he is going to leave at the counter.

---

The man who loads on that floor came past him at the bench with a bundle under each arm and stopped, which he does about once in a week, and put one of the bundles down on the boards.

"Nineteen years," the man said. "Nineteen years I have come up this yard and gone up those stairs and come down them, and in nineteen years the only two men who have said a true thing to me on that floor are the two of you, and one of you said it about a box and one of you said it about her."

"You have not been here for the second one."

"I have not been here for the second one and I do not need to have been here for it." The man put the other bundle down as well. "**You have made the same statement twice in front of two people. That is all I am going to say about it and I am going to say it in the ordinary voice and I am not going to ask you one thing.**"

---

"I said a thing about a box to a woman on a counter and I said a thing about a woman to the same woman on the same counter, and the two of them are not the same true thing."

"They are the same true thing and I have thought about the two of them for four days and here is the shape of it." The man sat down on the end of the bench with his elbows on his knees, which is a thing a man of forty-four does when he has decided to spend a quarter of an hour. "The first one you said in front of nobody. You said it in front of her, which is one person, and a true thing said to one person is a thing between two people and it stays where the two of them are. **The second one you said out loud on a floor with nine men on it and you said it after you had already said it once, and that is not twice in front of two people, that is once in front of two people and a second time in front of ten.**"

"There were two of you on that floor."

"There were two of you on that floor and I am the one who has been here nineteen years and the other one left in a month, and I am telling you that I have been sitting on this bench for four days working out that you have attached a thing to me."

---

"You did not think about it when you said she was right."

"I did not think about it when I said she was right," he said, "and I have thought about it every working day since, and I want the two halves of that in the order they happened. It mattered at about the fourth hour on the day and it has mattered every day since, and it does not matter until the second of them asks me."

"Nobody is going to ask you."

"That is not mine to know and it is the whole of what I have." The man got up off the bench and picked his bundles up off the boards. "**I have been in this yard nineteen years and I have never once been served with anything, and I did not know that was a thing I had until four days ago, and a man who does not know he cannot be served does not lose anything by it.**"

---

He went up the four flights at about half past the fifth with the sheet under his arm and went up the way he has gone up about four times a week for two years, which is at the pace of a man who is not thinking about the stairs.

The counter is at the top of them and there is a board on the wall behind it and about a hundred feet of floor between the near end of the boards and the top of the stair. Under the far end of those boards there is a box and it is three inches further forward than it was when he last stood on that floor, and there is dust on the lid of it, and the lid has not been off it in six years.

He put the sheet on the near end of the boards where it goes. He did not look at the box. **He stood at the near end of those boards for about as long as it takes to fold a coat, and he did not touch the box and did not go near the end of the boards where the box is, and neither of the two women at that counter said one word to him about where he stood, and one of them has told him out loud that she was not going to be thanked and he is not going to be thanked either.**

The man of about twenty-nine was at the far end of two joined tables with her pen down and she did not look up. She had said once that she would not say a second thing in that room and has not, and she has not said a third thing to him either, and there is no form in which a person may be told what a thing on a counter is going to be used for.

---

He came down the four flights at the sixth hour and the stair has no door at the foot of it.

**A man from the yard was on the bottom step of them with a satchel on his shoulder and a strap across it, and he had been standing there long enough that the cold had got into the leather.** He could not have been told what was in that bag if anybody had wanted to tell him. He asked nothing at the bottom of that stair and nobody said anything to him, and the two of them went in opposite directions and neither of them knew the other's name.

The box under the far end of those boards is where it was. The statement on that floor has now been made twice, and it has two people attached to it, and the man who made it has not taken it back and did not say it a third time, and he has not got a room to be served in, and there is no form anywhere for taking a statement off a person who has already made two of them.
