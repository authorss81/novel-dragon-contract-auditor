# Chapter 0437: A Form On The Board By The Door, A Reason Given On The Way Out, And A Lamp Not Lit Until The Seventh Hour

That building is the same one it was the last time anybody wrote a word about it. The light comes off the lane and off a wall standing a few foot past the open side, and the bench at the front takes a man lying down if he goes on his side. The chain is across the front of it and the chain is on him.

It is the fourth day of the third week of the eleventh month of the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is the fourth hour, and the lamp is not lit.

---

**He is about fifty-five. He can read a paragraph and has always been able to read a paragraph, and there are four men on the far side of this town who cannot get the sense of one of them and he has never once been put in a room with those four and is not going to be.**

He said four sentences here in the second week of the fifth month and he gave the price of them at the same time, in the same breath, in the way a man says a thing he has decided to say. That was about six months ago. Neither of them has opened it since and neither of them is going to this week.

Two of them are at the back of that shed. They have been at the back of that shed for as long as he has been in it, and nobody has said one word to either of them this morning, and they are not named anywhere in this matter and they are not going to be named this week.

They have not been looked at since they were put back there, and they have not been covered and nothing has been stood in front of them, and the back of a shed is not a place anybody goes without a reason, and no reason has ever been given for going there. Nobody in the lane has asked what is at the back and nobody has asked how long it has been there, and the man at the front has not been asked either.

---

She came up the lane at about the fourth hour to open the shed and she is fifty-one and she is a foreman and she is in a box that was entered by a class, and nobody in that lane has ever asked her how she came to be in it, and the form that put her there is not asked to be void by anybody and nobody has asked.

"You have had six months of it."

"I have had six months of it."

"Six months is a long time to carry a thing that has not got any lighter, and I am not going to say that as a question, because it is not one and I have not asked you one thing in this shed in six months and I am not starting now."

"It has not got any lighter and I did not expect it to and I have not got anything for you this week and I am not going to have anything by the end of the month either."

The light off the lane came past the open side and reached about the middle of the floor and no further, and he worked in what the light reached and she worked where she worked, and neither of them went behind it. Nothing was carried out of the shed in the hour before she came and nothing was carried in afterwards, and the front bench was not made up at either end of the hour.

---

**And she did not ask him for the rest of the sentence and he did not give it to her and it has still not arrived.** Nine words of it are in another man's hand four hundred yards up that lane and have been for about two years, and she has not sent the second half anywhere, and he has not asked for it, and neither of them is going to.

"You are not going to ask me for it."

"I am not going to ask you for it and I have not asked you for it in two years and the not asking is the arrangement and it is the only thing either of us has got, and I would not be able to use it if it came this week."

---

The form on the board by the door is the one that would take the two at the back of that shed out of the back of it, and it will be on that board again on the second of the two days next week, and it has been on that board twice a week for as long as anybody in that lane can remember, and nobody in that lane has standing to ask it to be void and nobody has asked.

"**Nobody in that lane reads that board. I have watched the length of this lane for about four years and not one of us reads it, and I have watched you at it and you are the only one who looks.**"

"I have looked at it about nine times today and I have never once read it, and a man who is handed a form every day for about four years stops reading it inside a month, and that is not a complaint and it is not a finding and it is not going to change."

The board goes back on the wall on the same two days every week and it went back on it last week, and it will go back on it next week, and nothing about the going back on and off is a matter anybody in the lane takes an interest in. There is no arrangement by which a thing on a board is read because somebody else has read it before, and the form would be the same form whichever week it was put up.

---

Then she said it on the way out, at the open side, with her back half turned, and she said it to the lane and not to him, and nobody had asked her for it and nobody asked her for anything after.

"**You are not a carrier because a class can be re-entered by whoever fills in the next form.** Whoever comes in after me fills in that board and they are in my class, and a person who carries a piece of paper can be put back into the class it came out of by the next person who fills in the next form, and a person who can be put back is a party, and a party is a document. **That is the whole of it and I have had it since the first week and I have never once been able to spend it.**"

He said nothing to that and she did not wait and nobody thanked her for it.

She did not wait for anything and she went out to the lane, and whatever a lane does with a thing said over its shoulder is not a thing anybody in it has ever been shown doing. The shed was as it was an hour earlier and as it was an hour later, and the man on the bench stayed where he was, did not turn his head, and said nothing else in it.

---

The four hundred yards up that lane was not walked that week. It was not walked on her account and it was not walked on his, and nobody went up it in either direction. The nine words of it are in a book-keeper's own hand four hundred yards off and have been for about two years, and he is not coming down for them and she is not going up, and nothing in that lane joins those two facts together or is ever going to.

The lamp is on its bracket at the high end of that shed and it was not lit until about the seventh hour and it was not lit before that hour on any day of that week, and no fire has ever been lit in that shed.

Nobody thanked her. Nobody asked her for a fifth sentence and nobody is going to and she was not thanked for the one she gave on her way out. Nobody sent for anybody and nobody was forgiven and nothing was resolved, and the two at the back of that shed did not move and were not spoken to, and the form went back on the board and will be on it again on the second of the two days next week.
