# Chapter 0438: Sixteen Volumes With Fifteen Of Them Out On Loan, A Notice With A Clean Space On The Back Of It, And A Cart Going Up On The Second Day

The counting room is at the back of a dye end and it has one window and the window has a board across the bottom of it, and there are shelves down both sides and the shelves go about as high as a person of twenty can reach without the stool.

**There are sixteen volumes on those shelves. Fifteen of them are out on loan and the sixteenth has her own name in capitals at the head of it and it has not been opened since it was written.**

It is the fourth day of the fourth week of the eleventh month of the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is the fourth hour.

---

She is twenty. The wage of nineteen days of a crew's money is held in a coat four miles off, in a room she has not stood in since she was seventeen, and it is not in this building and it is not in her name and nobody in this room knows it is there and nobody in that room knows it is in there.

A woman said a rule to her once, in a lane, in a week that is gone, and it has not been given to a third woman and it is not going to be given to one while she is alive, and she has not asked anybody anything since and nobody has asked her anything since, and one of the two of those is hers and the other one is not.

Whoever said it to her is not anywhere in this building, and the saying of it cost the woman who said it what it cost the woman who heard it, and not one of the two has put it anywhere since. It is carried in one person and in one other, and there is no third place it could be put, and a room where volumes are counted is not any kind of third place.

---

The man from the front office came in at about the fourth hour with the cart list in his hand and he did not knock, because there is no door in that room worth knocking on.

"They go up on the second day."

"They have gone up on the second day every fortnight since I was seventeen and none of them has gone anywhere else."

"**All sixteen of them. That is the finding and I did not arrive with it, I was told it upstairs before I came down, and I am telling you because I am not going to have you find out from a man with a cart.**"

---

She put her hand flat on the sixteenth volume, which is the one with her own name in capitals at the head of it, and she did not open it, and she has not opened it since it was written, and nobody in that building has ever asked her to and nobody ever is going to.

"They cannot all go on one cart."

"They will be on two, and the second one comes back empty and comes down again on the fourth day, and that is not my business and it is not a threat and I have not been sent to make it one."

---

**And here is the whole of the resistance, and it was not in him and it was in her own pocket, and she did not take it out and she is not going to.**

A notice came down the dye end about two months ago and it is in that pocket now, and on the back of it there is a printed ruled space, and the space is clean.

It has been clean for about two months. She has had it in a pocket through about two months of washes and it is clean and she has not put a mark in it and she has not asked anybody what the space is for and she is not going to, and there is a wage of nineteen days of a crew's money held against something about four miles off that she has not asked anybody the name of and is not going to and is not going up this river this season to find out.

A printed ruled space with nothing in it is not a mistake and it is not an oversight, and a printed space which nobody has filled in is the ordinary condition of a printed space. Whatever the space is for, it was not made for what she has been doing with it, and a person who leaves one empty for two months is not doing anything a person could be shown doing.

**A season is not a life. She has said that to herself about four times since she came and it has not got any less true and it is the only sentence she has got that is about all of it.**

---

"**That has been in your pocket since the dye end, and I am not going to ask you about it, and I want it noticed that I noticed.**"

"I know you noticed. I have not been waiting for you to notice and I have not been waiting for you to ask, and I would rather have neither than one more, and the space on the back of it is clean and it is going to stay clean."

"I am not going to tell you what that is for and I am not going to ask you what it is for, and I have not asked you one thing in about four months and I am not starting in a room with one cart standing in it."

---

He took the list out of his hand and put it back in his pocket and did not write anything on it, and nobody at the front office signed for the sixteen of them and nobody signed for the fifteen, and there is no form anywhere for a person to be entered as the one who counted sixteen and said nothing about the sixteenth.

The list in his pocket is not a document anybody could be produced for in a room, and it has never had a word put on it. The interval it travels on has never once been shortened or lengthened by anybody and nobody has ever put in a request to have it. It goes up on the second day and it goes up on the second day again a fortnight after that, and nobody has ever asked whether the day could be a different one.

The cart came up at the fourth hour on the second day and the man loaded fifteen of them and left the one with her own name on the shelf where it was, and he did not say anything about the one he left and she did not say anything about it either.

The second cart came back empty as it was always going to and came down again later in the same week, and the stool was not taken out from under the shelves at any hour of either day, and the shelves stood about as high as they have always stood. She had the count of them before he started loading and she had it again when he had finished, and nobody in the room was asked to confirm either count and nobody did.

---

Nobody paid her. Nobody sent for her. Nobody thanked her and nobody forgave her and nothing was resolved, and a season is not a life.

**The four hundred and thirty miles is walked zero times in this chapter and is not walked by her either, and she is not going up this river this season.** Fifteen of the sixteen volumes were on a cart about four miles off by the time that cart was loaded. The sixteenth is on a shelf behind her with her name in capitals at the head of it, unopened, and the space on the back of that notice is still clean, and she did not look at it once while the cart was being loaded.
