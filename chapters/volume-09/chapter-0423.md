# Chapter 0423: Fourpence On The Boards, A Piece Of Paper Filled In By Hand, And A Coat With No Column In It

About four people use that floor and there is not one of them under anybody. **The case is at the near end of the boards and the box is at the far end of them, and the wall board behind the counter has four items on it, and the drawer under that board has been shut for eleven years.** The man who comes in on the second of the two days is not in the building. Four people are in the room and all four of them are doing what they do on every ordinary morning.

It is the second day of the first week of the eighth month of the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is the fourth hour, and the counter shuts at the sixth hour.

---

**He has packed for two years and he has carried about four sheets a week up those four flights, and he has read the top of about six of them by accident, and none of the six was read on purpose and none of the six has ever been spoken about in that building.** He came up the stair with the four under his arm and put them on the boards and squared them against the edge, which he does.

He had a stub of pencil behind his ear and he did not take it out until after he had been at the boards for about a quarter of an hour.

---

**Reading the top of a sheet by accident is a thing that happens because a sheet is carried folded and the top of it is the part that shows.** The four came up off a pallet and the four that were not folded square had a head on them, and the head is the part a man sees, and he has had two years of about four a week and the same six or thereabouts, and he has never once written any of it down and has never once been asked for any of it.

**He is the only person on that floor who has read the top of a sheet and has not been asked what is on it, and he is not a carrier and has never claimed to be one, and the only reason he is the only one is that everybody else on that floor can read and he has never once been able to and has never once pretended to.**

Six months ago he said a true thing in a room to two people, and one of them put it back to him about four days later and said he had made the same statement twice in front of two people, and he did not take it back. That is the whole of what he has ever done in a room and it is not going to be in a room again.

---

Then he put fourpence on the boards.

Nobody said anything. **Tamsin Rook took it and went to the case at the near end and took out a piece of paper with nothing printed on it and came back and put it in front of him, and she did it in the order she has done it about four hundred times, and she said nothing at all.**

He could see her hands the whole time. That was the whole of what happened at that counter and there was nothing else in it and no part of it was unusual.

---

**He filled it in by hand.** Not in a hurry and not slowly, and with the stub, and about a third of the way down he stopped and turned the sheet a quarter round to get at the light off the boards and then carried on, and the two of them at the far end of that room went on with what they were doing and did not look up, and neither of them asked him one thing about it, and he did not say one word to anybody.

**And he said two sentences out loud into a room that had not given him anything to say them to, and neither sentence was addressed to a person, and neither of them was picked up.**

"**Everything with a heading on it has somewhere to go.**"

He said it to the boards, about the length of a breath, and went on with the pencil.

"**This has not got one.**"

---

The clerk of about twenty-nine at the two joined tables did not look up, and Marn Ottery at the far end of those boards did not turn round, and neither of them said anything, and **there is no form anywhere in this empire for a clerk to be recorded as the one who did not hear a thing said in a room she was standing in.**

He waited about as long as a person waits for somebody to take a thing off him, and nobody took it off him, and the room did the ordinary thing the room does, and he folded the sheet in three and put it inside his coat and buttoned the coat over it.

**He had it in him before he came up the stair. That is the part that is not about the counter. A thing you have carried in a coat for nine years gets heavy and there is no such thing as getting it down, because there is no box anywhere in this empire for a no and a thing nobody is offered is a thing nobody can do. **A man who puts a thing in a piece of paper is not getting it down. He is only getting it into a thing that has no column**, and there is no form anywhere that would let anybody show him what the column was going to be for.** He had it written out in his head in about the order he was writing it, and he wrote it in that order, and it took about a minute and a half, and nobody in that room knew a single line of it and nobody asked.

---

**She is the only person in that building who could have said something and she is not going to, and she did not say it, and she was not going to be asked, and nobody asked her.** She had two years of a floor in which not one person who came up those stairs had put a question to her, and this morning a man had put fourpence down and taken a piece of paper away with something written on it in his own hand, and she had handed it to him, and the fourpence went into the ordinary place and the paper did not go anywhere that anybody in that room could account for.

"I am not going to say a word about it," she said, to nobody, and did not say that either, twice.

---

He went down the four flights with the four sheets under his arm and the other thing inside his coat, and he had not bought a copy of anything that anybody there could be shown, and there is no form anywhere in that building that says which column a piece of paper belongs in, and he has never once in two years been asked what the top of any of the six was.

**Nobody thanked him. Nobody asked him what it was for. The box under the far end of those boards did not open and did not move, the drawer did not open, nothing was ruled, and a piece of paper with nothing printed on it went down four flights inside a coat and out of a building, and it is the smallest thing that has happened in this matter in eight volumes and there are two people in this empire who know about it and one of them has a mouth that is not going to be used.**
