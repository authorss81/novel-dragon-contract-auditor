# Chapter 0430: A Thing With No Column At The Foot Of A Sheet, A Third Woman In The Room, And A Folded Thing Put Down At The Fifth Hour

**The fourth of the four on that wall board is the one none of the three of them can account for, and it is in Marn Ottery's own hand, and it is about four years old, and she put it there herself.** The board behind that counter has carried four items for eleven years and not one of the four is time, and the three of them have between them looked at the fourth of those items and have never once agreed on what it is for.

**She is thirty-four and she has stood at those boards for eleven years and she is not a lawyer, and nobody else on that floor would know what to do with one of the blanks, and that is not modesty. She said it out loud once, in the first week of the second month, and nobody thanked her, and what she said along with it was that she was not going to be thanked then or later, and that is still the arrangement on that floor.**

It is the fourth day of the fourth week of the ninth month of the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is the sixth hour, and the counter shuts at the sixth hour.

---

**Something has gone out of that counter this month that has not gone out of it in eleven years, and nobody has been told what it was and nobody is going to be.** She has been at the near end of those boards since the fourth hour and she has not opened the drawer and she has not touched the box and she has not ruled anything, and fourpence has gone into the ordinary place four times this month and no paper at all has been left on those boards on any day of it.

**The man who comes in on the second of the two days went down that stair at about half past the fifth hour and took nothing with him.** There are three people in that room and there were two a month ago.

Four items on a board behind the counter is a small amount of paper to look at twice a day for eleven years, and every morning for eleven years all four of them have been looked at, and not once in eleven years have the three of them agreed about the fourth of the four, and agreeing is a thing three people can do in about a minute, and nobody has ever said out loud that the case and the board could be moved.

---

The clerk of about twenty-nine had been at the far end of the two joined tables since the fourth hour with a sheet squared in front of her and her pen down, and Tamsin Rook was at the near end with her back to the case and her hands in front of her doing nothing at all, which is the thing she does when there is nothing on that counter.

Nine seconds is not a long time and it is the length of a room deciding not to speak, and a good many of them have gone by at the counter more often than anybody there could put to, and nearly all of them were started by the same one of the two women and the rest by the other, and the third has never asked which of the two it was, and the answer has never come up.

Nobody said anything for about nine seconds after the boards went up.

---

"I am going to say one thing in this room and it is not a reason and it is not an answer to anything, because there is no question in this room and has not been for about four years." The clerk of about twenty-nine did not look up. "**A filled-in sheet is a thing with no column. A thing with no column cannot be filed. A thing that cannot be filed stays in the room it was put in. And that is not a failure of an arrangement. That is the arrangement, and it is the only part of this floor that has never once needed anything done to it.**"

Marn Ottery put both hands flat on the boards.

"**That is not an answer to anything and you have not given me one, and I am not going to thank you for it, and I want the reason for the not thanking you written down somewhere, because it is not that it is unhelpful.**"

"I did not offer it to you to use."

---

"You have offered it to the room and there are three of us in it, and one of us is not going to be able to do one single thing with it." Marn Ottery did not turn round. "**And I am not going to name which one and I am not going to look at her, and I have been not looking at her since the second week of the eighth month and it has cost me more than the fourth item on that board cost in four years.**"

Tamsin Rook did not move and did not say anything and nobody asked her for a word, and there is no form anywhere in this empire for a person to be recorded as the one who did not speak, and this is the second of those in about eight weeks. 

---

Then the stair, at about the fifth hour, and the man from the yard came up it with the satchel off the strap and the cold in the leather where it sits against a man's hip, and he put a folded thing on the boards the way he puts one there most days, and did not take the bag off his shoulder, and nobody on that floor has ever told him what is in it.

He has never once turned the bag round and nobody on the floor has ever asked him a single thing about what is in it, and the shape of a satchel and where the cold sits in it is the whole of what anybody there could ever be shown about what he carries.

"The cold is in it again," he said. "It is in the same place as always and it has not been out of it since the frost and I am telling you that because I tell you that."

The sixth hour is when the boards go up and the case goes behind them, and the counter empties before the hour is done, and the boards going up has been watched more times than any of the three could put to, and who the last one out of the room is has never once been settled by anybody, because the boards go up, the door opens, and they leave in the order they have always left in.

"You are not going to be told what is in it and you have never once asked and there is nowhere on this floor to tell you."

---

"**And I could have told you what was in it this morning, and I want that said out loud in this room, and I am saying it after he has gone down the stair.**" Marn Ottery took her hands off the boards. "A piece of paper with nothing printed on it is the only paper in this empire that cannot be laid against anybody. If I had put what that was down in words in front of him at the fifth hour it would not have made him a party to it and it would not have cost him anything and I would have been within my rights. **I did not do it and I am not going to, and the reason has nothing to do with him and everything to do with the fact that there is no form anywhere in which to be shown what a thing is for, and that has not changed in eleven years and it is not going to change because I have found out that I am allowed.**"

---

Nobody thanked her. Nobody asked her anything. Nobody was sent for and nobody was forgiven.

The clerk of about twenty-nine at the two joined tables had said one thing in that room in about four years and she said nothing further about it and she will not be asked for a second one, and the third woman in that room said nothing at all for the second time in about eight weeks and is not going to be asked and is not going to be thanked and is not going to be anybody's.

**The fourth item on that board is in Marn Ottery's own hand and it is about four years old and none of the three of them can account for it, and it went back on that wall at the sixth hour in the place it has been in all along. The case went behind the boards and the boards went up. The box did not open and did not move, and the drawer did not open and has not been opened in eleven years, and there are four things in it and two of them have never been looked at. No blank was filled in that room that day, and the one that was, in the first week of the eighth month, by a man who is not a villain and has no plan and is on no list in this matter, has no column and cannot be filed and is not in this room. Nothing anybody has said out loud in this matter has been put to anybody in a room, and the number of them is the same as it was on the morning this month began.**
