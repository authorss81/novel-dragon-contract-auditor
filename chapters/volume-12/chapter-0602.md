# Chapter 0602: An Armful Put Down At The Far End Of That Bench By A Girl Of Seventeen

That count has been running a couple of sheets light twice a week for about a month, and this morning it came out even, and nothing had been put on that bench all morning to make it come out even.

It is the fourth day of the fourth week of the fourth month of the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is about the fourth hour.

---

That bay is about nine foot by eleven. The chain along the front of that bench goes at one rate under a woman's hand and it has not slowed for her in about six weeks. A class put her at the back of that bay eleven years ago. The count is taken at the back end of that bench, the girl works at the other end of it, the two of those are about nine foot apart, and the floor between that bay and the wall it stands against is ninety foot the length of it.

A boy of seventeen is at the far end of that bench. He came out of another place a year ago on an engagement somebody wrote down for him, and a silence is sitting on that engagement as well, and he gave up reading the tops of those sheets two springs ago and has not gone back to it.

---

The boy carried the armful past the end of that bench and set it on the boards at the front of the bay and squared it with the heel of his hand. He has carried every armful past that end for about a month so that there would be nothing there for anything to be put down by, and this morning he put one down there himself, and the count at the far end of that bench came out right for the first time in about a month.

She came to the end of that bench with the next armful on her hip and she stopped, and she put it down on the same boards at the same end.

He did not look along nine foot of bench.

---

**I have been standing at the front of that bench for eleven years and I put one true sentence out loud into this bay in the third week of the seventh month and there were two people in it and neither of them turned round, and one of them was at that end where the count is made. I worked out in a month what that armful had been doing at the other end of those nine feet and I could not put one thing about it into any form in this city, and I walked ninety foot of my own floor a fortnight ago to stand at that end and I put nothing anywhere.**

The count has been short twice a week for about a month and the boy at that end has been the one carrying past it. A boy who does that has done it on purpose, and there is no form anywhere in this city that will take him for arranging that nothing should arrive in front of a girl, and nobody in this shed is going to put one word to him about it and nobody in this shed is going to give him anything for it.

This morning he put an armful down at that end himself. That is the whole of what happened in this bay today and it is the end of a month of a boy arranging the end of a bench so that nothing would be put there in front of a girl, and he has stopped arranging it, and the count is right, and a count being right is a thing about a bench and not about either of them.

And about an hour later the girl carried the next armful down and set it down on the same boards at the same end of the same bench, and the boy did not look along nine foot of bench while she did it.

---

She stayed at that end about as long as it takes to square a sheet and then she picked the two armfuls up together and carried them out of that bay one sheet at a time, at the rate she carries them, and the boy at the other end went on with what he was doing and did not turn round.

The count at that end of that bench is made at about the sixth hour and it is right again, and the paper on the board by that door has been left blank for eleven years, and not one word has been put to that boy about any of it and none is going to be.
