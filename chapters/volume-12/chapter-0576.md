# Chapter 0576: An Armful Set Down At The Far End Of That Bench For The First Time In Three Months, And A Girl Who Walked Past It

He had carried every armful past the end of that bench for about two months so that a girl who was standing there would never have it put down in front of her, and she had not been standing there for about a week, and he had gone on carrying them.

It is the second day of the second week of the tenth month of the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is about the fifth hour.

---

Three weeks ago the woman who puts work at that end came down the ninety foot of that floor and said a true thing out loud about the girl at the end by the door, in the ordinary voice, at the ordinary hour, to the boards in front of her.

He was at the far end of that bench, where the count is made. That bay is about nine foot by eleven. There was nobody else in it.

There is a chain along the front of the whole of that bench and the count for the whole floor is made once a day at the far end of it, and neither of those two things has ever been said out loud in that bay, and the two ends of that bench have been worked at by two people for longer than either of them has been able to say how long.

He is seventeen and there are four people in this matter who cannot get the sense out of a paragraph and he is not one of the four and has not been asked which of anything he is, and nobody in that shed has ever wanted one single thing out of him, and that is the whole of what has ever made him safe.

---

**I heard it. That is all. I was at the far end of that bench doing the count and the bay is nine foot by eleven and she said it at the ordinary volume in the ordinary way, and I heard it, and she was working, and there is no form anywhere in this city that takes me as the one who heard it and there is not going to be one, and there is not one that takes her as the one who said it.**

A person who has heard a thing cannot afterwards be shown to have been somewhere they were not, and cannot be shown to have agreed with it, and cannot be shown to have refused it, and there is nowhere to put any of that.

She has never asked me for anything either. That is the thing about her, and it is the thing about me, and it is the whole reason the two ends of the bench are nine foot apart and not four inches.

The girl was at the other end of that bench with her back to ninety foot of floor and she put a sheet down on top of a pile and squared it and picked up the next one, and she did not turn round, and she has never said one word about it, and she is never going to say one word about it, and she does not know that I was at the other end and she is not going to be told.

---

She has not come in a quarter of an hour early for about a week.

He gets in about ten minutes after the lane wakes and he is the only person in that shed who could tell the difference, because he is the only one there at the hour, and he has not said one word to anybody about it and he is not going to. There is nothing in this city that would take a boy of seventeen for having noticed when a girl stopped coming in early.

A boy who is the first one onto a floor every morning learns the sound of the floor before he learns anything else in it, and he can tell what hour it is by the light on the boards, and nobody has ever asked him to, and there is no heading anywhere in this empire for what a boy of seventeen knows about the sound of a floor he is on first.

He has gone on carrying the armfuls past the end of that bench for that week. There has been nothing on that end to carry past since the morning before the woman came down that floor, and a man who carries an armful past an empty end of a bench for a week is a man doing a thing that has no reason in it, and the whole of that shed can see that.

So this morning he set it down on his own end, at the back of the bay, where the count is made.

---

She came along that bench at about the ordinary rate at about the fifth hour and she did not stop at that end and she did not look down at it.

She went the nine foot on to the end by the door and set down what she was carrying and squared it with the heel of her hand, and she did not look back along the bench either, and she did not touch the armful that was standing at the far end of it, and she is not going to.

He stood there and did not move it and did not say anything and did not go and put it back where it was before, and the count at the end of that bench was not done for about an hour, and no one in that shed has ever once wanted to know why a count is late.

---

There are three things a person can do with a thing that is put where she can get at it, and about a year of that bay has been spent working out which of them a girl of seventeen could be shown having done.

She can push it back, and that is a refusal and there is a place for a refusal. She can carry it on to where she works, and that is a thing done and nobody can show she wanted it. And there is a third one, and it is the one that has happened: she can walk past a thing somebody has put at her end and leave it standing there, and that is not a refusal and that is not wanting it, and nobody in this empire has a name for it.

He wanted to be able to give her one. That is what the armfuls were for. She has not been at that end for about a week and he has not been able to give her one and he is not going to be able to, and that is not going to be a thing anybody finds out about in this shed.

A thing put down at the far end of the bench and left standing there is a thing somebody offered, and the offer is the whole of it. Whether she ever comes near it is not going to be recorded either way, and he has known that since the first of those two months and has not mentioned it to anybody.

The chain went along at the front of that bench at the rate it has gone for eleven years, and the count on that end of it was about an hour short, and the armful he had put down there went out with the rest of that floor at the sixth hour the way everything in that shed goes out.

Nobody in that lane is going to be told that he heard a true thing said out loud in that bay about three weeks ago, and nobody in that lane is going to be told that he heard nothing at all. Those two are one absence and not two, and there is no form in this city that separates a person who has heard a thing from a person who was in the air of it, and he has got nothing in his hands this month that is any use to him at all.
