# Chapter 0599: A Jug Of Water Two Days Old Standing On A Table Beside A Glass, And A Woman On A Landing Who Did Not Knock

He had filled that jug at about the fourth hour every morning for a fortnight and this morning he did not, and the water in it was two days old and had gone off, and he was not going to empty it either.

It is the second day of the first week of the fourth month of the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is between the first hour and about the fourth.

---

The jug is on that table at about a foot back from the edge and the glass is face up beside it with the top left corner gone off it, and there is one coin on that table that came out of that door at the first hour about four inches in and stopped, and it is his and it is not going to be spent. The bed is against the wall and is not made up. The stool is under the window and the window has been shut with its catch turned for about two years. The chair is where he dragged it a spring that is gone, with its back to the window and its face to four feet of plaster, and it is still there.

The pail at the end of that landing was about two-thirds full this morning and it has been about two-thirds full for two days.

---

She came up that stair at the first hour and went back down it, and she came up it again at about the fourth hour, and she has been doing that for about thirteen weeks, and the mat on that landing has its near corner standing up where the boards have lifted at a joint and it is the landing's mat and not his.

A boot came on the second tread and stopped there, and he did not go to the door, and it carried on down the rest of that flight and the stair went quiet behind it.

"There is more in that pail than there was two days ago."

"There is."

"You are not coming out for it."

"No."

"That is two days."

"That is two days."

"And the jug on your table has not been emptied."

"That is not the same jug."

"It is the same jug. You carried that jug out on to this landing the other day and you have not carried it out since."

---

**I had a quart of clean water in that jug every morning for a fortnight and it was the only thing I have ever done in this city that anybody could be shown having done, and I have let it go off in the jug rather than empty it, and that is the whole of a decision I made about a fortnight ago and I have not told one person.**

A man who changes the water in a jug in a room he takes by the week is a man who is in that room every morning. That is what it is and there is no other reading of it and it took me ten years to have something worth being entered in and I gave it up over a pail at the end of a landing.

And a man who leaves two days of water standing in a jug is a man that nobody in this house would want one single thing out of. I would rather be that man in a room I do not intend to be in for a week longer than the week costs than be a man with a jug changed every morning in a city of about nine hundred buildings where nobody could produce him for anything.

---

She did not knock. She has never knocked and she is not going to, and she stood on that landing with the light gone off it about as long as it takes a coin to travel under a door, and then she came down past the second tread and the stair went quiet and did not come back up.

The last hour of the light went off that floor at about the fourth hour and went off that landing with it, and the jug was standing on that table in the dark with two days of water in it, and he did not turn the lamp on, and he did not go out to the pail.
