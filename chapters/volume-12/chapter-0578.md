# Chapter 0578: A Bundle At The Far End Of That Bench, And A Boy Who Would Not Read The Top Of It

She had given up the quarter of an hour, and she had given it up about three weeks ago, and there was a bundle at the end of that bench on this morning and she was not there early enough to see who had put it.

It is the fourth day of the fourth week of the tenth month of the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is between the third hour and about the fourth.

---

She is seventeen, and in about a year nobody in that shed has wanted anything out of her at all, and she has done the sums on what that is worth. The sum comes to this: there has never been a single thing put in her reach for her to turn down, and so there is nothing in her that anybody could produce as a refusal.

She works at the end of the bench by the door with her back to the ninety foot of floor and her face to a wall, and she has worked there since she came out of somewhere else. The paper on the board by the door is not hers and it is not anybody's, and it has been on that board since before she was in that shed.

A thing has to be put on a bench to be any use to anybody, and the bench has two ends with about nine foot between them, and a person can be at one of them or at the other and not at both, and there are three people in that bay and two ends.

That had been the whole of what she knew about it in the third week of the ninth month, and it was enough to get her to that end of that bench about a quarter of an hour before the lane woke, four mornings running, to stand where a thing could be put that she could put back.

And then the woman came down ninety foot of that floor and said a true thing out loud about her.

---

**She said it at the ordinary volume in the ordinary way with her back to ninety foot of that bay and I was at the end by the door with a sheet in both hands and I put it down on the pile and squared it and picked up the next one and I did not turn round, and I have not turned round about it since.**

Here is what I have worked out since, and it is about a quarter of an hour and nothing else. A person who is in a room before the room is awake is a person who can be shown having been in it. And that bay is about nine foot by eleven and I had been standing at the far end of that bench in it about a quarter of an hour before anybody came in, four mornings, and there is a bay where a thing was said at the ordinary hour on the ordinary volume, and the two of those are about a quarter of an hour apart and nothing at all.

So I do not come in early. That is the whole of the decision and I made it about three weeks ago and I have not told anybody and I am not going to.

The lane wakes in the order the work does. There is a stretch of it with boots on it and then there is a stretch of it with nobody on it at all, and for four mornings a week that empty stretch was mine before anybody else was up, and it was the only thing in that building that had ever belonged to me.

And here is what it has cost and it is the only thing I ever wanted in that shed. I was standing at that end for something to be put where I could turn it down, and I was standing there in the open with a person in the room, and I have taken myself out of the only place in this empire where anything has ever been put where I could have put it back.

---

She came in about ten minutes after the lane woke and she went the nine foot down that bench to her own end at the door and there was a bundle at the far end of it where the count is made.

She looked at it for about as long as it takes a sheet to be squared.

Then she went to her own end and started work and she did not go back along that bench, and the bundle stood at the far end of it the whole of that morning.

It was a bundle of ordinary work and nothing else, and any person can turn down a bundle of ordinary work without saying a word, and that is the whole of the reason one is ever put on that bench at all, and neither of the two people in that bay was there to see who had put this one there.

---

He came in about ten minutes after her and he was seventeen as well and that end is his whole working day, and he did not look at the bundle either.

He stopped about four inches short of it and worked round it on the other side, and he did not move it and he did not read the top of it, and he had given the tops of that shed up in the spring before last and has not gone back to it. He went on with what he was on the whole of that morning at the end of that bench.

The bundle was standing in the middle of his own end of that bench, where he works, and reading the top of it would have meant stopping what he was on, and he did not stop what he was on.

Neither of them said a syllable about it and neither of them ever will, and nothing in this city takes a boy of seventeen for leaving a thing standing at the end of a bench, and nothing in it takes a girl for letting it stand there.

---

There have been two things put at that far end in about a month.

The first one the woman put there herself, on the eighth morning of a run, after she had stood at the front of that bench for a quarter of an hour the morning before, and the girl carried it the nine foot to her own end and squared it and it went out with the rest of that floor and nobody saw her want it and nobody saw her turn it down.

The second one came on a morning when the only person who had been in that bay early was not, and the girl cannot show it was for her, and the woman who puts work at that end has stayed where she is since the morning she said it, and the boy would not read the top of it.

If she carries that one on as well, then she has carried two of them on, and a person who carries two of them on can be shown to have wanted both, and she wanted one of them and has no idea which, and neither has anybody else.

So it stood at the far end of that bench until the sixth hour and went out with the rest of that floor the way everything in that shed goes out, and the count at that end was short again. She gave up a quarter of an hour about three weeks ago so that nothing could ever be produced about her, and there is nothing at that bench to give back for it, and it is the first thing she has chosen for herself in about a year and she chose it on her own.
