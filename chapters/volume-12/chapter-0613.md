# Chapter 0613: A Second Of A Line That Was Entered A Year And More Ago, With Nothing Written On It

A sheet that has been in that shelf since before the spring that is gone came out of it this morning, and she has made a second of it, and there is nothing on the second of it at all.

It is the fourth day of the third week of the seventh month of the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is between the first hour and about the fourth.

---

About four hundred blanks a year come out of the case at the near end of those boards and a copy is made at the same counter for fourpence, and the book the lines are entered in is kept a year and then a year, and a line a year old can still be copied and a line out of this month cannot. The woman of twenty-four has been at that end of those boards two years and came in twenty minutes early every working morning of them and the pen lies in the middle of her book where it has lain all that time.

That shelf is under the boards at the near end. Something else is shut under the far end of those boards and the lid of it has not been off in six years. There are four items on the wall at the other end of that floor and the fourth of them is in the hand of the woman who wrote it, about four years ago.

---

She copied the line at about the second hour, off a leaf that has been folded twice, and she did it in about as long as it takes to square a sheet, and then she put the copy down on those boards in front of her with the printed strip along the head of it facing away from her and left it there.

The woman of thirty-four came along those boards with the day's count in her hand and stopped at the near end of them.

"What is that."

"A second."

"Of what."

"Of a line."

"Whose line."

"A year's."

---

"There is a name on that sheet, and I can see that there is a name on that sheet from here, and if you take that down those four flights in your own hand with a name at the top of it, then you are the woman who copied it, and that is a thing a person can be shown having done, and there is a woman on this floor who has spent two years not being shown anything."

"I did not say I was going to take it down."

"You made it."

"I made it. I have not written one word on it, and it is going out of this room in my own hand at the end of this hour, and it is going to a person who works in a room I have never been in, and there is no name at the top of it and there is not going to be one."

"Then it is a sheet with a number on it and no name, and a sheet like that cannot be done anything with, and you know that as well as I do."

"Yes."

"You are doing it anyway."

"I am doing it anyway, and that is the difference between the two of us and it has been the difference for two years, and you are not going to argue me out of it at the second hour on a working morning."

---

**A second of a record is the only thing this matter has ever had, and anybody may have one and it costs fourpence and it takes about as long as it takes to square a sheet, and the whole of what I have worked out in two years at that end of these boards is that a copy has nobody standing in it once it has been made.**

That is why nobody in this building has ever thanked anybody for anything. It is not modesty and it is not manners. The first time a thing is made there is a person in it and the second time there is not, and there is no way of getting one back in, and a sheet with a name at the top of it is a sheet with a person in it, and a sheet with a person in it is a thing that can be pointed at.

So I have copied a line that was entered a year and more ago out of a book that is kept a year, and I have not written a heading on it, and there is not a date on it, and there is not a name on it, and it is going to a person who has never met me and does not know that I exist and will not be able to find out from anything I have written. The refusal travels with the sheet. That is the ordinary use of it. Somebody in another room is about to be handed a number with nothing standing against it and there is no form in this city in which that person can be shown having been handed it by anybody.

And I have a third place under the floor of my own room that I made out of a sheet I did not make, and nobody on this floor knows where it is, and I have never told one person, and I am not going to be thanked for the one in the shelf either, and I have not told the woman standing in front of me which of the two of those things is the reason.

---

The woman of thirty-four went along those boards and counted what had gone out of that case that day, out loud, at the rate she counts, which she has not done in two years, and the number of it went into that room in the air and stayed there.

"There is a sheet on your boards with nothing on it and you are going to walk it down four flights."

"I am."

"And I am not going to ask you what it is a second of, and you know that I am not, and that is the only thing I have got this morning, and it is not very much."

"It is enough for two years."

The woman of twenty-four picked the second sheet up at the end of that hour and carried it down those four flights in the front of her apron, and the third of those flights has a hollow in it, and she did not stop on it.

---

The case at the near end of those boards gave out about four hundred blanks in that year, and one of them was a second of a line that was already a year old when it was copied, and she went down that stair without slowing at the one that has a hollow worn in it.
