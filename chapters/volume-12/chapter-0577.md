# Chapter 0577: Ten Weeks Without A Mark On The Front Boards Of That Ground Floor, And Ten Minutes Of A Cloth Going Over Them

She had come down that lane for five weeks running and stood on the step below the turn in an empty passage, and this week she was standing in the middle of it, and she had the whole of a sentence ready and had had it ready since the lane.

It is the fourth day of the third week of the tenth month of the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is between the third hour and about the sixth.

---

The top step at the turn has the tin on it and the lid flat down on it, and it is the only thing in that passage that is exactly where it has always been, and there has been no mark on the boards at the front of that ground floor for ten weeks.

There are two ways into the passage and one way out of it. The front door is at the near end and the turn is at the far one, and she comes in by the back of the ground floor and goes out by the front, and she has not gone up those two steps to the turn for the whole of the ten weeks.

She had worked the whole of that out on the way down the lane and she had it ready in the ordinary way at the ordinary volume for a woman who was working.

It is the same sentence she has been saying out loud to the floor of a room at the back of her own house, standing in the middle of it, on about seven mornings out of a fortnight, with a window and a door and nobody else in it. The difference this week is that there is a person in the room, and the person has a cloth in her hand, and the sentence is the same one either way.

The passage is about as long as a table. A person standing in the middle of it is in the whole of it and cannot be anywhere else. That was the finding, in the third week of the seventh month, and she has not stopped turning it over since.

---

The woman at the bottom of that stair came through that passage from the back at about the fourth hour with the cloth in her hand, and she stopped.

The boards were under her the whole way along that run and she walked them at the rate she walks everything, and the sound of a boot on a floor that has not been done in ten weeks goes further down a passage than a voice does.

"You are in the way of the boards."

"I am not doing anything."

"You are standing in the middle of them."

And she went to the front of that ground floor and put the cloth on the boards and began, at the rate she does everything, and the marks went on about two inches apart down the whole of the front of that ground floor with a sound on them that the house had not had in ten weeks.

She did not say one word while she did it. She worked her way along the front and came back and worked along it again, and the passage filled up with the ordinary noise of a floor being done, and it took about ten minutes.

---

**There was a person working in that passage for ten minutes and I had a true thing ready about a column I have not ruled in nine years, and I said nothing, and the reason I said nothing is that I got there.**

A thing said out loud where a person is working takes that person's standing there away from them. It is the only instrument there is and it costs the one who is in the room everything they had, which was that nobody could ever show them having been anywhere they were not.

I have got it down to one thing and it is not a clever one. You say a true thing in a room with a person in it, and the person is in the air of it, and there is nothing in this city that will put one of them down afterwards as having stood where they were not. It took years to find and about six weeks to be sure of, and I was going to use it on a woman with a cloth in her hand at about the fourth hour, and it was in my mouth when she stopped.

She is twenty years at the foot of that stair. She has never given a receipt for anything. There is a number cut into the back of a key and it is the whole of what that house has ever had to say about anybody in it. And I have stood in that passage five weeks wanting her to be at the front of that ground floor with her cloth, and she has not been there for ten weeks, and this morning she was, and I had my sentence in my mouth, and I was going to take the only thing she has.

---

She stood on the step below the turn and let it be done.

She got back onto the step when the cloth went on the boards and she stayed on it, and the top step with the tin on it was one above her and she did not go up it, and the wall of the turn was at her shoulder for the whole of the ten minutes and the only thing in front of her was the length of a floor being done.

The cloth went over the front boards and came back and went over them again, and the marks came up out of the wood about two inches apart, and after ten weeks there was nothing on that front to be a record of anything, and one of the two of them had thrown away the only thing that could have been produced against her, and neither of them was going to say a word about it and neither of them was going to be asked.

When it was done the woman at the bottom of that stair picked the cloth up off the boards by the door and carried it along the passage and out at the front, and she did not look along that passage on her way, and she is not going to be asked about the ten weeks or about the ten minutes.

The tin stood there the whole of those ten minutes and it did not move once, and the only thing in the house that was different at the end of them from the beginning of them was the front of the ground floor.

---

There is another room and there has been for about a year, and she has been up that stair once and stood at the top of it, and there is a woman who comes four times a week to that room and has been handed a thing every week for eleven years and has got nothing back and has never once been told what is in it.

Once was enough, and not going up there again is the only decision she has made about the room, and it is not one anybody is going to ask her the reason for.

She is not going to think about that room on a step at a turn in another woman's house.

Ten minutes of a cloth went over the front of that ground floor for the first time in ten weeks, and there is nothing in this empire that will take either of the two of them for it, and she is going up that lane at the pace she came down it and she is not going to say one word about it to anybody for as long as she lives.
