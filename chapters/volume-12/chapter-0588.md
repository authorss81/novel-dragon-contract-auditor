# Chapter 0588: A Wall Board Behind Those Boards With Four Items On It, And A Woman Who Put One Of Them Up Herself

She had not been asked one thing about the fourth item on that board in about four years, and in the last fortnight she has gone past that board twice a day instead of once, and she has not stopped at it either time, and that is the whole of what she has done about it.

It is the second day of the second week of the first month of the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is about the fifth hour.

---

She is thirty-four and she is the oldest of the three at that floor and she is not a lawyer, and she has not left that room in eleven years except once. She has put the shape of a thing out loud in there four times in that time, and nobody on that floor has put one question back to her about any of the four, and she told them on the first morning of a spring that is gone that there would not be a fifth.

She put the fourth item on that board herself about four years ago, in the ordinary way, at the ordinary hour, with a pen off that floor, and she has gone past it about three thousand times since and has never once put a hand on it. It is in her own hand and it has been there about four years, and not one of the four items on that board is time, and she has never taken it down and she is not going to.

A man comes up that stair four times a week with a satchel and goes down it again at about a quarter past, and he has never once said one word to her about anything, and that has never once been a courtesy and he is not going to start and she is not going to ask him to.

---

The woman of twenty-four was at the near end of those boards with the case behind her and she had a book open in front of her and nothing in her hands at all, and she has never wanted one single thing out of anybody on that floor in two years.

"You have been at that end of those boards eleven years and not one thing has ever been asked of you about that board."

"Nothing has."

"You put that one up yourself."

"I did."

"And you are not going to take it down."

"I am not going to take it down."

---

**A person who stands in a place cannot afterwards be shown to have been anywhere else, and I have stood at that end of those boards for eleven years and it has cost me nothing, because nothing has ever been wanted out of me here and nothing is going to be. That is the oldest arrangement I have and it is worth having. It is also worth nothing, and both of those are one arrangement and not two of them, and not one column in this empire will take a woman as the person who stood there.**

There is a man who came up those four flights six times in his life and came down with nothing in his hands on every one of them, and I have never asked him for one thing and he has never asked me for one thing, and the whole of what is between him and that floor is that. He is not one of the people who comes up here, and he was not thanked on any of those six, and nobody in that building is going to be told that I have kept the count of them, and I have kept it.

And the fourth item is going to stay on that board. I put it there. Nobody has asked me what it is for and I am not going to say what it is for and I am not going to say that I cannot tell you, and it is going to be on that board after I am not in that room.

---

The satchel went out of that room at about a quarter past and the stair took it, and nothing else went up or down. The woman of twenty-four did not look up from the book in front of her, and the rest of that floor went on with what it was at, and the case at the near end of those boards and the box under the far end of them were where they are, and the drawer under that board is shut.

The fourth item is still on that board in the hand that put it there about four years ago, and a woman who has stood at that end of those boards for eleven years is going to be at it again tomorrow at the same hour, and nobody on that floor is going to be told that she went past it twice this week and stopped at it neither time.
