# Chapter 0617: A Count Two Out At The Far End Of That Bench, And A Space Beside It Left Empty

That count has been short twice a week for about six weeks and it is two out this week, and the sheet that came down the lane on the ordinary morning has a space at the foot of it for the name of the person who counted, and the foreman of fifty-one put the number in and left the space empty.

It is the fourth day of the third week of the eighth month of the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is about the fourth hour.

---

That bay is about nine foot by eleven and the chain along the front of that bench goes at one rate under her hand and has not slowed for her in about eleven years. A class put her at the back of that bay eleven years ago and the form behind that class is on the board by that door and has not been filled in since before she came to that bench. The count is taken at the back end of that bench and the girl works at the other end of it and about nine foot of bench is between the two of them, and ninety foot of floor runs from that bay to the wall it stands against.

---

She wrote the number in the line with the flat of the nib and then she put the nib down beside her hand and left the space under it empty, and she squared the sheet against the edge of the case with the heel of her other hand the way a person squares a thing that is finished.

"There is a space under that number."

"There is."

"That space has had something in it every week since before I came to this bench."

"It has had a number in it every week since before you came to this bench. It has not had a name in it in about eleven years, and it did not have one in mine either, and there was a class on that board with a space under it and I would not fill that one in either, and I have thought about that this week more than I have thought about anything else."

---

"Two out," she said.

"That is two out."

"It has been short twice a week since before the month that is gone and it has never once been two."

"It has never once been two with a boy at the far end of that bench carrying every armful past that end of it so that there is nothing there for anything to be put down by."

"You have not asked him that."

"I have not asked him anything, this week or any week, and I am not going to start on a working morning, and there is a foreman standing ninety foot off the end of that bench who could walk down there in about as long as it takes to square a sheet and count what is on those boards with her own two eyes, and I am not going to do it, and I would like you to think about what that costs."

---

**There is one place in this shed where a thing was said out loud about a girl, and it is the far end of that bench, and I said it myself in the third week of the third month and there were two people in this bay and neither of them turned round.**

A foreman who walks down to the far end of that bench to count armfuls is a foreman who has decided to look at that end. That is the whole of what it is and there is no way of doing the counting from ninety foot off. The number is either right or it is two out, and I know which one it is and I know it is the boy.

I cannot be shown to have gone and found that out, and neither can he be shown to have arranged it, and the girl at the other end of that bench has gone a year without one thing being put to her, and that is not going to change this month, and nobody in this shed is going to hand her the chance to turn anything down.

So I have put a wrong number in a line on a sheet and left the space under it empty, and the sheet goes down that lane this afternoon with two out on it.

Nobody in this city can be shown that this foreman got it wrong, and nobody in this city can be shown that she got it right either, and I have wanted a gap like that since I was put at the back of that bench and I have never in eleven years had one handed to me and I have just made one out of a number.

Nobody in this shed is going to be told any of it. That boy at the far end will not be asked about the two, and nothing will be said in his favour, and nobody will be sent for. The form on the board by that door is going to be exactly as empty tomorrow as it is this morning.

---

That number went down the lane at about the sixth hour two out, and at the far end of that bench a boy of seventeen went on carrying armfuls past the end of it, and he is not going to be asked about the two.
