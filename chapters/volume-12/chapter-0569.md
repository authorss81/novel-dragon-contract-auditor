# Chapter 0569: The End Of A Bench At A Quarter Of An Hour Before The Lane Wakes, And An Armful Carried Past It

She had been coming in about a quarter of an hour early for four mornings and standing at the end of that bench, and there had been nothing at the end of that bench for all four of them.

It is the fourth day of the third week of the eighth month of the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is between the third hour and about the fourth.

---

She is seventeen.

Nobody in that shed has asked her one thing in about a year and nobody has ever wanted one single thing out of her, and she has not been given the chance to turn anything down, and there is nothing in this empire that could put that choice in front of a girl her age.

She has worked out what that costs, and it is this: a person who has never been given a thing cannot afterwards be shown to have refused one, and a person who has been given one and carried it on cannot be shown to have wanted it.

Not a bundle. Anything. A thing at the end of a bench, an armful, a word. If nothing is ever put in her reach that she could turn down, then there is nothing she can be shown to have turned down, and nothing she can be shown to have wanted either, and she is that for the rest of her life, and there is no column anywhere that could take her as anything else.

She has been coming to that end of that bench for about a year, and the only thing that has ever been put in her reach in the whole of that year is ordinary work that somebody else had already decided about.

---

Then a bundle came to that end of the bench about a fortnight ago, and she took it the nine foot to her own end and squared it on top of what was already there, and she did not stop at that end on her way.

That was the only thing put in her reach in about a year, and the woman who had put it there had stood at the front of that bench for about a quarter of an hour on the morning before she put it down, and neither of them said one word and neither of them looked along it.

She worked out over about four days that she had wanted that bundle and had carried it past anyway, and that wanting it and carrying it past are both true, and that the second one is the one anybody could see.

---

This morning she came in a quarter of an hour early and stood at the far end of that bench and there was nothing on it.

There was nothing on it the morning before and the morning before that, because the woman who puts work at that end had not come down the length of that floor since about the eighth morning, and there is nobody in that shed who comes down that floor any more than she does.

She stood at the far end of that bench for about as long as it takes a sheet to be squared.

---

He came in from the door about ten minutes after her and he had an armful with him at that end.

He is seventeen as well and that far end is his whole working day, every day of it, and there is nowhere else in that shed for him to be. He can make sense of a paragraph and he is not one of the four who cannot.

He did not stop. He came along that bench at the same rate he comes along it and he did not put the armful down at the end where she was standing and he did not look at her and he carried it on the nine foot past her to the end by the door and set it down where arms get set down.

---

**He has carried four mornings of work past the end of this bench where I was standing, and he will not put one down at it, and I am not going to ask him to, and neither of us is going to say one word about it. The woman put one thing here in a year and I carried it on, and now there is going to be nothing at this end at all, and the three of us have worked that out without anybody asking anybody anything.**

Neither of them said one word about it and neither of them is going to, and a boy of seventeen who carries an armful past the end of a bench is not entered anywhere for it, and a girl of seventeen who wanted a thing put there is not entered anywhere either.

---

He worked at that end with what he had brought in and the armful went out with the rest of that floor at the sixth hour, and she stood at that end until about the fourth hour and then went the nine foot and worked at her own end, and the two of them were at that end of that bench all morning and nothing was put on it at all.

She had come in early four mornings running and there is no book in that shed with the hour on it and there is not going to be one.

The chain went along at the front of that bench the whole of that morning at the rate it has gone for eleven years, and the light came off the roof of that shed about a hand's breadth up the wall and stopped there, and nothing was put on that end of that bench that day, and she was the only person in that shed who had come in early for anything.
