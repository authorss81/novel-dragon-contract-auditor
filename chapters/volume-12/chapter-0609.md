# Chapter 0609: The Fourth Item Put Back Onto That Board, And Two Women On One Floor Not Looking

She had taken the fourth of the four items off that board about three weeks ago and put it in the front of her apron, and this morning she put it back.

It is the fourth day of the third week of the sixth month of the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is between the first hour and about the third.

---

She wrote the fourth of the four items on that wall herself about four years ago and it spent about three weeks in the front of her apron in between. About four hundred blanks a year come out of a case at the near end of the boards. A different box is shut under the far end of them and has been for about six years, and the drawer under the wall has been shut for eleven years and holds four things and two of them have never been opened. None of the four items on that wall is time.

It went into the apron folded once because that is the size of the front of an apron, and it stayed folded for three weeks and it has gone soft along the fold the way paper goes soft, and it has a smell of cloth on it now that it did not have when it came off that wall. She has taken it out and looked at it at home and put it back three times and she has not shown it to anybody, and it is not going to be shown to anybody.

The three that were on that board when she took it off are still on it, and none of them has been moved while it was in the front of her apron, and the middle of the wall is a place where four things were.

She is thirty-four. She has been at that end of the floor eleven years and is the oldest of the three that work at it.

---

The woman of twenty-four was at that end of the room with her book open and her hands flat on it, and she was in early, twenty minutes, as she has been every working morning for two years.

A man came up those four flights at about the first hour with a satchel over his shoulder and stopped about a yard off the case with the strap still round his forearm, and he did not put it down and he did not turn round.

She took the thing out of the front of her apron and opened it out along the fold with two fingers and it came out with the shape of the cloth still in it. The fold had gone soft across the width of it in three weeks and there was a set in the paper where her thumbs had to take it out before it would lie down, and it came out with a smell of cloth on it and nothing else on it at all. She held it up in front of her for about as long as it takes a cloth to be folded once and she did not read it, because she had written it herself about four years ago and there is not one word on it she does not know.

She put the fourth item back onto that board and squared it against the top of it with the heel of her hand.

The woman of twenty-four did not look. She worked at the far side of the case with a book open in front of her and she did not look along the boards at all that morning, and she was still on the floor after the bag went down the four flights at about a quarter past, which is a thing she has not done since before the spring that is gone.

---

**I took the fourth of those four items off that wall about three weeks ago because I wanted it off, and wanting is the whole of what I have not done on this floor in eleven years, and I have never once wanted one single thing out of anybody who works at it, and that has been the arrangement, and there is nothing in that arrangement for anybody else in nine hundred buildings and there is everything in it for me.**

Three items on a board and a bare patch where the fourth stood is a thing a person can be shown. A woman who has taken something down can be shown to have wanted it down and can be shown not to have wanted it up and can be shown to have said nothing about it for four years, and there is nothing in nine hundred buildings that enters a woman as the one who put it there in the first place or as the one who took it off.

I have gone over it every working morning for three weeks. There is no version of it that is a mistake. A sheet of paper does not come off a wall by itself and it does not go back by itself, and there is nothing in the working of that floor that would put it back except the woman who took it down.

And I put it back this morning and that is a second thing of the same kind in three weeks, and the second of them is the one that finishes it. There is no difference between a woman who takes a thing off a board and a woman who puts one back except which way her hand went, and I have spent eleven years on a floor where my hand has gone one way or the other and never once for me, and it has gone twice in three weeks now and both of the times it was for me.

And the woman at the near end of the boards did not look. That is the whole of it and it is the arrangement and I have had it since I came here. She has not asked me one thing in two years and she has not wanted one single thing out of me in two years, and I could have done that at the first hour this morning with a door shut behind me and nobody in that room. She came in twenty minutes early as she does, and she is still on the floor at the third hour, and I cannot be shown to have wanted that and I cannot be shown to have not wanted it, and neither can she.

---

The fourth of those four items is on that board in the hand that put it up about four years ago. The place behind it has gone the colour of the rest of that board again, and about four hundred blanks a year come out of the case, and the box under the far end of the boards is shut, and the woman of twenty-four was still on the floor at the third hour and had not looked at that wall once.

There is a man at the top of the stair with a satchel on his shoulder and nobody in nine hundred buildings has ever asked him what is in it, and he went down those four flights this morning with the strap mark still on that shoulder above the neck, and nobody stopped him, and nobody sent for him, and nobody has said one word to anybody about any part of this morning on the floor or off it.
