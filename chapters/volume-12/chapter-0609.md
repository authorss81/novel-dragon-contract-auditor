# Chapter 0609: The Fourth Item Put Back Onto That Board, And Two Women On One Floor Not Looking

She had taken the fourth of the four items off that board about ten days ago and put it in the front of her apron, and this morning she put it back.

It is the fourth day of the third week of the sixth month of the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is between the first hour and about the third.

---

She wrote the fourth of the four items on that wall herself about four years ago and it spent ten days in the front of her apron in between. About four hundred blanks a year come out of a case at the near end of those boards. A different box is shut under the far end of them and has been for about six years, and the drawer under that wall has been shut for eleven years and holds four things and two of them have never been opened. None of the four items on that wall is time.

She is thirty-four. She has been at that end of that floor eleven years and is the oldest of the three that work at it.

---

The woman of twenty-four was at that end of the room with her book open and her hands flat on it, and she was in early, twenty minutes, as she has been every working morning for two years.

A man came up those four flights at about the first hour with a satchel over his shoulder and stopped about a yard off that case with the strap still round his forearm, and he did not put it down and he did not turn round.

She put the fourth item back onto that board and squared it against the top of it with the heel of her hand.

The woman of twenty-four did not look. She worked at the far side of that case with a book open in front of her and she did not look along those boards at all that morning, and she stayed at that floor after the bag went down the four flights at about a quarter past, which is a thing she has not done since before the spring that is gone.

---

**I took the fourth of those four items off that wall ten days ago because I wanted it off, and wanting is the whole of what I have not done on this floor in eleven years, and I have never once wanted one single thing out of anybody who works at it, and that has been the arrangement, and there is nothing in that arrangement for anybody else in nine hundred buildings and there is everything in it for me.**

Three items on a board and a bare patch where the fourth stood is a thing a person can be shown. A woman who has taken something down can be shown to have wanted it down and can be shown not to have wanted it up and can be shown to have said nothing about it for four years, and there is nothing in nine hundred buildings that enters a woman as the one who put it there in the first place or as the one who took it off.

And I put it back this morning and that is a second thing of the same kind in ten days, and the second of them is the one that finishes it. There is no difference between a woman who takes a thing off a board and a woman who puts one back except which way her hand went, and I have spent eleven years on a floor where my hand has gone one way or the other and never once for me, and it has gone twice in a fortnight now and both of the times it was for me.

And the woman at the near end of those boards did not look. That is the whole of it and it is the arrangement and I have had it since I came to that floor. She has not asked me one thing in two years and she has not wanted one single thing out of me in two years, and I could have done that at the first hour this morning with a door shut behind me and nobody in that room. She came in twenty minutes early as she does, and she is still on that floor at the third hour, and I cannot be shown to have wanted that and I cannot be shown to have not wanted it, and neither can she.

---

The fourth of those four items is on that board in the hand that put it up about four years ago. The place behind it has gone the colour of the rest of that board again, and about four hundred blanks a year come out of that case, and the box under the far end of those boards is shut, and the woman of twenty-four was still on that floor at the third hour and had not looked at that wall once.
