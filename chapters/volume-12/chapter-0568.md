# Chapter 0568: A Landing With A Mat On It That Is Not Hers, And A Coin Under A Door She Goes Up For

She had begun lifting that tin with her knee instead of with both hands, and she had not worked out why, and she had decided not to work out why.

It is the second day of the second week of the eighth month of the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is between the first hour and about the fourth.

---

Twenty years at the foot of that stair and not one morning in the whole of them on the third tread, and the third tread is as good a place to stand as any other and has never once been stood on.

A rent comes once a week out of a hand and goes into her own and has never been written down anywhere in that house. She has never given anybody a receipt for anything in the whole of it, and there is a number cut into the back of a key that is the whole of what that building has ever had to say about anybody sleeping in it.

Whatever opens the top of that house is not on the board by that door and it is not in the tin either, and her hand has not gone after it since a spring that is gone.
The flight has a stone tread on the bottom of it and the second one above it is stone as well, and everything above the second is a tread that has been walked on by nobody in twenty years and holds the dust of the top of the house along the middle of it. There is a back room at the back of the ground floor with boards in it and a boiler standing at the far end, and the whole floor is done from the back first and the front second.

---

A coin comes under the door on the floor above the second stair at about the first hour, and it is not the same money as the week's money and it does not go into the same place, and it goes under a door and not into a hand.

She has been putting that coin under that door for about two years.

There is a mat on that landing.
She went up the stair at the rate she goes up it with a coin in her hand, and the whole flight was the same as it has been every working morning for about two years, except that there was a mat at the top of it now and there had not been one the last time, and a landing with nothing on it is not a thing anybody looks at twice.

---

She came up at about the first hour and the mat was square and flat with its near corner up about two inches where the boards have lifted at the joint, and it has been up more often than not for about a fortnight, and she stepped over it, and she got her hand down and pushed the coin under the door with the flats of two fingers, and she stepped back over it and went down.
The corner of the mat was up under her own boot going up and down under it coming back, and she did not put her hand out and flatten it, which she has not done on any morning of the three weeks before this one either.

That was the whole of that for about three weeks and she did not look down at the mat while she did it and she has not looked down at it once since.

On this morning she looked down at it.

---

Nobody can say where that mat came from.

That is not a finding, it is what a mat is. She has been at the foot of that stair for twenty years and there was never one foot of anything on that landing, and there is a mat on it now, and it is the right size for a landing and it is the wrong colour for a man who has never done anything with a room.

She has not seen him carry it. He is a door with a coin behind it and the sound of his own floor and about two years of nothing, and he has not come down that stair in all that time, and she has not been up past the second of those stairs in all that time. There are two floors and twenty years between them, and there have been three mornings in about a month on which the two of them have been on that landing at the same time, and not one of the three was about the mat, and there is nothing in this house that could show her up those stairs or show him down them.
She knows the sound of the door and she knows the sound of the boards on the other side of it, and she has never once had both at once, and she has worked out that a person on the other side of a door at the first hour is a person who is not coming in, which is a different thing from a person who is not there.

She did not stand on that landing for a quarter of an hour. She went back down.

---

**There is a mat on the landing of a man who has not come down that stair once in about two years, and he put it there, and I step over it at about the first hour of every working morning of my life, and nobody in this building is ever going to be told that I know it is his, and I am not going to ask him about it, and he is not going to come down and find out whether I know. And if he were not behind that door this would be a mat that came from nowhere and I would have gone down that stair at the first hour the way I have gone down it for twenty years, and the whole of what I have got this morning is a man on the other side of a door who is not coming out of it.**

He does not open that door. She has not knocked and she has never knocked and she is not going to, and a knock is the only thing in this empire that would put two people on a landing together and she is not going to spend it on a mat.
She has wanted to put one foot in his room for about three weeks and has not done it, and there is a difference between a woman who knocks and a woman who is stood on a landing at the first hour and does not, and the difference is a door that stays shut and neither of them being able to say afterward which of them did what. And there is nothing at the other end of the door she has been told about by anybody, and a woman who has not been told anything about a thing is not going to go and look at it.

---

The woman of about thirty-five came down that lane at about the third hour and went through the passage and stopped on the step below the turn, and the tin was on the top step where it is always on the top step.

"It has not moved."

"It has not moved."

"It has not moved since before the middle of that month and the sheets were dry at the back of it."

She had the cloth in her hand and she had been at the boards by that door and she did not turn round.
She came along the boards of the passage with the cloth in her hand the way she has come along them every morning since before she came, and there is about as long as it takes to fold a sheet between the front door and the step at the turn and she used the whole of it at the rate she uses everything.

"I have not come up for the mat," she said, without turning round. "I have not come up for the mat for three weeks now, and that is the last of the mat."

---

She stayed on the step below the turn for about as long as it takes to fold a sheet flat and fold it back.

"There is a mat on your landing," she said.

"That is a thing a man puts on a landing."

"That is not a thing you put on a landing."

"No. It is the landing's now, and it was the landing's by the fourth morning, and you did not have to be told that by anybody."
She said it without turning round and her hand stayed down on the cloth while she said it, and the other one took it the way a person takes a thing that is about herself and not about the person saying it, and neither of them looked at the other one at any point in it. Nobody in the house was going to be told that the woman of thirty-five knew about a mat on a landing she had never been up to, and that was two people in one passage again and neither of them can be shown to have been in it.

And she carried on down that passage after that and did not stop, and she is not going to be asked about the mat or the tin or the wet boards or any part of it, and there is nowhere standing on that lane at which either of them could.

---

The mat is on that landing and it is going to be there at about the first hour tomorrow, and there is a coin that goes under that door every working morning and it is not a receipt for anything and there has never been one for anything in twenty years.

Nobody in that house is ever going to find out that she knows it is his, and that is the whole of what three weeks of a mat has come to.
