# Chapter 0558: The Room At The Back Of That Floor, And A Woman Who Dusts Round A Mark And Does Not Wipe It Out

She had been dusting that shelf every morning for about a fortnight and going round a mark in the dust about the size of one question, and she had not wiped the mark out, and on the fourth day of that run a girl of nineteen came into that room with an armful and found her standing at it with a cloth.

It is the fourth day of the fourth week of the fifth month of the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is about the fourth hour.

---

Nineteen years of that book and she has been the only person in it. Nothing has been put to that room in about four years. She came out of that door four times in about a year, and every one of the four cost her something, and there is nothing anywhere in this empire that any of them can be entered as.

The window over that shelf is about two foot wide and the pane has been out of it for about four years. The sill takes the weather straight on. There is one thing on that shelf and it has been there about eleven years, and it carries no full stop at the end of it, and her own name is at the foot of it in her own hand.

She put it there on purpose. That is the whole of what that thing has ever been for.

She wanted a question in a room that somebody could come in and ask, because she has not been asked it in about eleven years and she has said out loud, more than once, that she wants it asked, and she is going to go on saying it and she is never going to be asked it, and nobody in that building has ever picked it up.

---

A question with a name at the foot of it on a shelf in a room with a shut door is not a question anybody can walk through. It is a name in a room.

She worked that out about eleven years ago, about as fast as it takes a sheet to be squared, and she has put the door of that room open about nine mornings out of twelve for a month, and in a month about one person has come through it, and that one person was nineteen and has not said one word about that shelf and is never going to be asked about it.

---

The shelf itself has not been done in about eleven years. The paper on it has gone the colour of the stone and the dust on that shelf has gone the colour of the paper, and there is a mark in it about the size of one thing that has not moved because nothing has ever taken it off.

She started on it about a fortnight ago and she found that the dust came away off the board in a grey roll and that the mark stayed, and that a mark left in dust is the only thing in this empire that will keep a question exactly where it was for eleven years by accident rather than on purpose.

So she goes round it. She has worked out that a woman who wipes the mark out has admitted that there is a mark, and that a woman who leaves it has admitted nothing, and that the only difference between those two women and a woman who has never touched the shelf is about a fortnight old and cannot be seen by anybody who was not in that room.

She goes round it with a cloth every morning and she has not once put her hand on the question and she is not going to.

---

**I have wanted that thing asked in a room for about eleven years and I have said so out loud to people who were not going to ask me, and I have said so twice. I put my own name under it on purpose so that I would be a door in that room somebody could walk through, and it is not a door, it is a name, and I have known that for eleven years. I dusted the shelf it is standing on this fortnight because it is the only thing in that room I am still allowed to do.**

The girl came in with an armful at about the fourth hour and put it on the table and did not look at the shelf and did not look at the woman either and she was in that room for about as long as it takes to put a stack down and come out again.

Neither of them said one word.

She has been under her four years and has gone down that corridor both ways forty times on a working morning. She said one true thing out loud in it about a month ago and nobody answered her, and the one thing she had that nobody could take off her was never being asked anything. She let that go. There is no form anywhere in this empire by which not being asked is put back.

So the girl is not going to ask her what she was doing at a shelf with a cloth in her hand. And the woman of fifty-three is not going to say, because saying it is asking, and she has waited eleven years for somebody to ask her and she is not going to do it herself on a working afternoon with an armful on her table.

---

The armful went out again at about the next hour and the door of that room stood open behind it and nobody in that building was told anything.

She went back to the shelf at about the fifth hour and went round the mark again with the cloth. The mark is sharper than it was a fortnight ago, because a mark in dust that has been gone round with a cloth two dozen times is a cleaner mark than one that has been left alone for eleven years, and there is not one person in nine hundred buildings who could be shown having made it.

The four who cannot make sense of a paragraph are four and none of them has ever been asked one question about a number. The woman of fifty-three keeps minutes and is not one of them. The girl of nineteen is not one of them. Neither is the man at the end of that corridor, who reads the top of every sheet before he signs the foot of it, and has been putting his other hand into that pot for about two months, and cannot account for one morning of it, and has not been asked.

She is not going to be asked either, and that question is going to stay on that shelf with no full stop on it and her name at the foot of it, and it is going to be there at the sixth hour and it is going to be there next week, and nobody is ever going to be thanked for a shelf.
