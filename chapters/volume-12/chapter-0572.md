# Chapter 0572: The Front Boards Of That Ground Floor Not Done For Five Weeks, And A Woman Standing In An Empty Passage

She had said one true thing out loud in that passage in the third week of the seventh month, and standing on the step below the turn she had decided she would do it again every week after that, and for a fortnight running she had done it to no room at all, and this was the first week she had come down that lane to try.

It is the second day of the second week of the ninth month of the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is between the third hour and about the sixth.

---

The lane is about as long as that table and it comes down onto the passage at the step at the front door and the passage runs the same again to the two steps and the turn, and the tin is on the top step at the turn, and the top step is bare.

Her own paper is not on it. She has had it under her arm in a fold since the third week of the seventh month, which is a thing she did on a step and not a thing she has come back for.

The boards at the front of that ground floor have not been done for five weeks.

There is a bloom on them, and a bloom is grey and comes up out of a board that has not been touched, and about two inches apart down the length of that front there are the marks of a cloth going over the same boards a hundred times a week, and there has not been one new mark in five weeks.

---

She came down the lane at about the third hour and went along the passage and stood on the step below the turn.

The passage is about as long as a table. You can see the whole of it from the step at the turn and the whole of it from the step at the front door, and there was nothing in it.

She stood there with her arms down for about as long as it takes to square a sheet and then square it back.

There is a woman at the bottom of that stair who has been at the bottom of it for about twenty years and who cleans the boards of that ground floor and nothing else in it, and there is a tin on that step with about nine years of one man's rent in it, and neither of those two things was in the passage this morning.

---

**I came down that lane for the twelfth week to be in a room with a person in it who did not want one single thing out of me, and there is no room, and I am going to have to go on coming down this lane for as long as that house stands.**

There is nothing wrong with the passage. It is dry, it is warm, it is about as long as a table, and a person standing on the step below the turn is in the whole of it and cannot be anywhere else. She had worked that out on the way up the lane in the third week of the seventh month and she has not stopped working it out since.

What has changed is not the passage. What has changed is that there is a person who used to be at the front of that ground floor with a cloth in her hand at that hour, and she has not been there for five weeks, and the boards say so, and the tin is the only thing in that passage that is exactly where it has always been.

She could have said it to the tin. It stands there with a lid shut straight down on it and about nine years of a man's money in it and it has never once been on paper and it is not a person and it does not want anything, and a sentence said to that tin would be a sentence said to a lid and a tin of about nine years, and she has had a room at the back of her own house for about a fortnight and she knows exactly what that is worth.

So she did not say it.

---

The woman at the bottom of that stair came along the back of that ground floor at about the fifth hour with the cloth in her hand and did not come into that passage at all.

She was at the far end of it, past the boiler, where there are more boards, and she worked them there at the rate she works everything, and she came back along the boards and went out at the front door with the cloth, and she was on the step at the front door for about as long as it takes a cloth to be folded once.

She did not come up the two steps. She did not come to the turn. She put her hand on the frame of that door and looked out along the lane and then she went along the passage with the cloth and out, and the sound of that door shut about as long ago as that happens.

---

Those front boards have a grey bloom on them and the last marks on them are five weeks old and they are about two inches apart and they are the only record in that house that the woman who keeps that floor has changed where she keeps it.

No book in this city has a line in it for a woman who has left a floor alone, and none is going to get one, and she is going to go on coming down that lane every week and she is never going to find out why those boards are not done.

The tin is on the top step at the turn with the lid flat on it, and it is going to be on that step when neither of them is in that house.
