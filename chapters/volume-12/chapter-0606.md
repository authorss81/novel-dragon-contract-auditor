# Chapter 0606: The Fourth Item Taken Down Off A Wall Board, And A Man With A Bag Who Turned Round

She had put two fingers on the edge of the fourth of the four items on that wall about six weeks ago and taken them off again, and this morning she took the whole of it off the wall and put it in the front of her apron.

It is the fourth day of the fourth week of the fifth month of the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is about the fifth hour.

---

The fourth of the four items on that wall is in her own hand and it has been on that board about four years. In eleven years on that floor she has gone by the wall about three thousand times, and nobody has ever put it to her and she has not put it to herself. Not one of the four items on that board is time. A case stands at the near end of the boards and gives up about four hundred blanks a year, and a different box is shut under the far end of them, and that one has not been opened in about six years and nobody has ever counted what is in it. Under the wall there is a drawer, and it stays shut.

The board itself is soft and dark and has had four things put on it and four things taken off it without anybody asking it either, and the four of them are not in a line. Two of them are near the top and one is low down at the end nearest the door and the fourth was in between. There is nothing in the way they sit that anybody in this building has ever been able to make into a list.

That case at the near end has a lid that goes up at the back and does not go down while anybody is at it. It gives out its day early and the day comes out of it in a stack that is squared at the edges and soft at the corners from being carried. About four hundred of them come out of it in a year and they go down four flights inside somebody's coat.

The box under the far end is a different thing from the case and it is a different size and it is a different colour, and it is shut, and the lid of it has not been off in about six years, and nobody on the floor has counted what is in it and nobody has asked. The drawer under the wall has been shut for eleven years and there are four things in it and two of them have never been opened.

She is thirty-four, and she is not a lawyer, and there have been four true things said out loud on the floor in eleven years, and not one of the four has had a question put back to it, and the fifth is not going to happen.

---

A man came up those four flights at about the first hour, and the satchel stayed on his shoulder where it has stayed for about a month now, and he halted the length of a room off that wall with the strap still round his forearm.

He puts the strap over and then through and takes his arm out and puts it back through and does it twice before he is satisfied, which takes him about the length of a breath. He does not look down the room. He looks at the case and he looks at the boards and he looks at his own boots, and the strap has cut a place on that shoulder above the neck where it goes in every week and the place is the colour of the rest of him again by the time he has the bag settled.

She took the fourth item off that board and put it in the front of her apron while he was standing there.

He turned round.

He had never once turned round on that floor. He had a mark on that shoulder above the neck where the strap goes in every week and he has been going up those stairs four times a week for eleven years and has never once been told what is in that bag, and this morning he turned round and looked at the wall for about as long as it takes to square a sheet.

"There is a clean place on that board."

"There is."

"That is four weeks of your going past it and about three days of you not going past it."

"I have not asked you to count my weeks."

"I have not asked you anything."

---

**I have said out loud in this room, four times in eleven years, the shape of a thing, and I have never once wanted one single thing out of anybody who works on it, and the arrangement I have is the oldest one I have got. There is nobody in nine hundred buildings who would get one thing out of that arrangement, and it is the whole of what I have got, and this morning I took the fourth of those four items off that wall while a man was standing nine foot off it.**

He came up at the first hour and he has not put that bag down for about a month and I have watched him not put it down from the other side of the case. I have wanted one true sentence in the room for about a month and I have not said it, because he has a mark on that shoulder and that mark is the whole of what eleven years has left him that anybody could ever be shown, and I was not going to be the one who took that off him so that a sentence of mine got said.

And then I took the thing off the wall, and he turned round.

Nobody in this building has ever wanted one thing out of him. That is what he has. He has gone up four flights four times a week for eleven years with a thing put into his hands at the bottom of them and he has never once been told what was in it, and nobody waits on him, and a person who nobody waits on can be shown absolutely nothing. And this morning a woman took a piece of paper off a wall nine foot in front of him and he turned round and looked at it, and there is nothing anywhere in this empire that can put that back.

I have not said one word to him about what that item is. I am not going to. The fourth of those four items is in the front of my apron and it is not going into the drawer and it is not going out of the room, and a man with a bag on his shoulder went down those four flights at a quarter past without one word said on any floor.

---

At the near end of the boards the woman of twenty-four had her book open and her hands empty, and she stayed on the far side of the case for the whole of the rest of that hour with her face away, and she did not turn round either.

She came in twenty minutes early as she comes in every working morning and she got her book open before anybody else was on the floor, and the pen lies in the middle of that book where it has lain for two years, and she put her hands flat on the open pages and kept them there while the whole of it happened at the other end of the room.

At about a quarter past the satchel went over that man's shoulder and he went out and down those four flights, and the fourth of them has a hollow in it, and there is a clean place on that wall about the size of a hand where the fourth of the four items was, and a board with three items on it is a thing a person can be shown, and a man at nine foot turned round and looked at it.
