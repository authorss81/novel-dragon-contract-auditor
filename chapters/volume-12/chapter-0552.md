# Chapter 0552: The Room At The Back Of That Floor, And A Girl Of Nineteen Who Goes In And Does Not Take It Off The Shelf

She had stopped going into the room at the back of that floor about a fortnight ago and this morning she went in and wrote out a week of minutes in her own hand because the second copy had not gone in for a fortnight and it was hers to do.

It is the second day of the second week of the fourth month of the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is between the second hour and about the fifth.

---

There is a sill at the near end of that corridor and a pot on it with two pens in it, and the two pens are the same pen, and it takes one out and hands it straight back and has never once had an opinion about which of them went in.

He has been putting his left hand into it for about two months and has not worked out one morning out of it and has not been asked, and nobody on that floor could tell you which hand of his went into that pot this morning and nobody is going to be told.

Nine hundred of those sheets go out of that building in a year and every one of them carries his mark at the foot, and a handful of them in every year carry the wrong writing above that mark, and the only man alive who could name them is the man who puts the mark there, and it is on nothing at all. He reads the head of every sheet before he signs the foot of it. That is the whole of the care he has ever added to anything.

He is not one of the four who cannot make sense of a paragraph. Neither is the woman of fifty-three at the back of that floor, and the reader at the end of a lane nine miles off is not one of them either, and none of those four has ever been asked one question about a number and none of them is going to be.

---

She had worked the second half of it out standing at that sill about a fortnight ago, and it took her about as long as it takes an armful to go down a corridor.

A person who stands where things are said cannot afterwards be shown to have been somewhere they were not.

That is all it is. It is not a trade and it is not a favour and it is not going to be thanked. There is not one form anywhere in this empire in which a person is entered as the one who heard a thing, and there is not one either in which the same person is entered as the one who did not hear it, and a girl of nineteen who has worked that out has two of nothing. She gave up the only thing she had to get it, which was that nobody in that building had ever wanted one single thing out of her.

She had been not being asked since she came there and she had let that go in a month that is gone.

So she stopped going into the room at the back of that floor, and she went on carrying the armfuls past that sill about forty times in each direction in every working day, and nothing in that building was different by it.

---

This morning she went in.

The woman of fifty-three was in there. She is fifty-three and she keeps those minutes and has kept them nineteen years. She is the only person who has ever been in that book, and that room has had nothing put to it in about four years.

"The second copy has not gone in for a fortnight," the younger one said.

"I know it has not. I am not going to do it for you and you are not going to do it for me and there is no reason in the world why either of us should."

"I am not doing it for you."

"Then do it the way it is done and put the day down and do not say one word about the other six days."

She did it the way it is done.

---

There is about four feet between the table and the shelf, and the shelf is under a window about two foot wide that has had no pane in it for about four years and takes the weather straight on, and the paper on that shelf has gone the colour of the stone.

There is one thing on it and there has been one thing on it for about eleven years and nobody in that building has ever taken it up.

She was in that room with the woman of fifty-three about four foot off her and she looked at it for about as long as it takes to write a day down, and she did not touch it, and she was not asked about it, and she is not going to be asked about it and would not answer if she were, which is a thing she worked out in about two seconds flat.

If she touched it she would be a person who had read it. If she did not touch it she would be a person who had stood next to it. Neither of those is a thing that can be put anywhere and both of them are the same absence, and she has worked out in the month she has been in that building that the cheapest thing in any building is to be nobody in particular, and she was somebody in particular in that room for about as long as it takes to write a day down and she cannot get it back.

---

**Nobody has picked it up in about eleven years. She stood in front of it this morning with an armful in her arms and did not pick it up either, and she is nineteen, and nobody is going to be thanked for a fortnight of mornings or for one morning. A question on a shelf with a name at the foot of it is the only object in this empire that has been put down where somebody could pick it up by a person who wanted it picked up.**

She put the bundle of the day's sheets on the sill and not on that shelf.

She did that on purpose and she stood there afterwards with her hand flat on the wood trying to find out why she had done it, and she found it: if she had put it on the shelf she would have been standing with her armful in the way of a thing that had been there for eleven years, and she is not going to be a person who stood in the way of it.

She did not tell the woman of fifty-three any of that and she is not going to and she is not going to be thanked for it.

The minutes went in and the pen went back into the tin and the sill went on being a sill, and the woman of fifty-three kept on writing.

The door of that room has been standing open about nine mornings out of twelve for a fortnight and about one person has come through it in that time. That one person is nineteen, and she went back down that corridor at the same speed she came up it, and she did not take anything off that shelf. Nobody in that building is going to be told she was in there and she is not going to be asked about it and she would not answer it if she were.
