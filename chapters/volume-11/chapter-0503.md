# Chapter 0503: A Hand Flat On The Outside Of A Coat, A Door That Was Not Opened, And A Person Nine Miles Off Who Was Not Asked Anything

The room is taken by the week two streets back from a street that runs down to the river road, and it is the same room it was a fortnight ago, and the landlord has never once been inside the door of it.

Marek Kest. Thirty-eight. Not one line written against that name in any register this empire keeps, and none is going to be written, and a bar of his own came off him about a year ago on an application of one line which he wrote himself. You cannot take back a rank that was never granted or withdraw a certification that was never issued, so a man in his position has nothing to surrender, and that has been the whole of it since before the fire that his want goes back further than.

On the inside of his left wrist there is a mark about three inches long. It reads nothing, it decides nothing, and it does not know where the ford is, and it is not the price of anything.

It is the second day of the first week of the fourth month of the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is the morning.

---

The coat is on the nail and the nail went in crooked about the height of his hand about two years ago, and for about a year and a half of that time it carried nothing at all.

There are two things in the inside pocket of it, one on the left of the seam and one on the right. One of them is a leaf, and a line has been struck through whatever stood at the foot of that line on it, and it came out of a bundle folded twice, and the crease a third of the way down it is worn through. The other is a piece of paper with a strip printed along the top of it and four things set along that strip, and not one of those four is anybody's name, and it came out of a case at the top of four flights for fourpence on a wet morning about two years ago and not about a month. Neither of the two has been out of that pocket in this building or in any other. Not one person in this city knows he is carrying both of them. Nobody has ever looked in the coat and nobody ever is going to.

About four feet of bare wall is beside the window and there is nothing on any part of it and there is nothing in that room that makes heat and nothing is going to this month.

---

He got through the middle of the morning with nothing decided, which is the ordinary shape of his working life and has been since a morning a long time ago.

Somebody put a hand on the door at about the fourth hour and said through it that there is a woman two floors down in that house who has a thing about the window in that room, that the thing has been going on for about a month, and that she would put it in writing if anybody in the building would stand to it.

He did not open the door.

He stood in the middle of that floor and let the hand go, and the hand went, and the stair did what a stair does. Nobody put one thing to him, and nobody asked him anything, and there is no form anywhere in this empire in which a man in a room on a weekly bill is entered as the one who was asked about his window, and the woman two floors down has a thing about a window and there is nowhere in this empire for her to put it, and she is not going to be thanked for it either.

That is the arrangement. It is not a decision he has made this month and it is not a decision he made this morning. It is what there is left of him once every kind of standing has been taken off him, and there is not one standing left, and no building in this empire will ever print a heading over him.

---

Then he worked out a thing about the way this matter is held together, and he worked it out in his own mouth, and it is the first time in eleven volumes of it that it has been worked out and said.

"**A person who is in a room and is not in a room is the only thing in this matter that cannot be shown.**"

He said that to the plaster beside the window, out loud, at about the volume a sheet takes to be signed.

"**A person in a room has not been asked to be in it. They cannot be shown having agreed to anything and they cannot be shown having refused anything, and nobody can put them down afterwards and ask what it was for. No column in this empire will ever carry the fact of a person having stood in a room. I have spent ten volumes being a person who cannot be shown, and I have spent them being the cheapest man in this matter, and I did not know until this morning that the two are the same arrangement, and it is not a thing anybody had to do to me.**"

He put his hand flat on the outside of the pocket and left it there.

"**And here is the other edge and I am not going to do anything with it. There is a woman of about nineteen nine miles off who keeps the minutes under a woman of about fifty-three, and she said a true thing out loud in a corridor of that building about a month ago, and it was that everything that man signs, somebody is holding, and that a second of any one of them is somebody holding his mark twice. She was not answered. Nobody answered her and nobody is going to, and the not being asked was the only thing she had that was hers, and she has let that go this month, and nobody in this empire has a form for handing it back.**"

---

He did not go and look, and he did not ask, and he is not going to.

That is not a principle. He has not got a principle and he is not going to get one, and the reason is the plainest thing he owns: a man who goes and looks is a man who has been shown something, and a man who has been shown something is a party, and a party is the only thing in this matter that can be told what a thing is going to be used for. He has nothing behind him. That is not humility and it is not a rule he made and it is not going to be written down anywhere.

He does not go up the four flights. He has been to the top of that stair six times in his life and has come away with nothing in his hands on every one of the six, and about four hundred people a year come down those same flights carrying a different object altogether, and a woman of twenty-four has been selling paper at the top of them for two years and nobody has ever wanted one single thing out of her.

He did not go up them this month and he is not going to.

---

About the middle of the afternoon he took the coat off the nail and put it over the back of the chair, which is the only thing in that room the coat has ever been on since the evening it went up, and he had his hand inside the pocket and his hand outside the pocket and he took nothing out of it.

The crease a third of the way down the leaf is worn through and the leaf is where it was and the four things printed along the top of the other one are where they have always been, and they are on opposite sides, and they have not moved since a wet morning about two years ago.

What belonged at the foot of that line is gone, and the hand that went through it was not his mother's. The claim that went out of this empire with that name went the same way on the same day, and he holds one of those two things and has never held the other, and neither of them is coming this month. He did not say one word about it out loud. There is no word in this empire that goes with what he would have to say if he opened his mouth about it, and a room cannot be asked, and a room is the only party in this matter that has ever received anything from him.

He hung the coat back on the nail.

---

He went down the street in the ordinary way at about the fifth hour, and he did not buy anything, and he did not go to the top of any stair, and he did not go to the end of the lane.

The four feet of bare wall was still bare and the nail was still in it and the coat was still on it, and nobody was thanked and nobody was sent for, and nothing about it is written down anywhere in this empire.
