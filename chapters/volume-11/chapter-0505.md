# Chapter 0505: A Rack Coming Down That Run With Nothing On It For The Sixth Time, And The Part Of A Sentence He Is Not Going To Say

About four hundred yards of cold flags run between two locks in this matter, and she is at one end of them and has been at that end for the better part of four years. The lock at the other end is not hers, it belongs to somebody else, and nothing has been stood at it since before she came to that building.

She is about twenty-six. No roll in that building has ever carried her name and she has never been one of its clerks. In the whole of her four years at that end, not one person in it has wanted anything out of her, not one has put anything into her hands, and not one has told her a single thing.

The four hundred yards is not walked in this month by anybody in this matter, and the room with the rail at the far end of it is not entered and is not described, and the person who is at the far end of four hundred yards of cold flags is not in this passage and has not been in it for four volumes and is not going to be.

It is the fourth day of the third week of the fourth month of the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is about the fourth hour.

---

The flags go the length of that run and they are bedded in the way flags are bedded where they have not been lifted for a long time, and the cold comes up out of them and goes through the sole of a boot and then stops, and after that it is not cold any more, it is a thing a person is standing in. At the near end of the flags there is a bar and a housing to take it into, and the wall behind it is a wall with nothing on it to look at and no light on it until the hour is late.

He came down out of that cold at about that hour with the rack over his hip and nothing riding on it, and it was the sixth time in a fortnight, and on five of the six it had been the same.

Forty years of a run teaches a man where it is under his own feet and he does not look down at it. The only noise in the whole of it was his boots and a smaller one the prongs made in the stone. He had not been within about nine foot of her before he was beside her, and he never is. There is a stretch of it at the near end where a person can hear a man coming and still has the time to decide, and she has never once needed it.

"**Nothing has come down that run and I have walked it six times with an empty rack behind me, and noticing that earns you nothing, and neither does telling you.**"

"I stand at the end of it," she said.

"I know where you stand."

She has three questions and she is not going to put one of them to anybody, and nobody is going to put one to her. There is nobody in that building she could ask and nobody anywhere who would answer. One of the three is about the second half of what he said at her back in a month that is gone, and she is not going to ask for that one either, and she is not owed an answer to it by anybody.

---

He put the rack down against the wall where it goes, which is the first time in four years he has put it down at her end instead of carrying it back up.

It stood up on its own edge against the stone with the four prongs on the ground and nothing on them, and it took up less room standing there than it did on a hip, and a thing that has been carried four years knows the angle it is carried at and does not like being set down. He had it steady with one boot. Then he took his hand off the strap and put the hand into the front of the apron and left it in there, which is where it goes when there is nothing else to do with it.

"**Here is the arrangement and not the second half, and I am giving you the arrangement because you are the person it is about and because giving it to you is the only thing I have got and I am not going to thank you for having to stand here.**"

The flags did what they do.

"**A person who cannot be shown anything can be given anything. That has not changed in four years and it is not going to. The fourpence is not the reason and I have had about a month to work that out and the fourpence is not the reason. The reason is that there is a thing in the world now that can be put in front of a person, and a person can be put in front of it without being asked one single thing about it.**"

She did not move.

"You have just told me the cheapest person in that building can be used," she said.

"I have told you the cheapest person in that building can be used, and I have told it to you and not to the flags, and that is the whole of what I have done and I have not thanked you for it and you are not going to thank me."

---

"I am not going to make a rule about one," he said, before she had got as far as wanting one. "**I settled that in my own head a month that is gone and I said out loud at the time that I had settled it so that somebody would know I had settled it, and nothing has come down that run since to make me settle it again, and I am not going to settle it again this month.**"

"That is the only thing you have ever said to me that was for my own good."

"It is the only thing I have ever said to you at all, and you have never once asked me for a second one, and that is not going to change and it is not a courtesy between the two of us and it never has been."

---

Then he said the thing about the room, and he said it to the wall above the lock rather than to her, and he said it once.

"**I have been forty years up and down that stone four times a day and I have never once stood in a room where a sentence was said in it, and that has been the whole of what I had, and I want you to know that I know what it was. You put me in one in a month that is gone and you did not do it on purpose and that is why it worked.**"

She looked at him for the first time in about four years and then she looked back at the lock.

"**And I am not going to tell you the second half. That is still mine and it is still where I put it, and it goes nine foot up those flags and the rest of it goes down that passage, and it is not yours and you have not asked for it and I would not give it to you if you did.**"

---

The second half of that sentence is not the half that concerns her.

She worked that out standing at that lock with her hands on the bar, and it took the length of time a rack takes to come up and go back, and she worked it out and she did not say it out loud, and it is the first thing she has worked out in four years at that end.

The bar was cold under her hands and had been cold every morning of the four years. She had her face to the wall and her back to the cold, and the cold is the side he comes down from, so that he has the last nine foot of the run to find her in and not a step more than that. Working a thing out standing is slower than working it out sitting down, and it does not go any faster for being the first thing in four years. She had the length of one rack coming up and going back and then she had it. Nobody in that building would have been the person she put any of it in front of.

The half that concerns her is the one he has not said. He has said the arrangement and the arrangement is for a person like him, who goes up and down four times a day and is in every room he passes, and the arrangement says such a person can be put in front of anything. **Her half is not that. Her half is that a person who is at the end of a run where something is said is in the room when it is said, and that is all, and there is nothing about it that anybody would have to be asked for, and there is no form anywhere in this empire in which she could be entered as the one who was standing at the end of that run.**

And the way it would reach her is not through him, because he is not going to say it and nobody can be made to. It would reach her by her being at the end of a run. That is all it would take. That is the entire requirement, and it is not a requirement, and it costs nothing at all, and it cannot be stopped by anybody who is not in the passage.

---

Nothing came down that run that week or the week before it or the week after that one.

The not coming is not a withholding. There is no form anywhere in this empire in which a lock is entered as a person who declined to carry something, and a man with an empty rack coming down a passage four times is not a rule and not a statement and not on anything.

The racks went down and came back light by one, and the lock was shut at the hour it is shut at. There is a figure in her head that has been there since the spring of her fourth year. It is on no paper and it went up out of that building once without ever touching anything, and there is nobody in this empire who could put a printed word over the top of it, and it is not going to be compared with a mark in the top edge of anything anybody else has ever done.

She is the cheapest person in this city and a man in a felt apron has said so out loud to her face in about four years, and nobody thanked her for it and nobody is going to. Being cheapest is the reason she cannot be reached, and it is also the reason she can be used, and those two are one fact. It has taken a man in a felt apron forty years to arrive at, and he has arrived at it out loud, and he is not going to say the rest of it and she is not going to ask.
