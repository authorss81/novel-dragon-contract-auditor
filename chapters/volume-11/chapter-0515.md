# Chapter 0515: A Satchel Set Down On Wood At The Far End Of Those Boards, Eleven Years Of Four Times A Week, And A Woman At The Other End Of The Room Not Turning Round

That floor is reached up one stair and by no other route, and it is open from the second hour until the sixth, when the case goes away behind the boards and the four go up over it on their own nails. The fourth of those four is the one nobody at those boards can account for, and it has been in the same hand for about four years.

Marn Ottery is thirty-four, has been at the far end of them for eleven years, and has never once in that time left that floor. She has put the shape of a thing out loud four times. She announced in advance that there would not be a fifth, and ten weeks have not been a reason since and neither has this month.

Tamsin Rook is twenty-four and is at her own end with her back to the room and an edge under her hands.

It is the second day of the first week of the seventh month of the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is the fourth hour.

---

He came up that last flight and stopped on the fourth step from the top, the way he has stopped there every week for eleven years, and then finished the stair and set the bag down on the wood at that end.

Four times that month. Nobody in this building has ever told him what is in it and he has never once asked and it has never been a courtesy between him and anybody on this floor and it is not going to become one this month.

He stood with his shoulder to the room and his hands on the strap and he looked at the far end of those boards for about as long as a sheet takes to be squared, and then he said the true thing out loud, to the boards, in the ordinary way.

"**I have put two people together in my head in the last month and I am not going to take them apart and I am not going to hand them to anybody, and I have wanted it said out loud in this room once and this is the first time in eleven years I have wanted a thing said in a room at all. I am not going to say which two and I am not going to say which week, and nobody is going to thank me for this and nobody is going to put a question to me about it, and neither of those two things is going to change what is in the bag or what is not.**"

Then he picked the strap up over his shoulder and stood with it there and did not go down yet, because four times a month he is on that floor for the better part of an hour.

---

Marn Ottery did not turn round.

She had the whole of that floor in view and she had had it every working morning for eleven years, and she is the only one of the three of them who has both of the two people and the room in the same field of view at once, and she had that now, and she is not going to say one word about it to anybody and nobody is going to thank her for it.

"Do not do that again this month," she said, to the boards.

"I have said it once in eleven years and I will not say it a second time this month and you are not putting anything to me about the first one."

"I have said the shape of a thing out loud in this room four times and I said before the first of them that there would not be a fifth, and there is not going to be a fifth, and I am not going to say one word tonight about what you have just done in my room."

"I did not do it in your room. I did it in a room and you were in it."

---

"You were at the other end of these boards for eleven years," Tamsin Rook said, without turning round, "and you have watched about four hundred people a year come down that stair and not one of them has been able to produce a line that says where they were, and that has been the arrangement on this floor the whole time."

"I know what has been the arrangement on this floor."

"And a man has just said a true thing out loud at the end of it with you standing there, and you have told him not to do it again, and that is the first instruction anybody has given anybody on this floor in eleven years."

Marn Ottery put both hands flat on the boards.

"**That is not an instruction and you know it is not, and I have not asked you anything and neither did he, and neither of you is going to be thanked for anything this month. What I said to him was a fact about the month and he put it in his own bag and he is going to go down four flights with it. That is the whole of what happened on this floor and there is nowhere at which any of it can be written down.**"

---

Here is the cost, and it is not the man's.

Eleven years ago a man put a satchel down on that wood and was the cheapest person in the building, because nobody had ever wanted one single thing out of him. He is not cheaper this month. He is the only person in this empire anybody can be shown handing a thing to once a week for eleven years without ever being shown getting anything for it.

And the woman at the other end of those boards is cheaper than he is. She was the only person on that floor who had ever noticed that two people were in a room, and that cost her nothing, because a person who is not in the room cannot be shown to have been in it. **She is in this one. She was in it for the whole of it and she has both of the two of them in the same field of view and she did not turn round, and from this morning there is no distance in this empire between that woman and what was said at the end of her own boards.**

Nobody has told her that she was in a room and she is not going to be told. Nothing in this empire will enter the woman at the far end of those boards as the one who was standing at that end of them when a man with a satchel said a true thing out loud, and nothing in it will enter her as the one who was somewhere else, and she is not going to be thanked for either.

---

He went down four flights with a thing in the bag and stopped on the fourth step going down and did not come back up, and the two people in his head went down with him, and he did not take them out of it on the stair and nobody in that building knows they were in it and nobody is going to be told.
