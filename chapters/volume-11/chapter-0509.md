# Chapter 0509: About Four Feet Of Lane Between Two Doors, A Woman With A Book Going Past, And Nothing Put To Anybody

The room is taken by the week and it is the same room, and the wall beside the window is still bare, and the nail is still in it going in crooked about the height of his hand, and the coat is on it.

He has a mark about three inches long on the inside of his left wrist and it reads nothing and decides nothing and does not know where the ford is, and it is not the price of anything, and a bar of his own came off him on an application of one line which he wrote himself.

It is the fourth day of the third week of the fifth month of the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is the morning, and it is two weeks since the last time he stood in the middle of that floor and said anything out loud to it.

---

The thing he is able to do takes about four seconds, and it needs a knife and a printed strip and about half a minute of a drawer in a building at the top of four flights. It would make a mark that could go into a coat and come out of a room with nobody knowing whose it was. The knife is up there in a drawer of its own and he has never said which one, and the drawer under that wall board is not it.

He thought about it at about the third hour, standing up, the way he has thought about it a good many times and has never once done.

He did not do it.

There is no form anywhere in this empire in which a man not doing a thing is entered as the one who did not do it, and that is the reason and it is not a good one, and he has been living on it for about two years.

---

Then he worked out the rest of it, standing in the middle of that floor, and he did not say it out loud to anybody because there is nobody in that room.

**The cheapest party in this matter is a man of thirty-eight with a bar of his own gone off him on a line he wrote himself and nothing behind him to be liable for. That has been true for about a year and it has cost him everything a thing can cost and there is no column in this empire that says he has paid it, and he would not be able to produce it if there was.**

**A person who is in a room is cheaper than that.**

He stood with that in front of him for a while.

Nobody asked that person to be there. They can be shown neither agreeing to anything nor refusing anything, and nobody will ever be able to hold a thing up in front of them and say you were given this and you said no, because saying no is not a thing that will sit in a column anywhere. **A practice that works on anybody cannot be told to do anything, cannot be listed, cannot be counted, has no heading over it, and there is no form in this empire that has a line for a person having been in a room.**

That is the first time in ten volumes that a thing in this matter has got better at the only thing it does.

---

About the fourth hour somebody put a hand on the door and said through it that there is a woman two floors down who has a thing about the window in that room and would put it in writing if anybody in the building would stand to it, and the hand went away again.

He did not open the door. That is the same as it was a fortnight ago and it will be the same as it is in a month, and there is nowhere in this empire for that woman's thing about a window to go, and he is not going to be thanked for it and she is not going to be thanked for it either.

---

The woman of about thirty-five went up the lane at about that hour with a book under her arm, and she was about four feet off the other side of the way, and neither of them slowed down and neither of them said good morning.

She said a thing out loud to the lane, in the ordinary way, the way a person says a thing out loud in a street in the middle of a morning.

"**I keep a book in my own hand and it has four headings over four columns, and in nine years I have never ruled a fifth, and I am not ruling one this month. A person who rules a fifth has decided what the fifth one is. I have not decided what it is and I am not going to be told, and I am not going to be thanked for the four I have got either.**"

Then she was round the corner and there was nobody on that lane.

---

He had been standing there when she said it.

That is the whole of what happened to him that day at that point, and it is a smaller thing than it looks and it is going to get bigger over the next four months, and he did not know that and there is no way in this empire that he could be told.

He had not been asked anything. He had not been spoken to. He had not agreed to anything and he had not refused anything, and nobody knew he was on that lane and nobody is going to know. **Nothing in this empire will ever put a man of thirty-eight into a column as the one who happened to be standing on a road when a woman said a true thing.**

He worked that out standing there and it took him about as long as a sheet takes to be signed, and it did not frighten him, and he is not going to let it frighten him, and it is not the same as the thing he worked out a fortnight ago and he knows that already and is not going to look at it.

---

He did not go and look at anybody.

He has not gone and looked at anybody in about two years and he is not going to start, and the reason is his own and it is not the reason he gave in a bay a long time ago in about nine words and it is not going to be given again in any form.

**A man who goes and looking is a man somebody has shown something to.** What somebody has been shown is a party, and a party is the single thing in this matter that can be told in advance what a thing is going to be used for, and he is the one person in this matter who cannot afford to be one of those. That is not humility and it is not a principle and it is not a decision and it is the only arrangement he has got.

He did not write any of it down. He did not send for anybody. Nothing has ever been sent to him and nothing has been sent to anybody this week.

---

The coat is on the nail. Two things are in its inside pocket, one either side of the seam, and they have been in there about two years, and the crease a third of the way down the leaf is worn through, and the four things printed along the top of the other one are the same four things, and not one of them is a name.

Nobody has looked in the coat. Nobody ever has and nobody ever is going to. Nobody is ever going to be told what is at the end of that struck line, and nobody is ever going to be told which hand struck it, and neither of those is going to be printed out anywhere at all. The claim that left this empire with that name left it on the same day, and one of those two things is his and the other has never been his at all, and it is not going to arrive this month.

Nobody has told him that there is a third place under the floor of a room in a building four hundred miles from here. Nobody has told him that the answer to a thing that happened in this matter about two years ago was a copy. Nobody has told him a great many things, and nobody is going to this month, and there is a woman of twenty-four in this city who has not been told a thing either and who is not going to be, and whether she takes what a colleague thinks about a thing like that has not been decided and is not being decided and is not going to be recorded as decided.

At the back of a shed at the end of a lane off the Slade there are two of seventeen who have not been spoken to and are not going to be, and one of them has not been asked one thing in about a year and is not being given the chance to say no about anything.

---

He is owed a heading over his name. There is no column in this empire that will take it, and there is not going to be one, and nobody is going to be thanked for his not having one.

He knows there is a thing going on in about nine hundred rooms a week that two people are in at the time, and it is written down nowhere, and it does not need to be, and he knows that nobody in nine hundred buildings can be instructed to ask about it.

He has not asked, and he is not going to ask, and there is nobody in nine hundred buildings who could be told to ask on his behalf. It is the one thing in this matter he is still certain of, and it is certain the way a wall is certain, and it is going to be there in the morning.
