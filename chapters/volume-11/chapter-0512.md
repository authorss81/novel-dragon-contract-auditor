# Chapter 0512: A Door With A Hand On It, Four Feet Of Wall With Nothing On Any Part Of It, And A Reason Given Once In A Bay That Was Not The Reason

The room is the one he has been on a weekly bill in for longer than he has held nothing, and the landlord has never once come inside the door of it.

Marek Kest. Thirty-eight. No register this empire keeps has a line against that name and none is going to get one. About a year ago he put in a paper of his own writing and a rank of his own came off him on the strength of it, and there is no taking back what was never granted and no withdrawing of what was never issued, so there is nothing on him to give up.

He gave the reason for keeping his hands off the one thing he is able to do out loud once, in about nine words, in a bay, a long time ago. It is not coming out again. What has happened in the last month is not that it turned out to be wrong.

He has a mark about three inches long on the inside of that wrist. There is nothing written in it. It settles nothing. It does not know where the ford is.

It is the second day of the second week of the sixth month of the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is the morning.

---

There were two things in the inside pocket of the coat on the nail, on opposite sides of the seam. One is a leaf out of a bundle folded twice, with a line struck through whatever stood at the foot of it, read and read and read by a man who is thirty-eight, and a third of the way down it the crease has worn right through to the other side. The other he paid fourpence for, at the top of four flights, on a wet morning about two years ago, and along the top of it is a printed strip with four things set on that strip, and not one of the four is a name.

Neither of them has left that pocket in this building or in any other. Nobody in this city knows he is carrying both, and nobody has ever looked in the coat and nobody ever is going to.

---

He worked the thing out on his feet in the middle of that floor, and it took as long as it takes to cross that room and come back, and he did not say it out loud at first.

There is nothing in the middle of it to walk round, so a man who is thinking crosses it and uncrosses it and crosses it again, and every crossing finishes against the same stretch of plaster beside the window. He had been at it long enough by then that the boards had taken a shine in the middle from the going over, and he had not yet put a hand on the bed or drawn the stool out from under the table, and the chair with the short leg was still standing against nothing at all behind him where it stands every morning.

The thing he is able to do takes about four seconds and needs a knife and a printed strip and half a minute of a drawer at the top of four flights, and what it leaves behind would ride in a coat through a hundred rooms and come out at the far side of one of them with no hand attached to it. Nothing else in ten years of this has ever travelled without somebody carrying it.

He has not done it in about two years and he is not going to and he was not going to before this morning.

Here is what is new. **He does not need to.**

A sentence said out loud in a room is the same object. It is a second of itself the moment it is said twice, and the two are not the same thing in any way that matters, because the first one is the making and the second one is only the carrying and nobody is asked for the carrying. The cut has to be made by him, alone, at the top of four flights, out of a knife he would have to open a drawer for. A sentence goes into a room for nothing at all, put there by a person who is not doing it on purpose, and there is no price on it and there is no maker standing in it afterwards and there is nothing anybody would have to be given before it was said.

**The cut is the expensive version. He has been refusing the expensive version of the cheapest instrument anybody in this matter has ever had, and that is what he has been doing for two years, and none of it was necessary.**

He stood in the middle of that floor and let that be true, and it did not get easier, and it was not a relief.

Then he said it out loud, to the plaster beside the window, because the room is the only party in this matter that cannot be asked and it has never once asked him a thing.

"**So I am not going to cut one, and I never was going to, and what I said out loud in that bay is still true and I am not going to say it again in any form. What I have worked out this month is a different thing and I have not decided whether it takes the place of that one or sits beside it, and I am not going to decide that in a room with nobody in it. Here is the different thing. A person does not have to want a thing before a thing can be used on them. Standing in a room is enough. That costs nothing, there is no form for it, and nobody can be instructed to be in one on purpose. I have been the cheapest man in this matter for two years and a person in a room is cheaper than me, and I did not know it until about a month ago.**"

He put his hand flat on the outside of the pocket and left it there.

The coat is still on the nail and nothing in this room makes heat, and about two years of that has taken the wool at the top of it to the colour of the plaster behind it, which is the colour everything in this room is the colour of. He has never had it brushed and there is nobody in this building who would come and do it, and a man who takes a room by the week is not a man anybody brings a brush to.

Whatever belonged at the foot of that struck line is gone, and it was not his mother's hand that put it there. The claim that travelled out of this empire with that name went the same way, on the same day. He has one of those two things in his pocket and has never had the other one, and neither of them is coming this month, and he did not say a word of it out loud.

---

At about the fourth hour there was a hand on the door.

It came up the four flights of stone at the rate a person comes up a stair when she has decided to get to a door before she loses the thing she came to say, and it stopped outside it, and there was then a gap of about four seconds in which nothing on the landing happened at all.

It was the same woman two floors down. She has a thing about a window in that room and it has been going on for about two months. The first time she came she brought an offer with her, and the offer was that she would set it down in writing if any one person in that building would stand behind it.

"**I am not going to stand to it," she said through the door, "and I have thought about it for two months and I want you to know that I have thought about it, and there is nobody else in this building I could ask and you are the one who does not open.**"

He did not open the door. He stayed where he was and let her get it said, and the stair went the way a stair goes.

"You have not answered me."

"I have not."

"That is not a no. Two months of a thing and a man who does not open a door is not a no, and I want it entered that I noticed the difference, and there is nowhere standing in this building at which anything is entered."

---

The change is that she has stopped offering and started wanting, and a person who has begun to want a thing in a room is a person who has made a decision about the room, and a decision is the only thing in this empire that can be told in advance what it is going to be used for.

She has no form. That is the whole of what has happened to her this month. There is no column anywhere that takes a tenant's decision about a door and there is no heading that could go over her for having made one, and nobody is going to find out that she made it, and nobody is going to be thanked for it, and she is not going to be asked again.

He cannot be shown refusing her. He cannot be shown accepting her. A person who has never been asked a thing in his life has just acquired somebody who wants something from him and there is no document in this empire that could say what came of it, and the two of them are on a landing and neither of them is going to say one word about it.

---

The hand went away up the stair and he did not open the door and he is not going to.

He did not go out of that door and along the street and he did not buy anything anywhere in this city. He did not go to the top of a stair and he did not go to the end of a lane. There is a thing in this city he has not been told about, and a woman of nineteen nine miles off who has not been asked anything, and a woman of twenty-four four hundred miles from here who has not been told what a second of a thing is for, and he is not going to find out about one of those three and he is not going to find out about the other two.

Those four feet of wall were still bare, that nail was still in them, the coat was still on the nail, and his hand was still flat on the outside of the pocket, and not one word of any of that is written down anywhere in this empire.
