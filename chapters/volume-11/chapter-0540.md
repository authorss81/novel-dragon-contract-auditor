# Chapter 0540: A Chain At The Front Of A Bench That Nobody Put A Hand On For About A Month, A Foreman Whose Hand Was Flat On The Wood Four Inches Away From It, And A Lamp That Came On By Itself Over Two People At The Back Of A Shed

The chain was across the front of that bench where it goes every working day of the year, and it had been across the front of that bench every working day of the eleven years he has been sitting behind it.

His hands were in his lap.

It is the second day of the second week of the first month of the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is about the fourth hour.

---

Halla Wray had her hand flat on the wood at the near end of that bench, about four inches off the chain, and she had been putting it there about nine mornings a month for two months, and a class can put her back at the back of that bench without anybody lifting a pen, and she has never once asked anybody to stop her doing it and nobody has ever offered to.

"That chain has had your hand on it every working morning for eleven years," she said, to the end wall, in the ordinary way. "It has had your hand on it up to the third link and no further, and I have watched that from the back of that bench for two months, and this month it has not had it. I have not asked you about it and I am not going to, and I would like it noticed that I noticed."

"I have not stopped touching it because of anything you have done and I am not going to tell you what I have stopped touching it for, and it is not going to be touched this month."

"You are not going to say what it is for."

"I am not going to say what it is for. You are not going to be thanked for noticing and you are not going to be asked about it, and there is nowhere standing at the end of this lane at which either of those could be put, and there never has been."

---

At the far end of that bench there were two of seventeen, and they were on the terms they are on, and neither of them has been spoken to by anybody in that lane in a long time and neither of them is going to be this month.

The girl of seventeen has not been asked one thing in about a year and is not being asked anything and is not being given the chance to turn anything down, and nobody in that shed is ever going to be thanked for that either.

The one with the book turned a page. That is the whole of what he did in that bay that day.

---

Here is what a man of about fifty-five has got at about the fourth hour of an ordinary morning, and it is not paid, and it is not going to be paid this month or in any month after it.

He has never been asked one thing in eleven years. About a month ago he said a true thing out loud in that bay with two people in it, and there was a foreman four feet behind him who could have asked him anything and did not, and a reader at the far end of a bench who was not spoken to and spoke nothing and does not know to this day that anything was said.

So there is nobody in this empire to put him in front of as the one who was heard, and there is nobody in this empire to tell him that anybody heard him, and there is nobody in this empire who is going to thank him for saying it or ask him what it was for. He is fifty-five and he has been at that bench eleven years and about a year of what he has said in that room has gone into a class and not into the air, and the one part of it that did go into the air went into a room with two people in it and is not going anywhere and cannot be recalled, because a person is not a place a person looks in.

The arrangement is still standing. Nothing was asked in that bay this month and nothing is going to be asked in it, and the paper on the board by that door has not been filled in and is not going to be, and the sentence she began in that doorway about two years ago did not arrive and is not going to arrive.

---

---

At about the fifth hour she went to that doorway and stood in it.

She has not been near it in about two months, on the ground that a doorway is the one place in a bay where a foreman can be shown standing, and a bench in her own room is not, and she had worked that out at the back of that bench and had said it out loud to nobody.

She got as far as the same two words. It has got to the same two words six times since the spring before last and there is a second half of it that has not arrived in about two years and did not arrive this morning either, and the lane did what a lane does, and she stood in that frame for about as long as a person stands in a frame.

She came back in and put her hand flat on the front of that bench again, at the near end of it, about four inches off the chain, and she did not say one word about having gone to the door, and the man at the front bench did not say one word about it either, and neither of them is going to.

Nobody in that lane has ever asked her for the end of that sentence and nobody ever is going to, and the man at that bench has told her twice in eleven years that he is not going to be the one who finishes it, and this is the second time this month.

---

The chain came off the stone at about the sixth hour and went back across the front of the bench, and he did not put a hand on it when it did.

His hands had been in his lap for the whole of the day with nothing in them.

There is no form anywhere in this empire in which a man at a front bench is entered as the one who did not touch a chain he has touched every working morning for eleven years, and there is nobody in that bay who is going to be told that he did not, and he is not going to be asked about it, and the one person in that bay who could have asked him has just gone to a doorway and come back again and is not going to.

The lamp came on by itself at about the seventh hour and put light the length of that bay, and the man at the front bench got up and went out of it in the ordinary way.

The mark in chalk on that end wall is at the height of the third link of a chain that is lying on the wood with nothing on it, and it is going to be there in the winter.
