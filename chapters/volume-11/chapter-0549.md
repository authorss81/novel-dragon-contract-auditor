# Chapter 0549: A Woman On Her Knees At A Housing With Her Back To The Passage, A Man Who Got To Ninety Yards And Went Back Up The Run, And A Bar That Went Home Smoother Than It Has Gone Home In Four Years

She had got down on the floor at about the half hour with her back to the passage and a length of rag in her hand, and there is a housing at the near end of that run that has not been cleaned out in about four years and that wants doing about twice a year, and nobody in that building had ever wanted her to do it.

It is the fourth day of the third week of the third month of the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is about the fourth hour.

---

"You are going to be at the last nine foot of this run in about twenty minutes," she said, to the bar, in the ordinary way, and not to him, because he was a hundred and fifty yards off and going up.

That was the arrangement and it had been the arrangement for four years. Four crossings a day, and the gaps between them are about two hours, and she had worked the shape of them out on her feet in about the first fortnight of being at that end of the run, and there is nothing in them that anybody could be shown she had worked out.

She had the rag in her hand and the housing open and about a quarter of four years of that dust gone out of it. The tin of oil was standing on the flags about nine foot off the bar where a man had put it about five weeks ago and where it was still standing, and she was not going to touch it. That was not a refusal of anything, and there is no form anywhere in this empire for a woman at a lock to be entered as the one who would not open a tin.

She worked dry. There is not a great deal in a housing that four years of a stone has put into it and a rag and about an hour will do most of it, and she was not going to be thanked for it, and she was not going to be asked what it was for, and there is nothing in this empire that takes a woman at a lock as the one who cleaned out a housing nobody asked her to clean.

---

He came down at about ten minutes early, which he had done perhaps six times in four years.

The light from the one high window was on about half of that run and she was on the floor on the far side of the bar with her back to the passage and her hand inside the housing, and from ninety yards out in that light she was a shape low down on the stone with a shoulder moving.

He got to about ninety.

*Then he stopped, and he stood there, and he did not come the last nine foot.*

---

There is no way in this empire to be shown having turned round.

A man who has come to the last nine foot of a stone four times a day for forty years and does not come it, on one morning, at about ten minutes early, for no reason anybody could produce, is a man who did an ordinary thing on an ordinary morning. He is a man who went up a run a bit early. He is a man who had something at the other end of it. There is no form anywhere that a man in a felt apron is entered as the one who stopped short, and there is not one form anywhere that a woman at a lock is entered as the one he stopped short of, and the two of those are the same gap and it is the widest one in this matter.

He went back up ninety yards and then the rest of the run, and the flags lay along about half the length of it with nobody standing in any part of them, and about four hundred yards of cold flags had a woman on the floor at the near end of them that he had not looked at.

---

She heard the boot on the stone go away and up and she did not turn round, and she went on with the rag for another half hour, and then she got up off the floor of that run for the first time in four years, and she put her two hands back on the far side of the bar, and she stayed there until the hour the bar goes home.

The bar went home into its housing and it went home smoother than it has gone home in about four years. The catch of that housing has been dry since the spring before last and it has caught dry about a hundred and forty times since, and this time it caught on something that had been moved out of the bottom of it with a length of rag by a woman who was not thanked for it and is never going to be asked about it.

There was no sound anywhere in that building to say any of that had happened. He came down at the fourth hour of the next working day with the rack on his hip and he got to the last nine foot of that run and stopped there the way he stops. The tin of oil was still on the flags where he had put it and the nine foot of stone in the middle of that floor was still not swept. He said one flat thing about the housing and she said one flat thing back and neither of them said a second word about any part of it.

He never found out and he is never going to. The housing at the near end of that run is cleaner than it has been in about four years and there is not one person in that building who knows why, and she is not going to be thanked for it, and he is never going to know.
