# Chapter 0510: A Counter That Shuts At The Sixth Hour, A Man Going Down Four Flights With A Satchel, And About Nine Hundred Rooms In Which Nobody Can Be Instructed

The second hour came round and the case was out and the boards were up, and the counter did what it does between the second and the sixth, and about four hundred people a year come down that one stair carrying a different object altogether. Every one of them comes down on its own account and not one of them has ever been sent.

Marn Ottery. Thirty-four, eleven years at the far end of those boards, and the only person on that floor who has never walked out of the room. She has put the shape of a thing out loud four times and has said in advance that there will not be a fifth, and ten weeks of ordinary mornings have not yet been a reason.

The four items are still on the wall behind her and not one of them is a time, and the fourth of them is still the one nobody on that floor will give an account of. The drawer under that board is where it has been for eleven years and the two of its four things that have never once been opened are still shut up in it, and the knife is in a different one and she has not said which and nobody has come up here to find out. The case at the near end is the supply. The box under the far end of those boards is not the case, has not been opened, has not been moved, and nobody in this room could tell you what is in it. Nothing in that room has been touched in ten weeks, and that is all that has happened in it.

It is the fourth day of the fourth week of the fifth month of the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is the second hour, and nothing has been asked of anybody in that room for ten weeks.

---

The work up there had been open for about ten weeks without anything at all being asked of anybody in it, and ten weeks is not long enough for a room to change and is exactly long enough for the people in it to stop noticing what it looks like. A room with nothing in it that anybody wants is a room a person can stand in for four hours without once being asked what she is doing there.

Tamsin Rook was at her end of the boards twenty minutes before the hour, which is what she has done every working morning for two years, and this week the twenty minutes have got a shape in them and she put it into a sentence at about the third hour and the sentence was shaped like a question and it was not one.

She had not brought anything up with her. There was nothing in her coat and nothing on the stair and nothing in her hand at any point in those twenty minutes, and a person who has come up early for two years with nothing in her hands is either the most disciplined person in the building or has not worked out yet what she is doing. There is no way of telling which of the two from the outside.

"**So which of the two of us is the one that is in the room, when the other one is.**"

Marn Ottery did not treat it as a question. She did not answer it and she did not tell the other woman not to put it like that, and about a sheet takes to be squared she squared the edge of the boards and then she said the other thing.

"**Coming in early with a reason behind it means you have already been used and have not found out. That is not a shape on a morning. That is a thing that has happened to you in a month that is gone and that nobody told you about and could not have told you about, and the not being told is the part that cannot be put back. Do not come in early for a reason. Come in early the way you have been coming in.**"

"I have not decided anything."

"You have not decided anything. I am not asking you what you have decided and you are not going to be asked and you are not going to be thanked, and I am not going to say that again this month."

---

About the fourth hour the strap came up the last flight and a man stopped on the fourth step from the top for about as long as a man stops, and then finished the stair and set the satchel down on the wood at that end.

Four times that month. Four times a week for eleven years, and nobody in this building has ever told him what is in the satchel, and asking has never once been a courtesy between him and anybody on this floor, and this is not a morning on which anybody on that floor is going to become a courtesy.

He stood with his shoulder to the room and his hands on the strap and looked at the far end of those boards for about as long as it takes a sheet to be squared, and then he did not look at anything for the rest of the hour.

A woman standing at a counter can see a bag go down where she is without turning her head at all. Not one of the three of them turned hers, and the counter carried on doing what it was doing. Three women and a man with a bag are the whole of what there is up there at the fourth hour of an ordinary morning.

---

She said it at about half past the hour, to the boards, in the ordinary way.

"**Nobody in this building has ever been asked what they are for. There is no form anywhere in this empire in which a person can be entered as the one who stood at this counter and sold a thing, and so nothing that has ever been said at this counter can be laid against anybody who has ever stood at it, and that is the whole of the arrangement. It is not a safety anybody in this empire built. It is what is left over.**"

She said it to the boards and she went on with them, and it did not sound like a thing that had cost her anything.

At that half hour there was more on the boards than there had been at any hour of that morning, because the blanks had been going across since the second hour and had not stopped. A woman saying a true thing in the middle of that is saying it with her hands full. Not one of the others stopped what she was doing, which is not a discourtesy and has not been one up there for eleven years.

The man with the satchel did not look up. He had been in that room for the whole of it, and at the far end of the two joined tables the woman of about twenty-nine had her head down over her book and was not waiting on anybody, and she was never waiting on anybody.

Nobody has any way of telling whether he heard it. He is not going to say that he heard it, and he has never in eleven years said that he heard anything, and the woman who had said it did not mention it again either, and neither of them mentioned it again that day or that week or after.

---

He said one true thing out loud in eleven years, and it was on a step going down, to nobody, and he is not going to be asked about it and he is not going to be thanked for not asking and this room is not going to be told what it was.

What is new this month is not that. What is new is that a thing was said on that floor this morning and a second person was standing on the floor while it was said.

He has been carrying paper up and down that stair four times a week since he was young and has never once known what was in his hands. This morning he was in a room for the better part of an hour with nothing in his hands and something else in it, and he does not know what to do with that, and there is no form in this empire in which he could be entered as the one who was standing there.

He does not know he has it. He is not going to be asked whether he has it, and nobody is going to be thanked about it, and he is not going to say one word about it to anybody on that floor, and the strap goes over his shoulder the way it has gone over that shoulder for eleven years and he will not stop on any of the landings.

There is one other thing he has worked out this month and has not said to anybody and is not going to put down that stair in that satchel, because that is a thing with a man in it and this is a thing with paper in it, and he has put two people together in his head about it and he is not going to take them apart and he is not going to hand them over.

---

The sixth hour came and the case went in behind the boards, and the four of them went up onto the wall above it, one to a nail each, and the room was shut.

Nobody was thanked. Nobody was forgiven. Nobody was sent for, and nothing was resolved. The fourth item on that board is still the one that none of the three of them can account for, and the drawer underneath it stays shut, and the box under the far end of those boards has not been opened and has not been moved, and the strip printed along the top of every one of those blanks is the same strip it was ten weeks ago and not one line has been cut into anything in this building in ten weeks.

The three of them stood where they were for a moment after the door was shut before any of them went, and none of the three has ever said out loud that any of the others did it. The floor is open four hours a day and the rest of it belongs to nobody who works in this building, and three women have to shut and lock and leave it, and there is nothing in that arrangement which any of them would want written down.

He went down four flights with a thing in the satchel and stopped on the fourth step going down and did not come back up.

She went down four flights with nothing in her hands and with a thing under the floor of her own room that nobody on that floor knows the position of, and nobody has told her what a copy of a thing is for, and nobody is going to. Whether she takes what a colleague thinks about that sort of thing is not a question anybody has put to her and is not going to be.

In this empire there is no form in which it is entered that a person was in a room when a true thing was said. There are about nine hundred of those rooms, and in none of them has anybody been asked anything, and in none of them can anybody be instructed to ask, and in none of them would the answer be entered if it were got.
