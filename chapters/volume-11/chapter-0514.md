# Chapter 0514: A Bar Put Back Into A Lock At The End Of About Four Hundred Yards Of Cold Flags, A Rack Over A Hip With Nothing Riding On It, And A Sentence That Went Past A Man Who Was Not Looking For One

She is about twenty-six and she has stood at one end of that run for the better part of four years. Nothing in that building has ever had her name on a roll, and she is not one of that building's clerks and never has been. In four years of standing at that end, not one person in it has wanted a thing out of her, not one has put a thing into her hands, and not one has told her anything at all.

Since the spring of her fourth year at that end there has been a figure in her head. Nothing has ever been written down about it and nothing is going to be, and it has already gone up out of that building once without touching a sheet of anything on the way, and there is nobody anywhere who could set a printed word above it for anybody to see.

Nobody in this matter walks that run in this month. The far lock belongs to somebody else, and whatever stands beyond it is not opened and not looked into, and the woman standing at that end of the cold is a person this passage has gone four volumes without and is not going to pick up now.

It is the fourth day of the fourth week of the sixth month of the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is about the fourth hour.

---

He came down out of that cold with the rack over his hip and nothing riding on it, and that was the ninth time in a month, and on eight of the nine it had been the same, and he has not made a rule about it and settled that in his own head a long time ago and said out loud at the time that he had settled it so that somebody would know.

He has been forty years up and down that stone four times a day.

"**You have come down that run nine times with nothing on it and I have stood at this end for four years, and there is no form anywhere in this empire that puts either of us down as the two people who were standing at the two ends of an empty passage. That is what we are. And I am not going to be thanked for standing at this end of it, and I am not going to be told what standing at this end of it is for, and I am not going anywhere, and that is the first thing I have decided in four years, and there is nowhere in this empire for a person's decision to go.**"

She said it to the lock, in the ordinary way, at about the fourth hour of an ordinary morning.

He was in that passage. He had been in it since the flags and he had his hand on the bar and the rack over his hip, and he did not put it down, and he did not look at her.

---

For about the length of time a rack takes to come up and go back, neither of them said anything.

"You knew I was here," he said.

"I knew you were somewhere in it. I have known that nine times this month and four years before that, and I did not look to see where, and that is not a courtesy between the two of us and it never has been."

"I am not going to be thanked for standing in a passage while a woman says a true thing at a lock."

"I am not going to thank you. I have never thanked you and I am not going to start this month, and the not starting is not for you and has never been for you."

---

And then he said the part of it that was his, and he said it to the flags nine foot up and not to her.

"**I told you in a month that is gone that I had never once in forty years stood in a room where a sentence was said, and that you had put me in one without doing it on purpose, and I was right about the first of those and I have been thinking about the second one for a month. The second one does not hold a second time. This time you knew where I was.**"

"That is not the same as me putting you in it."

"It is not the same and it is worse, and I would have been able to be shown to have been at the other end of that passage the first time, and there is nothing in this empire that can show me where I was this morning."

---

He did not say the second half of his own sentence. He said once that he was not going to say it and he was not going to say it a second time, and she did not ask for it and has three questions of her own that she is not going to put to anybody and never has.

The half that concerns her is still the half he has not said. That is where it has been since a month that is gone, and it has got no cheaper by standing at the end of a run four years and being the person who is cheapest in that building, and she worked all of that out standing at that lock with her hands on the bar in a month that is gone and she has not worked out the price of it and it is not this month.

What is new this month is that she has now said a true thing out loud in a passage with a man in it, and he was not asked, and he is not going to say that he heard it, and there is no form anywhere in this empire in which a man who goes up and down a stone four times a day can be entered as the one who was standing there when it was said.

**A man who has been standing in a passage when a true thing was said cannot afterwards be put at the other end of it. He had that for forty years and gave it away once by accident in a month that is gone, and this month he gave away the rest of it deliberately, and nobody is going to be thanked for either, and he is not going to be asked what it was for, and the two of them are not going to mention it again this week.**

---

He went back up the flags with the rack over his hip and nothing on it, and that was the ninth time in a month and it will be the tenth tomorrow, and nothing came down that run in that month or the one before it and nothing has been stood at the far end of it in four years.

Nothing stands at that end of that passage and no form anywhere in this empire takes the decline of a lock as a fact, and a man coming down an empty run four times is not a rule and not a statement and not on anything, and neither is a woman standing at the near end of one with her hands on the bar.
