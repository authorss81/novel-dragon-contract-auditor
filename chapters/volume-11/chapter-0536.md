# Chapter 0536: A Man At The Front Bench Of A Shed Saying One Of Four Things In About A Year Out Loud With Two People In The Bay, And Nobody In That Lane Answering Any Part Of It

The chain was out along the stone and his thumb was on it and he was at the third link, and he had been at the third link for about as long as it takes a sheet to be squared.

He is about fifty-five. He has been at that bench for eleven years and about nine foot by eleven of floor has gone past the front of it every working day of that time, and there is a mark in chalk on the end wall at the height of the third link of that chain, and there is a paper on a board by that door which has not been filled in by any hand at any time since before a foreman of fifty-one came to that bench.

A working sentence out of a paragraph has never once been beyond him in his life and that is a different thing altogether from the four people in this matter who cannot get sense out of one, and he has said so in that shed in terms, twice, and he is not one of those four and never has been and is not going to be.

He is the only person in this matter who could take a line out of a printed strip and say out loud whose it is, and nobody in this city is going to put a sheet in front of him.

It is the second day of the second week of the twelfth month of the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is about the fourth hour.

---

She was at the back of that bench and had been for a month, and she had stood at the front of that bay for one hour about ten weeks ago and had been sent back to the back of it by nothing whatever except the length of her own working morning.

Behind her, at the far end of that bench, were the two who are always at the far end of it, on the written engagement and on the silence, and neither of the two of them is going to be spoken to, thanked or sent for this month or in any month after it.

The girl has not been asked one thing in about a year. There has never been anything put in front of her for her to turn down, and nobody has ever told her that there was not, and that is not going to change in this bay in this month.

---

At about half past the fourth hour he said it.

He said it to the stone in front of the front bench, in the ordinary way, the way a man says a thing out loud in a bay where nothing is being wanted of anybody, and there was nobody in that bay he was saying it to.

"**Nobody has asked me for one thing in eleven years and I have just said a true thing out loud in this bay with two people in it, and there is nowhere in this empire to put a man who has.**"

Then he put his hand back on the chain at the third link and went on along it.

---

Nobody answered it.

That is not a discouragement. Nobody in that shed has answered one of the four he has said in about a year, and he never wanted an answer to any of them, and he is not going to be asked what that one was for, and he is not going to be thanked for having said it, and both of those are things that happen in the ordinary way in a bay nine foot by eleven where nothing has been wanted of anybody for eleven years.

The one with the book at the far end of the bench did not look up. He carried on with what he reads for about as long as it takes a sheet to be signed and the page he was on when it was said is the page he was on afterwards, and nobody in that shed is going to be asked which page it was.

The girl of seventeen did not move and was not given the chance to say no about anything and will not be given the chance about anything else, and she has not been asked one thing in about a year and nobody in that lane is going to ask her now.

The foreman of fifty-one at the back of that bench did not turn round and did not say one word, and she is the only person in that bay who could have asked him anything.

She is inside a class, and a class puts a person back where they have been, and it has been doing that to her in that bay for eleven years, and it does it by doing nothing and never has to lift a pen, and she is not going to.

---

Here is the whole of what that sentence cost and it is not paid and it is not going to be.

A man who has said a true thing out loud in a room with a second person in it cannot afterwards be shown to have been alone in the room. That has always been the only protection anybody in this matter has ever had, and it goes by standing still, and it does not take an arrangement and it does not take anybody's permission.

It went out of his hands at about half past the fourth hour on an ordinary morning, with a girl of seventeen at the far end of a bench and a foreman four feet behind him.

There is no form anywhere in this empire in which a man at a front bench is entered as the one a thing was said to. There is none in which he is entered as the one who was not in the bay. He is fifty-five and he has been at that bench eleven years and he is not going to be thanked for saying it and he is not going to be asked what it was for.

The two people in that bay are not going to be told one word about it by anybody, ever, and neither of them is going to say to him that they heard it, and he is never going to know that either of them was standing there when he said it.

The arrangement is still standing. Nothing was asked and nothing was answered and nothing was resolved and no form on that board by that door has been filled in and no paper in this empire has anything on it about the fourth hour of that morning.

---

The chain came off the stone at about the sixth hour and went back across the front of the bench, and he left it there with his hand on the third link, and the lamp came on by itself at about the seventh hour and put light into the far end of a bay where two people were sitting at the back of a bench and neither of them was looking at the other.
