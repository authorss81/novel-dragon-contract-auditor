# Chapter 0535: A Stair That Was Not Going Up And Was Not Going To, A Woman Two Floors Down Who Said That Was The Last Time And Was Right About It, And A Man Of Thirty-Eight In A Room With Nobody In It

He takes that room by the week and the landlord has never once been through the door of it. There is a table and a stool and a bed against one wall and a chair standing against nothing at all, and the window looks at the back of another house, and about four feet of bare wall stands beside that window with a nail in it, and the coat is on that nail, and nothing in that room makes heat and nothing is going to.

Marek Kest. Thirty-eight. He stands in no register in this city and never has, and he holds nothing that anybody could come for, and he is not at a stage and is not going to arrive at one.

The two things are in the inside pocket of that coat on opposite sides of a seam, where they have been since a wet morning about two years ago, and neither of them has been out of that pocket in this building or in any other building in this city, and no hand in this city has gone into that coat and no hand is going to.

It is the second day of the first week of the twelfth month of the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is the morning.

---

The stair did not sound.

She had come up those four flights about a month ago and said one true thing out loud on the other side of his door, and she had told him she was coming up to be in the room when she said it, and she had said that was the last time she was going to and that he was not going to be thanked for it and was not going to be asked what she had said.

He had not opened the door then and he has not opened it since, and the stair has been going up and down that whole of that month without stopping anywhere near his floor, and he had been listening for it in a way he is not going to put in words, and it did not come this morning either.

---

Here is the whole of what has been worked out in that room and none of it is going to be said to anybody.

She had made a decision, and a decision is a party, and a party is the only thing in this matter that a person can be given warning of what it is going to be used for. She had made it alone, in a room on a floor below his own, with a boiler at the back of it and a wall to say it to, and she had gone up four flights afterwards and put a second person in the room, and that person was him, and he was on the other side of half an inch of wood and did not open the door.

So he is a person who was in a room when a true thing was said, and there is no column anywhere in this empire that will take that, and there is none that will take her either, and the two of them are the only two people in that house who know it and they are not going to be told anything about it and they are not going to tell each other.

And she is the only person in that house who can say a true thing out loud with a second person in the room, and she has used that up, and he is the second person, and he has not gone and got another one.

He has never once in his life made a decision anybody could be shown having made. That is the whole of the difference between the two of them and it took him about a year to work out and it has cost him nothing, because a person who cannot be shown to have decided anything cannot be given anything either, and there is not one column in nine hundred buildings that will take a man who has decided to do nothing.

---

He said it out loud at about the fourth hour, to the plaster of a room with nobody in it, and there was nobody there to say it to and there never has been.

"**She came up those four flights to be in a room when she said a true thing, and she told me that was the only reason, and she was right, and she has not come up again, and I have not opened that door, and neither of those is going to change this month. The whole of what is left on that stair is a woman who is not going to be thanked and a man who is not going to be asked, and there is nobody in this house who is going to be told that either of those two people is the one who was on the other side of a door.**"

It was not a resolution, and there is not one thing in that room that a sentence could settle.

The walls of a room taken by the week are the only party in this matter that cannot be asked anything, and those walls did not answer him.

---

The top room of that house is let by the week and the landlord has never once been through the door of it.

The only thing anywhere in that house that says anybody has ever slept in it is a number cut into the back of a key to that room, and that cut is going thin at the top of it. The woman at the bottom of that stair has held it under her thumb about as long as a sheet takes to be squared and has never once said to anybody what is on it.

He is at the top of that stair at this hour and does not know any of that, and she is not going to be asked about it, and he is not going to be thanked for not knowing it, and there is no form anywhere in this empire in which either of them is entered as the one who knows what was on the back of a key.

---

About four feet of bare wall stands beside that window with a nail driven into it at roughly the height of his own hand and a coat on that nail, and he did not put his hand on the wall this month and he is not going to.

The stool was where the stool is and the table was where the table is, and the chair is still standing against nothing at all, and the window looked at the back of another house all morning and will look at the back of another house all afternoon, and the stair did not sound, and it is not going to.
