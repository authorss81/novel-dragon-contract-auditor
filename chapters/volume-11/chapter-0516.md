# Chapter 0516: A Hand Put Flat On The Inside Of A Door From The Side Nothing Is Standing On, A Woman Two Floors Down, And Half An Inch Of Wood Between Two People Who Have Never Been Asked Anything

There is a table, a stool, a bed against one wall, and a chair standing against nothing at all, and the window looks out at the back of another house, and the landlord has never once come through the door of it.

Marek Kest. Thirty-eight. There is not one line in this empire standing against his name in any column of any kind, and there never was and there is not going to be. He wrote his own single line of paper about a year ago and a rank of his own came off him on it, and a rank that was never granted cannot be taken back off a man, so there is nothing on him to surrender and nothing he could give up if somebody wanted it from him.

In the inside pocket of the coat on the nail there are two things, one on each side of a seam, and they have been there about two years. Nobody has ever looked in the coat and nobody ever is going to, and he has never taken either of them out of that pocket and he is not going to this month.

It is the second day of the second week of the seventh month of the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is the morning.

Nothing in the room makes heat, and the stretch of bare wall beside the window is about four foot of it with nothing standing on any part, and the one nail in the plaster is at the height of a man's own hand with the coat on it. The chair with one leg shorter than the others is why the chair is where it is and not anywhere else.

---

She came to the door for the third time in three months and she did not knock, which is a thing she has never done before, and she stood on the other side of it and did not knock and did not go.

There are four flights to the door and every one of them is stone, and a person going up them at an ordinary pace arrives at the top without having made a sound she was choosing to make, and nobody at the top of them ever hears her choose. He has had eleven years of her at the top of it and three months of her not being asked anything by anybody in this building, and a landing outside a shut door is where a person with nothing to say ends up standing on.

He was in the middle of that floor. He heard her not knock, which took longer to hear than a knock would have.

"There is a thing about a window," she said, "and I have said that three times now and it has got no further than this door and I am not going to say a fourth time and I am not going to stand here for it either."

He did not open it.

"I know you are in there. I know it the way I know it every week, because I hear the floor, and I am not saying that to put anything to you and I am not saying it so that you will open it. I am saying it because it is the end of the month and I have decided something and I would like one other person in this building to have been in the room when I decided it."

---

**A person who has been in a room when a thing was decided cannot afterwards be shown to have been nowhere near it, and there is no form anywhere in this empire in which a tenant is entered as the one who decided, and there is no heading that could go over her for it, and nobody is going to find out that she did, and nobody is going to be thanked.**

He had worked the whole of this out a month ago, standing in the middle of that floor, and he had worked it out about the cheapest instrument in the matter and not about a landing. He had it as a thing that happens to other people in other buildings.

Then he put his hand flat on the inside of the door and left it there, and the door is a half inch of wood and there is nothing behind it except a landing and a woman standing on it, and he did not open it and he did not take his hand off it.

A hand flat on a door from the side nothing is standing on is a different act from knocking and he knows the difference and has known it for years, because nothing in this empire takes a door for anything. He had worked all of it out a month before, standing in the middle of the room, and it had taken him the length of a walk across the boards and back, and none of it was any use to him, because the thing he had worked out was that a person in a room is cheap, and a hand on a door is not a person in a room.

---

"You are on the other side of this door," she said.

"I am."

"I am going to go up in a minute and I am not going to be back with anything else and I am not going to be thanked, and there is nobody in this building I could say it to twice to, which is the reason I said it to a door."

"Then say it to the door and not to me."

"I have said it to the door. You are the reason I did not have to."

"There is a thing I have not said out loud to anybody in the whole of the time it has been going on, and I worked out in the last month that the reason I have not said it is not that anybody would have used it. It is that if I say it out loud in front of one other person then the other person is in it, and there is nothing either of us could be shown afterwards. That is all. That is the whole of it, and it has taken me the whole of the time and a door."

"Then do not say it to a door either, because I am on the other side of it."

"That is the first sensible thing anybody has said to me in a month and I am not going to answer it and I am going up."

---

He stood with his hand on the inside of the door for about as long as a sheet takes to be squared and she stood on the other side of it and neither of them said one word, and after a while the stair went.

Two people not speaking through half an inch of wood make no sound at all, which is not the same as a quiet room. There is a pressure on a door from somebody standing against it who is not leaning, and he could tell from the inside where she had put her weight, and he did not shift his hand when he had worked that out, and neither of them ever said how long it had been.

He had been the cheapest person in this matter for about a year. A bar of his own came off him on an application of one line which he wrote himself, and a rank that was never granted cannot be taken back, and so a man in his position has nothing behind him to be liable for, and that has been the whole of it for about a year, and it goes back further than any fire does.

**And he has worked out this month that a person in a room is cheaper than that, and cheaper than a satchel, and cheaper than a piece of paper anybody may have, because a person does not have to carry it and does not have to know and is not asked and cannot be entered afterwards in any way at all.**

The only way a person is charged for standing in a room is by somebody else in the room. A second hearer has no price, no queue, no maker and no form, and it cannot be recalled and it cannot be found, and a person is not a place a person looks in.

Nobody in nine hundred buildings can be instructed to be in a room, or out of one. That is the whole of the protection and it is not a protection for him, because he is the one standing on the landing with his hand on a door he has not opened, and there is no column anywhere that will take that either.

---

About the middle of the afternoon he took his hand off the door and the wood had nothing on it and never has.

What he wants has not changed and it is not going to change this month. A name stood at the foot of a leaf once, and a hand that was not his mother's put it out, and whatever belonged underneath that line is gone. The claim that left this empire under that name left on the same day and has not been back since. One of those two things is his and the other one has never been his at all.

He is owed a heading over his name and no column in this empire will ever take one and none is going to be ruled for it.

The four feet of wall beside that window is still bare and the nail is still in it and the coat is still on it, and the two things are on opposite sides of the seam where they have been since a wet morning about two years ago.

He put his hand on the inside of the door once more at about the fifth hour and took it off again, and the wood had nothing on it and never has, and the stair did not sound, and nothing about a landing is written down anywhere in this empire.
