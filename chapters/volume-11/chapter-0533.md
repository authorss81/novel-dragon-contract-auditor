# Chapter 0533: A Woman At A Lock Putting Her Two Hands Back On The Bar On The Passage Side Where A Man Coming Down That Run Can See Her From About Halfway, And A Man In A Felt Apron Who Is Not Going To Say What He Thinks It Means

She is about twenty-six and four years at that end of it is the whole of what she is in that building. Her name is on no roll in it and never has been and she has never been one of that building's clerks, and there is nothing about her anywhere in there that anybody could go and look up.

The bar is at the near end of the flags and it goes back into its housing at the hour it goes back in. The lock at the far end of that run belongs to somebody else and whatever stands beyond it is neither opened nor looked into, and the four hundred yards between the two ends is not walked this month by anybody in this matter.

There has been nothing coming down that run for four years.

It is the fourth day of the third week of the eleventh month of the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is about the fourth hour.

---

He came out of that cold with the rack riding on his hip and nothing on the rack, the way it has been every one of the four times a day for about four years.

She had her hands in the sleeves of her coat and her back to the cold and her face to the wall, and a man on that run does not get within about nine foot of her at all before he is beside her, and that was where she had put herself about a month ago.

He was at the last nine foot of it when he saw the difference, and he stopped, and a man who has gone up and down a stone four times a day for forty years does not stop for a woman at the end of one.

"**You have gone back round to the passage side of that bar.** I have not asked you why and I am not going to, and I want it said out loud where you can hear it that I said all of that, and nobody is going to be thanked for me saying it. I said in a month that is gone that I had settled in my own head that I was not going to make a rule about an empty rack, and I have not changed that and I am not going to."

"I have not asked you to notice a bar."

"You have not asked me to. **What I have got is that a person who can be seen from halfway down a run is a person a thing can be put in front of. The whole reason you went round the back of that bar in a month that is gone was so that nothing could be, and you have undone it on purpose. I do not know what you think you have got now and I am not going to be told and I am not going to say it a second time.**"

---

She put her hands on the bar.

It took about as long as it takes that bar to go into its housing and come out again, and she did it on the passage side where she has stood for four years with both hands on it, and the hands went on flat and cold and did what they have always done.

"There was a time in a month that is gone," she said, to the bar, in the ordinary way, "when I went round the back of this and worked out that being the cheapest person in a building is a rate and not a state, and that the only way out of a rate is to be somebody who is wanted something from, and I did not take that and I have not taken it. **A decision is a party. I made one at the end of a run in a month that is gone and there is nowhere standing at which a decision of a woman at a lock could be put, and I have made two since the spring and I am not going to make a third, and that is the whole of why my hands are where they were this morning, and I am not saying it to you and you are not going to be thanked for hearing it.**"

He had the rack on his hip and he was nine foot off her and he had said he was not going to say it a second time and he did not say it a second time.

---

What she had actually done, and it was not what she had said out loud, and nobody in that building is going to be told either.

She had put herself back in the one place on that line where a man coming down it does not have to get to the last nine foot to find her. A person nobody can see is a person nothing can be put in front of, and that had cost her nothing and had made her the cheapest thing in a building, and a person seen from halfway is a person a sentence can be said to without anybody arranging it, and she has worked that out at that end of that run this month standing up.

So the cheapest second hearer in this city is now in the most findable place on four hundred yards of cold flags, by her own act, on purpose, and there is no form anywhere in this empire in which a woman at a lock is entered as the one who moved, and there is none in which she is entered as the one who did not.

A figure has been in her head since the spring of her fourth year. It is on no paper and is not going on any and it has got no heading over it anywhere, and it went out of that building once in a spring that is gone without ever touching a sheet on the way, and it is staying where it is this month.

---

He took the flags back up with the rack still empty on his hip. That was the fourth crossing of the day and the month has fourteen of them left in it and every one of them is going to be exactly like this one.

The second half of his own sentence is still the half he has not said. He said in a month that is gone that he was not going to tell her, and he has not told her, and it went nine foot up those flags when the first half of it went and the rest of it went down that passage and is not going anywhere, and she has not asked and is not going to.

The bar went home into its housing at the hour it always goes home, and this morning her two hands were flat on it when it did, on the one side of it where a man coming down that run can see her from about halfway.
