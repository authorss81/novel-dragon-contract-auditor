# Chapter 0550: A Window With Its Catch Turned And A Window Shut, And A Man Of Thirty-Eight Who Worked Out In About Two Minutes Why He Has Not Opened It In About Two Years

He put the stool under the window at about the third hour and got the catch between his fingers and turned it, and the catch turned, and the window did not open, and then he stood in the middle of that floor for about as long as it takes a sheet to be squared.

It is the fourth day of the fourth week of the third month of the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is about the third hour.

---

The catch is stiff and it has been stiff for about two years and the window has not been opened in about two years, and the reason a man does not open a window is a nail, or a frame, or a draught, or the fact that there is nothing on the other side of it worth the standing up.

The reason this window is shut is a well.

There is about four foot of air between the outside of that wall and the back wall of the house behind it, and the light comes up out of it in the morning the way light comes up out of a well, and beyond that, in the back of the house behind, there is a room with somebody in it for the first hour of the morning sweeping a floor, and nobody in it after that.

He worked all of that out in about two minutes, standing up, with a stool under a window and the catch turned.

He had not opened that window in about two years and he had worked out in about two minutes that he had not opened it because a person at the other end of about four foot of air can hear anything said out loud in a room, and he has said things out loud in that room for about ten years.

He said them to the plaster. He said them to the floor with his back against a door. He said them to a window, in the last year, for about four times as long as he meant to. There is not one column in this empire that a man of thirty-eight can be entered in for having said a true thing out loud in a room with nobody in it, and a second hearer is the one thing in this matter that cannot be put anywhere either, and a person who has been heard cannot afterwards be shown to have been alone in a room and cannot be shown to have refused anything and cannot be shown to have agreed to anything.

And he has not wanted to be entered anywhere in about ten years. He has gone into no register in this city and has asked nothing of it and it has asked nothing of him, and that has been the whole of his protection, and there is no way in this empire to have a room with an open window onto a light well about four foot across and still be a room nobody could be shown anything about.

**He is the only person in this matter who has worked out that he has been shutting the thing out of a room of his own, and he has not said one word of that to anybody, and there is nobody in that house he could say it to, and the room is the only party in this matter that cannot be asked anything.**

---

That chair is standing where he put it about two months ago, with its back to the window, and he dragged it across the boards to turn it, and it left a clean rectangle in two years of dust and a drag mark going out from it in a line.

He had turned that chair round so that he would not be looking at the window. This morning he had put a stool under the window and turned the catch and not opened it, and he stood in the middle of that floor and looked at the two of them together, the chair with its face to the plaster and the stool back against the wall, and neither of those things is going anywhere and neither of them is a person.

About four feet of bare wall stands beside that window with a nail in it driven in crooked about the height of his own hand, and his coat is on that nail. There are two things in the inside pocket of that coat on opposite sides of a seam. One of them is a leaf with a line struck through whatever stood at the foot of that line, and the other is a piece of that counter's own stock with four things printed along the top edge of it, and not one of the four is anybody. He paid fourpence for that one out of a case at the top of a stair on a wet morning about two years ago.

He did not go near the nail. He did not put a hand in the coat and nobody in this city has ever put one in there, and the crease a third of the way down that leaf has gone thin enough to feel through from the wrong side and it is not going to be handled this month.

There is no fire in that room and there is not going to be one, and the landlord has never once been through the door of it in about two years.

---

At about the first hour a coin came under the door at the bottom of that house and went into a tin on the top step at the turn, and the lid came down crooked on the top of it, and there are two bare things in that house and both of them are outside the reach of anybody coming in off that lane.

The woman who has stood at the foot of that stair for about twenty years has not been above the second of those stairs once in that time and did not go up them this morning either, and she is not going to start. A man at the top of them has come down four flights twice in about two years and has told nobody in that house that he has done it either time, and there is no form anywhere in this empire that takes a woman for carrying a tin up two steps and a turn, and none that takes a man for a coin under a door, and nobody in that house is going to be thanked for any part of it.

The stair went up at about the sixth hour and came down at about the seventh and did not stop on his floor, and he heard all of it go, and he did not go to the door, and the window looked at the back of another house and the back room at the other end of about four foot of air was empty and has been empty since about the third hour.

---

He put the stool back against the wall where the stool is.

He did not close the catch back and he did not open the window, and both of those are on the same morning and neither of them is a decision anybody could be shown he had made, and the standing of the stool against that wall is the standing it has always had, and about two years of a window being shut in this building is going to be about two years of a window being shut in it in the winter.

He had worked out the reason and he was not going to do one thing about it. The whole of what he wanted this morning was a room with somebody in it who had not come for him, and there has been about four foot of air between this window and the back room of that house for the whole of the two years he has lived in it. A man who has spent ten years refusing to make a person a party could have had the cheapest second ear in this empire for nothing at all, and he has had it on offer the whole time and has been keeping the catch shut against it.

**The catch is turned and the window is shut and the stool is back against the wall, and he is not going to open it, and nobody in that house is ever going to be told why, and there is nowhere in this empire to put a man who has worked that out, and it is the first thing in about ten years he has wanted set down anywhere.**
