# Chapter 0502: The First Week The Twenty Minutes Have A Shape, And A Thing Under The Floor She Is Not Going To Say Anything About

The counter shuts for the day at the sixth hour and it had shut about an hour ago, and the case is in behind the boards and the four of them are on the wall above it, and there is a young man counting nothing in particular at that end and a man with a satchel going down four flights and a clerk at the far end of the two joined tables putting a pen down in the middle of a book.

Tamsin Rook. Twenty-four. Two years at that end of those boards and she has been twenty minutes early every working morning for the whole of them, and it has never once been a decision.

Her undertaking is live and unretired and has never been brought into use and has never been given up, and its date is not said out loud on that floor, and nobody in that building has ever wanted to know when it is, and this chapter does not say it either and neither does any record this project's writer keeps.

It is the fourth day of the fourth week of the third month of the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is about the fifth hour.

---

She was still at her end of the boards at about that hour because there is nowhere in that room to go and nothing to shut, and Marn Ottery was still at the other end of them doing the thing she does at the end of a day, which is to stand at a set of boards that are already shut.

"You came in early again," Marn said.

"I have come in early every morning for two years."

"**I know how long you have. Here is the other half of it and I am saying it once and it is a fact and not a thing I am putting to you. A person who notices that two people were in a room has made a statement about it. There is no form anywhere in this empire that a person is entered in as the one who noticed, and there is not one because a person who could be entered as the one who noticed would be somebody's servant. That is the whole of what noticing costs, and you pay it whether you say anything or not.**"

---

She did not answer it and she did not agree with it.

She has not been thanked for anything this month and she is not going to be thanked for this, and she is not going to be asked about it, and neither of those is going to make her safer than she was four days ago. That is a thing Marn Ottery said to the boards about four months ago and it does not need saying again and Tamsin Rook has not said it again.

"You are telling me I cannot have seen what I saw."

"I am telling you what seeing it makes you. I am not telling you what you saw and I never am. **You did not tell me you saw anything. You have told me nothing at all this month and you are not going to, and if you had, I would have been the second person in a room and there is no column in this empire for the two people who noticed.**"

---

Then Marn stopped, and looked at the boards, and said the rest of it to the far end of the floor, where there was nobody.

"**And the other half, and this is the one I would take back if I could and I cannot. Nobody on this floor is waiting on anybody. That is the arrangement. It has been the arrangement for eleven years and it is the only reason this room is the room it is, and it works because nothing anybody could put in front of any of us has ever been anything any of us has wanted. That is not a protection anybody built. It is what is left when nobody has wanted anything, and it stops working the day somebody in this empire works out that a person who has wanted nothing is a person a thing can be put in front of.**"

Nobody answered that, and she had not said it to anybody.

---

Tamsin Rook stood at the end of those boards with the shutter over the case and worked out the first thing she has worked out in two years that does not have a form under it.

The two are not the same. Being in a room and being in it are two different things, and they come apart, and the place where they come apart is a person standing in a room who has not been asked to be in it and does not know that anything has been said. **A person in a room cannot be shown having agreed to anything and cannot be shown having refused anything and cannot be asked afterwards what it was for, and there is nowhere in this empire to enter anybody as the one who was standing there.**

She said none of it out loud. She is not going to say it out loud, and nobody on that floor is going to thank her for working it out, and nobody is going to ask her where she got it, and it is hers the way a thing under a floor is hers.

Under the boards of the floor of her own room, at the back, against the wall behind the drawer, out of the light, there is a piece of paper that is not in the case and is not in a drawer and is not in a hand. She did not make it and she cannot account for it. No column anywhere in this empire has a line for a third place, and she made one about two months ago, and she is not going to be thanked for making it and she is not going to tell anybody on that floor where it is.

She has told nobody that anybody could find it, and nobody has told her anything at all that would let her know what a copy of it is for, and that is a thing that has not been said to her and is not going to be said to her this month.

---

The twenty minutes have had a shape this week and the shape is not a plan.

She has not made a decision about the shape. She came in early the way she comes in early. On three of the mornings this month she was standing at the end of those boards before the second hour with the shutter not yet off the case, and she could not have said why.

The not being able to say why is new. For two years the earliness was a thing with no content at all, and this week it has something in it, and she has not looked at what.

She is not an organiser and nobody has made her one. She is not a clerk with a title. She is not a person somebody has finally asked something, and there is nobody on that floor who wants one single thing out of her, and that has not changed this week and is not going to.

Whether she takes what a colleague thinks about a thing like this is a thing that has not been decided. It was not decided last month. It is not being decided this month, and she is not going to decide it, and no state record in this project is going to record that it was decided, and the pull to decide it is the whole of what the week feels like and she is not going to let it.

---

She went down four flights at about the sixth hour with nothing in her hands, and she did not stop on any of the landings, and nobody sent her and nobody was thanked for the going.

The thing under the floor of her own room is where she left it. Nobody on that floor knows where it is, and nobody is going to be told, and nobody has told her anything this month, and nothing at all was said out loud in this matter this week, and the arrangement is still standing and nothing about it was resolved.
