# Chapter 348: The Top Of A Stair In Auremar With No Other Way Up, The Fourth Counter Open From The Second Hour To The Sixth, A Fee Board Of Four Items On The Wall, And A Box Of About Four Hundred Bought And Unfilled Blanks Under It That Nobody Has Asked For

The counter is at the top of a stair and there is no other way up, and the counter is open from the second hour to the sixth, and about nine people a day come up that stair in a week when the weather is against them.

She is twenty-four. Her name is Tamsin Rook, and she has been at that counter two years, and the fee board on the wall behind her has four items on it and not one of the four is time.

This is the second day of the second week of the twelfth month of the year after the year after the year after next, and it is the whole of the hours the counter is open, and the lamp over the received room is lit and the fourth shelf is where the fourth shelf is.

---

The undertaking of the fourth of the first month is live. It is unretired and it is unamended and it has not been exercised, and it has not been exercised for about two years, and it is not going to be different this week, and a correct non-use is as good as a use and is entered as one.

The stair stayed empty through the morning and she kept the counter open through the hours regardless, and the keeping it open was the whole of the work and nobody in the building remarked on it either way.

**A person who is not asked has not been given a duty**, and she has known that since about the seventh month of next year. She has never once known that it did not apply to her, and the reason the undertaking sits where it sits is that nobody ever put it to her in the first place.

Nobody has asked her anything in about two years. A man came up that stair once, in the second week of the tenth month, wanting nothing at all, and put one question in the air and did not wait for the answer, and that is the last time anybody has put a question to her and it is the reason she has wanted to be asked every working day since.

---

Nobody came up the stair this morning wanting anything, and that is not the same as nobody coming, and she is not told which of the two it is going to be. **Nothing in this empire would let a clerk of a counter be told that a person is not expected**, and nothing in it would let one be told that a person is, and if there were the second of those she would be a person with a day in it, and a day in it is the beginning of a document.

A woman of about thirty-four has been at the other end of that counter for eleven years and she is not a lawyer and she said the whole of what a blank is out loud to a person twice in eleven years, and both times she was not thanked, and she is at the other end of it this morning with a pen and a fee board and the same expression.

---

The fourth thing on that board is the one they cannot account for.

Three clerks have looked at it, including the two of them, and none of the three can say what the fourth item is for, and the woman of thirty-four wrote it there herself in her own hand about four years ago and cannot remember being in the room when she wrote it, and she has said that out loud twice, and nobody has thanked her for saying it either time.

What it is not has been looked at more than once and no look has come back with anything: it is not a time, it is not a sum, it carries no name of a person, and the writing in it is her own, and the hand is the same one that is on everything else she has ever written, and not remembering the room she was in at the time is not the same as never having written it, and nobody has ever put the choice to her.

They did not say anything about it this morning. **No form for signing a thing you cannot account for exists at that counter or anywhere else**, and so the item stands on the wall in a hand that does not vary, and the three of them look at it about four times a week and none of them has ever put one question to another about it out loud, and wanting to is not asking.

Under the counter there is a box of about four hundred blanks, bought and unfilled, and it has been there eleven years. **No form that holds it was ever drawn**, and nobody has ever asked her to account for it and nobody is going to.

**A blank is the only piece of paper in this empire that cannot be held against anybody**, and a box of about four hundred of them is the nearest thing to a place a person can put a question that anybody in this empire has ever had. It is not a place, and the only thing that would turn it into one is a person asking for it, and a person asking for it is a person who can be told no in about four minutes by a person who is not a villain.

---

In her own book, on a page ruled by hand, there are two boxes with the same name in both of them, and neither of the two has been merged with anything, and the name at the top of a line she put there about the woman of thirty-four is the name of a woman who has been at that counter eleven years and who put a coin back on it once rather than taking it.

She has worked out, in a room of her own, on her own time, that the line is a window and not a wall. **It is a window because a line in a book of her own with her own name at the head of it is the only thing in this empire nobody can serve anybody with**, and it is a wall the moment she is asked whether she minds it being there, and she is not going to be asked and she is not going to ask, and nothing anywhere has a column for either of those about a page of somebody's own book.

The fourth thing in her drawer is where it has been and it is untouched and it is unsent and it is unopened, with three others, and nobody has offered her a key to that drawer and she has not asked for one.

"Is that stair still the only way up?" she said it out loud at about the third hour, to the stair, about the stair, and it is, and it has been since the building was put up, and nothing in this empire lets a stair be said to a person, and a person who has wanted to be asked something every working day for about two years and has not been asked is not a person anybody in that building is looking for.

A page of her own book, ruled by hand, carries two boxes, the same name written into each, and neither has ever been merged into anything else, and two years of leaving the third page unruled is everything she has done about it.

---

Nobody thanked her and nobody thanked the woman of thirty-four and nobody was sent for and nothing was resolved, and the undertaking stands, and the box stands, and the fourth item stands, **and the count of things anybody has asked out loud in this matter is the same as it was when that counter was opened this morning and it is going to be the same in ten weeks.**
