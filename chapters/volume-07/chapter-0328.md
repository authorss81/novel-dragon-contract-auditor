# Chapter 328: A Rented Room Two Streets Off A Street That Goes Down To The River Road, The Rent On The Wall And Nothing Else, And A Woman Who Was Not In The Room

The room is two streets off a street that goes down to the river road and it is taken by the week, and the landlord has never once been inside it and has never once asked to.

Inside it there is a table and a lamp standing on the table, and a board fixed to the wall with the rent written on it and nothing else, and a window that looks at the back of another house, and about four feet of that wall with nothing at all on it.

She is twenty-four and her name is Tamsin Rook and she has been at the other end of the same counter about two years, and she is not a lawyer, and about nine people come up that flight in a day and not one of the nine has ever asked her anything.

This is the second day of the second week of the seventh month of the year after the year after the year after next, and it is about the fifth hour, and the counter shut at the sixth.

---

Under that table there are two things and both of them belong to her, and there is no office on the table and there never has been, and neither of the two is a copy of anything anybody else keeps.

The first is a line in her own hand and one line only: that a clerk of thirty-four turned her down on a stair and gave no reason for it. She put it there on her own motion, at nobody's request, and she wrote down the fact of the no and not one word of what went with it.

The second is four lines taken off the fee board off a shelf at the counter, and the fourth of those four is in a hand that nobody at that counter has ever been able to account for.

On another page there are two boxes drawn by hand with the same name written into both of them, and the name in them has stood at the other end of that counter for eleven years and has never once been asked about anything at all.

**A third page is blank and she is not going to put a line on it**, and no form in this empire can take a line back out of a page a woman keeps for herself, and that is why nothing goes on it and nothing comes off it.

---

What the last ten days gave her is the cost side of a thing she already had the other side of.

**Two instruments require her in any room he is in and they are not one instrument**, and neither has ever been merged with the two boxes she has drawn by hand, and one is a paper and the other is not a paper, and she has never been able to make them into a single thing and is not going to.

If he had gone up a stair to the building off the old river road she would have been in that room. That is not a figure of speech and it is not a likelihood. **It is what the two of them are for, and it is certain, and he is about nine miles off and did not go, and she is not told, and there is no form in this empire that would let anybody tell her that he did not go.**

So she is sitting at a table with a lamp on it and a board on the wall with the rent on it, and neither instrument has been exercised, and the whole of what that has cost her is that she is not told a thing about the one day in about nineteen years that anybody in this matter would have been able to speak about afterwards.

---

The undertaking of the fourth of the first month is live, and it is unretired, and it is unamended, and it has not been exercised.

The reason is the same one it has been, and it is about four minutes long, and it is that nobody asked her. **A person who is not asked has not been given a duty, and she has known that since about the seventh month of next year**, and she has never once known that it did not apply to her, and nobody has asked her this month and nobody has asked her this week.

It will not be exercised by her on the strength of being made, and it will not be exercised on her by anybody else, and if it ever is, the cause will not be a person coming up a stair wanting a thing.

---

The four things in the drawer under that counter are where they were this morning. One of the four she has never looked at, and it has not been opened and it has not been sent, and the other three have not been either.

**She is not going to give the two boxes a heading.** A heading is what a company is made of, and a company can hold wages against and take to law, and she is not going to be the one who puts a woman eleven years at the other end of the same counter into one four miles from where the woman stands.

**There is no form in this empire in which a woman can be told that a thing did not happen**, and the not being told is the ordinary condition of a person who is not a party and not a hardship that anybody arranged for her, and it is the whole of what the two instruments have cost her so far, which is nothing, and which she will not be able to put anywhere.

---

She lit the lamp at about the sixth hour, which is earlier than she lights it, and sat down at the table with the two things under it and the third page empty, and worked out in about four minutes why the not being told was the same thing as the not being asked and then had to sit with the rest of it for a while after that.

**How early?** She said that out loud to nobody, about the lamp, and then she did not put it out, and the board on the wall had the rent on it and nothing else.

The decision about whether she trusts a man's judgment is not made, and it is not in this room, and this month did not decide it and is not going to, and nobody in this empire is going to be told when it is made if it ever is.

Nobody thanked her and nobody sent for her, and nothing was written down that was not a figure about somebody else's day, and about nine miles off a room with a rail in it a man of sixty-one said about nine minutes of it standing up, and she was not in it and was not told and is not going to be told.
