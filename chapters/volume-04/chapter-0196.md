# Chapter 196: A Procedure Is Not A Person

He went at it on the second day of the second week of the sixth month of the year after, at about the sixth hour, in a room hired by the day above a shop in a street in this city, with a table and four chairs and a lamp, and it took him until about the eleventh hour and it failed, and the failure is not the interesting part.

Marek Kest is thirty-eight. He is the person who gave a dead woman's nine lines to a printer at fourpence a copy in the second month of this year and he is not thanked for it. He has no office, no fund, no register and nothing in this empire he could certify anything with, and his own bar came off him on the twelfth of the ninth month of last year on an application of one line which he wrote himself. There is a line about two inches long on the inside of his left wrist. It has not done anything since the fourth hour of the second of the fifth month of next year, when it was up for about nine seconds, and it told nobody anything, and it is not the price, and the wage is the price.

The room was not his and neither was the lamp and he had paid for the day out of about forty-four pounds a year with no fund and no form for a fund.

---

What he was doing is the thing nobody in this matter has been able to do, and it has a name in a book about a power he does not have and it is a power that runs on memory, and the memory it runs on is not anybody else's.

You do not reconstruct a person by remembering her. You reconstruct a person's reasoning by walking it, step by step, in the order she would have walked it, out of the terms she had, and if the terms and the order both hold then the person is inside the reconstruction and not behind it. That is the whole of it and it is not a metaphor and it is not a ghost.

**It can only be done by somebody who can hold four terms at once, and there is no form in this empire that says a person may do it.** He has no certification and no register and no office and a discharged bar, and the only instrument he has ever been given for it is a line about two inches long on the inside of his own left wrist that reads nothing.

He had the form in his head, and about four hundred lines of a bound book about four miles off the old river road in his coat, and a fair copy of nine lines in a dead woman's hand, and a sheet he had set in type himself.

---

The reconstruction is the order of a day.

It goes: a person is in a room. A second person is in the room and is not the person being written about. The second person asks, before the thing is said, whether the person is a party to it, and the answer is given, and the answer is written in a box. The day is entered in the first person's own hand and in nobody else's. The first person says the thing in a room. The first person does not read it back afterwards. The second person writes what was said and writes nothing else, and does not read it back to the person and does not show it to them.

That is the procedure. Five lines. It is printed in about nine hundred buildings and it is in about nine hundred registers and about four hundred of the lines in one of those registers carry no day at all, and one of those lines has nine words' worth of a living woman's hand in a box on a form.

He walked it eleven times: with the terms the schedule gives, with the terms the notice gives, with the terms a court would use. The eleventh time he did it in about four minutes and he wrote the eleven results out in a row and the results were the same eleven times.

**A procedure is not a person.**

That is the whole of the finding and it took him about nine minutes to write it out and it is the sentence the hearing has been building to since a clerk of thirty-four said at a counter that a record is what a person asks for when a person is going to do something with it.

**A record of what a person did in a room is a record of the shape of the room and the order the things were asked in.** The order is knowable. The shape is knowable. What a person was in that room to do is in neither of them, and the reason she was in the room is the only part of the whole thing anybody has ever wanted, and it is the part a procedure cannot carry. It is the part a form printed in nine hundred buildings was built to stop, and the building of it was not done by a villain and was done in about nine minutes by a person filling in a box.

He wrote that down and then he wrote the second half of it, which is the half that is the finding of the chapter, and the second half is that the procedure is not a machine for hiding the reason. **The procedure is a machine for making sure the reason is not needed**, and it does that by not asking, and the not-asking is in the first line, and the first line says the first person does not read it back afterwards.

---

Then he reached for the other instrument, because there are only two, and the other one is on his arm.

It did not do anything. That is the whole report.

**It reads nothing. It decides nothing. It does not know where the ford is**, and it has never known anything, and there is no office in this empire whose business it is to ask it anything. It is a symptom. It is not a power and it is not a seam and it is not a gift and it is not a price, and the price of the thing he did in the fourth hour of the second of the fifth month of next year is about forty-four pounds a year and five journeys and thirty days and three pounds and thirteen shillings and threepence gone out of it with no fund and no form for a fund, and the number is the wage and the wage is the price and the mark is neither.

He put his hand flat on the table and left it there for about a minute and a half and he was not disappointed, and he wrote that down too, because a man who is not disappointed is a man who expected something and the writing down of the not-being-disappointed is the useful part.

---

And then the day went, and it went in the middle of the ninth of the eleven walks, and nobody took it and nobody noticed it go.

**The sixth day of the third month. He was eleven. His mother put a sheet of paper into a box on a kitchen table and told him to leave the room, and he has written it down twice and told it to four people, and it is the first thing in a book with no office on it that has four pages headed *What the blankness is for* and a second heading *Written by a person it was done to, who was not asked*, and it is the whole of what he knows about where the idea came from and it is eleven words long and it is the only thing he has that is not in a copy of anything.**

It is not there any more.

Not blurred. Not faded. **He can say the date and he can say he was eleven and he can say there was a kitchen and a table and a box, and he cannot say whether he left the room or stood in the doorway.** He told that story in a rented room in the fourth week of the fourth month, out loud, to a woman of twenty-four, and it is gone, and it is not coming back, and there is no form anywhere in this empire for a person to have a day back.

He noticed it at about the ninth hour, because he went back to the fourth page to check what he had written there and there was a line on it and the line was about the district works and the handwriting was his and he had no memory of writing it.

He did not say anything for about nine minutes. He wrote the day down on a clean sheet with the day on the sheet, twice, in his own hand, and he read it back twice and the second reading was slower, and there was nobody in the room to hear either reading.

Then he picked up a sheet out of the corner of the table, because his hands wanted something in them. It was one of about four hundred of them and it was the sheet a witness is entered upon, and it is the same five lines that were put on a table in a hearing room in the second week of the fifth month of this year and read out there by a clerk of thirty-four who is a party, and he is not going to read it out again because he has it by heart and there is nobody in this room who needs it. The bundle was going to be sorted into a second order because somebody had asked for a second order, and the somebody was not the man who wrote them.

> *The first person says the thing in a room and the first person does not read it back afterwards.*
> *The day is entered in the first person's own hand and in nobody else's.*
> *The second person is entered in the room and the second person does not read it back to the person and does not show it to them.*
> *The second person writes what was said and writes nothing else.*
> *The second person asks the person, before the thing is said, whether she is a party to it, and the answer is written here.*

He held it. He did not read it out. He read it eleven times between the first and the fourth month of this year and he has it by heart and he had it by heart before that, and he had it by heart at the fifth hour this evening, and he was holding a piece of printed paper about nine feet from his face and going along it the way a person goes along a line of print when they have not got anything else to do with their eyes.

"You are checking it," said Tamsin Rook.

"I am holding it."

"You are checking it. You have checked that sheet eleven times since the second month and you have checked it in a corridor and in a rented room and once in a bay four hundred and thirty miles down a river with a lamp behind you." She did not look up. "**About four hundred forms, and you have compared the fifth line in every one of them with the fifth line in a copy of a form, and there is not one difference, and that is the most thorough piece of work anybody has done on that document in about a hundred and forty years, and it is work that can be done by a person with a good eye and a lamp and no instrument whatever, and you have never once been thanked for it and you are not going to be.**"

"I did it for my mother."

"You did it for your mother and you have been doing it four hundred times since and the two are not the same and I am not going to let you have them both," said Tamsin Rook, "because a fact said in a room with about thirty people in it is the only kind of fact this empire has, and a fact said in a room with two people in it at the eleventh hour is not that, and I know the difference and I am telling you the difference and I am not being kind, I am being exact."

He put the sheet back in the bundle, in the wrong place, and she put it right again about four seconds later without looking up, and neither of them said anything about that.

---

She was in the room and had been in it for the whole of it, and there are two reasons for that and they are two instruments and they have never been the same instrument and she has said so and nobody has ever merged them.

A paper, made in a form, in four days, with a day on it, requires her in any room he is in. It is a thing that can be produced by anybody holding it.

A watch on him is a separate instrument and it requires only that a person who is not from the office that cannot be named be in any room he is in.

**Both of them require her.** A person who is safe because nobody can instruct her and useful because somebody needs her standing in a room is a person with two boxes filled in on the same form, and she wrote that sentence down months ago and has not said it out loud to him until this month, and she is not going to say it tonight.

She did not stop working.

That is the sentence. She had a bundle of about four hundred forms on the corner of the table in about nine weeks of copying in two hands, and she was going through them putting them in a second order, and she went on going through them, and she did not put the bundle down, and she did not look up, and she did not say that she was sorry, and she had said in advance in the fourth week of the fourth month of this year that she was not going to comfort anybody and that a person who is being comforted has stopped being in the room.

"Give me a fact," he said.

"I am a party to an instrument about a dead woman's name," said Tamsin Rook, "**From the day I was served I can be served anything about it for the rest of my life, and there is no form anywhere for taking a party back out of a box, and that is the ninth time I have said it out loud.** Eight of the nine were about a person. That one is about about four hundred forms and I have not said it since the fourth month and I have not missed it, and I would like it recorded that I have not missed it."

"That is not a fact about me."

"No," she said. "It is a fact about the room. **A day of your own memory is not evidence.** It is not in a copy and it is not in a hand that anybody can compare and there is no form in this empire for producing it, and the one thing you had that no document in this matter has is the one thing that can be taken out of a book with a pen and a good reason. And there is a book in this matter with about four hundred lines in it about four miles off a road, and it has more of your mother in it than you are going to have again, and you cannot go and get it this week and you have not been able to since the second month, and there is no form that would let anybody help you with it."

---

Nobody comforted him. There was nobody in the room to, and the one person who was in the room had said in advance that she was not going to and gave the reason and the reason was true and it was not coldness.

**And Tamsin Rook did not put her own work down.** She finished the second order of about four hundred forms at about the eleventh hour and she wrote the day against the top sheet of it in her own hand and she did not show it to him and she did not read it out.

---

The last thing he did that night was to write the finding out fair, because a thing that is going in a book has to look like a thing somebody meant, and then he read it back to himself twice, the second reading slower, and there was nobody in the room to hear either of them.

> *The reconstruction is possible. It is possible out of a procedure and out of nothing else, and there is no other way to do it in this empire, and it will give a shape and not a woman. It will give the order of a room and the terms she had and the eleven steps and the fact that a box on a form was filled in by a person who was entitled to fill boxes. It will not give the reason she was in the room. It has never given anybody the reason. That is not a defect in the method. That is the method.*
>
> *I am going to do it anyway. A man who does a thing that cannot give him what he wants is doing a thing that is not heroism and is not cowardice and has no name on any form in this empire, and I have looked, and there is no form for it, and I am going to do it on the fourth day of next week.*

Nobody thanked him and he had said in advance, standing at the table with the lamp going, that nobody was to be, and nobody was, and the absence of it is a fact in this matter with a day on it somewhere else and not in this room.
