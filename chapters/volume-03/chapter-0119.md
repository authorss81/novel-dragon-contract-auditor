# Chapter 119: What a Party Is

Her term ran out in the third week of the third month.

Halla Wray has said out loud, in four rooms, to about ninety people, that she would rather be asked to hold it again than not. She said it in a shed, and she said it in a mill yard, and she said it in a kitchen, and she said it in a store, and she has not been asked, and she did not know until this week that she could not say yes twice.

The list of works in the store carries her name and a date against it, and the date is the third week of the third month, and after that date the list is a piece of paper. There is no line under it that says what happens after. There has never been a line under it. Sixty years of that list has never had a name at the foot of it, and it has had Halla Wray's for ten weeks, and there is no form anywhere in this empire that says what a woman does with a job that nobody has asked her to keep.

She was in the shed at about the seventh hour on the thirtieth day of the third month with nine men in it, and the boy, and the two of them and her own book-keeper, and nobody else.

---

"You are going to ask me," said Rennick Adley.

"I am going to ask you."

"Then ask me why I have not asked you before, because I have had a sheet in my coat for four days and I have not known what was on it, and the reason I have not asked you is that I did not know whether I was allowed to, and I have now looked, and I am allowed, and there is no form about it either way, and I have been reading a page of a book all week working out that a man who keeps a book is not a person who can ask."

He asked her, standing at the bench with the lamp not lit yet, in the ordinary way, in a shed, with nine men in it.

"The ford. The two posts and the nine stones, and the ford itself. **Two days' work and it is not on your list and it is not on anybody's list and it has not been on anybody's list since about thirty years ago, and I have got the page and I have read it out and the seventh work of eleven on that page is the ford, and the stones, and the two posts, and there is a boy of fifteen sitting on a wall at the north of the ford this afternoon with a leg.**

"I am asking you to hold it. In your own name. With a date on it."

"Yes," said Halla Wray.

Nine people said nothing at all for about four seconds, which is about nine seconds longer than a room of nine men usually takes, and then Piotr Wray said, "You have not asked her twice."

"I have not asked her twice," she said.

"Then you have just found out whether she can."

---

"Yes," she said. "**And here is the condition, and it is not a condition about the work.**

"The crew is paid out of something with a figure on it. Not out of a man's wage in a city. Not out of mine either.

"**I am not going to pay a crew twice in one year out of two men's pockets and say nothing about it.** That is the sentence and I have been carrying it since the sixteenth of the second month when a weigh-master asked me for a word and four of us argued for four minutes and settled on *held*, and the reason I have been carrying it is that I do not know whose pocket the second one comes out of, and the answer is that it comes out of Marek Kest's, because his is seventeen shillings and a penny a week and he has already paid three pounds and eleven and threepence of it into a bank, and if I put a second three pounds into him in the same year and nobody writes it anywhere, then in about four years somebody is going to ask how it is that the Slade Cut has been done for sixty years out of nobody's money and the answer is going to be a clerk's wage in a city, and it will be true, and it will be the only true sentence anybody has about this cut, and it will be about the wrong man.

"So: something with a figure on it. The works fund's head has been empty since the third week of the twelfth month and it held three pounds across a good quarter and never more than a fortnight of a crew, and I am not asking a district for it, because a company is not a person and cannot ask a district for anything, and a body cannot ask for money, and if nobody writes a name against this then the district does not know it is being asked.

"That is the condition. **I will hold the work. I will not pay for it out of him, and if nobody can pay for it out of anything then the work waits, and I would rather the work waited than that it be done free for the second time.**

"And the second half of the condition is a date and I am putting the date on it myself because nobody is going to give me one. **The fourth week of the fourth month, and not one day past it, because that is the week before the water.**"

---

Then she said the thing, and she said it standing, and it took about nine minutes, and there were nine people in the shed and about six of them repeated it afterwards and one of those six was a man who had been in the mill yard on the twentieth of the first month.

"**A party is a person with a door.**

"That is what a party is. I am one, and I have been one for ten weeks, and I have never once been asked what a party is, and I am going to answer it now and I am going to answer it for the room and not for myself.

"A party is a person who can be found. Who can be served. Who can be put in a room. **And who can say no.** And the last of those is the only one of the four that is worth anything and it is the one nobody asks about, and I know that because I put my own name in a box in the first month without being asked and my brother said no in nine words in a yard in front of forty people, and he was right, and the reason he was right is that the no is the only part of the box that is worth a farthing.

"Now. **Here is the thing I have been carrying since about the third hour of the afternoon and I have not said out loud, and I am going to say it now and then I am going to sit down on this bucket and I am not going to get up again tonight.**

"A boy of fifteen is on a wall at the north of the ford with a leg. **And there is no hearing for that.** I want that understood in this room. The work that hurt him was a cart and nine stones that a man took out of the throat of the ford in the fourth week of the second month, and there is no instrument in this matter that did it, and there is no box anybody can be put in about it, and there is no form on any desk in this district that says *a person may be heard about a thing that happened to him*, and there is not going to be one, and I have stopped waiting for one.

"And I have been sitting here for a quarter of an hour feeling like a coward, and I am going to say why. **The hearing that would have been held before that cart went in is the hearing we did not have, and it is gone, and nobody can hold it now for that boy.** The only thing anybody in this district can hold now is a hearing before the next one.

"That is all the road there is. There is not another one. I have looked."

---

"I have got a sheet," she said, "and I have had it in my coat since about the second hour of the afternoon and I could not read it because I cannot read a paragraph, and my brother read it out to me twice in a kitchen in the last week and I have got four words out of it and I am going to say the four words and then I am going to explain them badly.

"*Has no operation upon any person until that person has been heard about it in a room.*"

"That is a copy of an order of a court, in a copyist's hand, four pence, and the Company bought it out of the shilling, which is the first time that book has cost anything in eleven years except the rent, and there is one of them on the wall of a clerk's window four miles down the river that anybody may read for nothing, and neither of those is the reason I am telling you this.

"The reason is this. **That is a fact about a person and not about a document.** Everything else in this matter for two months has been a fact about a document, and I have learned this month that a document about nobody cannot be asked anything by anybody either, and that the whole of what I have been doing since the seventh of the first month has been arranging for a *person* to be in a room. Not a bank. Not a cut. Not the Company. A person.

"And it is available before the water. **That is the whole of what is available before the water.** And it is available to one person at a time, and it is never available to ninety-one households at once, and I have been trying for ten weeks to get a thing available to ninety-one households and there is nothing, and I have finally understood that there was never going to be anything, and that the shape of the thing is one person with a door.

"So. **A household can be made a party on purpose. In advance. By people who know exactly what a party is, because they have got one.**

"Which means a household can be put in a door before anything happens to it, and that is the only good use of a door in the history of doors, and I am going to say the price of it out loud in this shed because I have just learned it and I am not going to be the one who does it quietly.

"**The price is that the household is findable. From the day we ask, for ever, and for everything, and not only for the thing at the ford.** A man with a tray can come to this shed or to that store and ask about the four of them, and he does not have to have anything to do with a ford, and there is no way in this empire to give a name back, and none of us in this room can know in advance which tray it is going to be."

She stopped there for a moment.

"And I want to say the rest of it too, because I have been up that lane four hours' walk there and back since the fourth hour and I have been thinking about one man, and I have not stopped.

"**Aven Tace worked that out on his own, in his own kitchen, in about six years, with nobody to ask him.** He wrote a paper in the sixth year with his name at the top of it about four hundred and forty foot of bank behind his own mill, and he sent it four times and it came back four times, and he did not hand it to a soul for six years, and I know why and he told me in his own doorway in about nine sentences and he did not tell me that as a complaint. He told it to me as a fact.

"And the reason he could not hand it in is the reason I have just said out loud in this shed, and **I have been doing it to him for nine years.** Nine years of a list of works with a foreman's name at the foot of it, that the district could get hold of, about a bank four hundred feet from his own mill door, and I never once thought about the fact that every line of it was a line that could be asked about his ground.

"So: I did not know that was a thing you could be caught doing, and nobody ever told him either, and a woman of sixty-four found it in nine days because he told her in a yard. **That is the whole of how anybody in this matter has ever found out anything.**"

---

"And now the last of it, and then I am going to sit down.

"**Four households at the north of the ford are going to be asked, in advance, whether they will be heard, about a bank and a ford and a water, before the fourth month.**

"Four households. And I am not going to let this Company choose them, and I want that in a piece of paper with the day on it, and here is the reason and it took me nine minutes and I have said it twice in two rooms today.

"**The four are the four houses at the north of the ford because those are the four houses at the north of the ford and not because anybody chose them.** If this body had been allowed to choose four, it would have chosen four households that were not there. **It would have chosen the four that come to meetings. It would have chosen the four that can read a paragraph. It would have chosen the four that have not had a bad thing happen to them yet**, because those are the four everybody picks when anybody asks, and the four everybody picks are never the four the thing is about.

"So not choosing is the only honest thing available and it is the only thing this body has ever done that is worth a paragraph. And the four that are named are going to be people nobody in this room has chosen, and one of them is seventy-one and cannot read a paragraph and has a leg in her own lane, and one of them is a man of fifty-eight who has never once been asked to read anything in sixty years, and two of them are people this room has never met, and that is how we know it is right.

"And the day somebody asks them, they are parties. **Not on the day the water comes. On the day we ask.** And they will be findable from that day and not only about a ford, and I am saying it out loud in a shed in front of nine people so that in about four years when it is used against one of them, there are nine people who were in this room and heard a foreman say it first."

Piotr Wray said: "You understand what you have just done. You have taken a box out of a drawer in a water office where a keeper put two names in it without asking anybody, and you have put four households in a box of your own."

"Yes," said Halla Wray. "And I am in a box too and I have been since the first month, and the difference between me and that keeper is that **I am telling nine people in a shed, and she wrote it on her own sheet and told forty people in a yard, and neither of us asked anybody first, and the whole difference in the world is that we said so.**

"And I am not the First Seat and I am not any seat's agent and that is not a thing I am going to say in a shed for the fourth time in two months, and if you want it said then somebody can go and ask a keeper, and she will not repeat it either, and one hearing is enough for both of us."

She sat down on the upturned bucket that has been in that shed since her father's time.

"Nobody is to agree to anything tonight," she said. "**The Company has still chosen nobody. It has not chosen a holder and it has not chosen four and it is not going to.** Four doors are four doors and four people are going to be asked whether they will be heard, and if one of them says no then one of them says no, and that is the whole of what we are for, and it is the first time in sixty years that a thing I have said in a shed has been the shape of the next thing anybody does."

---

They put her name at the foot of it at about the ninth hour, and the date against it, and Rennick Adley wrote it in the store afterwards with the day on it in his own hand, and he wrote four things and read them back.

> *Held, the thirtieth day of the third month, at about the seventh hour, by H. Wray, foreman, in her own name. The ford: the two posts in their holes to the depth as they stood, the nine stones of the throat bedded, the ford looked at as a work. The fourth week of the fourth month, and not one day past it.*
>
> *To be paid out of something with a figure on it. Not out of a wage in a city. There is no such thing at present and the work waits rather than be done a second time for nothing.*
>
> *Four households at the north of the ford to be asked, in advance, whether they will be heard, about a bank and a ford and a water, before the fourth month. Asked by this Company on purpose, by people who know what a party is, and after the thing at the ford and not before it. **The four were not chosen. There were four houses and they are the four.** Asking them makes them parties on the day of asking and findable from that day, and this is written down before it is done and not after.*
>
> *Nothing is agreed. Nobody is chosen. The Company is not a guarantor, not a party and not answerable. — entered by R. Adley, and there is no name under this entry because I am not the holder of anything.*

Then they walked out to the bank at the third-of-the-way mark, which is about a quarter of a mile from the shed and is the place the whole matter is named after, and the outer face is eleven feet high where the crew left it on the twenty-first of the second month and the kerb stones are on the bed and the coping is on the bed and there is about four hundred and forty foot of it.

**A list of works with a foreman's name at the foot of it and a date, held for the second time in one year by a woman of fifty-one who was not asked the first time either, and four households that are about to become findable on purpose, and a boy of fifteen on a wall four miles up the lane who will get no hearing at all and is not a device and is nobody's heir.**

The water is in the third week of the fourth month.
