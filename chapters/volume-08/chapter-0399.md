# Chapter 0399: A Rented Room With A Table And Two Chairs, A Name In A Head, And A Woman Who Keeps The Ground Floor

The room is rented by the week and it is the same room as last week and the week before that, with the table in it and the two chairs and the lamp that is off until it is wanted, and what cold there is gets in off the plaster behind the chair instead of off the glass. **That room holds nothing to write on and nothing to write with**, and he has not wanted either of them on that table for about two years, and this is the first week in that time he has noticed the absence.

He is thirty-eight and Marek Kest is the name. He audits contracts and inheritances for an empire that does not pay him for it. **There is no office behind him of any kind, no money, no roll, no leave, and no person anywhere who is answerable for what he does with an ordinary day.** In the inside pocket of his coat there is a leaf with a line gone out of it, and the hand that took it out is not his mother's, and the name that belongs at the end of that line is the one thing he has wanted since before the fire. He has neither the name nor the claim that would go with it, and about four lights have gone over that leaf without ever producing an answer.

Nine weeks ago a clerk came the whole of the distance to this room and told him one thing out loud, and that thing is in his head and is not on anything. **There is no form for remembering**, and a name said once out loud to a lamp cannot be unsaid by anybody, and he has not written it down and has not gone and looked, and the woman who said it to him has not come back and has not sent for it and is not going to.

The woman who keeps the ground floor of that house has been at the bottom of those stairs for about twenty years and has never once been above the second one. She takes the rent on one day in the week and brings a lamp up at the end of it when one is wanted, and he has never had her name and has never given her his. **She is not the woman who came nine weeks ago.** That one keeps minutes about four hundred yards off the old river road and has been in that room nineteen years, and this one has never been inside that building and has never seen the inside of that room and does not know what was said in it.

It is the fourth day of the third week of the first month of the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is the seventh hour, and the lamp was not lit when she knocked.

---

She came up with the lamp in one hand and a folded slip in the other and put the lamp on the table and did not sit down.

"Half a week's rent is not due and I am not here about that."

"Then you have come up about the lamp."

"I have come up about the lamp and about something I have been turning over on that ground floor since the middle of the week, and I am going to say it once and go back down, and I am not going to come up about it again." She put her hands flat on the table, which is a thing she has never done. "**A person who knows a thing and does not write it down has to be the person who remembers it.** Remembering is not a form, and there is no book anybody keeps for it, and there is nowhere in this empire to put a thing you have to hold in your own head for as long as you live."

---

"I have been holding one for two years."

"You have, and it did not get heavier this week, and I know that it did not get heavier because I have had one of my own going about the same length of time. I have not slept properly since I worked out that I was going to be carrying it, and I have not said one word about it to anybody, including you, and that stops tonight." She took her hands off the table. "**There is no form for a person keeping a thing.** You are one of two people in this empire doing it. I have not asked you what it is and I am never going to ask you. The other one is me, and I have had mine since the year before last and I have carried it in that building every day since, and I am not complaining, and I want it entered that I am not complaining, because I have worked out that saying it out loud is cheaper than carrying it a third year."

---

"You have come up two flights of stairs to tell me you are not complaining."

"I have come up two flights of stairs to give you the other half of it, and I have been about twenty years getting to it and it took a week." She looked at the window with nothing in the middle of it. "And I am going to say one thing back at you that is not about the thing you know, because I do not know what it is and I am not going to. It is that I have had your money for two years and I have not asked you one question about where you go or what you do, and I am not going to start, and I would like that noticed as a thing I am doing and not a thing I have been failing to do. That is the whole of what I have."

---

**He stayed exactly where he was.** He sat there until about the tenth hour with the leaf on the table under both his hands, and the woman who keeps the ground floor went back down and did not come up again that night, and there is no pen in that room to write a thing with.

**A man who does not go and look in a week like this one has not been talked out of it by anybody, because there is nobody in that house who has said one word to him about it, and she said all the rest of it.**
