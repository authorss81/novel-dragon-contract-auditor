# Chapter 356: A Bay At The Top Of A Lane Off The Slade About Nine Foot By Eleven, A Bench Longer Than The Bay Is Wide, A Lamp Waiting For About The Seventh Hour, The South End Of A Hired Store Four Hundred Yards Off It, A Cross In Chalk At The Height Of The Third Link Of A Chain, And A Woman Of Fifty-One In A Box Nobody Has Asked Her About

The bay at the top of that lane measures about nine foot by eleven. The bench in it runs the length of a wall and is longer than the room is wide, and the lamp above the middle of that bench does not get lit until about the seventh hour, and it was not lit this afternoon either.

She is fifty-one. Her name is Halla Wray, and the foreman's job on that cut came to her because nine families agreed between themselves that it would be hers, and that was about nine years ago, and the nine of them met in a shed behind the second bay and agreed it in about an hour and a half.

This is the fourth day of the third week of the second month of the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is the afternoon, and the gate at the bottom of the lane is shut.

---

**Nobody has asked her about the box she is in, and nobody is going to.**

Not this week. Not in about two years. The men at the front of that bay have known since the day it went in, and not one of them has said a word about it since, and that is not a courtesy between them and it is not going to be lifted and it is not anybody's decision.

"**Being in a box and not being asked about it are the same arrangement, and that arrangement was made in that shed by nine families and not by me.** I have had about two years to find a way of saying it as though it were two things, and it is not two things, and there is not going to be a way."

"The entries that put me where I am were about a class and not about me. I am in it, and the class is a heading, and a heading is how a person turns into a company, and a company is a thing this empire can hold wages against and hand letters to and take to law."

"There is no form for disputing a class you have been entered in, and I have looked for one the way a person looks for a door in a wall they did not build, and the wall is about two years old and it is not coming down."

---

She said the rest of it once, in a room with about nine people in it, in the closed part of this, and she is not going to say it again.

What she said then was that the form had not been asking her whether she would represent anybody. It had been asking who she represents. **There is a column for the answer and there is no column for a refusal, and a person who writes nothing in it has not refused and has not agreed and is still in the class**, and that is the whole of what she has to say about it, and she has said it.

Nobody thanked her for saying it. There is not one form anywhere in this empire that requires a person to be thanked for telling the truth out loud in a room where nobody in charge of it is standing, and the going without being thanked is nobody's decision and is not going to become one.

---

At the back of that bay sit the two of seventeen, and they have been sitting there for weeks, on an engagement in writing with terms on it and a rate set out at the foot of it, and one of the four lines of it carries the name of a person who has never been in that lane.

**Neither of them was spoken to this afternoon, and not one of them was thanked, and not one of them was sent for, and not one of the two is going to start.**

The reader is paid forty-five pence a day and four days a week, and one day of that comes to anywhere between four pence and a shilling, depending on what gets set on the bench that day, and the rate is there because somebody who is not in that bay and never has been put a figure into a list.

The other one is seventeen as well, and nobody in this empire has ever said one word to her about any of it, and no paper anywhere asks a person of seventeen whether she would rather not, so there is no answer on the record anywhere, and no answer on the record is not the same thing as no.

Nobody moved them to the front of that bay. There has been room at the back of it for two for weeks, and about nine men are at the front of it, and the two of them are at the back, and that is where they are, and there is no answer in that bay about why and not one of the men in it has one.

---

**About four men in that bay cannot make sense of a paragraph, and not one question about a number has ever been put to any of them, and this afternoon was not the afternoon it happened.**

The man of about fifty-five with the chain is on the front bench. The chain is on him and it has been on him for years. He is not one of the four and says so out loud about once every few months to nobody in particular, and nobody has ever put him in a list of them and nobody is going to.

He has known for about four years that findability does not work on him. He has never once used it. Nobody knows that he knows it and he has never told anybody, and that is his business and not a thing anybody in that bay has standing to raise.

---

The man who keeps the books of that Company is four hundred yards up the same lane, and he has wanted the second half of a sentence of hers since the third month of the year after.

He has not asked. He is not going to. **The day he asks is the day she stops saying the half she has got**, and she has been giving him that half in a doorway about once a fortnight for about two years, and he has never once asked her for the rest of it and he has never once stood in a position where he could.

**There is no form for owning up to a thing you have only half said**, and a half is not a debt and is not a confession and is not an answer, and it is the only thing she has that anybody wants, and it is the shape of about half of everything in this matter.

She is not going up the lane to say the rest of it, and she is not going to make him come down it.

---

At about the fifth hour she went down the lane and across to the south end of the hired store and stood in the doorway of it a while, and there is nothing in that doorway that is hers and nothing in it that is anybody's.

The cross in the chalk on the end wall of the bay is at the height of the third link of that chain, and it means nothing at all. It belongs to nobody, and it stands about four years away from turning into anybody's evidence, and the chain it is level with is on a man, and nothing in that lane is ever going to turn either one of them into anything whatever, and she is not going to be the one who scrapes it off to see what is underneath.

"Is that bench still longer than the bay?" she said it out loud at about the sixth hour, to the bench, about the bench. It is, and nothing in this empire will let a bench be said to a person, and a foreman of fifty-one in a box nobody has asked her about is not somebody anybody is looking for, and the count of things anybody has asked out loud in this matter is the same as it was when the gate was shut and it is going to be the same in ten weeks.
