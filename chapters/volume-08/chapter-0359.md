# Chapter 359: A House With A Door On It About Four Miles Off, Four Steps Between The Paving And The Room, A Door Painted Twice In The Time He Has Been In It And Neither Time By Him, A Fire That Went Out At About The Fourth Hour, And Two People In It With An Arrangement And Not An Understanding

There is a house with a door on it about four miles off, and there are four steps between the paving and the room, and the door has been painted about twice in the time he has been in this and neither of the two times was his.

The fire went out at about the fourth hour. Neither of them said anything about it and neither of them went and got anything to put on it.

This is the second day of the second week of the third month of the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is about the fifth hour, and it is about an hour since the fire went out and about an hour before he has to be out of the door.

---

He is thirty-four or thereabouts and the hand is a fair one, and the fair hand is what a man is paid for when a man wants his figures copied out until two sets of them agree. A man has to be on somebody's list for that, and he is on one four days a season, and this is not one of the four.

She is at the foot of those four steps the way a person is of a place they live in. She has told him out loud that she is not going to ask him about his week, and he has never once asked her to change her position about it, and that is a thing between them that neither of them has to be grateful for.

---

The last time he did a thing of that kind he told her six weeks before he did it.

He told her out loud, on purpose, before anybody had asked him for anything, and he did not ask her first, and the reason he gave her at the time was in about as many words as it takes, and it is still the truest thing he has ever said to her.

**If he had asked her she would have said no, or said yes and been frightened, and either of those would have been him standing in a room arranging it.** He would rather be the villain of that than the man who checked first. That is the whole of his reasoning, and she has never once agreed with it, and she has never once asked him for it again.

**There is no form for agreeing in advance to something that has not been proposed**, and the not having one is what makes the whole of what he does possible, and it is also what makes it the thing she has never got used to.

And this time he is not telling her at all. He is going out of that door in about an hour and he has not said where to and she has not said where to, and **the not telling is the same absence as the not asking, and it is not a better way of doing anything and not a worse one either.**

---

**She is not thanked. She has not been thanked.** She did not ask him how it went the last time and she is not going to, and she said that out loud, and saying it out loud did not make it a rule and it did not make it a wall.

He does not thank her either and she has never asked him to, and nothing anywhere in this empire calls for a person to be thanked for putting up with a thing or for not putting up with a thing.

Those two are one arrangement and not two, and they have been in this house about as long as he has been in the room over the chandler's shop, and neither of them was arranged by anybody else.

"What he does with an hour is not a thing I have any standing to have an opinion about," she said out loud at about the fourth hour, to the fire, about the hour. The fire was going out while she said it and neither of those had anything to do with the other, and there is no form for saying a sentence like that to another person and having it be received, and it was received.

---

There is no form for settling it afterwards either. He looked for one for about two years. He looked in the places a man looks when a thing is going wrong between him and one other person, and what is in those places is a form for telling a person that a thing has been done and a form for a person to say that a thing has not been done, and neither of those is what the two of them would want and one of them is not a thing anybody in this house would accept.

**What there is instead is a person deciding, and a person having the deciding done to her.** That is not the same as a wall, because she can come out of it whenever she likes, and it is not the same as being asked, because she has not been asked and never will be, and those two together are the whole of what is in this house at about the fifth hour.

---

The bill for being findable is not his and it stands on somebody else's account.

He found that out at second hand, from a man who was told something by a man who was told something by a man who has not been in this room in about two years, and he did not go and look, and he still does not know whose account it is.

**The bill is not his and he is not going to find out whose it is by asking, and the not asking is not the same as not caring and it is not the same as care**, and the difference between those two has never once come up in this house.

A woman of about thirty-five lives next door. She came out on her own step twice while he was standing at the bottom of the four steps and put two things down and went in again, and she did not speak to him and he did not speak to her. The two doors are about four feet apart, and nothing in this empire would let him put to her the question of whether she would mind being told a thing before she is told it.

That absence is the reason he told his wife the one time he ever told her anything, and it is the reason he is not going to tell her this, and it is one absence and not two, and it is not going to be filled.

"Is that fire still out?" he said it out loud at about the fifth hour, to the room, about the fire. It is, and it has been since about the fourth hour, and nothing in this empire will let a fire be said to a person, and the two people in that room have an arrangement and not an understanding, and the count of things anybody has asked out loud in this matter is the same as it was when it went out and it is going to be the same in ten weeks.
