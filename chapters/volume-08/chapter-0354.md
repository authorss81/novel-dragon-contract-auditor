# Chapter 354: The Same Counter Two Weeks Later, The Other End Of It, The Last Hour Before It Shuts, A Drawer With Four Things In It That Have Not Been Opened, And A Woman Nobody Has Asked Anything In About Two Years

The counter is the same counter at the top of the same stair, and she is at the other end of it, and the fee board is on the wall behind her with its four items on it and not one of the four being time.

She is twenty-four. Her name is Tamsin Rook, and she has been at that counter about two years.

This is the fourth day of the fourth week of the first month of the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is about the fifth hour, and the stair is empty and the counter shuts in about an hour.

---

**Nobody has asked her anything in about two years, and that is a choice and not a fault**, and the whole of what that costs is what this is: the only person in a room who is never asked anything, wanting to be asked every working day since about the seventh month of next year.

**A person who is not asked has not been given a duty**, and she has known that since about the seventh month of next year, and she has never once known it to be untrue of her, and nothing at that counter was ever put to her to begin with.

Wanting is not owing and owing is not asking. She has gone about two years with this sitting nearer a hunger than a grievance, and the distance between those two is about fourpence a copy and nothing else at all, and nobody has ever put a figure on it either.

---

A man came up that stair once, in the second week of the tenth month, wanting nothing whatever from that counter, and he put one question into the air and did not wait to find out what came back, and she gave him nothing.

That is the last time anybody has put a question to her. It is a fortnight out of about two years ago, and she has not been able to decide since whether she wishes she had answered. **She is answering nothing this week either, and nobody is putting anything to her this week**, and those two facts are not going to be set side by side by anybody except her, and she has not set them side by side.

The woman at the other end of that counter has been there eleven years and has said the whole of a rule out loud to a man who came for a copy four times, and one of those four was in front of this one, and she was not thanked for any of the four and she was not asked about any of the four.

---

The undertaking of the fourth of the first month has not been retired and has not been amended and has never been exercised. It has now gone about two years that way, it is not going to be different this week, and a correct non-use is worth as much as a use and is entered as one.

The reason she gives for not exercising it is not one of the reasons she gave before. She has given three others, and the third of those was about the room and the fourth was about the season, and this one is that **nobody asked her, and nobody has asked her since, and there is no form anywhere in this empire for asking a person whether they will mind being told a thing before they are told it.** The reason there is no form is that there is no form.

She has been looking for one for about two years. She looked in about nine places where one would have been kept, and what she found in every one of them was a column with a heading over it, and a column with a heading over it is a machine, and a machine is the thing that never asks anybody anything.

"There is no form for objecting to a thing nobody has ever asked of you, and I have wanted to object to this for about two years and there is nowhere to put the wanting," she said it out loud at about the fifth hour, to the fee board, about the undertaking. The board has four items on it and none of the four is an undertaking, and the woman at the other end of the counter did not look up, and there is no form for hearing a woman say that to a board.

---

In a book of her own, on a page she has ruled by hand, there are two boxes with the same name written in both of them, and neither of the two has been merged into anything.

At the head of a line she put there about the woman of thirty-four is the name of a woman who has stood at that counter eleven years and who once put a coin back on it instead of taking it. She worked that out in a room of her own, on her own time, and what she worked out was that the line is a window and not a wall.

Nothing in this empire can be served out of a line in a person's own book when that person's own name is at the head of it. It turns into a wall the moment she is asked whether she minds its being there, and she is not going to be asked, and she is not going to ask, and nothing anywhere has a column for either of those two things.

The box of about four hundred blanks under that counter is not hers and she has never had it open and nobody has ever told her it was there to be opened.

---

There are four things in a drawer at her end of the counter. **The fourth of them has not been opened and has not been sent and has not been touched, and it is where it has been, with three others, and no key to that drawer has ever been offered to her and she has never asked for one.**

She has not ruled a third line this week and is not going to. The decision about whether she trusts a man's judgment is unmade, has not been made in any of the years before this one, and is not going to be made in a dead hour at the other end of a counter with a board on the wall behind her.

"Is that drawer still shut?" she said it out loud at about the fifth hour, to the drawer, about the drawer. It is, and it has been for about two years, and nothing in this empire will let a drawer be said to a person, and a woman at a counter who has not been asked anything in about two years is not somebody anybody in that building is looking for, and the count of things anybody has asked out loud in this matter is the same as it was when the stair emptied and it is going to be the same in ten weeks.
