# Chapter 354: The Same Counter Two Weeks On, The Other End Of It, The Last Hour Before It Shuts, A Drawer With Four Things In It That Have Not Been Opened, And A Woman Nobody Has Asked Anything In About Two Years

The counter is the same counter and it is at the top of a stair with no other way up, and she is at the other end of it, and the fee board is on the wall behind her with four items on it and not one of the four is time.

She is twenty-four. Her name is Tamsin Rook, and she has been at that counter about two years.

This is the fourth day of the fourth week of the first month of the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is about the fifth hour, and the stair is empty and the counter shuts in about an hour.

---

**Nobody has asked her anything in this chapter and that is a choice and not a fault**, and the whole of the chapter is what it costs to be the only person in a room who is never asked anything, and to have wanted to be asked every working day since about the seventh month of next year.

**A person who is not asked has not been given a duty**, and she has known that since about the seventh month of next year, and she has never once known that it did not apply to her, and the reason the undertaking sits where it sits is that nobody ever put it to her in the first place.

Wanted is not owed and owed is not asked. She has gone about two years with a thing in her that is closer to a hunger than to a grievance, and the difference between those two is about fourpence a copy and nothing else, and nobody has ever put a figure on it either.

---

A man came up that stair once, in the second week of the tenth month, wanting nothing at all at that counter, and put one question in the air and did not wait for the answer, and she did not give him one.

That is the last time anybody has put a question to her, and it is a fortnight of about two years ago, and she has not been able to decide since whether she wished she had answered it. **She is not answering anything this week either, and nobody is asking her anything this week, and the two of those are not going to be put together by anybody but her and she has not put them together.**

The woman at the other end of the same counter has been there eleven years and has said the whole of a rule out loud to a man who came for a copy four times, and one of those four times was in front of her, and she was not thanked for any of the four, and she was not asked about any of the four.

---

The undertaking of the fourth of the first month is live. It is unretired and it is unamended and it has not been exercised, and it has not been exercised for about two years, and it is not going to be different this week, and a correct non-use is as good as a use and is entered as one.

The reason she gives for not exercising it is not one of the reasons she gave before. She has given three others and the fourth of them was about the room and the fifth was about the season, and this one is that **nobody asked her, and nobody has asked her since, and there is no form anywhere in this empire for asking a person whether they will mind being told something before they are told it, and the reason there is no form is that there is no form.**

She has looked for one for about two years. She looked in about nine places that would have had one and found a column with a heading over it, and a column with a heading over it is a machine, and a machine is exactly the thing that does not ask anybody anything.

"There is no form for objecting to a thing that has not been asked of you, and I have wanted to object to this for about two years and there is nowhere to put the wanting," she said it out loud at about the fifth hour, to the fee board, about the undertaking, and the board has four items on it and none of the four is an undertaking, and the woman at the other end of the counter did not look up, and there is no form for hearing a woman say that to a board.

---

In a book of her own, on a page ruled by hand, there are two boxes with the same name in both of them, and neither of the two has been merged with anything.

At the head of a line she put there about the woman of thirty-four is the name of a woman who has been at that counter eleven years and who put a coin back on it once rather than taking it. **She worked that out in a room of her own, on her own time, that the line is a window and not a wall.**

It is a window because a line in a book of her own with her own name at the head of it is the only thing in this empire that nobody can serve anybody with. It is a wall the moment she is asked whether she minds it being there, and she is not going to be asked, and she is not going to ask, and nothing anywhere has a column for either of those about a page of somebody's own book.

The box of about four hundred blanks under that counter is not hers and she has never opened it and nobody has ever told her it is there to be opened.

---

There are four things in a drawer at that end of the counter. **The fourth of them has not been opened and has not been sent and has not been touched, and it is where it has been, with three others, and nobody has offered her a key to that drawer and she has not asked for one.**

She has not ruled a third line this week and is not going to. The decision about whether she trusts a man's judgment is unmade, is not on the page, has not been made in any of the years before this one, and is not made this week, and she is not going to make it in a dead hour at the other end of a counter with a board on the wall behind her.

"Is that drawer still shut?" she said it out loud at about the fifth hour, to the drawer, about the drawer, and it is, and it has been for about two years, and nothing in this empire lets a drawer be said to a person, and a woman at a counter who has not been asked anything in about two years is not a person anybody in that building is looking for, and the count of things anybody has asked out loud in this matter is the same as it was when the counter opened this morning and it is going to be the same in ten weeks.
