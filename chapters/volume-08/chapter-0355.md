# Chapter 355: A Rented Room Two Streets Off A Street That Goes Down To The River Road, A Table, A Stool, A Bed Against The Wall And A Chair Against None, About Four Feet Of Wall He Does Not Use, And A Morning In Which Two Things Are Refused And Two Are Not Used

The room is taken by the week, and it is two streets back from a street that runs down to the river road, and there is a table in it and a stool, and the bed is against one wall while the chair is against none, and there is a book on the table with no office on the cover of it.

The window looks at the back of another house. Four feet of the wall beside it is bare and has been bare for as long as he has been in this room, and he does not use it, and nothing has ever hung on it, and there is no fire in the room and there is not going to be one.

He is thirty-eight. His name is Marek Kest, and he chose a kind of labour a man is free to turn down, out of his own hand and with nobody's advice, and that happened a long time ago and is not happening this morning. **A bar of his own came off him on the twelfth of the ninth month of last year on an application of one line which he wrote himself.**

This is the second day of the first week of the second month of the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is the morning.

---

The gate is a bar of iron across the end of a yard with a hut at one side of it, and the man at the gate is about forty and has never once argued with anybody in his life.

The man at the gate comes out of the hut to lift the bar and goes back in afterwards, and he does that without looking along it, which is not what a man does at a bar he has lifted every working morning for years. There is nothing on the other side of that bar that has not been there since he last stood at it, and he stands at it anyway, and standing at it is the whole of his part.

"Two days," he said. "You have the second and the fourth this week. Two of the five are shut and I did not shut them."

"The rate is the rate and the rate is what it is."

"It is the rate and the rate is not why," said the man at the gate, and he said it without any weight at all, the way a man says a thing he has said four hundred times. "A day on the wall that is not on a district's list is three shillings and a day on the wall that is on one is a shilling, and the man who put those two figures next to each other is not stood here and neither are you. I am stood here. You are stood here on two days and you were stood here on one of them last week, and if you want a third you are not getting it from me, and if you want to know why you are not getting it from me you are not getting that either."

"I know what the rate is not."

"You know a good deal for a man with no paper on him," said the man at the gate, and lifted the bar, and the bar came up about as far as it always comes up, and he stood at it until the third hour and then went home along a street that goes down to the river road.

He got up at the hour he gets up at and was at that bar while the man in the hut was still getting into his coat, and he has not once been late at it and the man at the gate has not once said that he was, and there is no book anywhere with the two of those facts in it.

He was not stood at the gate on the second day. He was not stood at it on the fourth day either. He was at the room, and the stool, and the four feet of wall, and a clean sheet of paper that stayed clean.

**He holds nothing at all.** There is no paper in this empire that says he has leave to do what he does, and there has not been one in the whole of the time he has been doing it, and he is in no office and in no fund and on no list, and no keeper anywhere has his name written down for the purpose of finding him. That is the arrangement. He chose it and he has never asked anybody to confirm it and he never will.

**That labour is not being re-decided this week and is not going to be.** It is the worse of the two positions and he has known that since the third week of the fourth month of the year after next, and knowing it is not the same as going back, and there is nothing behind him that would be any use to anybody, himself included.

---

**He does not attempt the reading, and he said the reason out loud in that room, to himself and to four feet of wall, and it took him about as long as it takes to cross a floor.**

It has been done to him twice. Each time it took a whole day out of his mother's own history, and he was not in the room for that day, and he was in a house in a lane in this town watching her at a table. He has the address of it. He has the length of it. He has the two of them the way a man has two things somebody else wrote down on his behalf.

He has never written the address down and he has never been asked for it and he does not think anybody in this town would know the house if it were described to him. What he has kept of it is the order of the two days, and the order is a thing a person can hold on to for eleven years without meaning to, and he has held on to it without meaning to.

He is thirty-eight and he cannot tell you whether he came out of that house or stood in the doorway of it and waited, and it is eleven years since, and it is the two of them together that have gone and not one of them alone.

**The third one is not a thing he is going to spend on a man at a rail.** The man at a rail is about nine miles off, in a building off the old river road, and he is sixty-one, and there is a hand at the foot of about nine hundred sheets a year at that building, and nobody has asked that hand one thing in about nineteen years. There is no form for remembering, and there is no form for arguing with a wall, and four feet of that wall is bare and nothing is going on it this week either.

---

**And he does not do the other thing either, the one he is able to do, and a correct non-use is entered as one.**

It would take him about four minutes and a clean sheet, and about four seconds to say out loud, and he has done it once and it worked, and four people heard what it cost before any of it had been said, and about nineteen days of a store's rent came out of the price of it.

He knows the nine words and has known them since the page they are on was written, and he could get at them without looking, and that has never once been the difficulty. The difficulty is the four seconds and what a room does with a person in the four seconds after a person has said a thing, and you cannot practise that at a table with a book in front of you.

He did not do it this week and he is not going to do it this month. The reason he gave for that is nine words long and it is on a page in another year, and he is not going to say it a second time to four feet of wall, and a thing said once and not said again has not got any smaller.

He is not at a stage. He is not going to arrive at one, and there is nothing in this empire that would put a man in a rented room and examine him about one, and no office that could hand one out, and nobody he has ever met who is the sort of person a question could be put to.

---

There is a mark on him about three inches long. It is on the inside of his left wrist, and it is a symptom and not a price.

He has looked at it often enough to have it by heart and not often enough to have made anything of it. It is the length it was and the shape it was, and it has not once been any use to him in front of a person, and he has never held it up where a person could see it, and he is not going to.

**It reads nothing and it decides nothing and it does not know where the ford is**, and it has settled nothing in two years, and he turned the sleeve back this week about as often as a man turns a sleeve back, which is for the water.

What the thing in that store cost him was a shilling and sixpence and this. A shilling and sixpence came out of it as a day he was not stood at a gate for. **The wage is the price and the mark is what he has instead of a receipt for it**, and there is no form anywhere in this empire that gives a man a receipt for what he has paid, and not having one is the ordinary condition of a man who is on no list.

Nobody in that store was saved and he has said so out loud, once, in about four seconds, and nobody has thanked him for it, and not one form anywhere requires a person to be thanked for not doing a thing.

---

He is not going up a stair. There is a woman four miles off at the top of one who would be standing in any room he stood in, because two instruments want that and they are not one instrument. She has been the price of a party out loud ten times and has not been thanked once. He said the reason out loud to about nine men in a bay, and she is not told that he said it and nobody is going to tell her.

There is no fund and no list and no form and no carrier with a thing for him. Nobody has sent him and nobody is going to.

Nobody anywhere has ever asked him to keep a thing out of that book. He has written in it in the mornings and he has never once carried it out of the room, and there is no rule anywhere about that and no person anywhere who would know if there were one.

He folded the clean sheet and put it in the book on the table, which has no office on the cover of it, and put his hand flat on the bare wall for the length of a breath, the way a man touches a thing on his own account, and then he sat down on a stool that is not against anything and drank tea he had made too strong, because that is what he does when he is afraid.
