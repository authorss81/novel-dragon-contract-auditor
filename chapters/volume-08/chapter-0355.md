# Chapter 355: A Rented Room Two Streets Off A Street That Goes Down To The River Road, A Table, A Stool, A Bed Against The Wall The Chair Is Not Against, A Book With No Office On It, And About Four Feet Of A Wall He Does Not Use

The room is taken by the week and it is two streets off a street that goes down to the river road, and there is a table in it and a stool, and the bed is against a wall the chair is not against, and there is a book on the table with no office on it.

The window looks at the back of another house. About four feet of the wall beside it is bare and has been bare for as long as he has been in the room and he does not use it and has never hung anything on it.

He is thirty-eight. His name is Marek Kest, and he chose a kind of labour a man is free to refuse, out of his own hand, and that is a thing that has already happened and is not happening today. **A bar of his own came off him on the twelfth of the ninth month of last year on an application of one line which he wrote himself.**

This is the second day of the first week of the second month of the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is the morning, and the room is cold in the way a room is cold when the wall behind the bed shares a wall with somebody else's stove, and there is no fire in it and there is not going to be one.

---

**He holds nothing.** There is no paper in this empire that says he has leave to do a thing and there has not been one in all the time he has been doing this, and he is in no office and in no fund and in no list. There is no name of his in anything anybody keeps for the purpose of finding him, and that is the arrangement and he chose it and has never asked anybody to confirm it and never will.

The labour he chose is a gate that can refuse him and the man at that gate is about forty and does not argue. He was stood at that gate on two days out of the five this week and was not stood there on the other three, and nothing in the arrangement explains the two and the three and nobody at the gate has been asked to.

**That labour is not re-decided this week and is not going to be.** It is a worse position than the other one and he has known that since the third week of the fourth month of the year after next, and knowing it is not the same as going back, and there is nothing to go back to that would be any use to anybody.

---

**He does not attempt the reading, and he gave the reason out loud in the room, to himself and to about four feet of wall, and it took him about as long as it takes to cross a floor.**

Twice it has been done to him and it takes a day out of his mother's own history every time, a whole one, and he was not in the room for the day and was in a house in a lane in this town watching her at a table. He has the address of it. He has the length of it. He has the two of them the way a man has two things somebody else wrote down for him.

He is thirty-eight and he cannot say whether he came out of that house or stood in the doorway of it and waited, and it is eleven years since and it is the two of them together that have gone and not one of them alone.

**The third one is not a thing he is going to spend on a man at a rail**, and the man at a rail is about nine miles off in a building off the old river road and is sixty-one and has a hand at the foot of about nine hundred sheets a year and has not been asked one thing in about nineteen years. There is no form for remembering, and there is no form for arguing with a wall, and about four feet of that wall is bare and has been bare since the day he came in and nothing is going to be hung on it this week either.

---

**And he does not do the other thing, the one he is able to do, and a correct non-use is entered as one.**

It would take him about four minutes and a clean sheet and about four seconds to say out loud, and he has done it once and it worked and about four people heard what it cost before any of it was said, and about nineteen days of a store's rent came out of the price of it.

He did not do it this week and he is not going to do it this month. The reason he gave for that is nine words long and it is on a page in another year and **he is not going to say it again to about four feet of wall, and a thing said once and not repeated is not a thing that has grown smaller.**

He is not at a stage. He is not going to get to one, and there is nothing in this empire that would put a man in a rented room and examine him about one, and no office that could hand it out, and nobody he has ever met who could be asked to look at a question and answer it.

---

The mark is about three inches long and it is on the inside of his left wrist and it is a symptom and not a price.

**It reads nothing and it decides nothing and it does not know where the ford is**, and it has not settled anything in about two years, and he turned the sleeve back this week about as often as he turns a sleeve back, which is for the water.

The price of the thing he did in a store was a shilling and sixpence and this, and the shilling and sixpence was a day he was not stood at a gate for. **The wage is the price and the mark is what he has instead of a receipt**, and there is no form anywhere in this empire that gives a man a receipt for what he has paid and the not having one is the ordinary condition of a man who is not on a list.

Nobody in that store was saved and he has said so out loud, once, in about four seconds, and nobody has thanked him and not one form anywhere requires a person to be thanked for not doing a thing.

---

He is not going up a stair. There is a woman about four miles off at the top of one who would be in any room he stood in, because two instruments require that and they are not one instrument, and she has been the price of a party out loud ten times and has not been thanked once, and he has said the reason out loud to about nine men in a bay and **she is not told that he said it and nobody is going to tell her.**

There is no fund, no list, no form and no carrier with a thing for him. Nobody has sent him and nobody is going to.

"Is that wall still bare?" he said it out loud at about the fifth hour, to the wall, about the wall, and it is, and nothing in this empire lets a wall be said to a person, and he is a man nobody has sent for and nobody has thanked, and the count of things anybody has asked out loud in this matter is the same as it was when he got up and it is going to be the same in ten weeks.
