# Chapter 353: The Fourth Counter Of A Table In Auremar At The Top Of A Stair With No Other Way Up, A Fee Board Of Four Items On The Wall And Not One Of The Four Being Time, And A Box Of About Four Hundred Bought And Unfilled Blanks Under The Counter

The counter is at the top of a stair and there is no other way up to it, and it is open from the second hour to the sixth, and about nine people a day come up that stair in a week when the weather is against them.

She is thirty-four and her name is Marn Ottery, and she has been at that counter eleven years, and she is not a lawyer, and **she is the reason she is still standing at that counter after eleven years and she has never once said that out loud to anybody and it is not a thing anybody has thanked her for.**

This is the fourth day of the third week of the first month of the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is the second hour, and the lamp over the received room is lit.

---

One man came up the stair in the whole of the morning and he came for a copy and the standing costs fourpence and he paid it and he stood at the far end of the counter and did three copies and a half of a fourth and gave the half back.

Nobody put a question to him. He did not put one to her. **That is not a courtesy between them and it is what a counter at the top of a stair with no other way up is.**

And then she said the thing, out loud, to the man and to the stair, standing where she stands, and it was not a speech and it was not for him, and he has never in his life asked what a blank is.

---

"A border household has to answer. That is the first thing and I am going to say it before I say anything else, because people come up here with the second thing already in their heads and they have not earned it yet."

"A thing offered to a border household at no charge has to be answered, and the answer has to be entered, and the entering of it has to be a form and not a letter. Three things in one sentence, and the third of them is the whole of it, and the third of them is why it worked and it did not work by being kind."

"It got an answer in about three weeks. The same question at a House's own table took about eleven and then said no, and the no cost a hundred and forty years, and a hundred and forty years is not a slow answer. It is the answer you get when the thing doing the answering is somebody."

"**And the reason it worked is that the law does not need a person.** A border household is not a person either. It was written about two hundred years ago by people who had noticed that, and they had better sense about it than we have, and they wrote it down in about nine sentences and did not give it a name that wanted watching."

---

"Now here is the shape of it and I am going to say the shape first, because the shape is the part that carries.

"A column with a heading over it is a machine. A machine gets asked things all day and it does not mind being asked, because it cannot say no and it cannot be hurt by the answer, and that is the whole of what makes it useful and the whole of what makes it dangerous at a size.

"A column with no heading over it is a person. **A person is only ever asked one way, and the way is out loud in a room by somebody who wants something**, and about nine hundred rooms a year have a column like that in them and not one of the nine hundred has ever had anybody put to it in one."

"There is no form for explaining what a blank is to the person it is printed for, and that is not a failure of the making of the page. The page works. A blank is the only piece of paper in this empire that cannot be held against anybody, and a box of about four hundred of them is the nearest thing to a place a person can put a question that anybody in this empire has ever had. It is not a place."

---

Under the counter there is a box of about four hundred blanks, bought and unfilled, and it has been under that counter eleven years.

**No form that holds it was ever drawn.** Nobody has ever asked her to account for it and nobody is going to, and the box is not a post and it is not an offer and it has not been used as a place to leave anything, and it is four hundred and some pieces of paper that cost a district money and cannot be returned and cannot be entered anywhere.

"There is no form for checking a box of about four hundred blanks against anything, and the not having one is the reason it is still under there, and I would like it recorded that the reason a thing is allowed to stay is very often that nobody can be shown the end of the road about it."

---

The fee board on the wall behind her has four items on it and not one of the four is time.

The fourth of the four is the one three clerks cannot account for. Three clerks have looked at it, including the two of them, and none of the three can say what it is for, and she wrote it there herself in her own hand about four years ago and cannot remember being in the room when she wrote it, and she has said that out loud twice, and nobody thanked her for saying it either time.

They did not say anything about it this morning. **The fourth item is hers and the two other clerks' and it is not the book-keeper's, and nobody at that counter has put one question to another about it out loud in about four years, and wanting to is not asking and has never been asking.**

"Is that stair still the only way up?" she said it out loud at about the fifth hour, to the stair, about the stair, and it is, and it has been since the building was put up, and nothing in this empire lets a stair be said to a person, and a woman who has been at a counter eleven years and has said the whole of a rule out loud four times has not been asked one thing back, and the count of things anybody has asked out loud in this matter is the same as it was when that counter was opened and it is going to be the same in ten weeks.
