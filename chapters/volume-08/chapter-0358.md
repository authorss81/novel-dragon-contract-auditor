# Chapter 358: A Passage Of About Four Hundred Yards Off A Flood Terrace At The Back Of A Building Off The Old River Road, Racks Going Down It And Coming Back Light By One, A Lock At One End And A Room With A Rail At The Other, And A Woman Of About Twenty-Six Who Counts In Her Head

The passage runs off a flood terrace at the back of a building off the old river road and it is about four hundred yards long, and the flags of it come up cold underfoot in every month of the year, and there is no fire at either end of it.

There is a lock at the end she stands at. There is a room at the other end with a rail in it standing about four feet off the floor, and that rail is not on the passage's work.

She is about twenty-six and she has been in that building about four years and she is not one of the three clerks in it and she is not in this and has never been in it.

This is the second day of the first week of the third month of the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is about the fourth hour, and the racks go down all day and come back.

---

A rack went down at about the second hour and came back light by one, and one is the usual number and one is what it is for as long as she has been standing at this end of it.

**She counts them in her head and she does not touch the rack**, and she has not touched one in about four years, and nobody has ever asked her to and nobody has ever asked her anything at all.

**Her figure has gone up since the seventh month of the year after and she has not said by how much and is not going to.**

She worked that out in about four minutes, standing at a lock, one time. The figure is the only one this empire keeps on how many people are sitting inside a thing nobody can be asked about. Everything else anybody counts in this empire is a count of hands, hours, yards, stone, days, and a count of persons under a heading. Hers is a count of persons under nothing.

---

A number in a book is a column, and a column wants a heading, and a heading over that figure would be a heading over about nine hundred rooms at once.

**A person cannot be under a heading like that and stay a person**, and she is not going to be the one who puts it there, and it is not that a heading would be wrong. A heading is how this empire does arithmetic and it is the right instrument for counting most things, and it is the one thing on earth that would take the last of the air out of about four hundred yards of cold flags.

So the figure stays where it is, which is in her, and it is not written down, and it is not going to be handed on to anybody this month or in any month after it.

"There is no form for answering three questions a person has not asked, and I have three of them for a man of about sixty-one who stands on the wrong side of a rail about four hundred yards from where I am, and he has been in that room and I have not been in that room, and I am not going to be."

"There is no form for repeating what was said in a room at the end of those flags, and I am not going to repeat it, and the not repeating is not loyalty to anybody and is not a thing I have been asked to do."

---

**Nothing that comes down those flags goes into that room and nothing that comes out of it comes up them.**

There is no list that reaches it, no rack that stops at it, and no person whose work takes her to that door. A person carrying things for a living has no reason to stop at a door and nothing to carry back, and that is the whole of why a thing said out loud in that room stays in that room.

**It is not because anybody is careful.**

The rail is about four feet off the floor and there is nothing on it this week and it is not a thing anybody is waiting for, and she has looked at it from four hundred yards off twice in four years and has not gone up.

---

**She is the only other person in this matter who knows what was said in that room.**

She was not in the room when it was said and she has never been in that room. About nine people were in it the last time anything was said in it, and people come out of a room and stand about four hundred yards apart in the cold and say things to each other, and she is at one end of that and is one of the people they say them to, and she has never once repeated any of it to anybody.

There is no form anywhere in this empire that would let anybody put to her the question of what she heard. That is the whole of what her knowing is worth, and it is worth nothing, and she would not give it away if there were a form for giving it away.

She has three questions for him and the fact that he came at all gave her no fourth. It has never once given her a question of any kind and she is not a party to anything and has never been served with a paper about any of it.

---

"Is that rail still four feet?" she said it out loud at about the sixth hour, to the flags, about the rail, and it is, and nothing in this empire lets a rail be said to a person, and a woman who counts in her head and does not touch the rack is not a person anybody has a use for, and the count of things anybody has asked out loud in this matter is the same as it was when the first rack went down this morning and it is going to be the same in ten weeks.
