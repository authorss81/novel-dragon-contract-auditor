# Chapter 351: The Room Over A Chandler's Shop Taken By The Week, Four Steps And A Landing, About Four Feet Of Wall With A Nail In It And Nothing Ever Hung On It, And A Man At A Table With A Number In His Head That He Has Not Given Away

The room is over a chandler's shop and it is taken by the week, and the shop keeps tar and rope and lamp oil, and the smell of all three of them comes up between the boards all day and has come up between the boards all day for about two years.

There are four steps and a landing, a table, a chair stood against the wall the bed is against, and a window about a foot wide looking at the back of another house. About four feet of the wall beside that window has a nail in it and has had one since he came into the room, and nothing has ever hung on it, and he has not decided not to hang anything on it. There has never been anything in his hand to hang.

He is thirty-four or thereabouts and the hand is a fair one, and what he is paid for is the copying out of other men's figures until the totals agree. Four days a season he stands on somebody's list. **He is not on anybody's list this week and did not put himself on one and there is no form anywhere in this empire for putting yourself on one.**

This is the second day of the first week of the first month of the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is about the fifth hour, and the lamp in that room waits for the seventh hour and is not going to be waited for twice.

---

He has a number in his head. He has had it about two years and he has not written it and he has not said it and nobody has ever come up those four steps and asked him for it.

**The reason he cannot give it away is not modesty and is not a rule anybody made.** Handing it over would be a list, and a list of persons is a company, and a company is a heading, and a heading is what a person turns into the moment the thing gets bigger than a room. There are about nine hundred rooms in this empire where a person might come up the steps of somebody like him, and a figure that covered all of them at once would have to be a heading over all of them, and there is no shape of his own that would still be in it afterwards.

So it stays in a room with about four feet of bare wall in it, and the room is over a chandler's shop, and that is the whole of the arrangement and he has been in it since before the last winter.

---

He came into this room off a counter at the top of four flights four miles off, and he paid fourpence for a copy there, and before that he paid fourpence for a copy in a third room, and neither of those two rooms is this one.

Fourpence a copy is what it costs to be a person who can be found. **He has been findable in three rooms in about two years and he has been findable in this one, and nobody in this one has ever come for a copy, and the reason is not that they do not want one.** There is no form for being reached at a counter in a room a man has left, and the people who might have come up those steps four miles off have about nine hundred rooms between them and him and no way of knowing which one he is in, and he did not tell them, and not telling them is the arrangement and not an oversight.

**Nobody has thanked him for any of it, and there is not one form anywhere in this empire that requires a person to be thanked for standing still and letting a thing be copied out of his figures.**

---

There is a bill for being findable. It is not his and it does not stand against his wages and it is on somebody else's account, and he has known for about two years that it is on somebody else's account and he has not found out whose and is not going to.

He is owed four other things and three of them are about nine minutes of somebody's time. A form for asking a person whether they will mind being told something before they are told it, which he has looked for for about two years and which does not exist and never has. The thing nobody thanked him for, which is the same absence seen from the other side of it. And a woman at the foot of four steps about four miles off who is not thanked either, and who has said out loud that she is not going to ask him about his week, and who is not thanked by him about anything and has not asked to be.

**There is no form for being reached where you are, and there is no form for minding what you are going to be told before you are told it, and the two of those absences are one absence and not two**, and the price of it is that he is in a room that nobody comes to and he is a man that nobody asks, and the price of it is also the whole of what he has.

---

The count of things anybody has asked out loud in this matter is seven.

It was seven before the last winter and it was seven when the last man said a true thing standing up in a room with a rail in it, and it is seven now, and it is going to be seven in a week and in a month.

**He is the only person in this whole of it that anybody could put a question to, and he is the one nobody has asked, and the two of those are not two things. They are one thing and they have been one thing for about two years.**

He is not going to be the eighth. He has had a question ready for about two years and it takes about four seconds to say, and he is not going to say it, and nobody has asked him to say it, and the not being asked to say it is the shape of the whole of the last two years and he is not going to be the one who complains about it either.

The next asking is not his to make.

---

He put a sheet of his own paper on the table at about the fifth hour and copied out a column of a man's figures until the two of them agreed, and it came to eleven shillings and it is due in the spring and the man does not know his name.

The lamp in that room is not lit.

"Is that nail still in?" he said it out loud at about the fifth hour, to the room, about the nail, and it is, and it has been since he came, and nothing in this empire lets a nail be said to a person, and he is a man that nobody has asked anything, and the count of things anybody has asked out loud in this matter is the same as it was when he came in and it is going to be the same in ten weeks.
