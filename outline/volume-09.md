## What the volume is, in one paragraph, and what the title means

**A volume in which something moves.** That is the whole of the change, and it is not a small one, because the machinery of this matter has been built for eight volumes to hold things still. A record is stationary by definition. A form is stationary. A heading is stationary. A class is stationary. A box is stationary. **Volume 08 was fifty ordinary weeks of an absence with a shape, and the shape was that a person may say a thing out loud in a room and may not ask a thing out loud at all, because being asked is being served, and being served is a party, and a party is the only person who can be told what a thing is going to be used for. Volume 09 is the volume in which the first thing that moves in this matter since the machinery was built moves, and the pressure is not a threat and not a season. The pressure is this: nothing that exists can be told what to do about it, and a thing that moves in a building full of things that hold still gets used, because that is what paper is for.**

**And the thing that moves is a blank, and a blank is on the page already, and the page said in Chapter 0353 what it is for: there is no form for explaining a blank to the person it is printed for, and that is not a bad piece of printing, and the page works, and a blank is the only paper in this empire that cannot be laid against anybody, and a box of about four hundred of them is as near as this empire has ever come to a place where a person could put a question, and it is not a place.** Under the fourth counter of a Table in Auremar at the top of a stair with no other way up there is a box of about four hundred blanks, bought and unfilled, and it has been under that counter for eleven years, and no form was ever drawn to account for it, and nothing has ever been left in it, and about four hundred of them a year are bought by somebody and walked out of that building by that somebody.
