## What the volume is, in one paragraph

**A volume in which nothing happens, in rooms, at the pace of an ordinary week, and the nothing is the finding.** The premise of the series is a male-led story about a legal clerk who audits dragon contracts. **Volume 08 is the volume where the machinery stops moving in front of the reader and the people inside it are shown instead**, and it does that by refusing every event the genre would use: nobody is asked anything, nobody is thanked, nobody is forgiven, nobody is sent for, nothing is resolved, no door opens, and no document is produced. **The pressure is not a threat. It is an absence with a shape**, and the shape is this: *a person may say a thing out loud in a room and may not ask a thing out loud at all, because being asked is being served, and being served is a party, and a party is the only person who can be told what a thing is going to be used for.* Everything else in the volume is that sentence being true in about nine hundred rooms at once.
