## What the close hands Volume 08, and it is a shape and not an instrument

**The next-volume question, as this volume's own last page gives it:** what it costs the man at the foot of a sheet to be asked once, given that he has said the same true thing twice in nineteen years and been asked nothing, and that about nine of about nine hundred a year are not what the person above him wrote, and that there is no form for a disagreement at the foot of a sheet, and no form for the asking, and that a question in his own building is a record and a record is what the sheet goes in. **It is the next asking and it is nobody's yet, and it is not the who and it is not the second half.**

**The shape, and not an instrument:** a practice in two people about four hundred and thirty miles apart with no list, no form, no heading and no fund; a man of about sixty-one who cannot read a paragraph at the foot of about nine hundred sheets a year; and nobody having worked out how a thing is asked in about nine hundred buildings at once by people who cannot be instructed to ask it. **Nobody is going to work it out in Volume 08 either, because the answer is no, and the no took two years and is still worth a room.**

**Three standing instructions, and they are the ones a close is actually for.** *One, do not open with the machinery:* the man of about thirty-four is findable by a room — four steps and a landing, a table, a chair stood against the wall the bed is against, a window about a foot wide looking at another house's back, about four feet of bare wall with a nail in it and nothing ever hung on it, and a lamp that is not lit until about the seventh hour — and **he is the only person in this matter anybody could put a question to and he is the one nobody has asked, and a volume about a question that cannot be asked has to open on a man who does not ask one.** *Two, the calendar is a dated week and it is a sum and not an eye:* Chapter 0350 is the fourth day of the fourth week of the twelfth month of the fifth named year, and the fifth name is the one this file added and wrote out in full at every occurrence, and **Chapter 0351 is the second day of the first week of the first month of a sixth named year, which does not exist yet and is a name a Volume 08 outline has to add on this file's own rule rather than move one; the day inside the week is the inherited cycle of second, second, fourth, fourth; and no calendar month name and no weekday name goes into anything, including a state file and including a prompt.** *Three, a check that returns zero is evidence of nothing until you have checked what shape of damage it is blind to:* a duplicate-paragraph test blind to a duplication that landed inside a different paragraph in each of its two appearances; a parity test blind to a run that holds two sentences; a per-paragraph parity test blind to a run that opens in one paragraph and closes in a later one; the fifteen-word window's floor set by a date line the calendar lock requires; a window blind to a run broken by one comma; a window blind to a nineteen-word sentence; a per-batch bound blind to a volume agreeing with itself; the emphasis measure's unnamed joint, now named for one of its two readings; a string sweep and a sense sweep that have disagreed in five consecutive batches; and a phrase whose head is not inside it.

**And the one thing this close will not do, which is the whole of its discipline: it will not name the who, it will not restate the second half of the question in any form a writer could put in a mouth, it will not resolve anything, it will not thank anybody, and it will not tidy.** The measure of a good close is how little it changes and how exactly it states what is still standing. **Nothing has been resolved, nobody is thanked, the lamp is not lit, and the volume is closed.**
