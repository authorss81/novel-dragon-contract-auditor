**Three craft locks, produced by the review of Volume 03's closing batch and recorded here so that Volume 04 does not rediscover them. These are craft and not canon, and they are not rules of the world, and nothing in this file or in the manuscript may be read as making them so.**

1. **Emphasis is an exception and not a default.** No more than about a fifth of a chapter's lines may carry bold, and no more than one emphasised clause in any one speech turn, and a blockquoted form may keep its own typography. **The Volume 03 review measured this two different ways and the two ways do not agree, and the outline binds both.** Measured as a share of **lines**, Volume 03 runs from 2% to 22% with a median of 14% and the bound holds. Measured as a share of **prose text inside `**…**`**, with blockquote lines removed and the asterisks themselves removed from the denominator, it runs from 3% in Chapter 0101 to 59% in Chapter 0117 with a median of 24%, and twelve of Volume 03's fifty chapters sit above 30% and four above 40%. **Chapter 0117 is the outlier the closing batch's review did not find and did not fix: 10,172 characters of bold in a chapter of 17,173 characters of prose, carried on fifteen lines, which are fifteen long speeches — it is 9% of lines and 59% of text, and that is the whole warning.** The bound for Volume 04 is therefore **about a fifth of the lines and about a fifth of the prose text**, and a batch must state which of the two it measured, and with what method, before it claims the bound holds. **One measurement trap, learned in this pass: `grep -c` counts matching *lines* and `grep -o | wc -l` counts *occurrences*, and a line count is not a character count.**
2. **`about nine` is bound to about eight per chapter, and it is thinned and never varied.** It is the narrator's precision tic and it is load-bearing: Volume 03 carries **three hundred and eighty occurrences of the string `about nine` across fifty chapters, counted with `grep -o 'about nine' file | wc -l` and summed, with a median of 7 a chapter, a range from 1 in Chapter 0112 to 17 in Chapter 0137, and 17 of the fifty chapters above eight** — the inherited bound of about eight therefore sits just above the measured median and seventeen chapters exceed it. **The instances the state files record verbatim may not be changed to thin it**, and the variation lock holds — `about four`, `about forty`, `about thirty`, `about twenty`, `about sixty`, `about nineteen` are the sibling figures and they are not interchangeable.
