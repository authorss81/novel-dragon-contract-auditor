Power or resource change: **One sheet, one number in a corner, one mark, one day, and no name anywhere on any of it; and the volume's question for its final two batches stated out loud in a lane by a person who is not a lawyer.**

Continuity fact: **The woman of about sixty who has cut plates for twenty-two years in a lane is asked for her name and refuses it, in four words, and is not named.** The reason is on the page and is that she is the only person who can say which numbers out of the run are hers. **She writes a number from the edge of a plate in the corner of a sheet, with her own mark beside it and the day on it, and gives it back, and the sheet goes nowhere and is in no register and the mark is her own signature mark and is not the counting-house mark.** The **volume's question for its final two batches is stated in this chapter, in a room with no office in it: what happens to a rule about records when the person it is about cannot get to the room.** **The Reed Cut image is not spent here** — the schedule on the gate rail, fourteen months old, and a woman of eleven who cannot read it and is read to, is the volume's last page and not this batch's.

Ending type: A person, a bench, a number in a corner, and the volume's question in the mouth of a woman who is not a lawyer.

## Divergences Between These Cards And The Page, To Be Recorded At The End Of The Batch

None of these is a canon change. The page governs, and each one is here so that Batch 0004 does not write against the card.

1. **The limited audit is discharged on the page, and the cards only said it would be conducted.** Chapter 0072 gave the grant's terms and the page adds the outcome: **it was conducted in the Received Room in the third week of the third month, in two afternoons, and it produced one sentence which was written out and entered nowhere**, because the grant said a finding made under it may be used in no proceeding in any court other than the granting court, and there was no proceeding, and Marek Kest declined to make one in order to put a sentence into. **Oswin Marrow came into the room at about the fourth hour of the second afternoon, unasked, and said the sentence was correct, that it was one line, and that it would fit on the back of a form.** Chapter 0077 opens with this in one paragraph so that a later batch does not have to go back for it. **A further grant would have to be asked for and would come back the same size.**
2. **The custody form is used in this batch in the direction the cards did not anticipate, and the finding is new.** The card said the form was "the only instrument in this book that has ever been any use against a document nobody can be compelled to produce." The page goes further and it is the batch's structural finding: **the form protects a holder and does not protect a paper**, and the moment a question is asked about the document rather than the holder, the door is behind the questioner; **and `None` in the second box is also the reason a person who cannot read a paragraph can use a form at all**, because the second box is about a person and a clerk at a counter fills it in without asking anything. **Batch 0004 inherits both halves and may not write the form as a protection that is still whole.**
3. **The card for Chapter 0078 did not fix the heading of the column and the page fixed it as one word: *Given*.** Four other headings were written out and crossed out and the reason is in her book. **No later batch may write a different heading.**
4. **The card for Chapter 0079 did not name a caught person's town and the page does not either.** The card's support-cast section says the caught certifier is described and not named. Both stand.
5. **Chapter 0076's POV is Joane Tranter, not the young clerk of the intake desk.** The prompt allowed the clerk, the works or a magistrate. The page chose the magistrate, because **the young clerk of the intake desk stays unnamed and may not be turned into a character**, and the point the chapter had to make — a number on a card is a number of a bar — is a thing a magistrate can enter in a minute and a twenty-five-year-old cannot. The clerk is used exactly as the lock allows, in reported speech only: **he came out on a cart on his own time on the twenty-ninth of the third month, stood in a yard for about an hour and a half, asked what the figure is a number of, and wrote the answer in a book he had made up himself.**
6. **The card for Chapter 0074 said "one operative line of the leaf." The page gives the line as the heading of the schedule — *schedule of the term, the days named, the term held for seven years and no longer* — and the finding of the chapter is that the copy is short by a line, so what Trost copies out is a heading and there is nothing under it.** The card's version implied a more informative line survived. **The page governs: a heading is what there is, and a schedule with a heading and no dates in it is a piece of paper.**
7. **The card for Chapter 0077 said the seat's notice "mentions no series, no run, and no sheet." The page says that, and also puts the MC/FS sheet on the page once, in a registrar's mouth, for the purpose of establishing that the notice does not mention it. The First Seat quarterly return does not appear on the page in this batch at all, deliberately.** The card is silent on the quarterly return and the page keeps it off, which is the correct handling: **a later batch may not bring the quarterly return onto the page in order to be near the circular.**
8. **The card for Chapter 0071 said the answer "comes down as one side." The page gives it five points in full, and the fifth point — that nothing in it is a finding that any person has done wrong and nothing in it is a finding that any record has been altered — is the sentence that makes the whole answer safe, and the card did not have it.** The answer is quoted in full in `state/continuity.md` under **The Answer Of The Eighth Of The Third Month, In Full**, and no later batch may paraphrase it.

The page is Iolanthe Rees herself coming back, with a neighbour from the strip above whose name Sarn took down into a book, and which is not on a wall and not on a board and is not otherwise used.** No new named figure was created.
