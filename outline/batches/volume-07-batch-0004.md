## WHAT THIS CARD FILE DOES NOT DO, AND WHY

**It does not resolve anything and it does not plan to.** It does not name the who, open a post, make a commission, rule a fourth box, add a thirteenth line, call or arrange a hearing, thank anybody, send anybody, forgive anybody, walk the four hundred and thirty miles, light a lamp early, convert a mark in chalk, open a sealed page, name a notary or a seat or a quarterly return or either of the two documents or the bundle or the three red things or the counting-house mark or the ninety-second leaf, print the guarantee in its own words, add a fifth appearance of the substituted wording, or answer the question in the second of the eleven books.** **It adds no named figure, because sixteen consecutive batches have added none and a surname is not a door into anything.** And it prints no figure for the count of askings in any of its ten cards, because a card is a plan and a figure in a plan is a figure somebody later inherits as a fact.
