### Chapter 341 — The Bay At The Top Of The Lane Off The Slade

1. **The day.** The fourth day of the third week of the tenth month of the year after the year after the year after next, about the fourth hour, the gate shut at about the fourth hour by a man of about forty who does not argue.
2. **The room.** The bay at the top of the lane off the Slade, about nine foot by eleven, a bench longer than the bay is wide, a lamp over the middle of the bench not lit until about the seventh hour, a mark in chalk on the end wall at the height of the third link of a chain, a gate at the bottom of the lane, and about nine men in the front of it.
3. **The point of view.** Halla Wray, fifty-one, foreman of the Slade Cut, nine years of a thing nine families agreed to in the open, in a box since the first week of the seventh month of the year after, entered by a class, never asked. Named in the second paragraph.
4. **What happens.** The man of about fifty-five with the chain goes up to the gate while it is shut, stands there, comes back down, sits down, and says nothing about it. Nobody asks him. She does not ask him. At the back of the bay the two of seventeen are on their written engagement and neither of them is spoken to, thanked or sent for, and neither is going to start. She measures a length of wall for a man who is not thanked. The second sentence she owes the book-keeper is not asked for, because the day he asks is the day she stops saying the half she has got.
5. **What the reader has to hold.** That the only person on that bank that findability does not work on has used the one thing he has had for about four years, and that the whole of what it bought was nine men saying nothing and a foreman not asking, and that a thing said out loud in a bay is a thing nine people hear of and a clerk writes none of.
6. **What it may not do.** It may not ask the four who cannot read a paragraph one question about a number. It may not ask the man with the chain about the gate. It may not speak to, thank or send for either of the two at the back. It may not move either of them. It may not put the man with the chain in a list with the four. It may not ask the book-keeper for the second sentence, and it may not give that sentence to any other man, and it may not name him by his trade. ### Chapter 342 — The Counting Room At The Top Of The Same Lane

## WHAT THIS BATCH DECIDES NOT TO DO, IN ONE PLACE, BECAUSE A CLOSING MOVEMENT IS WHERE A VOLUME GETS TIDIED
