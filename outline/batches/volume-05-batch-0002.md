35. **Four askings. A man read a man's own paper to him in a yard and nothing was written down. A foreman of fifty-one has been translating for four men for nine years without being asked and has just found out. A foreman of fifty-one's second sentence went out of her mouth to a stranger and cannot be got back. A woman of sixty-three went to a room and gave a clerk her name out loud, and the clerk has refused, on her own account, to look her up. A man of about thirty-four was told the price because he asked for it, and the fear he took it away for was unfounded, and the cost was not refunded. A woman of twenty is answerable and a man with no office is answerable and neither of them can be un-answered. A clerk of thirty-four has announced in a room that a person may ask, and has been refused by the room's shape, and has decided not to put a question on a board. A foreman of fifty-one has decided not to make herself easier to ask, and has said out loud that it is the wrong decision and has named the right one and the woman who did it.**
