## Divergences Between These Cards And The Page, To Be Recorded At The End Of The Batch

None of these is a canon change. The page governs, and each one is here so that a later batch does not write against the card.

1. **The box in the water office holds two names, not a foreman and a boy, and one of the two is entered without being asked.** The card said *the two names at the foot of Halla Wray's list of works — the foreman and the boy*. The page puts **H. Wray** and **P. Wray** in the box, and the boy, **Raff Ordway, sixteen**, is on the list of works and **deliberately not in the box**, and leaving him out of it is entered on the face of the sheet as the one protection the keeper was able to give anybody. This was changed because putting a sixteen-year-old into a box is the volume's own subject, and because the second name had to be a man who can refuse: **Chapter 0104 is Piotr Wray's refusal in nine words and it is the batch's hinge.** The weigh-master's line in Chapter 0101, which refers to *the two names above*, was written to match and is consistent.
2. **The chapter titles on the page are not the cards'.** They are: 0101 *A Name at the Foot of It*; 0102 *The Box at the Water Office*; 0103 *Nine Hands*; 0104 *No Resolution*; 0105 *A File Is Made of Other People*; 0106 *The Fourth Question at a Table*; 0107 *A Copy of a Copy*; 0108 *The Wrong Form*; 0109 *The Weigh-House*; 0110 *Eleven Names*. **A later batch may not renumber or retitle these.**
3. **The page adds a second instrument on the page in Chapter 0108 that the card did not have: the north-bank works instrument of about nineteen years back, with a seat's device at the head of it and no person's name, whose eleventh, fourteenth and nineteenth clauses give the work to *the body of the households from time to time having the use of it*, and the cost to *the same, and by none other*.** It is read out of a file in the magistrate's own press, it is the fact behind about a hundred and forty files in which a balance has stood for about nineteen years, **and the minute expressly enters that it is not an instance of anything else that has been before that court and is not joined to the substituted wording, because a line between two things is a chain and a chain is an invention.** A later batch may use it as a fact about a file and **may not join it to anything, may not convert it into a finding about the substituted wording, and may not say that it is a seam.**
4. **The page changes what Leda Rell says about the ninth of the eighth.** The card had her state her own finding. **She did not make it: it is the finding of the district court of Auremar's lower district, four hundred and thirty miles away, and she is a district court and so is that one and neither takes a recognition from the other.** She has had the transcript in a file in the back room for six months and has never read it out, and says out loud that the reason is not humility and is that reading it out would oblige her to say what she thinks about it, and she says on the same page that she has never in nine years written down that she does not know whether she was right. **The three sentences are therefore not joined by her and are not joined in this volume, and the two halves remain nine days out of order with each other and neither court has read the other.** A later volume may join them only in a room with a witness in it.
5. **The page does not date the fifth term's schedule or restate its age.** The card referred to that schedule in Chapter 0106; the page refers to it, and to the fourth term and the fifth term by number, **without a date and without the figure of an age**, so that the recorded ambiguity about the fourteen months is inherited unchanged. A later batch may not print the date the sheet was nailed up.
6. **The undertaking of the fourth of the first month is exercised in Chapter 0109 and it catches an absence, which the card allowed and which the page has made the fifth use and the third kind.** The card said *catches a fact*. The page: three words, one fact, and **one absence**, and the absence is that Marek Kest had a sheet in his coat for nine days that went to a household and did not bring it to her, and the reason he did not is that he knew she would say no. **The undertaking is not retired and is not amended and the rule two people made with each other in the twelfth month of last year is not used a fourth time in this batch.**
7. **Chapter 0105 is over the ordinary band at 4,091 words** and under the about-4,200 ceiling for a formal sitting, and it is kept because it carries a nine-item list of what nine other people did to one man, a reading-out of about nine minutes, an afternoon lost by a man of sixty with no fund, and an entry in that office's day's book. The other nine chapters are inside 3,200–4,000. **Nothing was padded and nothing was split.**
8. **Two defects were found in the prose after the first measurement and were corrected on the page before the batch closed:** a calendar month name in Chapter 0101 (*in February*), and two weekday names in Chapter 0104 and Chapter 0108 (*the following Friday*, *the twenty-fourth of the first month is a Thursday*). All three are gone and a check of the ten files for the twelve month names and the seven day names now returns nothing. **The page also withdrew the public-necessity sentence from Leda Rell's opening paragraph, where a first draft had placed it and the quoted phrase *the present holder* in a file of the fifth month; the sentence is not quoted anywhere in this batch and the phrase does not appear in it at all.**
