## Required world reveals

1. **A bar is not a punishment. A bar is a register entry, and a register entry is a thing that happens to a person because nobody wrote to an office asking how to be entered.** A register of persons is safe, and the safety is also the trap, and the only procedure for being entered in it is to ask, and nobody ever asked.
2. **A high court will answer a question about a *person* and will not answer a question about a *record* that has no person in it, and it will say so in a form with a name at the foot, and the form is the answer.**
3. **A document with a man's name at the top of it is a document about the man.** Pell Vey's own argument, from the caption of the referral, used against him by a clerk who learned it from him, and it works, and it works on the young clerk of the intake desk's officer's return before it works on anybody important.
4. **You can hear a person and still lose them, if the record is put in front of them first.** The volume's one instrument — *a person affected may be heard about it in a room, in a place that recognises the room* — has a hole in it, and the hole has a shape, and the shape is a third issue of a witness order drawn in the copying room of a river court with a struck certificate impression in it.
5. **A price is a thing that lets a person answer without owing anybody, and a shop window asked a woman a question a woman could not.** Tested a second time, in pounds, against a man who says yes before he is asked, and the finding is that a clear answer from a person with power is not evidence.
6. **A mark is a mark and is not a name, and a matrix is not a ledger, and eleven years in a drawer is not custody of anything.** About nine certifiers learn in one week what a mark is for, and one of them has been holding the body of it.

## Divergences Between These Cards And The Page, Recorded At The End Of The Batch

None of these is a canon change. The page governs and each one is here so a later batch does not write against the card.
