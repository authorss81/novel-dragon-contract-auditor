6. **A foreman of fifty-one who has entered the words of a document with the day on them and refused to enter herself, and who has about four people in a bay who cannot read a paragraph and who is not going to be the reason any of them is found.**
7. **A clerk of about fifty-five with two documents in the matter with her hand at the foot of them, one of which she has not chosen, and a form she has taken out of a press and not put back into it.**
8. **A day of a man's own memory gone, and a woman in four bays who has not decided whether to trust his judgment, and the decision is not made in a room and is not on the page.**
9. **A reader of seventeen on a written engagement at forty-five pence a day who was the one way of reaching four people and was not used, and this is the last season in which anybody on that bank can ask her something and be answered for nothing.**
10. **And the volume's question, unasked and unanswered: if the office was meant to be temporary, who changed it into an inheritable one, and why are dragons themselves bound by the altered compact.**
