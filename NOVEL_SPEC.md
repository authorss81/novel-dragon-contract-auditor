# Novel Specification

Title: The Dragon Contract Auditor
Repository slug: novel-dragon-contract-auditor
Genre: legal fantasy / court mystery
Male lead: A legal clerk who audits dragon contracts
Length target: 620 chapters
Relationship policy: One slow-burn relationship. Decided in the bible phase: Tamsin Rook is the only slow-burn relationship, and it does not begin in Volume 1.
Power rule: Growth must be earned through practice, cost, failure, relationships, and changed decisions. The lead must not become instantly overpowered unless the approved genre premise requires it.

## Premise
A young clerk discovers that dragon contracts are secretly rewriting human inheritance law.

## Ending
The empire accepts a legal system that cannot be controlled by dragon bloodline alone.

**And one decision that is the author's and has not been made, recorded here because the drift is now eleven volumes deep and should not be left to accumulate silently: the premise in the *Premise* field above is not in the book.** The subject of this repository's name occurs twenty-five times in Volume 01 and twenty-eight in Volume 03 and **zero times in Volumes 05, 07, 09 and 11**, and the outline of record for Volume 11 forbids the word in any file the volume writes. The manuscript is about unasked questions, unfilled forms and the cost of a second ear. That is a legitimate novel and the ending of record is still reachable from it, and the question is not whether the drift is a defect — it is whether it is the book. **Nothing in `state/` and nothing in `reviews/` may record the answer, because the answer is an author's decision and this project's own standing is that a premise handed to a phase is not a fact.** It is carried as an open item in `state/open-threads.md` and it is the one finding in the Volume 11 Batch 0004 register that this repair did not apply.
