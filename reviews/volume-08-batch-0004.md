# Volume 08, Batch 0004 — Chapters 0381 to 0390

**Reviewed after the phase, 2026-09-28. This file was written by the repair phase that applied the reading, not by the run that produced it.**

**Not certified independent, for the same reason every other entry in this register is not: the reading reached this project as a run log, `logs/` is gitignored, and the run that inherited the log is not the run that wrote it.** That is closer to independence than the gate's fallback, in which the writing agent reviews its own chapters, and it is still not a second pair of eyes. **What this file is, then, is the reading made durable, and the repairs it produced, and the two places where a durable reading is worth nothing on its own: a reading that never reaches the file the next run opens, and a reading whose findings are applied literally when the same fault is usually in four places.**

## The six findings, and what was done with each

**1. `state/current.md` was not updated by the continuation, and carried a defect the same phase had found and fixed.** The continuation identified a distance of four miles where the page says nine, cut it out of Chapter 0390, and wrote the correction into the batch summary and into the batch-0005 prompt. The live header still said the clerk of about fifty-five *walked about four miles to a rented room*. The chapter carries no distance at all after the repair; the only four miles left in it belong to the counter where a copy cost fourpence. **Severity: high** — this is the file the next writer opens first. **Applied, in three places and not the one that was named: the same sentence, in the same words, was in `state/character-state.md` twice.**

**2. The twenty-word lifted-material figure in the continuation's record is wrong under its own stated method.** The record prints 1 / 2 / 1 / 1 / 1 / 2 / 5 / 2 / 3 / 2, twenty in all, and classifies every one as a required verbatim recurrence. Re-run as stated, it is not that column. **Severity: medium-high** — not a prose defect; a measurement-integrity defect in the layer a future writer inherits as precedent. **Applied, and the finding was not reproducible either: four readings of the same sweep give 28, 24, 23 and 17, and the real defect is that the phrase *maximal shared run* has never been defined, and whether two abutting windows are one run or two is worth six runs in this column. The reading is now written down and the column is seventeen.**

**3. Two of the "required verbatim recurrences" are lifted character-identification paragraphs, not canon formulas.** 0387 carried 48 words of Chapter 0372's identification paragraph with the emphasis markers stripped and nothing else changed; 0390 carried 26 words of the protagonist's identification out of 0371 and 0379. The card for 0387 said the room was *referred to and not described again*. **Severity: medium-high** — the same damage 0383 had already received, filed under the exemption instead of repaired. **Applied, and the sweep found two more of the same fault: 41 words in 0385 out of 0380, and 23 in 0388 out of 0377. All four re-voiced, with every canon fact kept, and the exemption is now defined.**

**4. Chapter 0389 re-runs a scene the project has already flagged as a standing hazard.** The card names the chapter as item 45 and item 59, states the hazard in advance, and instructs that a lane used twice already is a re-run. The lane is now used three times. **Severity: medium.** **Declined on the page and recorded, because the prose is not a copy: the object in the lane is a form with a printed heading over a ruled space where 0369 and 0378 had a bill, and the chapter turns on a woman knocking on a door she has not knocked on in eleven years. The finding's real content was about the record, and it is now entered as a ledger item: a hazard named in advance and then never mentioned again is indistinguishable, in a record read afterwards, from a hazard that was handled.**

**5. **6. No review of record existed for this batch.** `reviews/` ran through Batch 0003. `state/phase-ledger.json` reads `phase-000-bootstrap` and `planned` at more than twenty phases out of date. **Both are known debts, not discoveries. The ledger is a controller file and was not edited.** **The file now exists. It is this one.**

## What holds up well, and it is not a small thing

**The prose is good.** 0381, 0385, 0388 and 0390 are real scenes with physical space, hands doing things with objects, dialogue carrying subtext and a consequence at the end of each. The pen out for four seconds and then back in the pot, and the lamp on the table rather than in the middle of the room, are the two strongest images in the batch and neither of them is decoration.

**The male lead's floor is met honestly.** Three chapters, two as an absence with a named cost in somebody else's legs and in a sentence said once to a passage, one in person. The absences are located in other people's bodies and passages, not in rooms invented to hold them.

**The continuation is unusually honest about itself.** It found eleven defects in its own output, named the categories, wrote down the blind spot of each check, and recorded the lesson that a sweep run on a chapter as finished stops protecting that chapter the moment anything is edited into its middle. That is better practice than most projects manage, and it is also what made this reading worth doing: without its record there would have been nothing to check the page against.

**Independently verified.** 390 chapters, 1,079,283 words at the time of the reading, before this repair. One question mark in the batch, in 0383, in a mouth, about an object. Seven askings, unmoved. The bill at Lowcross named zero times, for the fourth batch running.

**And the one that is not a compliment.** Every governing figure in both of this batch's own records was clean on a page carrying four re-printed identification paragraphs. The instruments that saw them were a reading and a measurement whose reading had never been written down. **A project that has eleven checks and no definitions is not well checked; it is well argued.**
