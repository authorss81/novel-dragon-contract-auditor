### One. `chapter-0229.md:132` and `chapter-0230.md:97` put the same man four hundred and thirty miles **down** the river from the bank, and `chapter-0225.md:144` puts him four hundred and thirty miles **up** it — from the same bank, with the same figure, in the same words

| Location | The clause |
