## The process note, and it is not a defect

The close wrote no marker in `workspace/volume-07/volume-close/`, which is correct: `scripts/novel_runner.sh` writes `.done` on the phase directory, and a phase that marks its own directory cannot be finished or retried. It does follow that the close's directory is the first one the selector reaches until the runner writes that marker, which is worth watching and is not a fault in the phase.
