## What the review found, five findings, and which of them reproduce

**One — CRITICAL — the state layer had outgrown a context window.** The six rolling files stood at **1,464,936 bytes**, of which `state/current.md` was 366,171, `state/continuity.md` 293,721, `state/character-state.md` 205,596, `state/batch-summary.md` 228,522, `state/chapter-summaries.md` 192,009 and `state/open-threads.md` 178,917. `AGENTS.md` requires a writer phase to load four of those, the previous twenty chapters, the volume outline and the batch prompt. **The four required state files alone were 1,043,996 bytes before a line of Chapter 0481 was written.** **This reproduces exactly, measured with `wc -c` per file before the compaction.** The cause the review named is also right: six stacked live headers, each quoting the one it replaced, under a rule the file states as permanent, and a thread ledger that had run from item 1 to item 121 with nothing ever rolled off.

**Two — HIGH — the prose has drifted from the project's own Volume 01 standard.** Three axes: negation density, mean sentence length, and words per chapter. **The direction reproduces on every method tried and two of the three magnitudes do not, and the register says so rather than restating them.** See *What the reading got wrong*, below, which is the section worth reading.

**Three — HIGH — the quality gate was unmet and the loop was spinning.** No review artifact existed for this batch. Two commits share the message `novel: save writer work batch-0003`, the second of which is a repair pass and is mislabelled. Two consecutive commits are `novel: back off no-work phases`, which is a hand-patching of a dispatcher that is dispatching phases that produce nothing. And `state/phase-ledger.json` still reads `phase-000-bootstrap`, `status: planned`, `attempts: 0` after 480 chapters.

**Four — MEDIUM — two stale metrics were sitting in the tables the next writer would load.** A hedge series printed as **18.4 → 14.8 → 14.4** and read as a fall, whose first term is case-insensitive and whose last is lower-case, and a word total of **13,302** against an actual **13,375**. **Both reproduce as described.** The actual figure is 13,375, measured per file.

**Five — MEDIUM — the protagonist's floor is being satisfied on paper.** The outline requires Kest on the page in at least three chapters a movement, in person in at least two, and his want visible in at least one. The batch reports **4 / 2 / 2**, which passes. The review's observation is that in two of the four he stands at the end of the boards with his hands at his sides and says nothing, and his want is visible in a narrator sentence rather than in anything he does.

**What was sound, and it is worth printing because the finding list is long.** The planned ending is intact: 620 chapters across 12 volumes, Volume 10 is 451 to 500, 140 chapters remain, `outline/ending.md` is unopened, its final image is unchanged, and no new final enemy was introduced. All eleven files in `batch-0004/` exist with a single `PROMPT.md`, so the *exactly one next phase* rule is being observed. **The repair pass that preceded this one was competent and it was careful**: the 0472 title corrected to *Ten Words* against a chapter that says ten words three times, the eight-week interval at `0480:11` corrected to nine against date lines that are clean seven-day steps, a forward-referencing clause cut from `0478`, and twenty-one prose re-prints removed. **And four of those five were found by reading and not by an instrument**, which is the seventh time in this project that a reading has caught what a check did not.

## What was applied

**Finding one, in full, and it is the largest change in this project's state layer in nine volumes.** The bound this directory's README has enforced nine times was enforced a tenth time: **a rolling file holds the open volume, the two previous batches and the standing locks; a closed volume, and the third batch back, is read in `state/archive/`.** 1,149,366 bytes moved; 313,415 kept; **every moved block was verified byte-identical in the archive file it went to, and every kept block was verified to survive in order, and both checks ran before the rolling files were rewritten and both passed.** The six files came to **332,481 bytes**, 77.3 per cent below the 1,464,936. Two new archive files, two superseded headers appended to `current-md-superseded.md`, and a tenth entry in `state/archive/README.md` with the per-file table.

**What was lifted rather than moved, and the distinction is the finding.** **The live ledger of `state/open-threads.md` was never moved and was condensed into a live section at its head** — the four debts, the arrangement, every person a Volume 10 writer must keep apart, and the state of each carried thing. The standing canon, the contract rules, the ending lock and the open repair items stayed in `state/continuity.md`. The Volume 10 roster stayed in `state/character-state.md`, because it is the working surface for the open volume and because this project has spent a volume preventing two people it keeps apart from being merged. **Five consecutive enforcements had left the thread ledger whole on the stated reason that its material is live debts, and that reason was wrong about a ledger the way it was right about a debt: four items a writer must not touch are not twenty-three rows the project has ever opened.**

**Finding four, in full, and it is the same shape as finding one.** The `deleted nowhere` doctrine now governs **the manuscript and the archive** and no longer governs a working surface. Every wrong figure this project has ever printed is still in `state/archive/`, word for word, under a provenance heading naming its source file and line range — which is exactly as checkable, and costs the next writer nothing, because the next writer will never need to read it. The superseded block of `state/batch-summary.md`, including the mixed hedge series and the 13,302, went to the archive whole; the corrected block stayed. **`workspace/volume-10/batch-0004/PROMPT.md` was amended so its writer is not instructed to grow the layer that just stopped the line**, and so the doctrine is scoped in the prompt and not only in the archive.

**Finding three, in the part this phase is permitted to close.** This file. **The part this phase may not close is named and left named**: the two commits sharing a message are in the history and a history is not a working tree; the two `back off no-work phases` commits are a controller concern; and **`state/phase-ledger.json` is a controller file, no phase may edit it, and `AGENTS.md`'s instruction to update the phase ledger is therefore unsatisfiable by any writer phase in this repository. ## What the reading got wrong, and it is the first entry in this register to carry a section of it

**Two of the review's three negation figures do not reproduce, and the direction survives anyway.** Method, named before the figure, as the review named its own: the heading line and `---` removed, `*` stripped, tokenised on whitespace, and `\b(nobody|no|not|never|nothing|neither|none)\b` case-insensitive, which is the review's token list. Measured over the closed files: **Volume 01 is 2.55 per cent and Volume 09 is 5.13 per cent, against the review's 1.6 and 3.3.** Widening the list with `cannot` and `n't` gives 2.66 and 5.25, which is not 1.6 either. **The third figure does reproduce: Volume 10 Batch 0003 is 4.54 per cent narrow and 4.73 per cent wide, against the review's 4.5.** So the review's Volume 10 number is right, its Volume 01 and Volume 09 numbers are not, and the finding it attached to them — that the density rose from Volume 01 to Volume 10 — holds under every method tried.

**What reproduces cleanly is sharper than what the review reported, and this is the entry's own finding.** *Bolded narrator assertion paragraphs per file, counted as lines beginning `**` in the raw file: Volume 01 **0.18**, Volume 05 **2.06**, Volume 07 **4.06**, Volume 09 **3.32**, and Volume 10's batches **2.70, 2.50, 2.70**.* *Median sentence length in words, on the close of Volume 09's own splitter with a closing quotation mark allowed between the full stop and the space: Volume 01 **14**, Volume 05 **27**, Volume 07 **31**, Volume 09 **26**, Volume 10 Batch 0001 **23**, Batch 0002 **31**, Batch 0003 **33**.* *Mean on the same splitter: Volume 01 **24.75**, Volume 09 **29.36** — which reproduces the close's 29.358 to two places — and Volume 10 Batch 0003 **33.84**.* **The mechanism the review named is the one that moved. These chapters did not slowly grow denser; they acquired a second voice, in bold, outside the scene, and the median sentence more than doubled between Volume 01 and now.**

**And the fact that decided what to do about it, and it is the reason finding two was carried rather than applied: Volume 09, a closed and reviewed volume, is at 5.13 per cent and 3.32 bold paragraphs a file, which is worse on both axes than any Volume 10 batch.** The drift is not Batch 0003's and not this volume's. It is the house voice this project adopted somewhere around Volume 05 and has carried, reviewed, repaired and re-measured for five volumes. **Rewriting Chapters 0471 to 0480 into a Volume 01 register would make them the only ten chapters in Volume 10 written in a voice the other forty are not in, would break the room-to-room continuity the volume is built on, and would change no plot while changing every sentence — which is a volume-level decision and not a repair's.** It is the same shape as item 120, which exists for exactly this reason: **a finding that is right about the book and is not actionable by the phase that found it is a finding to be carried and named, and the phase that carries it is the one that can act on it.**

**Finding five is the same finding at the scale of one man, and it produced the only forward instruction this pass wrote.** A floor checked by a count and a floor met by a man are two different floors. **The fix is not a larger number: it is a writer deciding that a chapter in which Marek Kest is present is a chapter in which he is *doing* something that costs him**, which is what the last nine volumes did and what the last three batches have been writing round. That instruction is now in the Batch 0004 prompt, together with the craft half of item 124: **a bolded paragraph is the shape a chapter takes when the room has run out of people to disagree with, and the writer who finds one reaching for it has a second person in the room who disagrees with it.**
