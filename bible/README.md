# Bible

Author-facing canon for *The Dragon Contract Auditor*. The bootstrap phase owns these files. A later phase may correct a fact, but it must record the change in `state/continuity.md` and reflect it here in the same batch.

| File | What it fixes |
|---|---|
| `premise.md` | One-sentence premise, opening situation, long-form engines, protagonist spine, ending promise, guardrails. |
| `world.md` | The Cindral Empire's geography, institutions, factions, history, everyday work, and the six world rules that must never bend. |
| `characters.md` | Every named character's want, need, flaw, limits, and relationships, plus the Volume 01 support cast and fixed anchors. |
| `power-system.md` | The five necessary parts of a contract, the three contract families, inheritance echo, the Roll-Answer, Stages 0–6 with their costs, reading limits, and worked examples. |
| `themes.md` | Core themes, recurring motifs, and the thematic tests each new volume or chapter should pass. |
| `terminology.md` | Canonical vocabulary, the exact public-necessity sentence, blank-heir rules, and the prose usage rules that keep the world's voice stable. |

## Read Order for a Writing Phase

1. `terminology.md` — for the words and the exact forms of a ruling.
2. `power-system.md` — for what a contract can and cannot do, and what a reading costs.
3. `characters.md` — for whose want the scene serves.
4. `world.md` — for the institution, jurisdiction, and material consequence.
5. `themes.md` — for the test the chapter must pass.
6. `premise.md` — for the spine and the guardrails.

## Canon Boundaries

- No secret dragon bloodline, chosen-one birthright, stat screen, or new final enemy.
- One romantic relationship only: Marek and Tamsin, slow, reciprocal, and allowed to refuse each other.
- Magic can show a contract's current effect. It cannot create consent, declare fairness, or replace a hearing.
- Seryn Oris is the final antagonist and survives constrained and accountable.
