# Batch Summaries

Rolling long-term memory, one entry per completed batch, newest last. A later batch reads this whole file plus `state/current.md` and `state/continuity.md`; keep each entry short enough that the file stays readable in full.

### Volume 01, Batch 0001 (Chapters 1–10) — The First Correction

- **Goal and outcome:** The batch set out to make the Venn alteration a human problem with a winter deadline, prove it was already in a second Mosswake household, and force Marek to choose public accountability over a quiet fix. The Venn case was won on the twelfth of the month: Tavi's personal debt is void, the field and the remaining term are preserved, Sivra Oris's gate and navigation duty survives, and the bank works are taken off the Venn family by the district water office. Corvin Dray is arrested after his ledger is copied, and a nine-paragraph audit request is in Examinations custody with its scope undecided. The public-necessity sentence was read at two o'clock and the Dunn household was broken on it in the same minute, eighty minutes before the five terms were ruled, which is the intended reversal and is slightly worse than a later batch might assume.
- **New canon:** (1) The Venn summary carried a third witness notch that was never pressed into the original, and the filed correction bore no attached authority; the Reed Cut flood memorandum was dated the eighth of the eighth month and the correction was received on the ninth. (2) The back of the correction leaf carries a flat, even certificate impression and a broker's fee entry naming Candlewright Lane in Auremar, which Marek has on the page in Chapter 4 and cannot use until Chapter 7. (3) The source leaf stays in the Notaries' Table's cabinet in Auremar, and a comparison cannot be had by a clerk who is not in the room when the sleeve comes out; the leaf reaches Mosswake only because Ivet Sarn sent her own rider on the district court's custody slip on the night of the tenth. (4) The ruling of the twelfth has **five** operative terms: the void debt, the preserved field, Sivra Oris's unaffected duty, the new-signature rule (always "the fourth term"), and the district works with the household's release from field-side upkeep (always "the fifth term"). The fifth term is Tavi's own ask, made on the step of the Venn house on the evening of the tenth, and Rell put it last on purpose. (5) The first Roll-Answer was earned by exercising the limited Venn audit at the Chapter 8 hearing and was taken against the operative filed record after the ruling, so it names the present holder of the field, not Tavi Venn. (6) The public-necessity sentence, read once in full at the hearing, is now the first portable institutional wording of the inheritance echo, and the Mosswake registrar filed the previously unfiled Dunn summary on its authority in the same minute the sentence was read at two o'clock, which is before the five terms were ruled at twenty past three. (7) Corvin Dray's business is a sold mark and a sold date, not written sentences: sixty-one copied certificates and ninety-eight witness sequences at four shillings each, with entries for Venn, Dunn, and two provincial files at Glasswater and Emberwatch. (8) A sealed First Seat document is a printed four-paragraph administrative form, *Continuity Filings, Flood Seasons, Categorisation*, carrying a series number and a print run, which requires flood-season continuity filings to be entered in the routine column and not itemised or heard individually except on quarterly review. (9) An unexplained faded red running stitch with a sequence of small marks appears in the margin of a leaf at the bottom of a lane-man's mother's bundle, and one of the twenty-six public copies was written on that leaf because it was the paper to hand; a thread of the same colour is caught in the fold of the sealed form. (10) Ivet Sarn stated in Chapter 3 that it took her four days to open a file in the fifth year and that on the fourth day the relief rolls could not be certified; which file and which household are still unspent. (11) Minor new named figures bound for reuse: Garren Pyle, Corvin's attending certifier; Ivo Harker, the old lane-man who keeps the lane's papers in a tin; Brammel, one of Corvin's waiting clients; Hallis Dren, who testified as the water-office clerk who signed the memorandum; Wend and Pella Osk, the two court working-staff Tamsin names in open court; and Doctor Perrin Ashe of the Mosswake river landing.
- **Costs and consequences:** Marek paid for the first reading in fatigue, a persistent headache, an ash taste, and a temporary inability to lie about one named obligation, still active at the end of the batch. He spent his supervisor's goodwill, his private comparison advantage, and his habit of deciding what other people could safely know; Nell and Tamsin both made him pay for it in conditions, and Tamsin's trust is conditional rather than repaired. Tavi's field is safe until the second month, when the seven-year term expires, and not a day longer, and Tavi is refused a guild seat until the Dunn question is decided. Orra Dunn has accepted a hearing on the twenty-third that can only expose her whole household. The seed-barn summary was already in the machine before Marek proved anything, and the machinery has a name now: a form with a run on it, four hundred miles off.
- **Carried forward:** Open: who ordered the corrections, who writes the four sentences Corvin will not name, what the First Seat form is and who prints it, what the red stitch is, and why the Dunn summary predates the Venn correction by four months. Sarn's refusal must still be explained in Chapters 11–18. Nell's exit clause has been signed by Nell and the wage schedule but not yet accepted by the guild, which is Vare's Chapters 11–18 problem. The audit request sits in Examinations with no scope and no named office answer. The next batch inherits one dated question: on the twenty-third, a separate sitting decides whether fifteen-year-old Nessa Dunn can be joined as guarantor on a summary she never signed, and nobody in the room yet knows that a person who is owed must first be heard.

### Notes for the next batch

The overrun is concentrated in Chapters 6–10, which carry the listing, the hearing, the ruling, the public aftermath, and the ledger inventory. The scenes were not padded and were not split; the later batches should be read as calibrated to these lengths rather than to the guide's soft range.

- Established on the page and load-bearing for later batches: the fourth term requiring a separate signature; the fifth term putting the bank works and gate reset on the district water office and releasing the household from the field-side upkeep; the twenty-six public copies and who made them; Tavi's Chapter 6 warning that her unpaid ditch-cutting will be read as acceptance; Tavi's undismissed request for Odile Fenn's examination in the fourth month; Orra's separate sitting requested in the register in Sarn's hand; Sarn's four days in the fifth year; Corvin's promise to testify in a cell with the roll-keeper present; the eleven clients on the Candlewright Lane bench; Pella Osk, offered in open court as a witness to the copying room; and the customs clerk on the Auremar quay who will tell people to make a broker read a paper aloud.
- **Two traps to avoid.** The source leaf does not travel on request: it needs a written application, a court direction, and a recorded custody entry, and Sarn has no Notaries' Table key. And the provincial hold carries no office name; the First Seat's device is named once in private by Tamsin on the terrace in Chapter 6 and once in open court by Rell in Chapter 7, and nowhere earlier or later about that document.

## Format for each entry

`### Volume NN, Batch NNNN (Chapters X–Y) — <short title>`

Then, in this order:

- **Goal and outcome:** what the batch set out to do and what actually happened, in two or three sentences.
- **New canon:** only facts that change the world, the power rules, or a character's standing. Do not restate the cards.
- **Costs and consequences:** what Marek paid physically, materially, or relationally, and what remains unpaid.
- **Carried forward:** the open threads this batch advanced, and the exact question the next batch inherits.

Do not put scene description, prose excerpts, or chapter-by-chapter retellings here. Per-chapter detail belongs in `state/chapter-summaries.md`.
