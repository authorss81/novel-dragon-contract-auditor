### Volume 01, Batch 0001 (Chapters 1–10) — The First Correction

- **Goal and outcome:** The batch set out to make the Venn alteration a human problem with a winter deadline, prove it was already in a second Mosswake household, and force Marek to choose public accountability over a quiet fix. The public-necessity sentence was read at two o'clock and the Dunn household was broken on it in the same minute, eighty minutes before the five terms were ruled, which is the intended reversal and is slightly worse than a later batch might assume.

(6) The public-necessity sentence, read once in full at the hearing, is now the first portable institutional wording of the inheritance echo, and the Mosswake registrar filed the previously unfiled Dunn summary on its authority in the same minute the sentence was read at two o'clock, which is before the five terms were ruled at twenty past three. ### Volume 01, Batch 0003 (Chapters 21–30) — The Process and the Copies

- **Goal and outcome:** The batch refused the certification and replaced it with a process, and the process was built by people who did not trust it and who were each charged for it. Hallis Dren priced the fourth month in four columns on his own letterhead with his mother's canal house on it; Marek refused the offer in the Received Room on the twentieth of the eleventh, in a room with a witness, and accepted a named, reviewable flood-season continuity process instead; Tamsin wrote it into a four-box form, a hearing slate, a witness order and a cost ceiling, and Sarn kept both registers on one board; the reply of the thirteenth of the twelfth went out signed by Pell Vey to forty-six district offices; Tamsin built a list of forty-one copy-holders out of her own wages and refused the Chancery's paper; Nell put four questions on a chalk board in the lane and took the levy fight; Orra asked for an early hearing and got four weeks and a refusal on a public slate; a cooper's gauge went out of a shop in a third district on the fourth of the twelfth, before anybody warned anybody, and Tamsin went up the water and came back with a document that says on its face that it is worth nothing.
- **New canon:** (1) A **review date with no named person is not a review.** The difference between the certification and what is actually needed is not the sentence. (2) The refusal is entered, read back, and signed by the applicant, and the office is under an obligation to state the refused offer's terms to every district on the distribution, so the refusal had to go to all forty-six. (3) The **Standing Office of Provincial Continuities has no person at the top of it.** It is a seal, a series, a run, and a line at the head of a district's flood standing instructions saying that no further authority is required. The authority for the circular is the drawer the circular is in. This is the volume's midpoint answer: the local precedent was **issued, not invented**, and the issuing is traceable to a series and a date and not to a man. (4) The **flood-season continuity process** of the Mosswake Roll Court: a flood-season continuity filing is not categorised away, may be heard individually on request, is entered in a public **hearing slate** kept on the same board as the register of pending duties, is witnessed by a person who is not the applicant and not a creditor, carries a **cost ceiling of eleven shillings for six weeks** found by the district works, and is reviewed on the **fourth of the fourth month**. Exceeding the cost stops the window and the book records the day it stopped and why. (5) The form has **four boxes** and no number, no series, no seal and no office at the head of it, because a form with a run on it is a model. The *entry* in the book is numbered, not the form. (6) The cost: one hearing is three hours and a shilling and sixpence for a witness; fifty-two hearings is a hundred and fifty-six hours, which is twenty-six days of a window that has forty-two days in it at eleven relief rolls a day, so that sixteen days remain, a hundred and seventy-six certified against four hundred and sixty needed, and **two hundred and eighty-four relief rolls uncertified before the rate is struck on the sixth of the fourth month**. (7) The **four questions**, in the lane, on a chalk board, in nobody's name: *Is there work in it? Whose name is on it? If you stop, whose name answers for it? Who read this to you, and do they get paid if you sign?* The fourth came from a woman of about seventy at the seed barns and is the one that decides whether the other three can be trusted. (8) The **independent copy list**: forty-one holders, in whose hand, of what, dated, with one column that is the only one that matters. It lives in Tamsin's own book under a winter coat and nobody in the Chancery knows it exists. (9) A **third district is confirmed affected** — Millbrook Reach, three hundred miles down the water — and the instrument that took the gauge is a creditor's filled-in copy of the printed model, stamped by the landing office into the routine column with **no number against it**, so that the document is a line in a book that reads *guarantee, shop goods, bond, and the name of a district office.* The absence of a number is the entry.
- **Costs and consequences:** Two hundred and eighty-four households eat late in the fourth month because the hearings take a clerk's afternoons; four families who are not covered by the process are told so out loud before it is agreed (Venn, Dunn, Sallow, Greyfen); the Greyfen levy is recalculated onto the four signing houses on paper and Crenn says it will be fought and lost; a house of forty years standing with nothing wrong is charged for a clause about girls who leave; Grice serves a month early and the rye goes thirty-one miles up a canal; a cooper's gauge is gone and a girl of sixteen is in a surety line with no document to show anybody; Tamsin pays two shillings and ninepence and eleven nights out of her own wages and is the person who will be asked when a copy is wrong; Pell Vey's name is on the reply for the first time; and Marek conceals something for nine days and is caught doing it by the person he concealed it from.
- **Carried forward:** The scope decision on the nine paragraphs is still owed, and the reply asks for it before the fourth month and from a named person. The unsigned ninety-word corridor instruction of the ninth of the eleventh is unfiled in Trost's hands and both of them have agreed not to move it without a name. Tamsin's objection to leaving the First Seat quarterly return out of the reply is in Marek's coat pocket and unpaid. The hearing window may stop for money at any point and Sarn has to write the day on the board. Orra's early-hearing request is ungranted and a creditor's paper is already served. ## Volume 01, retrospective

**What the volume established**.

**A district that obeyed a form is not safe. It is quiet, and quiet is the injury.** A landing office stamped a creditor's model into the routine column in four seconds and cannot produce a document, a cooper's shop has lost a quarter of an inch of iron and a daughter's bench, and nobody in the chain did anything wrong. The absence of a document is not a defect in the system. It is the system working as designed.

**A person must be heard in a room and must sign before a guarantor duty attaches.** The Mosswake court said it in May; the Cinder Court said it again in June in a form binding itself and any court recognising it. Neither reaches a decision already made in a district, so the rule is narrower than the need and wider than the harm.

**A printed sentence is a record of a document and is not a record of a law, and a number is a fact about the book and is not a fact about the document.** The first half is a high court's second order and it does not stop nine hundred and forty printed copies. The second half is three index cards copied standing up out of an unlocked rack, and it dissolved a mystery that looked like a seam and was a coincidence.

**A copy of a decision is a weapon and it is a target.** Twenty-nine of forty-one said yes to keeping the Cinder Court's record. The twelve who said no are the finding: one burned hers and gave her reason in full; one has eleven hundred pounds of debt behind her and thinks a copy is a thing you keep if you are going to be proved right — a fair position.

**The thing that travels is not a form and not a ruling. It is a person who turns up.** A trade with one door will find out it has four, leaving no series and no run and no office, and in nine years nobody will be able to say which one she opened.

**What it deliberately left open**, each with a named holder: who signed the MC/FS sheet of the twenty-second of the ninth (the Standing Office of Provincial Continuities, with no person at the top of it); who authorised the sealed instruction (nobody knows, and two courts have said so in writing, each with a name at the foot); why the Emberwatch clerk itemised a filing the form said to leave alone, and whether he and the Glasswater sender are one person, and what Perry Ostrow answers to — the three of them held by a trade's own book, a rider-post and a district nobody has written to; what is in the second sealed page, refused twice and never a third time, and Examinations holds both refusals; what the red stitch marks; what route a burned archive wrapper with a thread through it leads to. Plus the largest unpaid debt, in the Mosswake court's own minute: sixty-odd households of the sixth year it hurt in letters and cannot name.

**What the machinery of this project turned out to be.** The corpus is institutional documents; nearly everything that moved the plot was a series number, a print run, a distribution line, a form's second box, or a box headed *the person who holds*. His power is nearly useless: two readings in fifty chapters, neither deciding anything. The wins come from ordinary competence — a raking lamp, a witness order's issue number, a docket count, a woman's memory that a stamp took four seconds. And every beat has the same shape: a person being asked to say a thing out loud.

Three things worked. The procedural record doubled as characterisation: a refusal, a limitation, a register entry and an escalation each revealed a person, and a court that enters its own failure earned more trust than a victory. Costs written in material units — two shillings and sixpence, a farthing, four pounds of seed, six months of saving — did the emotional work a scene of feeling could not. And handing the record to other people's hands, so his best work is a list he no longer solely keeps, produced the real climax without magic.

One thing did not work. The volume ends on a red working stitch in a burned archive wrapper, and the red mark planted in Chapter 9 is a different thing on a different paper, and the two may not be joined. What is planted at the end is a route, not an answer: Volume 02 must plant a different mark or pay the Chapter 9 one on its own evidence. The length profile is in the word-count record.
