The craft record of this batch is the section above this one and nothing in it is disturbed.** What follows is the six prose repairs, the three record repairs, the figures taken after the last edit to any chapter, and the two things this repair found and did not fix.*

**The six prose repairs.** *One.* Chapter 0364 stated its central fact twice inside the same four years — that he had known for about four years the one thing he can do would not reach him, and that he had used it about four years ago and it worked — and it now says it once: it reached him at a shut gate at the end of a lane about four years ago, it has not reached him since, and he has not so much as turned it over to look at it. *Two.* The same chapter's man of about twenty-eight claimed a bay population of about nine hundred people who cannot read what is on a slate, which contradicted closed Chapter 0356's four men in that bay who cannot make sense of a paragraph, and he now claims the four men in different words and a different complaint. *Three.* Chapter 0361's box of about four hundred bought and unfilled blanks is now in Marn Ottery's mouth, in the flat voice she keeps for stock, on the second day, which is what the batch's own debt table said was there and what the page did not have. *Four.* Chapter 0365's four witnesses are now four people: a man with a tray who has to come up those four steps and stands in the doorway, and a woman at the far end of that floor who comes in for a stamp and goes straight back out. *Five.* Chapter 0368 carries *no form for being told that a sheet was not as written*, the second frame its card assigned and the page did not have, and 0362 and 0366 carry theirs. *Six.* Chapter 0370's two women no longer share a pronoun across four paragraphs, and the paragraph that restated the quarter of her finding answered in the second month now carries what that restatement costs her, which is that there is no column in any of the eleven books for a quarter of an answer.

**What this repair did not do, and the reasons.** **The batch is one voice and that is not fixed.** Ten of ten chapters carry the same date line in the same position, seven of ten close on a bolded thesis, the register does not change with the point of view, and *the whole of what* appears nineteen times in 11,208 words. **Rewriting ten chapters is restarting the batch, and a repair that restarts a batch is a second writing phase wearing a repair's name.** The finding is carried as item 55 with the standing instruction for the next ten chapters and no measure in this project can check it, because every check here asks whether two files agree. **The protagonist is on the page in one of ten chapters against an outline floor of three, and that is a decision of the writing phase, defended in `state/current.md` rather than left as a shortfall in a measurement, with the cost written into the next movement's cards.** **This phase wrote no review file, because the hand that read the review and made these repairs cannot produce another, and the review it acted on is in `logs/`, which is gitignored and cannot be opened on a clean checkout — which is the standing recorded for every earlier repair in this project.**
