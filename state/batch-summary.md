# Batch Summaries

Rolling long-term memory, one entry per completed batch, newest last. A later batch reads this whole file plus `state/current.md` and `state/continuity.md`; keep each entry short enough that the file stays readable in full.

### Volume 01, Batch 0001 (Chapters 1–10) — The First Correction

- **Goal and outcome:** The batch set out to make the Venn alteration a human problem with a winter deadline, prove it was already in a second Mosswake household, and force Marek to choose public accountability over a quiet fix. The Venn case was won on the twelfth of the month: Tavi's personal debt is void, the field and the remaining term are preserved, Sivra Oris's gate and navigation duty survives, and the bank works are taken off the Venn family by the district water office. Corvin Dray is arrested after his ledger is copied, and a nine-paragraph audit request is in Examinations custody with its scope undecided. The same ruling then broke the Dunn household inside the hour, which is the intended reversal.
- **New canon:** (1) The Venn summary carried a third witness notch that was never pressed into the original, and the filed correction bore no attached authority; the Reed Cut flood memorandum was dated the eighth and the correction was received on the ninth. (2) The first Roll-Answer was earned by exercising the limited Venn audit at the Chapter 8 hearing and was taken against the operative filed record after the ruling, so it names the present holder of the field, not Tavi Venn. (3) The public-necessity sentence, read once in full at the hearing, is now the first portable institutional wording of the inheritance echo, and the Mosswake registrar filed the previously unfiled Dunn summary on its authority within the hour of the ruling. (4) Corvin Dray's business is a sold mark and a sold date, not written sentences: sixty-one copied certificates and ninety-eight witness sequences at four shillings each, with entries for Venn, Dunn, and two provincial files at Glasswater and Emberwatch. (5) A sealed First Seat document is a printed four-paragraph administrative form, *Continuity Filings, Flood Seasons, Categorisation*, carrying a series number and a print run, which requires flood-season continuity filings to be entered in the routine column and not itemised or heard individually except on quarterly review. (6) An unexplained faded red running stitch with a sequence of small marks appears in the margin of an old copy of the Venn terms kept in a tin under a lane-man's bed, and a thread of the same colour is caught in the fold of the sealed form. (7) Minor new named figures bound for reuse: Garren Pyle, Corvin's attending certifier; Ivo Harker, the old lane-man who keeps the lane's papers in a tin; Brammel, one of Corvin's waiting clients; and Hallis Dren, who testified as the water-office clerk who signed the memorandum.
- **Costs and consequences:** Marek paid for the first reading in fatigue, a persistent headache, an ash taste, and a temporary inability to lie about one named obligation, still active at the end of the batch. He spent his supervisor's goodwill, his private comparison advantage, and his habit of deciding what other people could safely know; Nell and Tamsin both made him pay for it in conditions, and Tamsin's trust is conditional rather than repaired. Tavi's field is safe for three years and one flood season and no longer, and Tavi is refused a guild seat until the Dunn question is decided. Orra Dunn has accepted a hearing on the twenty-third that can only expose her whole household. The seed-barn summary was already in the machine before Marek proved anything, and the machinery has a name now: a form with a run on it, four hundred miles off.
- **Carried forward:** Open: who ordered the corrections, who writes the four sentences Corvin will not name, what the First Seat form is and who prints it, what the red stitch is, and why the Dunn summary predates the Venn correction by four months. Sarn's refusal must still be explained in Chapters 11–18. Nell's exit clause has been signed by Nell and the wage schedule but not yet accepted by the guild, which is Vare's Chapters 11–18 problem. The audit request sits in Examinations with no scope and no named office answer. The next batch inherits one dated question: on the twenty-third, a separate sitting decides whether fifteen-year-old Nessa Dunn can be joined as guarantor on a summary she never signed, and nobody in the room yet knows that a person who is owed must first be heard.

## Format for each entry

`### Volume NN, Batch NNNN (Chapters X–Y) — <short title>`

Then, in this order:

- **Goal and outcome:** what the batch set out to do and what actually happened, in two or three sentences.
- **New canon:** only facts that change the world, the power rules, or a character's standing. Do not restate the cards.
- **Costs and consequences:** what Marek paid physically, materially, or relationally, and what remains unpaid.
- **Carried forward:** the open threads this batch advanced, and the exact question the next batch inherits.

Do not put scene description, prose excerpts, or chapter-by-chapter retellings here. Per-chapter detail belongs in `state/chapter-summaries.md`.
