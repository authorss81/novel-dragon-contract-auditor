## What Volume 10 was, in five sentences, for a cold reader

A man of thirty-eight holds nothing in this empire — no office, no fund, no list, no leave, no certification, no heading over his name in any building — and he has wanted one thing since before a fire, which is a name that was struck out of a leaf by a hand that was not his mother's, and the claim that travelled out of this empire with that name went the same way on the same day. He has one of those two things and has never had the other. He is carrying both of them, on opposite sides of the same pocket of the same coat, and he has told nobody. A counter at the top of four flights sells a sheet of paper for fourpence with four things printed along the top of it and not one of the four a name, and about four hundred of them go out of that room in a year in the hand of somebody who is not doing anything anybody asked them to do, and there is no form anywhere in this empire in which anybody can be told what one of them is going to be used for. In the last movement, somebody worked out that a copy of a copy has nobody's name in it, and that after a thing has been made once there is no maker left in it to be shown having made it, and **that is why nobody in this matter has ever been thanked**, and it was said out loud to a room, and a room cannot be asked and cannot answer and cannot repeat itself to anybody. And then a woman put fourpence on a counter and asked for the same thing again, and the thing that had been safe for one man stopped being safe for him, and **a thing in this matter that used to require somebody to be alone now requires only two people to be unlucky.**
