# Archive

*Everything the rolling state layer used to carry and no longer carries, moved here verbatim on the review repair of `batch-0004` and nothing else. No file in this directory has been edited, summarised, condensed or deleted from. The rolling files in `state/` point here; a phase that needs a closed volume reads the file named below and does not need to load it twice.*

**Why this directory exists.** The six rolling state files had reached about 4.8 MB and had no upper bound, because every batch appended a full per-batch record to every one of them and never retired the last one. `AGENTS.md` asks for summaries that are *compact and useful for the next batch*, and a file that grows by 20 KB per batch forever is neither. The bound now in force is: **a rolling file holds the open volume, the two previous batches, and the standing locks; a closed volume is read here.** Each of the six files carries a pointer at its head.

## Moved again on Volume 06 close, and the bound was enforced a second time

**Volume 06 is closed, so no per-batch record of it belongs in a rolling file. Three more files were created here on Volume 06 close, verbatim and unedited, and the six rolling files came to 860,284 bytes against a bound of about nine hundred and forty — a figure that the review repair of that close found wrong and that is corrected here, because it was taken before the close's own last edits: they are 882,353 bytes as they stand, measured with `wc -c` over the six and summed, and every later state edit moves the total, so it is re-measured and not inherited.** Continuity: Batches 0001 to 0003 in `continuity-volume-06-batches-0001-to-0003.md`, moved by Batch 0005, and Batches 0004 and 0005 with their three repair records in `continuity-volume-06-batches-0004-to-0005.md`, moved by the close. Principals: Volume 06's per-batch notes in `character-state-volume-06-batches.md`, moved by the close. **What stays in the rolling files is the standing canon and the standing locks, the Volume 06 outline of record and its repair, the Volume 05 consolidated roster as the base the Volume 06 changes are measured from, the Volume 06 and Volume 07 thread ledgers, and the volume-close sections. `state/chapter-summaries.md` was not appended to at all and holds one hundred and twenty entries for Chapters 181 to 300.**

**One thing in this directory is a record of a failure and is kept for that reason.** The first header in `current-md-superseded.md` claims that an independent review by a different hand was run against Chapters 0281 to 0290. It was not. `.opencode/agent/novel-reviewer.md` declares `mode: subagent` and `bash: deny`, and `reviews/` holds a written review for Volume 06 Batch 0001 and Batch 0002 and for no other batch, and its own `README.md` records the fallback in its first section. The eighteen defects that header names are real and the repairs are real; the word *independent* was not. `.opencode/agent/novel-reviewer.md` is a controller file and was not edited. **A correction to what that paragraph used to say, made by the review repair of the Volume 06 close and narrower than what it replaced: the two batch review logs are not in the working tree a run can open, and the reason is that `logs/` is gitignored — the first line of `.gitignore` is `logs/` — so no log in it is tracked and none can be read on a clean checkout. The stronger claim that they do not exist in this repository, and that `logs/` holds two files and nothing else, was false, and the runner writes `logs/${phase_id}.review.log` for every phase that reaches the review step, so `logs/volume-close.review.log` exists now. A record may say a log existed and that it read it at the time; it may not quote one it cannot open; and it may not print the inventory of `logs/`, which is the one directory here that changes between two runs of the same phase. The finding stands on `reviews/README.md`, which does exist and is tracked, and the absence of the logs makes it harder to check and not weaker.**

**`current-md-superseded.md` — the superseded live headers of `state/current.md`, appended verbatim in the order they were live, and nothing was edited, summarised or deleted.** The live header is the one in `state/current.md` and it is the only section of that file. **There are fourteen headers in the archived block and the fourteenth was appended by the review repair of Volume 07 Batch 0005, which is a repair phase and not a writing phase and which wrote no chapter; the thirteen before it are the bootstrap header, the ten writing-run headers of Batches 0001 to 0005 across Volumes 06 and 07, and the two earlier review-repair headers.** Every figure in every one of them is superseded by a measured figure in the live header or in `state/continuity.md`, and every superseded figure is printed beside the figure that replaced it rather than edited out, because a corrected figure that is not named is a figure that gets corrected twice. **A header that claimed an independent review of Chapters 0281 to 0290 is in the archived block and is false, and it is left standing there with the correction in the live header rather than deleted.** **The fourteenth header is kept for one more reason: it is the first live header in this project that was written by a phase which repaired another phase's batch, and the fact that a repair is not a review and does not claim to be one is stated in it, which is the standing for every repair this project has run.**

**`continuity-volume-06-batches-0001-to-0003.md` — the three per-batch records of Volume 06's first three batches, moved here verbatim on Volume 06 Batch 0005, which is the batch that closed the volume, and nothing was edited, summarised, condensed or deleted.** The rolling `state/continuity.md` holds the standing canon, the contract rules, the ending lock, the open repair items, the Volume 06 outline of record and its repair, and the last two batches, being Batch 0004 and Batch 0005. **The bound this restores is the one the Batch 0004 review repair set and Batch 0005 broke by adding to a file that was already over it: a rolling file holds the open volume, the two previous batches and the standing locks, and a closed volume is read in the archive.** A phase that needs the record of what Batches 0001 to 0003 of Volume 06 did reads that file, and the figures in it are the ones those runs measured, and two of them were superseded in place by the Batch 0004 repair and one of those supersessions is itself in the archive block at the head of `continuity-volumes-01-to-05.md`.
