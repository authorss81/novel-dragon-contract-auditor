# Archive

*Everything the rolling state layer used to carry and no longer carries, moved here verbatim on the review repair of `batch-0004` and nothing else. No file in this directory has been edited, summarised, condensed or deleted from. The rolling files in `state/` point here; a phase that needs a closed volume reads the file named below and does not need to load it twice.*

**Why this directory exists.** The six rolling state files had reached about 4.8 MB and had no upper bound, because every batch appended a full per-batch record to every one of them and never retired the last one. `AGENTS.md` asks for summaries that are *compact and useful for the next batch*, and a file that grows by 20 KB per batch forever is neither. The bound now in force is: **a rolling file holds the open volume, the two previous batches, and the standing locks; a closed volume is read here.** Each of the six files carries a pointer at its head.

Every section from `## The Word-Count Record, Measured At The Volume 01 Close` onward through the Volume 05 close, being the Volume 01 to 05 per-batch records, the Volume 03 audit, the four review-repair records and the two volume-close records. The two `Open Repair Items` sections were **lifted out and kept in the rolling file**, because they are live debts. |

**One thing in this directory is a record of a failure and is kept for that reason.** The first header in `current-md-superseded.md` claims that an independent review by a different hand was run against Chapters 0281 to 0290. It was not. `logs/batch-0004.review.log` shows the reviewer agent falling back to the writer agent on the same model, and `reviews/` holds a written review for Volume 06 Batch 0001 and Batch 0002 and for no other batch. The eighteen defects that header names are real and the repairs are real; the word *independent* was not. `.opencode/agent/novel-reviewer.md` is a controller file and was not edited.

**`current-md-superseded.md` — the four superseded live headers of `state/current.md`, appended verbatim on Volume 06 Batch 0005 in the order they were live, and nothing was edited, summarised or deleted.** The live header is now the one in `state/current.md` and it is the only section of that file. The five headers in the archived file are the bootstrap header, the Batch 0001 header, the Batch 0002 header, the Batch 0003 header and the review-repair-of-Batch-0004 header, and every figure in every one of them is superseded by a measured figure in the live header or in `state/continuity.md`. **A header that claimed an independent review of Chapters 0281 to 0290 is in the archived block and is false, and it is left standing there with the correction in the live header rather than deleted, because a corrected figure that is not named is a figure that gets corrected twice.**

**`continuity-volume-06-batches-0001-to-0003.md` — the three per-batch records of Volume 06's first three batches, moved here verbatim on Volume 06 Batch 0005, which is the batch that closed the volume, and nothing was edited, summarised, condensed or deleted.** The rolling `state/continuity.md` holds the standing canon, the contract rules, the ending lock, the open repair items, the Volume 06 outline of record and its repair, and the last two batches, being Batch 0004 and Batch 0005. **The bound this restores is the one the Batch 0004 review repair set and Batch 0005 broke by adding to a file that was already over it: a rolling file holds the open volume, the two previous batches and the standing locks, and a closed volume is read in the archive.** A phase that needs the record of what Batches 0001 to 0003 of Volume 06 did reads that file, and the figures in it are the ones those runs measured, and two of them were superseded in place by the Batch 0004 repair and one of those supersessions is itself in the archive block at the head of `continuity-volumes-01-to-05.md`.
