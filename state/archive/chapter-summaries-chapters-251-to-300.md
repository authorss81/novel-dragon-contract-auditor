## Notes on these ten summaries

**The word counts in the headings are measured per file with `sed 's/[[:space:]]*$//' file | wc -w` after the last edit was finished, and every one of the ten files returns the identical figure under `len(text.split())`. The batch total is 26,510 and the ten of them are 2,841 / 2,789 / 2,737 / 2,616 / 2,636 / 2,688 / 2,686 / 2,565 / 2,504 / 2,448, ten of ten between 2,400 and 2,850. The manuscript is 881,832 across two hundred and sixty chapters. Where a summary and the prose disagree the prose is the authority.**

**Five conventions this batch used, recorded so that a reader of these summaries does not misread an absence.** **One:** the batch uses the strict quotation form, a speech turn being one paragraph that opens and closes, and the strict-form test returns zero; a paragraph carrying two complete turns with a narration beat between them is used twenty-three times and the closed volume's last thirty chapters use that variant sixty-two times on the same method. **Two:** there are fourteen question marks across the ten files, per file 2 / 2 / 2 / 1 / 0 / 1 / 0 / 2 / 1 / 3, all in a bare short line in the mouth of a person, and a chapter carrying none is not evidence that nothing in it was asked. **Three:** the count of the askings moved once, from five to six, on the page of Chapter 0258, and **six is the only numeral of the count in the batch**, and chapters 0251 to 0257 print no figure for it because a total printed before the asking is a total that disagrees with the one the batch ends on. **Four:** a man of about thirty-four appears in five chapters of this batch and is one person throughout; the man of about thirty-four who came four miles in the rain to a dye end in Chapter 0237 of the closed volume is a different figure and is not in this batch. **Five:** the reader of seventeen is on the page in two chapters, at the back of a bay, and is not spoken to in either, and the phrase *the reader of* is not used in any of the ten files, so a string search for the card's short forms returns nothing.

**And the three figures a later writer must not inherit wrongly.** The word *the reader of* does not occur in the ten files, and the girl of seventeen is referred to as *a girl of seventeen at the back of that bay on a written engagement*, which is the same person. The card's *about four hundred yards* between a counting room and a fee board is not on the page, and the fee board is about four hundred and thirty miles down this river. And the third rule of the volume is reached in two halves in Chapters 0253 and 0257 and not in the movement the outline gives it, so Batch 0002 is handed it whole and unspent.
