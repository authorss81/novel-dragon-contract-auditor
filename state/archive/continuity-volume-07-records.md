## The declared shape, in one paragraph, and the thing it turns on

**A practice is in two people about four hundred and thirty miles apart with no list of them and no form and no heading and no fund, and nobody in either building knows that the other one exists. About nine hundred entries a year still come in under a heading, and a second hand is at the foot of every one of them, and that second hand has never been asked anything in about nineteen years, and there is no form anywhere in this empire that would let anybody ask him. About nine of the nine hundred a year have not been what the person above him wrote and he signed them and he is the only person alive who knows which nine. And a single return goes to one district out of about nine hundred districts, and nobody has worked out how a thing is asked in about nine hundred buildings at once by people who cannot be instructed to ask it.** The volume turns on the difference between saying and asking, and the difference is this: **a person may say a thing out loud in a room and may not ask a thing out loud at all, because being asked is being served and being served is a party and a party is the only person who can be told what a thing is going to be used for — which is the second of Volume 06's three rules and it was argued in its second movement and it is carried, not re-derived.** **The count of askings is seven, it is a count of things done and not of people, a count that counted people would be six, and it does not move in this volume, and the whole of the volume is built on the space on the other side of that number.** The who is Volume 06's and goes on unanswered, and **the second half of the question is not asked and is not restated here**, and neither of those two is this volume's.
