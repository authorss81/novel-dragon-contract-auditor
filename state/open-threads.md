## Current blockers

- **Marek has refused the certification and the process is not a rule.** Chapters 31–40 may not quietly turn it into one, and it may not be described as precedent, empire-wide, or Marek's.
- **The word "exception"** remains confined to the invalid draft in Chapter 5. No valid exception, counter-seal, mixed warrant, true-name reading, Roll Assembly, or Stage 2 ability appears in Volume 1, and **Marek performs no magic in Chapters 11–30.**
- **The two documents must stay two documents.** The MC/FS circular is a Chancery standing office's. The First Seat quarterly return is a dragon seat's. There is no document connecting them and no character knows both exist except Marek and Tamsin. The circular is **not** the First Seat's, and that distinction is the volume's midpoint answer.
- **The four lines and the sentence have different ages.** Four lines: the Sallow file, fourth month of the fifth year, and twenty-two of thirty-one sheets in the rack. Sentence: stated the twelfth of the ninth, printed the twenty-second. Never backdate the sentence. What Chapter 30 found is a third district reached by the **printed model**, not by an older file.
- **The source leaf does not travel on its own.** A written application, a court direction, and a recorded custody entry, and Sarn has no Notaries' Table key.
- **Sivra Oris and Seryn Oris must never be merged.** Seryn is not named in Volume 1. The quarterly return is her seat's paperwork. **Sivra testifies in Chapters 34–40 and answers only for her own seal.**
- **The second sealed page stays shut.** Anyone who opens it must be witnessed and must pay for it, and must not find a person in it.
- **The red stitch and the thread in the form's fold are not the same thing**, and no chapter may assert, imply, hint at, or let a character assert that they are connected.
- **The ruling has five operative terms, not four.** "The fourth term" is the new signature. "The fifth term" is the district works. The lane says them by number.
