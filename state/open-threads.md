**What Volume 01 answered, and still stands.** A guarantee may not attach to a person who has not been heard and has not signed. A printed sentence is a record of a document and is not a record of a law. A district that obeyed a form is not safe, it is quiet, and the quiet is the injury. A name is not a thing an office can be compelled to produce, and what a court can produce instead is a list of offices, which is a route and not an answer.

**What Volume 02 Batch 0002 answered, and it is new.** **A bar is a register entry, and the only procedure for being entered in that register is one line long and had been there for nine years.** **A high court will answer a question about a person and will not answer a question about a record with no person in it, and it says so in a form with a name at the foot.** **A document with a man's name at the top of it is a document about the man.** **A person who signs a column headed *by whose authority* is discharging an office, and a supervisor who arranged a thing is not a person who wrote a clause.** **You can hear a person and still lose them, if the record is put in front of them first.** **A matrix is not a ledger.** **A mark is not a signature; a mark carries a direction.** **A price is a thing that lets a person answer without owing anybody, and a door with no charge on it is still a door.** **A pad is not a book and an instruction handed to a man in a corridor leaves no stub.** **A temporary office is the only office in this empire that is honest about when it stops, and the only one nobody in the Roll Hall has ever had to administer.**

**What Volume 02 Batch 0001 answered, and it is new.** **An office can accept your case and shrink it in the same sentence and put a name under it while it does.** **A private book of one's own, with no office on it, is outside the reach of every office in this book.** **None is not nothing: None is a person who cannot be instructed.** **A substituted word does worse damage to a public duty than to a private one, because a body can be dissolved and a household cannot.** **A correct ruling can cost four hundred people their route to market and be entirely right, and the sentence that costs more is the one that is true.** **A duty that nobody owes is still a duty that has to be done and it is carried by whoever is standing nearest.** **A review date with no named person is not a review, and a register that is honest can look like a register that has not bothered.** **Nobody in nine years had ever gone and looked at an outgoing rack.**
