## Current blockers

- **The batch did not win the case and Chapters 41–46 must.** The limited sitting narrowed nothing, compelled nothing and struck nothing. The one permanent gain is Sivra Oris's sworn statement, and it answers only the question Marek can prove.
- **The batch did not produce the motive, and no document produced in Chapters 31–40 may come close.** The only documents produced are authentic-looking, correct on their face, and traceable to an office rather than a person.
- **Marek writes no order that is his own.** Every order given in the sitting is the Mosswake court's or the Reed Cut water office's own authority. The one instruction that would have been his — a page to forty-six districts — is written in his own book and filed nowhere.
- **The word "exception"** remains confined to the invalid draft in Chapter 5. No valid exception, counter-seal, mixed warrant, true-name reading, Roll Assembly or Stage 2 ability appears in Volume 1, and **Marek performs no magic in Chapters 11–30 and exactly one reading in Chapters 31–40.**
- **The two documents must stay two documents.** The MC/FS circular is a Chancery standing office's. The First Seat quarterly return is a dragon seat's. No document connects them and no character knows both exist except Marek and Tamsin. Chapter 38 makes this a deliberate, recorded decision rather than an accident.
- **The four lines and the sentence have different ages.** Four lines: the Sallow file, fourth month of the fifth year, and twenty-two of thirty-one sheets in the rack. Sentence: stated the twelfth of the ninth, printed the twenty-second. Never backdate the sentence. **Chapter 39 records that a resemblance between a guild notice and the fourth term was seen and not proved, and no chapter may turn that into a finding.**
- **The source leaf does not travel on its own.** A written application, a court direction, and a recorded custody entry, and Sarn has no Notaries' Table key. **The source leaf was not needed in Batch 0004 and was not moved.**
- **Sivra Oris and Seryn Oris must never be merged.** Seryn is not named in Volume 1. The quarterly return is her seat's paperwork. **Sivra swore on the third of the fourth and named nobody, and no one may make her.**
- **The second sealed page stays shut.** Anyone who opens it must be witnessed and must pay for looking, and must not find a person in it.
- **The red stitch and the thread in the form's fold are not the same thing**, and no chapter may assert, imply, hint at, or let a character assert that they are connected. Neither is mentioned in Batch 0004.
- **The ruling has five operative terms, not four.** "The fourth term" is the new signature. "The fifth term" is the district works. The lane says them by number.
- **A card reading is on the record in `state/continuity.md`:** Chapters 31 and 32 sit inside the second month as the cards place them, with the assessor's rider-post answer arriving on the twenty-fourth of the second. No fixed date moved.
