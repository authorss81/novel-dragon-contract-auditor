## Current blockers

- **Marek has until the fourth month to answer the certification.** Chapters 21–25 own the refusal and the named, reviewable emergency process. The answer may not be made inside this batch and may not be made cheaply.
- **The ruling has five operative terms, not four.** "The fourth term" always means the new-signature rule. "The fifth term" always means the district works. Never describe it as a four-term ruling.
- **The word "exception" stays confined to the invalid draft in Chapter 5.** No valid exception, counter-seal, mixed warrant, true-name reading, Roll Assembly, or Stage 2 ability appears in Volume 1. The first Seam-Sight is spent and does not recur.
- **The two documents must stay two documents.** The MC/FS circular carries the words and is a Chancery standing office's. The First Seat form is the quarterly return that makes the honest answer small, and is a dragon seat's. There is no document connecting them and no character knows both exist except Marek and Tamsin.
- **The four lines and the sentence have different ages.** Four lines: the Sallow file, fourth month of the fifth year, and twenty-two of thirty-one sheets in the rack. The sentence: stated the twelfth of the ninth, printed the twenty-second. Never backdate the sentence.
- **The source leaf does not travel on its own.** A written application, a court direction, and a recorded custody entry, and Sarn has no Notaries' Table key.
- **Sivra Oris and Seryn Oris must never be merged.** Seryn is not named in Volume 1. The First Seat form is her seat's paperwork. Sivra testifies in Chapters 34–40 and answers only for her own seal.
- **The second sealed page stays shut.** Anyone who opens it must be witnessed and must pay for it, and must not find a person in it.
- **The red stitch and the thread in the form's fold are not the same thing**, and no chapter may assert, imply, hint at, or let a character assert that they are connected.
