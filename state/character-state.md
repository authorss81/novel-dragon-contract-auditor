## The one thing nobody has been told in this batch, and the one decision that is still not made

**Nobody has been told that they were in a room. Not once, in any of the ten chapters, not in a way that could be softened into a hint, and not by the narrator.** What is permitted happened: a man looked along four feet of stone and a woman was standing at the far window; a courier stood in a room and went down four flights with a mark on his shoulder; a tin was lifted off a step and put back in the same finger's width of space; a woman stood on a step at the hour a person was working in that passage and did not say a sentence she has had ready since the third week of the seventh month; a girl stood four foot inside a door on purpose and was told she was not going to be entered anywhere; a jug was filled for a fortnight and then not filled; a woman put two fingers on an item on a wall board and took them off again; a foreman walked ninety foot of her own floor and put nothing anywhere; a door at the back of a floor opened for the first time in a month and shut again. **And the one thing that is new and that is about a person is that the man at the near end of that sill has looked along four feet of stone, and he cannot be shown not to have looked and cannot be shown to have seen nothing at the end of it, and the girl at the other end of that stone is not going to be told that he looked and he is not going to be thanked for it and neither of them is going to be told anything.** **And the decision about whether a woman of twenty-four takes a colleague's judgment on a thing like this is still unmade, still not on the page, and no row of this section may be read as having made it.** The pull to resolve it is the correct sign that the volume is working.
