## The one thing nobody has been told in this batch after the repair, and the one decision that is still not made

**Nobody has been told that they were in a room. Not once, in any of the ten chapters, not in a way that could be softened into a hint, and not by the narrator.** The repair removed a line in which the foreman referred to the sentence out loud, because a character who refers to it is a character who is placed as knowing, and that was the only breach of this standing on the page and it is gone. What is permitted happened: a person was in a room and did not know it, and is not going to be told, and the reader is left holding the mechanism — a finger on a corner of a page, a hand taken off a piece of wood, a pair of hands in a lap through a whole ordinary day. **And the decision about whether a woman of twenty-four takes a colleague's judgment on a thing like this is still unmade, still not on the page, and no row of this section may be read as having made it.** The pull to resolve it is the correct sign that the volume is working, and the last movement of this volume is the one the outline of record has already given its own smallest thing, which is a refusal that cannot be recorded.
