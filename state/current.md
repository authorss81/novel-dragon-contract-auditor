## What Batch 0001 Did

Chapters 1–10 are complete finished prose, a little over 40,000 words. The Venn packet is certified wrong, the source leaf is produced in a court, Tavi Venn's personal debt is void on the twelfth, the field is preserved until the second month, when the seven-year term expires, Sivra Oris's gate and navigation duty survives, the bank works are moved to the district water office by the fifth term, the fourth term requires a separate signature from anyone who assumes a continuing duty, and a general public-necessity sentence is recorded. That sentence is read at two o'clock and the previously unfiled Dunn summary is filed on it in the same minute, before the five terms are ruled at twenty past three, which is the mini-arc reversal: the local win creates the second household, and it does so while the court is still working on the first one. Corvin Dray is arrested after his ledger is copied. A hand-signed nine-paragraph audit request naming the First Seat form is in Examinations custody with a stamped number and no scope.

The whole batch sits inside one month, the ninth, and runs from the seventh to the eighteenth of it. The calendar is fixed in `state/continuity.md` and must be used rather than re-derived. Three facts are easy to get wrong and are load-bearing: the source leaf lives in the Notaries' Table's cabinet in Auremar and a junior clerk cannot get it out, so the rider Sarn sends on the court's custody slip is the only reason it reached Mosswake; the provincial hold's reference carries no office name until Tamsin names the First Seat seal in private in Chapter 6 and Rell names it aloud in open court in Chapter 7; and the ruling has five operative terms, not four.

Marek's first Roll-Answer appears once, in Chapter 8, earned by exercising the limited audit at the hearing and taken against the operative filed record after the ruling. It names the present holder of the field, not the girl whose debt the same ruling voided. He pays in fatigue, an ash taste, a persistent headache, and a temporary inability to lie about one named obligation. The first time that inability is shown is in Chapter 9, when Mara Venn asks in her own yard whether anything left in the file can come back for them and he cannot answer; the second and third are on the Auremar quay in Chapter 10.
