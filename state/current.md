## The two standing rules this compaction changed the reading of, and they are the whole of what a next writer needs to know about them

**One: a correction is not deleted, and a record is not carried.** The doctrine this project has written in eleven guises — the wrong claim named beside the right one and deleted nowhere — is right about the **manuscript** and about anything a reader could check, and it is the wrong tool for a **state layer**, because a state file is not a record of what was believed, it is a working surface for the next phase, and a working surface that grows by ninety kilobytes a batch is a working surface that eventually cannot be opened. **What is superseded now goes to `state/archive/`, whole and verbatim, under a provenance heading that names the source file and the line range.
