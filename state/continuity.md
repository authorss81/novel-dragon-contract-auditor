## Contract Rules

- Ordinary plain and scale contracts lose force when terms, assent, witness, record, or continuation is missing; a defect does not automatically void every ordinary contract.
- A foundational compact binds only named ratifying powers to publicly stated duties. It cannot create a perpetual office, inherited debt, or beneficiary for an unnamed future population.
- The Cinder Clause is an unlawful later proxy practice, not a new compact or a sentient mechanism. Its use can be preserved as evidence and may ground restitution for harm, but it cannot enlarge the Compact or create a new inherited duty by itself.

## Rules Established On The Page In Batch 0001

These are recorded because Chapters 1–10 turned planning into text, not because they are new mechanics.

- **The summary is the door.** A summary is what a later file copies from. Compressing a packet is ordinary practice and is where an alteration survives; a court acting on a summary acts on the copy, not on the source leaf. The Chapter 8 reading is the proof of this in the reader's hands.
- **A witness mark is a device with a copy in it.** A notch in the left margin records who was standing in the witness line. Two means two were heard. A third is cut at a different angle because the third person stood differently. A notch that is cleaner than the original, or at the wrong height, is a copied notch. Third notches were sold as a service, at a fixed height, for a fixed fee.
- **A true seal is laid, not pressed.** A true seal is made from a scale impression, spoken assent, and a witness record; it takes about eleven seconds and the holder is present throughout. Its rim is uneven. A cold press mark in a workshop with a screw press is a copied seal, and a copied image is not a true seal. This is how Corvin's certificate is broken.
- **A general paper can shelter a particular one.** A district flood memorandum that authorizes bank works and names no parcel is a real document, and anything filed under it in the following day inherits its standing. The memorandum dated the eighth of the eighth month and the correction received on the ninth of the eighth month are a day and a night apart, and the memorandum is the schedule, not the authority.
- **A form with a run on it is not a person.** *Continuity Filings, Flood Seasons, Categorisation* requires a flood-season continuity filing to be entered in the routine column and not itemised or heard individually except on quarterly review. It does nothing and creates nothing. It arranges that a class of correction arrives already inside a rule. Its print run implies every court that files a flood docket, which is the Volume 01 volume-level question and is not answered in this batch.
- **A public-necessity sentence travels by being quoted.** A court that needs to act fast in a flood will say a general thing, and clerks in a hurry will copy it. The sentence is the first portable institutional wording of the inheritance echo; the copied Dunn summary shows the vehicle before the court supplies the authority; the authority then makes it operative. This is the mechanism Marek's narrow win turns into a second household.
- **A reading is only evidence once a recognized body admits the record.** The Chapter 8 reading was taken against the exhibit as the court had just amended it, with the ruling in the same sleeve, and it was entered in the minute book and copied into the widow's hand by Sarn's direction on Marek's request.
- **Ordinary competence defeats a forger.** The witness order was exposed by memory plus a register that lacked a third issue plus a raking lamp. The broker's missing leaves were exposed by a docket count and a boy's habit. The file number was preserved by a young intake clerk writing it in ink and initialling it. None of this is magic and all of it wins cases.


## Succession and the Cinder Proxy

- The original First Custodian is a human, temporary public office, limited to seven years, with no enforceable inherited asset or right at appointment. Halden Ro is the sitting lawful Custodian in the final crisis; his term expires during the convocation.
- The office is chosen at a public succession convocation by at least five of nine human provinces, five of nine dragon Seats, and three of six independent civic assemblies. The First Seat may call a convocation but cannot appoint alone.
- During a vacancy, the Emberroot Interim Keepers are two human record keepers, one dragon notary, and one civic witness selected by the affected local court, recognized dragon seat, and nearest civic assembly. They may maintain existing duties and immediate relief for no more than forty-five days, but they cannot create or transfer a perpetual duty, appoint a successor, or invoke the Cinder proxy route. The First Seat alone cannot select all of them or fill the office.
- The Cinder route pairs a First Seat seal with a human who has a recorded inherited claim, names the human a proxy guarantor, and claims control behind the proxy. Seryn Oris cannot hold the original human office; she seeks permanent First-Seat control behind a controlled proxy.
- After Halden's term expires, the First Custodian is vacant. Only a new 5/5/3-of-6 convocation could appoint a temporary successor; the final Covenant records that none will be appointed and transfers the maintenance function to rotating Roll Assemblies and a bounded Interim Continuity Council.

## Volume 1 Case

- Aldren Venn signed a fixed seven-year water-and-field instrument. Mara witnessed the original and did not sign a later continuation, correction, or inheritance stipulation.
- The later correction substitutes “the present holder” for the named signer and routes Aldren's winter-seed debt to the field's current occupant. The substituted sentence on the page reads, in part, that the field-side upkeep and the winter-seed repayment, “being a work of the ground and not of a person,” shall be held against the field, the present holder answering for the same “until full review, the named signer having ceased.”
- The original leaf, witness marks, ink, seal pressure, and filing sequence prove the correction was added later. Tamsin preserves a comparison copy. Established on the page: the leaf carries Aldren's double-reed shorthand, two ragged notches, an embossed signature, and the requirement that anyone assuming a continuing duty sign a new obligation.
- The restored ruling clears Tavi's personal debt, preserves the field during the term, keeps Sivra Oris's gate and navigation duty, and requires a separate signed obligation for a successor. As ruled on the twelfth: the personal debt is void; the channel and lower field are preserved to Aldren's household for the remaining three years and one flood season against any creditor, tenant, surety, or assignee; the gate and navigation duty remain with Sivra Oris unaffected; any future assumer of a continuing field-side duty must sign a new obligation; and the district water office must perform the bank works and the gate reset under its own flood authority and not under the instrument, with the household not required to perform the field-side upkeep for the purposes of the order.
- The court's broad public-necessity sentence is applied to Orra and Nessa Dunn: “Where immediate public necessity requires a recorded continuation to prevent loss of food or shelter, the present holder of the burdened property may be joined as guarantor until full review.” The Dunn summary is the vehicle; the sentence supplies the authority that makes it operative. This is the Volume 1 mini-arc reversal and the first visible inheritance echo. The formal volume midpoint arrives in Chapters 19–25, when the sentence is traced to a higher administrative template. **On the page:** the Mosswake registrar filed the previously unfiled Dunn summary on the authority of the sentence within the hour of the ruling, on the same afternoon.
- The Venn instrument entered its fourth year when Aldren died, so three years and one flood season remain on the term. “For the fixed term” is therefore a real limit, not a formality.
- **The ruling's stated limits, which later batches must respect:** the ruling decides the Venn file and nothing else. It does not void the correction, does not open other files, and does not name who wrote the sentence. Its reach is exactly the fourth term.
