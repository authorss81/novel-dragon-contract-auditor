The climax is not fifth because a person cannot become a party twice, and a person who has already become one is a resolution, and a resolution in a fifth movement is the failure the arrangement forbids; **Movement Four is the last place a person can do the thing and have it still be a cost.** **And the standing for both: a phase that finds the page has put one of them somewhere else names that in the state layer with the page's position beside the outline's, and does not edit the outline.**

## Five, the three things the volume may not do, which are the volume's own and not inherited

*One:* **no hearing and no arrangement of one, and no new form for anything — and in particular not a new form for a person having been in a room, and not a new form for a refusal, because there is not one and this volume's finding is that there is not going to be one.** *Two:* **no House, no seat and no office is named in the volume in the outline's own voice, and no new final enemy is introduced.** The series plan's block for this volume names one, and that person is already on the closed page at the foot of a notice in two closed files in an early volume, is treated in one of them as a seal with a person written under it and nothing else, and is not in this volume, and the name is not used. *Three:* **no romance, nothing implying one, no quiet mutual commitment, and no moment in a doorway, and no second person who is a second ear because of anything except standing there.** No romance has begun in any volume of this project, the ending of record registers a domestic partnership through a clear agreement at a final hearing in Volume 12, **and that arc is not refused by the outline and is not paid early by it.**

**And a fourth, and it is the one that will be broken by accident, because telling somebody they were in a room is not asking them anything and feels like neither: a person being told that they were in a room is forbidden. What is permitted is a person being in a room and not knowing it, and the whole of the volume is that the second person is not told and is never going to be told.**

## Six, the rooms, and what is walked zero times

The volume's six rooms are the same six the closed page has already been inside, and the list with what may not be touched in each is in the outline of record and is not restated here. **A chapter may not open a room that is not on that list without a reason that is a person's habit and not a plan's geography.** **Either road and the four hundred yards of cold flags are each walked zero times in this volume**, the walking of all three is on the page as something that has already happened, and a person who has walked one of them says nothing about what was said at the other end of it. **And the room with the rail at the far end of the four hundred yards has no person in it in this volume, it stays shut, and the woman of about forty with a scuttle is in no chapter of it: the page has given her two rooms, one lamp, one cloth, twice a week, one true thing said out loud and no history at all, and a chapter that put her in a third room would be writing her out of the page and into the plan.**

## Seven, what the outline did not touch, in one paragraph

It did not resolve the arrangement in any room. It did not open the question with no full stop, the box of about four hundred blanks, the drawer with four things in it, the sixteen volumes, the two sealed pages, or the coat. It did not exercise or retire an undertaking or say its date. It did not fill in a ruled space under a printed heading, turn a day-book round, rule a fifth column, date a coming or count one, or find the form of a shed void. It did not print the number in a man's head, the figure at the end of the cold passage, the figure on the bill at Lowcross, the count of things asked in a chapter, the name at the foot of a struck line, the name of the hand that struck it, the disputed family claim, or what is written on any piece of paper in this matter. It did not walk either road or the four hundred yards. It did not describe a hearing, the arrangement of one, a notice, a post, a commission, a warrant, an office, a new heading over anything, or a new form for anything. It named no House, no seat and no office in its own voice. **It did not make the decision about whether a woman of twenty-four takes her colleague's judgment, in either direction, and no phase may record that it did.** It did not discharge a debt, resolve a thread, renumber a ledger, close an open item or add one.

## Eight, the standing locks, and none of them is touched by the outline

**No romance has begun in ten volumes and none begins in this one. No walk is paid twice and the four hundred and thirty miles is walked zero times in this volume. Nobody is thanked, nobody is forgiven, nobody is sent for, and nothing is resolved. The count of things asked out loud is seven and does not move. The count of times a person has said a thing in a room is not recomputed inside one movement and is not asserted in a chapter. A number that appears in three chapters of a volume is a number that cannot be recomputed inside one movement. A second hearer is never added to a closed event. An absolute about a person is not built on, and where a closed chapter holds an absolute that its own scene contradicts, the later prose wins and the earlier absolute is carried and named. A calendar month name and a weekday name go into nothing this project writes, and the one false positive is the modal verb *may*. A year name is written out in full at every occurrence and abbreviated nowhere. The four debts are owed and this phase discharged none of them.**

**And the one lock this volume adds, which is the one it is about: a person who has been heard is a person who cannot be shown having agreed to anything and cannot be shown having refused anything, and there is no form anywhere in this empire in which a person is entered as the one who heard it, and a chapter that enters one has written a form and left this page and entered the plan's.**
