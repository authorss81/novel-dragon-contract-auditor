# Chapter 9003: Planted C

It is the second day of the second week of the third month of the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after the year after next, and it is the second hour, and the hours at that counter are the second to the sixth.

The shed at the end of the lane off the Slade is about nine foot by eleven and there is a chain on the front bench.

The counters were not yet down and the case had not been lifted off its shelf behind them.
